## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Flameless Taper Candles With Remote Control?



Flameless taper candles with remote control are battery-operated LED candles. They mimic real wax candles with flickering 3D flames. The remote allows easy on/off, dimming, and timer settings for convenience and safety.



### How Long Do Battery-operated Flameless Taper Candles Last?



Battery life depends on usage and battery type. Typically, these candles last 100 to 200 hours on three AA batteries. Using the timer function helps conserve battery life by automatically turning candles off.



### Are Flameless Taper Candles Safe For Home Use?
