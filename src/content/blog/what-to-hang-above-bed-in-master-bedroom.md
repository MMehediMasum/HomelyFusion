**Your master bedroom should be a place where you feel calm and inspired. But what do you hang above your bed to make that happen?**

Choosing the right piece can change the entire mood of the room. You want something that reflects your style, adds comfort, and maybe even sparks joy every time you see it. If you’ve been staring at a blank wall or unsure how to decorate this important space, keep reading.

We’ll explore simple, smart ideas that will transform your bedroom and make it truly yours.




## Choose The Right Art

Choosing art for above your bed sets the tone for your master bedroom. The right piece adds personality and style.

Consider size, color, and theme to match your room’s look and feel. Pick art that makes you feel calm and happy.

### Large Statement Pieces

A big art piece draws the eye and anchors the room. It works well if your bedroom walls are plain or neutral.

Choose a piece that is wide enough to fit above your bed without crowding the space. Bold colors or detailed images can create a strong focus.

### Gallery Walls

Gallery walls mix smaller frames to create a personal and lively look. You can include photos, prints, or small paintings.

* Pick a theme or color scheme to keep it balanced.
* Arrange frames before hanging to find the best layout.
* Use different frame styles for interest but stay consistent in size.
* Leave enough space between frames to avoid clutter.

### Abstract Designs

Abstract art adds a modern and creative touch to your bedroom. It can bring energy or calm depending on colors and shapes.

| Color Palette | Mood | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cool Blues and Greens | Calm | Relaxing and peaceful |
| Bright Reds and Oranges | Energetic | Invigorates and excites |
| Soft Pastels | Gentle | Soothes and comforts |

## Add Mirrors For Depth

Framed mirrors give a classic and polished look. The frame can match your bedroom furniture or add a pop of color.

* Keep spacing even for balance

### Unique Shapes And Styles

Mirrors come in many shapes like ovals, hexagons, or sunbursts. These add character and style above the bed.

Choose a shape that fits your room’s vibe. Unique mirrors become a focal point and make the space feel special.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are The Best Art Pieces To Hang Above A Master Bed?

Choose large framed prints, abstract art, or serene landscapes. These add style and create a calming focal point in your bedroom.

### Can Mirrors Be Hung Above The Bed In Master Bedrooms?

Yes, mirrors can enhance light and space. Use decorative or vintage mirrors but avoid placing them directly above the bed.

### How To Choose The Right Size Wall Decor Above The Bed?

Select decor that spans about two-thirds the width of your bed. This balance creates visual harmony and avoids overcrowding the wall.

### Is It Okay To Hang Shelves Above The Bed?

Yes, floating shelves can work well. Use them for small plants or decor, but ensure they’re securely installed for safety.
