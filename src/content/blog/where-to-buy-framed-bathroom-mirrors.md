## Tips For Choosing The Right Mirror

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=picking-the-right-framed-bathroom-mirror-makes-your-space-look-better.-it-also-helps-with-daily-routines.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Picking the right framed bathroom mirror makes your space look better. It also helps with daily routines.

Think about size, style, and where you want to put the mirror before buying.

### Size And Placement

Choose a mirror size that fits your bathroom wall space. A mirror too big or small can look odd.

Place the mirror where it reflects light and is easy to use. Above the sink is common.

* Measure your wall before buying
* Leave space around the mirror
* Think about mirror height for all users

### Frame Material Matters

Bathroom mirrors face moisture. Pick frame materials that resist water and do not warp.

Common materials include wood, metal, and plastic. Each has a different look and durability.

* Wood frames add warmth but need sealing
* Metal frames are strong and modern
* Plastic frames resist water and are affordable

### Matching Bathroom Decor

Choose a mirror frame that matches your bathroom style and colors. This creates a nice look.

Think about other fixtures like faucets and towel bars to keep a consistent style.

* Rustic mirrors fit farmhouse or vintage bathrooms
* Shiny metal suits modern or industrial spaces
* Simple frames work well in minimal designs

### Budget Considerations

Set a budget before shopping. Mirrors come in many prices based on size and materials.

Look for quality within your price range. Sometimes spending a little more saves money later.

* Compare prices from different stores
* Check for sales or discounts
* Consider DIY framing for savings
