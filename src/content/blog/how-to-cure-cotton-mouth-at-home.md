## Causes Of Cotton Mouth

Cotton mouth happens when your mouth feels very dry. It can be uncomfortable and make it hard to talk or eat.

Many things can cause cotton mouth. Knowing the causes helps you treat it at home.

### Dehydration Effects

Dehydration is a common cause of cotton mouth. When your body loses too much water, your mouth dries out.

Not drinking enough fluids or sweating a lot can lead to dehydration. This reduces saliva, causing dry mouth.

### Medication Side Effects

Some medicines can cause cotton mouth as a side effect. These include allergy pills, antidepressants, and painkillers.

These medications reduce saliva production, making your mouth feel dry and sticky.

* Antihistamines
* Antidepressants
* Diuretics
* Muscle relaxants

### Lifestyle Factors

Some habits and lifestyle choices can cause cotton mouth. Smoking and drinking alcohol are common reasons.

Stress and anxiety also reduce saliva flow. Poor oral hygiene can make dry mouth worse.

* Smoking tobacco or vaping
* Drinking alcohol
* High stress levels
* Not brushing teeth regularly
