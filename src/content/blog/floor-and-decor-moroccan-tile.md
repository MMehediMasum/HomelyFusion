## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Moroccan Tile Stickers Made Of?



Moroccan tile stickers are usually made from waterproof vinyl material. This makes them durable, oil-proof, and easy to clean. They are designed for peel-and-stick application, allowing easy installation and removal without damaging surfaces.



### Can Moroccan Tile Decals Be Used In Bathrooms?



Yes, Moroccan tile decals are waterproof and oil-proof. They are perfect for bathrooms, kitchens, and laundry rooms. Their self-adhesive feature makes them ideal for backsplash and wall décor in moist environments.



### Are Moroccan Tile Stickers Reusable Or Removable?



Most Moroccan tile stickers are removable but not reusable. They peel off easily without leaving residue. However, reusing them might reduce adhesive strength and affect their appearance.
