## Common Shelf Types In Living Rooms

Shelves in living rooms serve both practical and decorative purposes. They hold books, photos, and decor items while adding style to the space.

There are many shelf types used in living rooms. Each type fits different wall spaces and design needs.

### Floating Shelves

Floating shelves have no visible brackets. They look like they float on the wall. These shelves create a clean and modern look.

### Built-in Shelves

Built-in shelves are fixed inside walls or nooks. They maximize space and often blend with the room’s architecture. These shelves add a custom and sturdy feel.

### Corner Shelves

Corner shelves fit into room corners. They use space that often stays empty. These shelves come in various shapes and sizes.

* Triangle-shaped shelves
* Curved corner shelves
* Tiered corner units

### Wall-mounted Shelves

Wall-mounted shelves attach directly to the wall with brackets or supports. They hold heavier items and come in many styles and materials.

| Floating Shelves | Hidden brackets | Modern, minimal |

## Installation Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-shelf-high-in-the-living-room-is-often-called-a-floating-shelf-or-a-wall-shelf.-installing-it-properly-helps-keep-your-items-safe-and-your-wall-strong.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The shelf high in the living room is often called a floating shelf or a wall shelf. Installing it properly helps keep your items safe and your wall strong.

Good installation saves you from accidents and makes your living room look neat. Follow these tips for the best results.

### Choosing The Right Wall

Pick a strong wall for your shelf. Drywall alone may not hold heavy items. Look for studs behind the wall to support the shelf.

Use a stud finder to locate these wooden supports. Installing the shelf on studs makes it safe and stable.

### Safety And Weight Limits

Know the shelf’s weight limit before installation. Do not overload the shelf with heavy objects. Overloading can cause it to fall.

Always follow the manufacturer’s weight guidelines. Keep heavy items closer to the wall for better balance.

* Check shelf weight limit on the package
* Use lightweight decorations on the shelf
* Place heavy items near the wall
* Do not hang from the shelf

### Tools And Hardware Needed

Gather all tools before starting. You need a stud finder, drill, level, screws, and wall anchors. These help secure the shelf safely.

Using the right hardware is important. Wall anchors are needed if you cannot find studs. They keep the screws tight in drywall.

* Stud finder
* Drill with drill bits
* Level to check straightness
* Screws and wall anchors
* Measuring tape
