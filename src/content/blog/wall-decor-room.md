## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Using Artificial Wall Plants?



Artificial wall plants add greenery without maintenance. They stay fresh-looking year-round and are perfect for low-light rooms. They enhance boho and modern decor styles effortlessly. Plus, they are allergy-free and ideal for busy lifestyles or rental spaces.



### How Does Double-sided Tape Work For Wall Decor?



Double-sided tape sticks securely to most surfaces without nails. It’s ideal for hanging lightweight decor like posters or artificial plants. Heavy-duty variants hold heavier items firmly. It’s easy to remove without damaging walls, making it renter-friendly and convenient for seasonal decor changes.



### What Makes Wooden Wall Vases A Stylish Decor Choice?



Wooden wall vases add rustic charm and warmth to any room. Their natural texture complements farmhouse and boho themes beautifully. They hold dried or artificial flowers and save floor space. These vases create a cozy, inviting atmosphere, perfect for entryways or living rooms.



### How Do 3d Butterfly Wall Decals Enhance Room Decor?
