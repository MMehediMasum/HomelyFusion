## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Lamp Ideal For Reading?



An ideal reading lamp offers adjustable brightness and color temperature. It reduces eye strain with even light distribution. Flexible gooseneck designs help direct light precisely. USB charging ports or rechargeable batteries add convenience. Dimmable settings customize light intensity for comfort during long reading sessions.



### How Does Adjustable Color Temperature Benefit Reading Lamps?



Adjustable color temperature lets you switch between warm and cool light. Warm light is relaxing, while cool light improves focus. This feature reduces eye fatigue and adapts to different times of day. Lamps like luckystyle and BOHON provide customizable color settings for better reading comfort.



### Why Choose Rechargeable Clip-on Book Lights?



Rechargeable clip-on book lights offer portability and convenience. They attach easily to books or desks without cords. Long battery life, like 80-hour runtime in Gritin models, ensures uninterrupted reading. Multiple brightness and color modes protect eyes and suit various lighting needs.



### Are Floor Lamps Better Than Desk Lamps For Reading?



Floor lamps provide broad, adjustable lighting and save desk space. They often feature height and angle adjustments, ideal for living rooms or offices. Desk lamps offer focused light for close-up tasks. Choose based on your reading environment and space preferences for maximum comfort.



### What Is The Benefit Of A Dimmable Reading Lamp?



Dimmable lamps allow you to control light intensity for eye comfort. They prevent glare and reduce eye strain during extended reading. Models like O’Bright and Dimunt offer full-range dimming. This feature helps create the perfect ambiance for various lighting needs.



### How Important Is Eye-care Technology In Reading Lamps?



Eye-care technology minimizes flicker and harsh glare to protect vision. It reduces eye fatigue during long reading periods. Lamps with this feature, such as Gritin and BOHON, use LED lights with steady brightness. This ensures safer, more comfortable reading experiences.
