## Visiting Showrooms

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=visiting-showrooms-is-a-great-way-to-find-a-bathroom-vanity-near-you.-you-can-see-different-styles-and-designs-in-person.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Visiting showrooms is a great way to find a bathroom vanity near you. You can see different styles and designs in person. This helps you choose the best vanity for your bathroom.

Showrooms let you compare materials and finishes up close. You can also get advice from staff. This makes shopping easier and more confident.

### What To Look For

Look for vanities that fit your bathroom style. Check if the design matches your taste. Notice the type of wood or material used.

See if the vanity has enough storage space. Look at drawer and cabinet sizes. Make sure the handles and knobs feel good to use.

### Testing Quality And Finish

Touch the vanity to check its quality. Feel the smoothness of the finish. Look for any scratches or dents on the surface.

Open and close drawers to test how they work. Check the sturdiness by pressing lightly on the cabinet. Good quality vanities last longer and stay nice.

* Finish should be smooth and even
* Drawers and doors must open and close easily
* Materials should feel solid and durable

### Checking Available Sizes

Measure your bathroom space before visiting. Bring the measurements with you. This helps in choosing the right size vanity.

Ask showroom staff about size options. Check if they have vanities that fit small or large bathrooms. Make sure the vanity fits well without crowding the space.

* Measure width, depth, and height of your space
* Look for vanities that fit your bathroom layout
* Consider extra space for plumbing and cleaning
