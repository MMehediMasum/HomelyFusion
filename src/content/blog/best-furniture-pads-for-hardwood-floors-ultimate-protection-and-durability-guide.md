## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Felt Pads For Hardwood Floors?



The best felt pads for hardwood floors include Scotch Felt Pads, Felt Furniture Pads X-PROTECTOR, and SoftTouch pads. They offer self-stick designs and come in assorted sizes, protecting floors from scratches, dents, and nicks effectively.



### How Do Self-stick Felt Pads Protect Hardwood Floors?



Self-stick felt pads create a soft barrier between furniture legs and floors. This reduces friction and prevents scratches, dents, and scuffs on hardwood surfaces. They are easy to apply and stay firmly in place for long-lasting protection.



### Can Felt Pads Be Reused On Hardwood Floors?



Most felt pads, like SuperSliders and Ezprotekt movers, are reusable. They slide furniture smoothly without damaging floors. However, self-stick pads generally stick once and may need replacement after removal.



### Are Nail-on Felt Pads Better For Heavy Furniture?



Nail-on heavy-duty felt pads provide stronger attachment for heavy furniture. They prevent shifting and protect hardwood floors from dents and scratches better than self-stick pads on bulky items.



### What Sizes Of Felt Pads Are Ideal For Furniture Legs?



Assorted sizes from 1" to 2. 5" round or square felt pads fit various furniture legs. Choosing the right size ensures full coverage and optimal floor protection without affecting furniture stability.



### How Often Should I Replace Felt Pads On Hardwood Floors?



Replace felt pads every 6 to 12 months or when they show wear. Worn pads lose cushioning, risking floor damage. Regular checks help maintain hardwood floor protection.
