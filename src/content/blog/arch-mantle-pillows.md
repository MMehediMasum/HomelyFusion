## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In Arch Mantle Pillows?



Arch Mantle Pillows are crafted from soft, durable materials like plush lambswool, boucle, velvet, and cotton linen. These fabrics ensure comfort and long-lasting use. Handmade knotted and embroidered designs add aesthetic value. The pillows are ideal for home décor and provide a cozy, stylish touch.



### How Do Arch Mantle Pillows Enhance Home Décor?



These pillows add texture, color, and style to any room. Their unique knotted, pleated, or embroidered designs create visual interest. They complement sofas, beds, chairs, and mantles. Available in neutral tones and vibrant accents, they fit various interior styles, including farmhouse, modern, and vintage.



### Are Arch Mantle Pillows Easy To Clean?



Most Arch Mantle Pillows come with removable covers for easy washing. Pillow covers made of cotton linen or polyester can be machine-washed on gentle cycles. Spot cleaning is recommended for delicate knotted or embroidered designs. Always check care labels for specific instructions to maintain pillow quality.



### What Sizes Do Arch Mantle Pillows Come In?



Arch Mantle Pillows are available in multiple sizes, including 8 to 20 inches in diameter or length. Options range from small round pillows (8-14 inches) to larger lumbar pillows (12x20 inches). This variety allows you to choose pillows that best fit your furniture and design needs.



### Can Arch Mantle Pillows Provide Lumbar Support?



Yes, certain Arch Mantle Pillows, especially rectangular lumbar pillows, offer firm back support. Their plush filling and shape help relieve pressure on the lower back. These pillows are perfect for sofas, chairs, or beds, combining comfort with decorative appeal for everyday use.
