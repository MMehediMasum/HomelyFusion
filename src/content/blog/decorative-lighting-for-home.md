## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Types Of Decorative Lighting Suit Living Rooms?



Tabletop bonsai tree lights and floor lamps add warmth and style. Warm white LED lights create cozy ambiance. String lights also enhance festive or everyday decor. Choose battery or USB-operated options for convenience and flexibility.



### How To Choose Battery Vs. Plug-in Decorative Lights?



Battery-operated lights offer portability and no wiring hassle. Plug-in lights provide continuous power for longer use. Consider location, ease of use, and desired brightness when selecting. Battery options suit temporary setups; plug-ins work well for permanent fixtures.



### Can Decorative Lights Improve Bedroom Ambiance?



Yes, warm white LED lamps and night lights create relaxing moods. Soft lighting helps with sleep and comfort. Adjustable brightness or color temperature lamps enhance personalization. Battery or USB-powered lamps add convenience near the bedside.



### Are String Lights Suitable For Outdoor Decoration?



Yes, fairy copper string lights with multiple modes are ideal outdoors. USB-powered and weather-resistant options enhance garden or patio aesthetics. They provide gentle illumination for parties, weddings, or everyday charm. Always check the product’s outdoor rating before use.



### What Are The Benefits Of Led Flameless Candles?



LED flameless candles offer safe, flickering warm light without fire risk. They include timers and remotes for easy control. Perfect for festivals, weddings, or home ambiance. Battery operation enables flexible placement anywhere without cords. They mimic real candles closely.
