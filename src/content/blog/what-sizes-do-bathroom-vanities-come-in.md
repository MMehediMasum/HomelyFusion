## Standard Vanity Sizes

Bathroom vanities come in many sizes to fit different spaces. Choosing the right size helps make your bathroom functional.

Standard vanity sizes vary by the number of sinks and room size. This guide covers common vanity size options.

### Single Sink Vanities

Single sink vanities are common in most bathrooms. They are simple and fit well in small to medium spaces.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vanity Width | Typical Depth | Height |
| 24 inches | 18 to 21 inches | 32 to 36 inches |
| 30 inches | 18 to 21 inches | 32 to 36 inches |
| 36 inches | 18 to 21 inches | 32 to 36 inches |
| 48 inches | 18 to 21 inches | 32 to 36 inches |

### Double Sink Vanities

Double sink vanities are larger and fit two sinks side by side. They work well for shared bathrooms.

1. 48 inches wide: Small double sinks in tight spaces
2. 60 inches wide: Standard size for most double vanities
3. 72 inches wide: Extra space for storage and comfort
4. 84 inches wide: Large bathrooms needing more surface area

### Compact Vanities For Small Bathrooms

Compact vanities save space in small bathrooms. They still offer storage and sink functionality.

**Common Compact Vanity Sizes:**

* 18 inches wide
* 20 inches wide
* 21 inches wide
* 24 inches wide

Depth usually ranges from 16 to 18 inches. Height is about 32 to 34 inches.

## Tips For Measuring Your Bathroom

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-size-bathroom-vanity-starts-with-good-measuring.-accurate-measurements-help-you-find-a-vanity-that-fits-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right size bathroom vanity starts with good measuring. Accurate measurements help you find a vanity that fits well.

Measuring your bathroom space carefully avoids problems during installation. Take your time and double-check numbers.

### Tools You Need

Use the right tools to get exact measurements. This makes the process faster and more accurate.

* Tape measure for length, width, and height
* Notepad and pen to write down numbers
* Level to check if floors and walls are even
* Laser measure for quick, precise distances
* Step stool to reach higher places

### Avoiding Common Mistakes

Many people make errors when measuring bathrooms. Avoid these common mistakes to get better results.

* Don’t measure only the floor space; include wall space too
* Measure twice to prevent errors
* Check for pipes, outlets, and door swings that affect space
* Remember to measure the height for tall vanities
* Note uneven walls or floors that might need adjustment

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are The Standard Bathroom Vanity Sizes Available?

Bathroom vanities commonly come in widths of 24", 30", 36", 48", 60", and 72". Heights typically range from 32" to 36". Depths vary between 18" and 24". These sizes fit most bathroom layouts, offering options for small to spacious bathrooms.

### How To Choose The Right Vanity Size For My Bathroom?

Measure your bathroom space carefully before selecting a vanity. Consider clearance for doors and traffic flow. Choose a size that balances storage needs and room size. Small bathrooms benefit from 24" to 30" vanities, while larger spaces can accommodate 48" or wider units.

### Do Double Sink Vanities Come In Standard Sizes?

Yes, double sink vanities usually start at 60" wide. Common widths are 60", 72", and 84". Depths and heights are similar to single vanities. These sizes provide ample space for two sinks and storage, perfect for shared bathrooms.

### Can I Get Custom-sized Bathroom Vanities?

Custom vanities are available for unique bathroom dimensions. They allow tailored width, height, and depth. This option suits irregular or small spaces. Custom vanities often cost more but maximize functionality and style in your bathroom.
