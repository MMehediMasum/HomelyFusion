Most Tj Maxx Christmas pillow covers are machine washable. Use a gentle cycle with cold water to preserve colors and fabric quality. Always check the care label for specific washing instructions to ensure long-lasting holiday decor.

Materials include soft faux fur, cotton burlap, plush fabric, and faux wool. These materials provide comfort and a festive look. They are skin-friendly and perfect for indoor holiday decoration on sofas and beds.



### Do These Pillows Come In Festive Designs?
