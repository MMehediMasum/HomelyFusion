### What Is The Best Cleaner For Luxury Vinyl Plank Floors?



Rejuvenate and Bona Pro Series are top cleaners designed for luxury vinyl plank floors. They gently clean without damage. These formulas remove dirt and revitalize the surface, ensuring a lasting shine and protection for your vinyl flooring.



### How Do Peel And Stick Vinyl Floor Tiles Work?



Peel and stick vinyl tiles come with an adhesive backing. Simply peel off the protective layer and press the tile onto a clean floor. They are easy to install, perfect for DIY projects, and ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and living areas.



### Can I Use A Rug Pad Under Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring?



Yes, non-slip rug pads like RUGPADUSA Vinyl Lock protect vinyl plank floors. They prevent slipping and cushion rugs without damaging the floor. This helps maintain floor integrity and adds comfort underfoot while securing rugs in place.



### Are Luxury Vinyl Plank Cleaners Safe For Pets?



Most luxury vinyl plank cleaners, like COREtec ENCORE and Quick Shine, are pet-safe. They use non-toxic, gentle ingredients that clean effectively without harmful fumes or residues. Always check product labels for specific pet safety information.



### How Often Should I Clean Luxury Vinyl Plank Floors?



Daily or weekly cleaning is recommended using a luxury vinyl floor cleaner. Light mopping removes dirt and prevents scratches. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive tools to maintain the floor’s finish and durability over time.
