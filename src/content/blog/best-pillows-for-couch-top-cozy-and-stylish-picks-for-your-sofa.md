## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Pillows Are Best For A Couch?



The ideal pillow size for couches is 18x18 inches. This size fits well and offers comfort. Many top options, like Utopia Bedding and EDOW, come in this size. It balances decoration and support without overwhelming the couch space.



### Are Down Alternative Pillows Good For Couch Use?



Yes, down alternative pillows are excellent for couches. They provide soft, hypoallergenic comfort and are easy to maintain. EDOW and Utopia Bedding offer lightweight polyester down alternatives that resist allergens and are machine washable, making them practical for everyday use.



### How Do Decorative Pillow Covers Enhance Couch Decor?



Decorative pillow covers add color, texture, and style to couches. Options like MIULEE and Fancy Homi offer farmhouse and boho designs that refresh your living space. Changing covers seasonally is an easy way to update your couch look without new pillows.



### Can Pillow Inserts Be Reused With Different Covers?



Yes, pillow inserts like those from Utopia Bedding and EDOW are reusable. You can swap covers seasonally or for style changes. Inserts maintain shape and comfort, making them versatile for various decorative pillow covers and home styles.



### Are Machine Washable Pillows Suitable For Couches?



Machine washable pillows are ideal for couches. They simplify cleaning and maintain hygiene. Brands like EDOW and Utopia Bedding provide washable pillow inserts and covers, ensuring durability and freshness for everyday use in living rooms.



### What Materials Are Best For Couch Pillow Covers?



Soft materials like velvet, chenille, and corduroy work best for couch pillow covers. MIULEE and Topfinel offer cozy, durable fabrics that complement various decor styles. These materials provide comfort, style, and easy maintenance for living room pillows.
