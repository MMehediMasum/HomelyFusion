## Steps To Set Xbox One As Home Console

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-your-xbox-one-as-the-home-console-lets-you-share-games-and-apps.-it-also-lets-others-use-your-xbox-live-gold-benefits.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting your Xbox One as the home console lets you share games and apps. It also lets others use your Xbox Live Gold benefits.

Follow these clear steps to set your Xbox One as your home console easily.

### Accessing Settings Menu

First, turn on your Xbox One and sign in. Press the Xbox button on your controller to open the guide.

Scroll right to find the “Profile & system” tab. Select “Settings” from the options shown.

### Navigating To Personalization

Inside the Settings menu, look for the “General” section. Click on it to expand more options.

Choose “Personalization” from the list. This is where you will find the home console settings.

### Selecting My Home Xbox Option

In the Personalization menu, select “My home Xbox.” This option controls your home console setup.

You will see a button to make the current Xbox your home console. Click this button to continue.

### Confirming The Home Console Setup

A confirmation message will appear asking if you want to set this console as your home Xbox. Choose “Yes” to confirm.

Once confirmed, your Xbox One is set as your home console. You can now share games and benefits with other users on this console.

## Managing Home Console Settings

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-your-xbox-one-as-the-home-console-lets-you-share-games-and-subscriptions.-you-can-control-which-console-is-your-home-to-enjoy-offline-play-and-shared-access.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting your Xbox One as the home console lets you share games and subscriptions. You can control which console is your home to enjoy offline play and shared access.

It is important to know how to change these settings and the limits that apply. This guide explains the main tasks for managing your home console.

### Changing Your Home Console

You can change your home console to a different Xbox One device. This is useful if you buy a new console or want to switch which device shares your games.

1. Sign in to your Xbox account on the new console.
2. Go to Settings, then select Personalization.
3. Choose My home Xbox and select Make this my home Xbox.
4. Confirm the change to set the new console as your home.

### Removing Home Console Designation

If you want to remove your current home console, you can do so by setting another console as your home or remotely removing the designation from your account online.

* Sign in on a different Xbox One and set it as your home console.
* Visit your Microsoft account online to remove the home console remotely.
* Note that removing the home console will stop sharing games and subscriptions on that device.

### Limitations On Number Of Changes

Microsoft limits how often you can change your home console. This helps prevent abuse and protects your account.

| Limit Type | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Changes Allowed | Up to 5 times per year |
| Reset Period | After one year, the limit resets |
| Exception | Microsoft can reset limits for special cases |

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-your-xbox-one-as-your-home-console-can-sometimes-cause-problems.-these-problems-can-stop-you-from-sharing-games-or-using-apps.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting your Xbox One as your home console can sometimes cause problems. These problems can stop you from sharing games or using apps.

This guide helps you fix common problems. You can try simple steps to solve errors and reset settings.

### Error Messages During Setup

You may see error messages when you try to set your Xbox One as your home console. These errors stop the setup from finishing.

Check your internet connection first. A weak or no connection can cause errors. Make sure you are signed in with the correct Microsoft account.

* Verify your internet is working well
* Sign in with the account linked to your games
* Restart your console and try again
* Check if Xbox Live services are online

### Problems With Game Sharing

Game sharing lets you share games and apps with other users on your home console. Sometimes, game sharing may not work as expected.

Issues may happen if you set more than one home console or if your account is signed in on multiple consoles. Also, some games may need updates to work properly.

* Ensure only one Xbox is set as your home console
* Update games and apps before sharing
* Sign out from other consoles to avoid conflicts
* Check Xbox Live status for any service problems

### Resetting Home Console Settings

If problems continue, resetting your home console settings can help. This removes the current home console status from your Xbox.

After resetting, you can set your Xbox One as the home console again. This can fix issues caused by account or software errors.

* Go to Settings > Personalization > My home Xbox
* Select "Remove this as my home Xbox"
* Restart your console
* Set the Xbox One as your home console again
