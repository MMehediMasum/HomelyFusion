## Festive Pumpkin Spice Latte Martini

The Pumpkin Spice Latte Martini is a perfect holiday treat. It blends creamy coffee flavors with warm pumpkin spice. This drink adds a festive touch to any gathering.

This cocktail is easy to make and tastes delicious. It combines pumpkin, spices, and a splash of alcohol for a cozy flavor. It works well as a holiday party drink or a special dessert.

### Ingredients Needed

* 2 oz vanilla vodka
* 1 oz pumpkin spice liqueur
* 1 oz espresso or strong coffee
* 1 oz cream or milk
* 1 tbsp pumpkin puree
* 1/2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
* Ice cubes
* Whipped cream for garnish
* Ground cinnamon or nutmeg

### How To Prepare

1. Fill a shaker with ice cubes.
2. Add vanilla vodka, pumpkin spice liqueur, espresso, cream, pumpkin puree, and pumpkin pie spice.
3. Shake well until the mixture is cold and frothy.
4. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
5. Top with whipped cream and sprinkle cinnamon or nutmeg.

### Serving Tips

**Serve your Pumpkin Spice Latte Martini with:**

* Ginger snap cookies
* Cinnamon sticks for stirring
* Caramel drizzle on top
* Festive glassware for holiday cheer

### Brewing The Coffee Base

Start by making strong coffee. Use fresh coffee beans for a rich flavor. Brew about half a cup of espresso or strong coffee.

Let the coffee cool to room temperature before mixing. Hot coffee will dilute the martini ingredients.

* Grind fresh coffee beans
* Brew espresso or strong coffee (½ cup)
* Cool coffee to room temperature

### Mixing The Martini

Pour the cooled coffee into a shaker. Add pumpkin spice syrup, vodka, and Irish cream liqueur. Fill the shaker with ice.

Shake well for 20 seconds to mix and chill. Strain into a chilled martini glass carefully.

* ½ cup cooled coffee
* 1 oz pumpkin spice syrup
* 1.5 oz vodka
* 1 oz Irish cream liqueur
* Ice cubes

### Garnishing Tips

Garnish your martini to add flavor and style. Use whipped cream on top for creaminess. Sprinkle pumpkin spice or cinnamon powder over the cream.

Add a cinnamon stick or a small piece of pumpkin for decoration. Serve immediately while cold.

* Whipped cream on top
* Sprinkle pumpkin spice or cinnamon
* Cinnamon stick or pumpkin piece
* Serve cold

### What Ingredients Are Needed For A Pumpkin Spice Latte Martini?
