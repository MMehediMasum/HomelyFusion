## Common Names For Living Room Tables

Living room tables come in different styles and names. These tables serve various purposes and add to the room's look.

Here are some common types of living room tables and what they are called.

### Coffee Tables

Coffee tables sit in front of the sofa. They are low tables used for drinks, books, or decorations. Most living rooms have a coffee table as a centerpiece.

### Side Tables

Side tables are small tables placed beside chairs or sofas. They hold lamps, drinks, or remote controls. These tables help keep things within easy reach.

### End Tables

End tables are similar to side tables but usually sit at the ends of a sofa. They provide extra surface space and often match the coffee table.

* Hold lamps or decorations
* Offer storage with drawers or shelves
* Match other living room furniture

### Console Tables

Console tables are narrow and long. They often stand against a wall or behind a sofa. These tables display photos, plants, or keys.

| Table Type | Common Use | Typical Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coffee Table | Center space for drinks and decor | In front of sofa |
| Side Table | Hold lamps and small items | Beside chairs or sofas |
| End Table | Extra surface and storage | At sofa ends |
| Console Table | Display items and hold keys | Against a wall or behind sofa |

Glass tables look modern and sleek. They make rooms feel more open and bright.

* Easy to clean

* Adds elegance to any room

### Metal

Metal tables are sturdy and stylish. They fit well in industrial or modern homes.

* Very durable and strong

### Mixed Materials

Mixed materials combine two or more types like wood and metal or glass and wood.

* Combines strength and beauty

* Balances warmth and coolness
* Fits various decorating styles

Protect your table from scratches and stains. Use coasters for drinks. Place mats under hot dishes.
