## King Bed Dimensions

Knowing the size of a king bed helps you decide if it fits your room. A king bed is large and comfortable for two people.

This article explains king bed dimensions and if a 10x12 bedroom fits a king size bed.

### Standard King Size Measurements

A standard king bed is also called an Eastern king. It is one of the largest bed sizes available.

The width of a standard king bed is 76 inches. The length is 80 inches.

* Width: 76 inches (6 feet 4 inches)
* Length: 80 inches (6 feet 8 inches)

This size gives plenty of space for two adults to sleep comfortably.

### Variations In King Bed Sizes

King beds come in different sizes to fit various rooms and needs. Some variations are wider or longer.

| King Size Type | Width (inches) | Length (inches) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard King (Eastern) | 76 | 80 |
| California King | 72 | 84 |
| Split King | 76 (two 38") | 80 |

A California king is longer but narrower than a standard king. It fits tall people better.

A split king is two twin XL beds placed side by side. It allows different mattress firmness on each side.
