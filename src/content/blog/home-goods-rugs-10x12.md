## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of A Washable 10x12 Area Rug?



Washable 10x12 rugs are easy to clean and maintain. They resist stains and odors, making them ideal for homes with kids or pets. Their large size covers floors well, adding comfort and style to living rooms, bedrooms, or offices.



### How Does A Low Pile Rug Suit High Traffic Areas?



Low pile rugs are durable and easy to clean, perfect for high traffic spaces. They resist wear and tear better than shaggy rugs. These rugs maintain their appearance longer and provide a smooth, safe surface for daily use in busy rooms.



### Are Non-slip Rugs Safe For Homes With Kids?



Yes, non-slip rugs prevent slipping and sliding, reducing fall risks. They stay securely in place on hard floors. This makes them ideal for playrooms, nurseries, and living rooms where children frequently move around.



### Which Rug Style Fits A Modern Farmhouse Decor?



Vintage or distressed rugs with neutral tones suit farmhouse style. Look for low pile, stain-resistant rugs in soft beige, taupe, or gray. These add rustic charm while being practical and easy to maintain in dining rooms or living spaces.



### Can Large Rugs Improve Room Acoustics?



Large area rugs absorb sound, reducing echo and noise. They soften footsteps and create a quieter environment. Placing a 10x12 rug in living rooms or offices enhances comfort and acoustics, making spaces more inviting and peaceful.
