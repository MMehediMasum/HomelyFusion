## Finding Clay Locations

Clay is an important resource in Ocean is Home. You need it for crafting and building. Knowing where to find clay saves time and effort.

Clay usually appears in wet and muddy areas. Look carefully in specific places to gather enough clay for your needs.

### Shallow Water Spots

Shallow water areas near beaches often have clay deposits. The water is calm and clear, making it easy to see clay patches.

Dig gently in the shallow mud to collect clay. These spots are safe and close to shore.

* Look near sandy beaches with muddy edges
* Check tidal pools after the tide goes out
* Search in calm bays or lagoons

### Underwater Caves

Underwater caves often hold clay deposits on their floors. These caves are hidden below the water surface.

Diving into these caves requires caution. Use a light source to explore and collect clay safely.

* Look for cave entrances near rocky coastlines
* Use diving gear to reach deeper areas
* Collect clay from soft, muddy cave floors

### Riverbeds And Shores

Riverbeds and shores where fresh water meets salt water are good places for clay. The soil here is often soft and rich in clay.

Walk along riverbanks and dig in wet soil to find clay patches. These spots can be easier to access than underwater caves.

* Check riverbanks after rain for soft mud
* Dig in wet soil near river mouths
* Avoid dry or rocky shore areas

Keep track of how much clay you have by checking your inventory often. This helps avoid running out unexpectedly.

Keep clay dry and away from water to stop it from spoiling. Wet clay can lose its value and usefulness.

Store clay in cool and shaded areas to protect it from heat and sun, which can dry it out too fast.

* Use containers that block moisture
* Keep clay off the ground to avoid dirt
* Check stored clay regularly for damage
* Avoid mixing wet and dry clay together
