**Are you wondering where to place a chair with a footrest in your living room to create the perfect cozy spot? Finding the right spot can transform your space into a relaxing haven where you can unwind after a long day.**

## Choosing The Right Spot

Placing a chair with a footrest in your living room adds comfort and style. The right spot helps you relax and enjoy the space better.

Think about light, nearby surfaces, and the room’s main focus. These factors make your chair more useful and inviting.

### Near Natural Light

Put the chair close to windows or glass doors. Natural light makes reading or relaxing more enjoyable.

Sunlight also helps create a warm and cozy spot for resting your feet.

* Choose a spot with good sunlight during the day
* Avoid direct glare that may cause discomfort
* Use light curtains to soften harsh sunlight

### Close To A Side Table

Place the chair near a side table to keep drinks or books within reach. It adds convenience and keeps your space tidy.

A side table also offers a place for lamps or decorations, enhancing the chair’s area.

* Keep the table close enough to reach comfortably
* Choose a table size that fits the room
* Use the table for essentials like remotes and snacks

### Facing The Focal Point

Arrange the chair so it faces the room’s main focus. This could be the TV, fireplace, or a large window.

Facing the focal point helps you enjoy the view or entertainment without turning your body.

* Identify the room’s focal point first
* Place the chair for a clear view
* Leave enough space to move around the chair

### Allowing Enough Legroom

Legroom is essential for comfort in a chair with a footrest. You need enough space to stretch your legs freely.

* Keep at least 18 inches of space in front of the footrest.
* Allow room to move without hitting furniture or walls.
* Make sure the footrest can fully extend without obstruction.
* Check that there is enough space to get in and out of the chair easily.

### Positioning For Relaxation

Place the chair where you can enjoy calm and quiet. Good lighting and a view add to relaxation.

| Position | Benefit |

### Avoiding High Traffic Areas

Choose a spot away from busy walkways. This keeps your chair peaceful and safe from bumps.

Consider these tips to avoid high traffic zones:

* Place the chair away from main doorways.
* Keep it out of paths between the kitchen and living room.
* Leave clear space around the chair for easy movement.

Placing a chair with a footrest in your living room depends on the room size. Each space needs a different approach. This guide helps you find the best spot for comfort and style.

Think about space, movement, and how you use the room. These factors guide your choice for chair placement.

### Arranging In Small Living Rooms

In small rooms, space is tight. Place the chair near a corner or next to a window. This keeps the room open and lets light shine through.

* Use chairs with slim profiles to save space.
* Choose a footrest that can tuck under the chair.
* Place the chair where it won't block walking paths.
* Consider wall-mounted shelves nearby for extra storage.
