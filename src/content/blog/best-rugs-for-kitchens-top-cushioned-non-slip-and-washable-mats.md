## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Materials For Kitchen Rugs?



The best kitchen rugs use materials like polypropylene, chenille, and cushioned foam. These materials are durable, stain-resistant, and easy to clean, perfect for high-traffic kitchen areas. They also offer anti-fatigue comfort for prolonged standing, enhancing both safety and comfort in your kitchen space.



### How Do Anti-fatigue Kitchen Mats Improve Comfort?



Anti-fatigue mats reduce leg and foot strain by cushioning hard floors. They provide support during long cooking or cleaning sessions. This helps prevent discomfort and fatigue. Mats like StepRite and Mattitude offer cushioned surfaces that improve posture and reduce tiredness in the kitchen.



### Are Non-slip Kitchen Rugs Safe For All Floor Types?



Yes, most non-slip kitchen rugs have rubber or latex backing. This prevents slipping on tile, hardwood, or laminate floors. Rugs like Tritard and eqivei ensure safety by firmly gripping the floor. Always check product details to confirm compatibility with your floor type.



### Can Kitchen Rugs Be Machine Washed?



Many kitchen rugs, including Artoid Mode and HEBE sets, are machine washable. This makes cleaning spills and stains easy. Machine washable rugs help maintain hygiene in busy kitchens. Always follow manufacturer care instructions for best results.



### What Sizes Are Ideal For Kitchen Rugs And Mats?



Ideal kitchen rug sizes vary by space. Common sizes include 17"x29", 17"x47", and runners around 20"x48". Sets often combine small and large rugs for sink and floor coverage. Choose sizes that fit your kitchen layout and provide ample floor protection.



### How Do Kitchen Rugs Help With Floor Protection?



Kitchen rugs protect floors from spills, scratches, and wear. They absorb moisture and prevent damage to hardwood or tile. Rugs like COSY HOMEER and YDKGL add a protective layer while enhancing kitchen decor. This prolongs floor life and keeps kitchens cleaner.
