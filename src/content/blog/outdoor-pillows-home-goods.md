## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In Outdoor Pillow Inserts?



Outdoor pillow inserts are typically made from water-resistant polyester or faux linen. Some include down or feather fillings for softness and durability. These materials resist moisture and fading, making them ideal for patios, porches, and garden furniture.



### Are Outdoor Pillow Covers Waterproof Or Water-resistant?



Most outdoor pillow covers, like those from MIULEE and Allorry, are water-resistant, not fully waterproof. They repel light moisture and prevent stains but may not withstand heavy rain. Water-resistant covers keep pillows dry and fresh during typical outdoor use.



### How Do I Maintain And Clean Outdoor Pillows?



Outdoor pillows should be spot cleaned with mild soap and water. Removable covers can be machine washed on gentle cycles. Regular maintenance prevents mold and mildew, ensuring pillows stay vibrant and fresh for longer periods outdoors.



### Can These Pillows Withstand Outdoor Weather Conditions?



Yes, pillows like Utopia Bedding and puredown® are designed to resist weather elements. They handle sun exposure, moisture, and wind well. However, storing them during harsh weather extends their lifespan and keeps them looking new.



### What Sizes Are Available For Outdoor Pillows?



Common sizes include 18x18 inches and 12x20 inches, suitable for various furniture types. Sets often come in multiples, providing options for sofas, patio chairs, or benches. These dimensions fit standard outdoor cushions perfectly.



### Are Decorative Pillow Covers Easy To Change Seasonally?



Yes, most decorative outdoor pillow covers are removable and easy to swap. This allows quick seasonal updates, like switching to fall colors or summer patterns. It’s a simple way to refresh outdoor decor without buying new pillows.
