## Ingredients List

Making Christmas Candy Cane Brownies is fun and easy. You only need a few simple ingredients. These ingredients give your brownies a rich chocolate flavor and a festive touch.

Below, you will find the key ingredients for the brownie base and the candy cane toppings. Prepare these before you start baking.

### Brownie Essentials

The brownie ingredients create a soft, chocolaty treat. Use fresh and good quality items for best results. These basics are easy to find in most kitchens or stores.

* 1 cup unsalted butter
* 2 cups granulated sugar
* 4 large eggs
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 1 cup all-purpose flour
* ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
* ½ teaspoon salt
* ½ teaspoon baking powder

### Candy Cane Toppings

The candy cane toppings add a festive and minty flavor. They also give the brownies a colorful, holiday look. Crush the candy canes into small pieces for even coverage.

* 1 cup crushed candy canes or peppermint candies
* Optional: ½ cup white chocolate chips for extra sweetness




## Step-by-step Preparation

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=this-recipe-shows-how-to-make-delicious-christmas-candy-cane-brownies.-follow-each-step-carefully.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

This recipe shows how to make delicious Christmas Candy Cane Brownies. Follow each step carefully. You will get perfect brownies with festive candy cane swirls.

We will cover mixing the batter, baking tips, and adding candy cane swirls. Each section helps you make this treat easy and tasty.

### Mixing The Batter

Start by preparing the wet and dry ingredients separately. Mixing them well is key to a smooth brownie batter.

1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. In a bowl, melt 1 cup of butter and 2 cups of sugar together.
3. Add 4 eggs one at a time, stirring well after each.
4. Mix in 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract.
5. In another bowl, combine 1 cup of flour, 3/4 cup cocoa powder, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
6. Slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until smooth.

### Baking Tips

Use these tips to bake your brownies perfectly every time. Watch the baking time and the pan type for best results.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Tip | Details |
| Oven Temperature | Set to 350°F (175°C) for even baking |
| Baking Time | Bake for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean |
| Pan Type | Use an 8x8 inch square pan for correct thickness |
| Cooling | Let brownies cool before adding candy cane swirls |

### Adding Candy Cane Swirls

Adding candy cane pieces and swirls gives a festive look and taste. Follow these simple steps to finish your brownies.

Here is how to add the candy cane swirls:

* Chop 1 cup of candy canes into small pieces.
* Sprinkle half of the pieces evenly over the brownie batter before baking.
* After baking, while brownies are still warm, melt 1/2 cup white chocolate.
* Drizzle the melted white chocolate on top.
* Sprinkle the remaining candy cane pieces on the melted white chocolate.
* Let the brownies cool completely to set the swirls.

## Storage And Freshness

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-christmas-candy-cane-brownies-fresh-is-important-for-taste-and-texture.-proper-storage-helps-maintain-their-softness-and-flavor.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Christmas Candy Cane Brownies fresh is important for taste and texture. Proper storage helps maintain their softness and flavor.

Brownies can dry out quickly if not stored right. Follow simple tips to enjoy them longer.

### Keeping Brownies Soft

Store brownies in an airtight container to keep them soft. This prevents air from drying them out.

You can also wrap brownies tightly with plastic wrap before putting them in a container. Keep them at room temperature away from heat.

* Use airtight containers or resealable bags
* Wrap brownies with plastic wrap or foil
* Keep at room temperature, not in direct sunlight
* Add a slice of bread inside the container to keep moisture

### Freezing Tips

You can freeze brownies to keep them fresh for weeks. Wrap each brownie piece tightly before freezing.

Label the wrapped brownies with the date. Thaw them at room temperature before serving for best taste.

* Wrap brownies in plastic wrap or foil
* Place wrapped pieces in a freezer-safe bag or container
* Label with the freezing date
* Thaw at room temperature before eating
