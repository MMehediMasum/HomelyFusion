## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Rug Is Best For A Living Room?



An 8x10 rug is ideal for most living rooms. It fits well under furniture and defines the seating area. Smaller sizes like 6x9 work for compact spaces or layered looks. Choose based on room dimensions and furniture layout for the best effect.



### Are Washable Rugs Suitable For High-traffic Living Rooms?



Yes, washable rugs like Srugn and AMADA HOMEFURNISHING are perfect for high-traffic areas. They are durable, easy to clean, and maintain their look. Their non-slip backing also ensures safety. These rugs combine convenience with style, making them practical for busy living rooms.



### Which Rug Material Is Best For Softness And Durability?



Faux wool and shaggy plush materials offer softness and durability. Rugs like Deerhoo and Tepook provide a cozy feel and long-lasting quality. Low pile rugs with durable edge binding resist wear and tear. These materials are great for comfort and easy maintenance.



### How Do Non-slip Rugs Improve Living Room Safety?



Non-slip rugs prevent slipping and sliding on hard floors. Rugs such as AMADA HOMEFURNISHING and Soalmost feature non-slip backing. This enhances safety, especially in homes with kids or pets. They also keep rugs in place, preserving room aesthetics and comfort.



### Can Vintage Print Rugs Match Modern Living Room Decor?



Yes, vintage print rugs like RELEANY and Srugn blend well with modern decor. Their retro designs add character and warmth. These rugs create a stylish contrast and complement contemporary furniture. They are versatile and enhance both classic and trendy living spaces.
