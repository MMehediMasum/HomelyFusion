## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Rug Is Best Under A Dining Room Table?



A 6x9 or 8x10 rug size works best under dining tables. It allows chairs to move without catching the rug edges. Choose based on your table size and room space for ideal coverage and comfort.



### Are Washable Rugs Good For Dining Rooms?



Yes, washable rugs are perfect for dining rooms. They offer easy cleaning from food spills and stains. This convenience keeps your dining area fresh and hygienic, especially with stain-resistant features.



### What Rug Material Suits Dining Room Use?



Low pile synthetic or cotton blend rugs are ideal. They resist stains, are easy to clean, and handle foot traffic well. Natural fibers like jute offer style but require more care.



### How To Prevent Rugs From Slipping Under Dining Tables?



Choose rugs with non-slip backing or use a rug pad underneath. This prevents slipping and protects floors. It ensures safety and keeps the rug in place during chair movement.



### Can A Vintage Or Abstract Rug Fit A Dining Room?



Yes, vintage and abstract rugs add character and style. They complement farmhouse or modern decor. Their patterns help hide stains and wear, making them practical and decorative choices.



### Are Thin Rugs Suitable For Dining Room Floors?



Thin rugs work well under dining tables. They allow chairs to slide easily and reduce tripping hazards. Look for soft, stain-resistant, and non-slip options for best performance.
