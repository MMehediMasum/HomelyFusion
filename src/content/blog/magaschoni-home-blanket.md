### What Materials Are Used In Magaschoni Home Blankets?



Magaschoni home blankets typically use high-quality cashmere or cashmere blends. These materials ensure softness, warmth, and durability. The luxurious fabric feels gentle on the skin, making it ideal for cozying up on couches or beds. Their premium craftsmanship guarantees long-lasting comfort and style.



### How Do Magaschoni Blankets Compare To Faux Fur Throws?



Magaschoni blankets focus on cashmere luxury, while faux fur throws prioritize plush texture. Faux fur throws like GONAAP are soft and shaggy, adding visual warmth. Magaschoni offers a lighter, breathable feel with natural fibers. Both provide coziness but appeal to different style preferences and comfort needs.



### Are Magaschoni Blankets Easy To Care For?



Magaschoni blankets require gentle care, often dry cleaning recommended. Hand washing with mild detergent may work, but avoid harsh chemicals. Proper care maintains softness and fabric integrity. Always check the specific care label for best results and to prolong the blanket’s lifespan.



### What Sizes Are Available For Magaschoni Home Blankets?



Magaschoni blankets come in various sizes, including throws and larger bed blankets. Common sizes range from 50"x60" throws to queen and king bed covers. This variety fits couches, sofas, or beds perfectly, offering versatile options for different living spaces and uses.



### Can Magaschoni Blankets Be Used Year-round?



Yes, Magaschoni blankets are suitable for year-round use. Their breathable cashmere fabric provides warmth in winter and comfort in cooler summer nights. Lightweight and cozy, they adapt well to different seasons, making them a practical and luxurious addition to any home.
