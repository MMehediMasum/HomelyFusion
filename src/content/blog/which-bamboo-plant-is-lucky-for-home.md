## Care Tips To Keep Bamboo Lucky

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=lucky-bamboo-is-a-popular-plant-that-brings-positive-energy-to-homes.-it-is-easy-to-care-for-with-the-right-steps.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Lucky bamboo is a popular plant that brings positive energy to homes. It is easy to care for with the right steps.

Knowing how to water and light your bamboo helps it grow healthy. Avoid common problems to keep it lucky for a long time.

### Watering And Light Needs

Lucky bamboo grows best in clean, fresh water. Change the water every week to stop it from smelling bad.

Place the plant where it gets bright, indirect light. Avoid direct sunlight because it can burn the leaves.

* Use filtered or distilled water if possible
* Keep water level just enough to cover the roots
* Do not let the water dry out
* Keep away from direct sun but in bright rooms
* Low light can slow growth but does not kill it

### Common Problems And Solutions

Yellow leaves often mean too much sun or poor water quality. Cut yellow parts to keep the plant healthy.

Roots turning brown can mean the water is dirty or the plant is old. Clean the container and change water often.

* Remove yellow or dead leaves quickly
* Use clean water to prevent root rot
* Keep the plant away from cold drafts
* Trim roots if they get too long
* Use a balanced fertilizer once a month if needed
