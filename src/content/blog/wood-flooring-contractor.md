## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Vinyl Peel And Stick Floor Tiles?



Vinyl peel and stick floor tiles are easy to install and budget-friendly. They resist water and stains, perfect for DIY projects. These tiles offer a quick flooring update without professional help, making them ideal for renters and homeowners wanting a stylish, low-maintenance floor solution.



### How To Repair Scratches On Wooden Floors Effectively?



Use wood scratch repair kits or wax sticks for quick fixes. These products fill scratches and blend with floor colors. They are simple to apply and restore wood floors’ appearance without sanding or refinishing, saving time and money while maintaining your floor’s natural beauty.



### Can Swiffer Powermop Pads Clean Hardwood Floors Safely?



Yes, Swiffer PowerMop pads are designed for hardwood floors. They gently remove dust and dirt without scratching. The disposable pads are convenient and compatible with cleaning sprays. Using them regularly helps keep your hardwood floors clean and streak-free while preserving the finish.



### What Does A Hardwood Floor Repair Kit Include?



A hardwood floor repair kit typically contains wax sticks, touch-up markers, and filler putty. These tools help fix scratches, cracks, and stains on wood surfaces. Kits are user-friendly, allowing homeowners to restore floors and furniture efficiently without professional intervention.



### Are Self-adhesive Wood Planks Suitable For All Rooms?



Self-adhesive wood planks work well in most rooms except high-moisture areas like bathrooms. They provide an easy DIY flooring option with a natural wood look. These planks are durable, peel-and-stick ready, and ideal for quick renovations in living rooms, bedrooms, and kitchens.
