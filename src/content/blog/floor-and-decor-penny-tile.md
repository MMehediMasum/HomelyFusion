## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Penny Tiles Used For In Home Decor?



Penny tiles are popular for backsplashes, bathroom floors, shower walls, and accent walls. They add a classic, stylish look with easy installation options like peel and stick.



### Are Peel And Stick Penny Tiles Waterproof?



Yes, most peel and stick penny tiles, like VEELIKE and CRE8TIVE, are waterproof. They are ideal for kitchens and bathrooms where moisture is common.



### How Easy Is It To Install Penny Round Peel And Stick Tiles?



Installation is simple. Just peel the backing and stick tiles to a clean, smooth surface. No grout or special tools needed, perfect for DIY projects.



### Can Penny Tiles Be Used On Floors?



Yes, porcelain penny tiles like Tenedos and Antiqued USA are durable for floor use. They withstand foot traffic and provide slip-resistant texture.



### What Sizes Do Penny Round Mosaic Tiles Come In?
