## Frequently Asked Questions



### Where Can I Buy Self-adhesive Vinyl Plank Flooring?



You can buy self-adhesive vinyl plank flooring at stores like Achim Home Decor and FloorPops. These brands offer easy peel-and-stick options ideal for DIY projects. Check online marketplaces such as Amazon for convenient delivery and a wide variety of styles and sizes.



### How Do Vinyl Peel And Stick Floor Tiles Work?



Vinyl peel and stick floor tiles come with an adhesive backing. Simply peel off the protective layer and stick the tile directly onto a clean, flat surface. This method allows for quick, mess-free installation without needing glue or nails, perfect for kitchens and bathrooms.



### What Thickness Is Best For Vinyl Plank Flooring?



A thickness of 1. 2mm to 2. 5mm is common for vinyl plank flooring. Thicker planks, like 2. 5mm, provide better durability and comfort. Choose based on room traffic and subfloor type. Thinner tiles suit low-traffic areas or quick remodels.



### Can Vinyl Flooring Withstand Moisture In Bathrooms?



Yes, self-adhesive vinyl plank flooring is water-resistant and ideal for bathrooms. Products like Nexus and VEVOR planks resist moisture well. Proper installation over moisture barriers enhances durability and prevents water damage, making them suitable for wet areas.
