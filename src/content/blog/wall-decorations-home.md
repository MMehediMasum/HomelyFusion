## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Popular Types Of Wall Decorations For Homes?



Popular wall decorations include 3D butterfly stickers, floating shelves, artificial greenery garlands, metal flower arts, and rustic wood signs. These pieces add texture, color, and style to living rooms, bedrooms, and kitchens. They suit various themes like farmhouse, boho, and modern designs.



### How To Use Floating Shelves As Wall Decor?



Floating shelves provide both storage and decoration. Mount them in bathrooms, kitchens, or living rooms. Use them to display books, plants, or decorative items. Their rustic wood design adds warmth and character while keeping spaces organized and stylish.



### Are Artificial Ivy Garlands Good For Wall Decoration?



Yes, artificial ivy garlands mimic natural greenery and require no maintenance. They create a lush, jungle vibe perfect for bedrooms, parties, or weddings. These vines brighten walls and add freshness without the worry of watering or sunlight needs.



### What Makes Metal Flower Wall Arts Ideal For Home Decor?
