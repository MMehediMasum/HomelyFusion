### How To Choose The Right Size For Christmas Pillows?



Common sizes are 18x18 inches and 30x50 cm. Choose based on your sofa or bed dimensions. Smaller pillows fit standard couches, while larger lumbar pillows suit beds and larger furniture pieces.



### Are These Christmas Pillow Covers Machine Washable?



Yes, most covers are machine washable. Use gentle cycles with cold water to maintain fabric quality. Always check care labels to avoid damage and keep colors vibrant through seasons.



### Can Christmas Pillow Covers Be Used Outdoors?



Some covers, like those made from durable linen blends, are suitable for indoor and outdoor use. Check product descriptions for weather resistance to ensure they withstand outdoor elements.



### What Materials Are Used In Christmas Cushion Covers?



Common materials include cotton, linen, and polyester blends. These fabrics offer durability, easy care, and a soft feel. Linen blends provide a rustic farmhouse look, perfect for holiday aesthetics.



### How Many Pillow Covers Come In A Typical Christmas Set?



Sets vary from 2 to 6 covers. Popular packs include 4 covers for coordinated sofa or room decor. Multiple covers allow mixing different festive patterns for a lively holiday display.
