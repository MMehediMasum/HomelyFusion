**Is your large wall feeling empty around your TV? Decorating such a big space can be tricky, but it doesn’t have to be stressful.**

You want your TV to be the star without making the wall look bare or cluttered. Imagine turning that blank wall into a stylish focal point that balances your room perfectly. In this guide, you’ll discover simple and smart ways to decorate around your TV, making your space look cozy, modern, and inviting.

Ready to transform your wall and impress everyone who walks in? Let’s dive in!




## Choosing The Right Tv Placement

Choosing the right TV placement on a large wall is key to creating a comfortable and stylish viewing area. The TV should fit well with the room’s layout and not feel out of place. Proper placement improves your watching experience and helps balance the wall space. This section covers how to find the best height and angle, and how to balance the TV size with the wall space.

### Optimal Height And Viewing Angle

Place the TV at eye level when seated. This reduces neck strain and makes watching easier. The center of the screen should be about 42 to 48 inches from the floor. Tilt the TV slightly downward if it’s mounted higher. The ideal viewing distance is about 1.5 to 2.5 times the diagonal size of the TV. This helps prevent eye fatigue and keeps the picture clear.

### Balancing Wall Space And Tv Size

Choose a TV size that fits the wall without overwhelming it. A very large TV can make the wall look crowded. A small TV might seem lost on a big wall. Leave some space around the TV for decoration or shelves. This creates a balanced and appealing look. Think about the room’s size and furniture layout too. The TV should be the focus but not the only thing on the wall.

## Creating A Focal Point

Creating a focal point around a TV on a large wall makes the space feel balanced and inviting. The TV should not just hang there alone. It needs to blend with the room’s style. This helps the whole wall look purposeful and stylish.

Simple design tricks can turn your TV wall into the room’s highlight. Drawing attention to the TV without overpowering it is key. The goal is to make the TV part of a bigger, beautiful picture.

### Framing The Tv With Artwork

Artwork can frame the TV and add personality to the wall. Choose pieces that match your room’s colors and style. Place art on both sides or above the TV to create balance.

Smaller art pieces grouped together work well. They guide the eye and soften the TV’s sharp edges. Use frames with similar colors to keep a clean look.

### Using Accent Walls And Paint

An accent wall behind the TV changes the entire vibe of the space. Pick a color that contrasts with the rest of the room. Dark colors like navy or charcoal make the TV pop.

Textured paint or wallpaper adds depth. It creates interest without cluttering the wall. Keep the rest of the room light to highlight the accent wall’s impact.

## Incorporating Shelving And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=incorporating-shelving-and-storage-around-a-tv-on-a-large-wall-creates-balance-and-function.-it-helps-fill-the-empty-space-while-keeping-the-area-organized.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Incorporating shelving and storage around a TV on a large wall creates balance and function. It helps fill the empty space while keeping the area organized. Shelves and cabinets provide room for decorations and everyday items. They also make the TV wall a focal point that looks neat and stylish.

### Floating Shelves For Display

Floating shelves offer a light and modern look. They appear to float on the wall without visible supports. Use them to show small plants, books, or photo frames. Arrange shelves at different heights to add visual interest. Keep the items simple to avoid clutter. Floating shelves do not take up floor space, which keeps the room open and airy.

### Built-in Cabinets And Consoles

Built-in cabinets and consoles give a clean and polished appearance. They provide closed storage to hide cables, games, and media devices. Cabinets blend into the wall, making the TV area look sleek. Consoles can hold sound systems and accessories neatly. Choose designs that match your room’s style for a seamless look.

## Lighting Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=lighting-plays-a-key-role-in-decorating-around-a-tv-on-a-large-wall.-it-sets-the-mood-and-highlights-the-space.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Lighting plays a key role in decorating around a TV on a large wall. It sets the mood and highlights the space. Proper lighting reduces eye strain and makes the room cozy. Different lighting techniques create a balanced and attractive look.

### Ambient And Accent Lighting

Ambient lighting provides general light to the room. It helps to fill the space with soft, even light. Use ceiling lights or wall sconces for ambient lighting. Accent lighting draws attention to specific areas. Place small spotlights or lamps around the TV. This adds depth and interest to the wall. Mixing ambient and accent lights creates a warm and inviting feel.

### Led Backlighting For The Tv

LED backlighting is a popular choice for TV walls. It adds a glow behind the screen, reducing eye strain. Choose LED strips that match your room’s color scheme. This lighting creates a floating effect for the TV. It also enhances picture quality by softening the contrast. LED backlighting is easy to install and energy-efficient. It gives a modern look without cluttering the space.

Textiles and soft furnishings bring warmth and comfort to large walls with TVs. They help balance the hard surfaces and add color and texture. These elements make the space feel cozy and inviting. Using textiles wisely enhances the overall look without cluttering the wall.

Rugs anchor the seating area near the TV. Choose a rug that matches the room’s color scheme. It softens the floor and adds pattern or texture. Curtains frame the wall and draw attention away from the TV. Use curtains in a fabric that complements the wall color. They also help control light and reduce glare on the screen.

### Cushions And Throws

Cushions and throws add layers to the seating area. Pick cushions in different sizes and fabrics for variety. Throws bring warmth and a splash of color. They also invite relaxation and comfort. Use soft fabrics like wool, cotton, or velvet. These textiles make the space feel lived-in and stylish at once.

## Maintaining A Balanced Look

Decorating around a TV on a large wall needs a balanced look. Balance helps the room feel calm and inviting. It stops the TV from being the only focus. The right balance makes the whole space work together.

### Symmetry Vs. Asymmetry

Symmetry means placing items evenly on both sides of the TV. This creates a neat and organized feel. Use matching shelves, lamps, or art on each side. Symmetry works well for classic and simple styles.

Asymmetry means arranging items unevenly but still balanced. This looks more casual and creative. Use different sizes or shapes on each side. Make sure the visual weight feels equal. Asymmetry adds interest without chaos.

### Mixing Styles And Textures

Mixing styles adds personality to the space. Combine modern and vintage pieces for contrast. Try smooth and rough textures together. For example, pair a sleek TV with a woven basket or soft rug. This mix keeps the wall from feeling flat or boring.

Use colors that connect different styles. Soft tones or natural shades work best. Textures like wood, metal, and fabric can live in harmony. The key is to keep a steady flow around the TV.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How To Balance A Large Wall With A Tv?

Balance a large wall by adding symmetrical decor on each side. Use shelves, art pieces, or plants to create harmony. This approach draws attention evenly and complements the TV, making the space feel cohesive and inviting.

### What Decor Complements A Tv On A Large Wall?

Decorate with framed art, floating shelves, or textured panels around the TV. These elements add depth and interest without overshadowing the screen. Choose colors and styles that match your room’s theme for a polished look.

### How To Hide Tv Cables On A Large Wall?

Use cable management systems like raceways or in-wall conduits. These keep cables organized and out of sight, maintaining a clean, clutter-free appearance. Concealing wires enhances the overall aesthetics and safety around your large wall TV setup.

### Can Lighting Enhance Tv Wall Decor?

Yes, strategically placed lighting highlights decor and reduces eye strain. Use LED strips, sconces, or spotlights around the TV to create ambiance. Proper lighting adds warmth and elevates the visual appeal of your TV wall.

## Conclusion

Decorating around a TV on a large wall can feel tough. Keep balance and style in mind. Use shelves, art, or plants to add interest. Choose colors and shapes that match your room. Don’t overcrowd the space; leave some empty areas.

This makes the TV the main focus but still looks nice. Small touches make a big difference. Take your time and enjoy the process. Your wall will look inviting and organized. Simple ideas can create a cozy and stylish space.
