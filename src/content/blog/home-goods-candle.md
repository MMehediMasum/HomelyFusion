## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Scents Are Popular In Home Goods Candles?



Popular Home Goods candle scents include lavender thyme, vanilla sandalwood, jasmine oud, spiced vanilla apple, and Hawaiian breeze. These fragrances offer calming, fresh, and warm aromas to suit various preferences and home atmospheres, enhancing relaxation and ambiance effectively.



### How Long Do Home Goods Candles Typically Burn?



Home Goods candles generally have burn times ranging from 40 to 80 hours. For example, the Chesapeake Bay Candle burns for about 50 hours, while some vanilla bean candles offer up to 80 hours, ensuring long-lasting fragrance and value.



### Are Home Goods Candles Made With Natural Wax?



Many Home Goods candles use natural soy wax blends. Soy wax is eco-friendly, burns cleaner, and offers a longer-lasting scent compared to paraffin. Brands like Lulu Candles and M&SENSE highlight their use of premium soy wax for a healthier burning experience.



### Can Home Goods Candles Eliminate Odors Effectively?
