## Grammar Rules For Compound Nouns

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=compound-nouns-are-two-or-more-words-combined-to-form-a-new-word.-they-can-be-written-in-different-ways.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Compound nouns are two or more words combined to form a new word. They can be written in different ways. Understanding these rules helps you write correctly.

One common question is whether to write "bedroom" as one word or two words. This depends on grammar rules for compound nouns.

### Closed Compounds

Closed compounds join two words together with no space or hyphen. This creates one single word. "Bedroom" is an example of a closed compound.

Closed compounds often form when two words are used together often. They become easier to read as one word.

* Bedroom
* Toothpaste
* Notebook

### Open Compounds

Open compounds write two or more words separately. They work together as one noun but keep a space. These often describe items or ideas.

Some compound nouns start as open compounds and may change to closed compounds over time.

* Post office
* High school
* Ice cream

### Hyphenated Compounds

Hyphenated compounds join words with a hyphen. This shows the words belong together but are still separate. Hyphens help avoid confusion.

Hyphenated compounds appear often in adjectives before nouns or in some nouns for clarity.

* Mother-in-law
* Check-in
* Well-being
