## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Purpose Of Adjustable Threaded Bed Frame Tools?



Adjustable threaded bed frame tools stabilize bed frames and prevent shaking. They secure headboards and support furniture for enhanced durability and comfort.



### How Do Felt Furniture Pads Protect Hardwood Floors?



Felt pads prevent scratches, dents, and nicks on hardwood floors. They create a soft barrier between furniture legs and flooring surfaces, preserving floor quality.



### Can Adjustable Bed Risers Support Heavy Furniture?



Yes, adjustable bed risers support up to 1,300 lbs. They raise furniture height, improve under-bed storage, and enhance room accessibility safely.



### Are Self-adhesive Paper Towel Holders Easy To Install?



Self-adhesive paper towel holders are simple to install without drilling. They stick firmly to surfaces, saving space and adding kitchen convenience.



### What Benefits Do Silicone Under Sink Mats Offer?



Silicone mats protect cabinet surfaces from water damage. They provide waterproof cushioning, prevent slips, and organize kitchen essentials efficiently.



### How Do Floating Bathroom Shelves Improve Storage?



Floating bathroom shelves maximize vertical space for toiletries. They offer rustic decor appeal while keeping essentials organized and accessible in small bathrooms.
