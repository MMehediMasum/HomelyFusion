## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Flameless Candles And How Do They Work?



Flameless candles are battery-operated LED lights designed to mimic real candles. They use flickering LEDs and real wax for authenticity. Controlled by remotes or timers, they provide safe, smoke-free ambiance without fire hazards, ideal for home, events, and outdoor use.



### How Long Do Battery-operated Flameless Candles Last?



Most flameless candles offer 200+ hours of continuous use on fresh batteries. Timer functions help extend battery life by auto-shutting off after preset hours. Usage time varies by candle size, LED type, and battery quality, ensuring long-lasting decorative lighting.



### Are Flameless Candles Safe For Outdoor Use?



Yes, many flameless candles are waterproof and suitable for outdoor settings. Products like the 14" outdoor waterproof LED pillars resist weather elements. Their safe design eliminates fire risks, making them perfect for patios, gardens, or camping trips.



### Can I Control Multiple Flameless Candles Remotely?



Yes, many sets include a multi-functional remote control. Remotes offer features like on/off, brightness adjustment, flicker modes, and timers. This allows convenient control of several candles simultaneously, enhancing ease of use during parties or daily decor.



### Do Flameless Candles Look Realistic Compared To Real Candles?



High-quality flameless candles use real wax and 3D wick designs for realism. Flickering LED lights simulate natural flame movement. These features create a warm, authentic glow, making them perfect for weddings, holidays, or cozy home ambiance without mess.



### Are Flameless Candles Suitable For Allergy Sufferers?



Flameless candles produce no smoke, soot, or scent, making them ideal for allergy sufferers. They avoid triggering asthma or sensitivities linked to real candle smoke. Their clean, safe light source enhances indoor air quality and comfort.

From elegant ivory pillars to festive Halloween designs, there's a flameless candle for every occasion. Their long battery life ensures they will last through your events. Plus, they are safe around children and pets. No wax drips or fire hazards.



Overall, flameless candles are a practical and beautiful addition to any space.
