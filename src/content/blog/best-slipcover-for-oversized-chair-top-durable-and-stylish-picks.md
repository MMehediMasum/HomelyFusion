## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Slipcover Material For Oversized Chairs?



Jacquard and velvet fabrics are top choices for oversized chair slipcovers. They offer durability, stretch, and comfort. These materials protect furniture from pets, kids, and spills while maintaining a stylish look. Stretch fabrics like spandex ensure a snug fit on various chair shapes.



### How Do I Measure My Oversized Chair For A Slipcover?



Measure the width, height, and depth of your chair including arms. Check if cushions are separate or attached. Compare measurements with product details to ensure the slipcover fits perfectly. Most oversized chair slipcovers list size ranges for easy selection.



### Can Slipcovers Protect Furniture From Pets And Kids?



Yes, slipcovers act as excellent protectors against pet hair, scratches, and spills from kids. Durable fabrics like jacquard and velvet are stain-resistant and washable. Slipcovers keep your furniture clean and prolong its life, making them ideal for busy households.



### Are These Slipcovers Easy To Wash And Maintain?



Most slipcovers are machine washable and quick drying. Fabrics like spandex and jacquard retain shape after washing. Follow care instructions on labels to avoid damage. Regular washing keeps slipcovers fresh and extends their usability.



### Do Slipcovers Fit Chairs With Separate Cushions?



Many slipcovers come in 2-piece sets for chairs with separate cushions. This design ensures a snug fit on both the seat and back cushions. It also improves comfort and appearance by preventing slipping or bunching.



### Can Slipcovers Update The Look Of An Old Oversized Chair?



Absolutely! Slipcovers are an affordable way to refresh and modernize old chairs. Available in various colors, patterns, and textures, they instantly change your room’s style. They also hide stains, wear, and tear effectively.
