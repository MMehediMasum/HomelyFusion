## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Artificial Plants For Wall Decor?



Artificial plants like eucalyptus, ivy vines, and Boston ferns add natural beauty without maintenance. They suit boho and modern styles perfectly. These faux plants brighten any room, from living rooms to nurseries, providing a fresh, green look all year round.



### How To Hang Wall Art Without Damaging Walls?



Use heavy-duty double-sided tape or nano adhesive mounting strips for secure, damage-free hanging. These tapes hold frames, posters, and decor firmly. They’re ideal for renters or those avoiding nail holes. Always check weight limits before applying to ensure safe installation.



### What Styles Suit Farmhouse And Boho Wall Decor?



Farmhouse decor features rustic wood and simple designs, while boho style includes vibrant, eclectic elements. Wooden wall vases and hanging paper lanterns fit both styles. Mixing these creates a cozy, artistic vibe perfect for entryways, living rooms, or bedrooms.



### How To Organize Kids’ Rooms With Wall Decor?



Stuffed animal hammocks and toy nets provide functional, cute storage. They keep toys off floors and add charm. Use colorful wall shelves for books and small decor. These solutions maximize space while keeping rooms tidy and visually appealing.



### Are Floating Shelves Good For Bathroom Storage?



Yes, floating shelves save space and add rustic charm to bathrooms. They hold toiletries and decor above toilets or counters. Installing them is simple and they help keep essentials organized. Choose moisture-resistant materials for durability in humid environments.



### What Are Eco-friendly Options For Wall Decor?



Artificial plants made from non-toxic materials and reusable wooden vases are eco-friendly. Paper lanterns made from recycled paper also reduce waste. These options combine style with sustainability, perfect for environmentally conscious home decorators.
