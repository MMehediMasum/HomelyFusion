## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Floor And Decor Quartz Countertops?



Floor And Decor quartz countertops offer durability, stain resistance, and low maintenance. They provide a stylish, modern look and resist scratches and heat, making them ideal for kitchens and bathrooms. Their non-porous surface also prevents bacteria growth, ensuring a hygienic workspace.



### How Do Quartz Countertops Compare To Granite?



Quartz countertops are engineered, offering consistent color and pattern unlike natural granite. They require less sealing and maintenance. Quartz is non-porous, making it more resistant to stains and bacteria. Granite is more heat resistant but needs regular sealing to maintain durability.



### Are Floor And Decor Quartz Countertops Easy To Clean?



Yes, quartz countertops from Floor And Decor are easy to clean. Use mild soap and water or a gentle cleaner. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive pads to maintain their polished finish. Regular wiping prevents stains and keeps the surface looking new.



### Can Quartz Countertops Withstand Kitchen Heat?



Quartz countertops can withstand moderate heat but are not heat-proof. Use trivets or hot pads to protect the surface from hot pots and pans. Direct exposure to high heat may cause discoloration or damage, so caution is recommended.



### What Styles And Colors Are Available In Floor And Decor Quartz?



Floor And Decor offers a wide range of quartz colors and styles, including white, gray, marble-look, and colorful gemstone-inspired patterns. This variety fits modern, classic, and eclectic kitchen designs, ensuring a perfect match for any décor style.



### Are Floor And Decor Quartz Countertops Suitable For Bathrooms?



Yes, quartz countertops are excellent for bathrooms due to their water resistance and easy cleaning. Their non-porous surface prevents mold and mildew buildup, making them a hygienic choice for bathroom vanities and countertops.
