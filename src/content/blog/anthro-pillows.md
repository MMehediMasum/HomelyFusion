## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Anthro Pillows Made From?



Anthro pillows use high-quality materials like cotton, linen, faux fur, and velvet. These fabrics ensure durability and comfort. Many covers feature soft textures and breathable weaves, ideal for home decor and cozy living spaces.



### How Do I Care For Anthro Pillow Covers?



Most Anthro pillow covers are machine washable or spot clean only. Always check the care label for specific instructions. Gentle washing preserves fabric texture and color, keeping pillows fresh and vibrant longer.



### Are Anthro Pillows Suitable For All Home Styles?



Yes, Anthro pillows come in various designs like boho, farmhouse, vintage, and modern. This variety allows easy integration into diverse home decor themes, enhancing living rooms, bedrooms, and couches stylishly.



### Do Anthro Pillow Covers Have Hidden Zippers?



Many Anthro pillow covers feature hidden zippers for a clean look. This design makes removing and washing covers easy without compromising style or comfort.



### Can I Mix And Match Anthro Pillows Sets?



Absolutely. Anthro pillows come in sets and individual pieces with complementary colors and patterns. Mixing designs creates unique, personalized decor that suits different tastes and spaces perfectly.
