## Setting Up Google Home With Nest

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=google-home-can-control-your-nest-thermostat-easily.-you-need-to-connect-them-first.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Google Home can control your Nest Thermostat easily. You need to connect them first. This guide will help you set up your devices.

Follow the steps below to connect and configure your Nest Thermostat with Google Home. We also cover fixing common setup problems.

### Connecting Devices

Start by linking your Nest Thermostat to the Google Home app. Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network.

* Open the Google Home app on your phone.
* Tap the “+” icon to add a device.
* Select “Set up device” and choose “Works with Google.”
* Find and select “Nest” from the list.
* Sign in with your Nest account to link devices.
* Follow on-screen instructions to finish setup.

### Configuring Settings

After connecting, customize your thermostat settings in the Google Home app. You can set temperature preferences and schedules.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Setting | Description |
| Temperature Range | Set minimum and maximum temperatures |
| Eco Mode | Save energy by setting eco-friendly limits |
| Schedules | Choose when to heat or cool your home |
| Voice Commands | Enable commands like “Set temperature to 72 degrees” |

### Troubleshooting Setup Issues

If your Nest does not connect or respond, try these fixes. They help solve common problems during setup.

* Check your Wi-Fi connection and signal strength.
* Restart both Google Home and Nest Thermostat devices.
* Make sure your Google Home app is updated.
* Remove and re-add the Nest device in the app.
* Reset the Nest Thermostat if needed.
* Verify that your Nest account credentials are correct.
