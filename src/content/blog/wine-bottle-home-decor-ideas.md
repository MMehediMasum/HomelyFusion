Decorative lanterns and candle holders made from wine bottles create a soft, inviting glow. Colorful glass vases add a bright touch to tables or shelves.



Wooden wine bottle signs and wall art enhance rustic or farmhouse themes easily. Unique items like melted wine bottle serving boards offer both function and style.
