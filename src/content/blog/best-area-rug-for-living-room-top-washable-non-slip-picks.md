## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Area Rug Fits Best In A Living Room?



An 8x10 rug works well for most living rooms. It covers ample floor space and anchors furniture comfortably. Smaller rooms may benefit from 5x7 rugs for a cozier feel. Measure your room to ensure the rug complements your layout and furniture size.



### Are Washable Rugs Suitable For High-traffic Living Rooms?



Yes, washable rugs like Srugn and GENIMO are ideal for high-traffic areas. They resist stains and are easy to clean. Their durable construction and non-slip backing make them practical and safe for busy living rooms, maintaining appearance over time.



### How Do Low-pile Rugs Benefit Living Room Spaces?



Low-pile rugs offer easy maintenance and a sleek look. They reduce dust accumulation and are perfect for allergy sufferers. Rugs like AMADA HOMEFURNISHING provide durability with a vintage style. They also fit well under furniture without creating uneven surfaces.



### What Styles Of Area Rugs Complement Modern Living Rooms?



Abstract and geometric patterns suit modern living rooms perfectly. Rugs such as Ciicool’s modern abstract or Puremy’s geometric shag add visual interest. Choose neutral or bold colors to match your decor. These rugs enhance contemporary aesthetics while providing comfort.



### Are Shag Rugs Good For Kids’ Rooms And Nurseries?



Yes, shag rugs like OLANLY and Deerhoo offer softness and warmth for kids’ spaces. Their plush texture cushions falls and adds coziness. They are machine washable and safe for indoor use, making them practical and stylish choices for nurseries and playrooms.

Choosing the best area rug for your living room can truly change the room’s feel. Whether you want a soft shaggy rug like Deerhoo or a washable low-pile option like Srugn, there are many choices. Rugs with non-slip backing add safety and keep the rug in place.



Patterns and colors, from vintage floral to modern abstract, help match your style. Size matters too—larger rugs like AMADA HOMEFURNISHING’s 8x10 cover more space and create a cozy area. Easy-to-clean rugs such as GENIMO and Soalmost save time and keep your space fresh.
