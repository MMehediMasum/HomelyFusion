Metallic spray centerpieces add shimmer and sophistication. Use them as event highlights for New Year’s, awards nights, or adult birthday celebrations.



### Are Handmade Pop-up Birthday Cards Good Table Gifts?



Yes, pop-up cards offer a unique, personal touch. They serve as memorable keepsakes and enhance the table’s celebratory vibe for all ages.
