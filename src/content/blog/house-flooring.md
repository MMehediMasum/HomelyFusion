## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Do Self-adhesive Mini Floor Tiles Work?



Self-adhesive mini floor tiles have a sticky backing for easy application. Simply peel off the protective layer and press firmly onto a clean surface. They are perfect for quick and easy decoration in mini house projects, offering a realistic wood or brick look without the hassle of traditional tiling.



### What Are Mop Slippers Used For?



Mop slippers are designed for hands-free floor cleaning. They have microfiber soles that attract dust and dirt as you walk. Ideal for quick cleaning, they're perfect for use in kitchens, bathrooms, and offices. These slippers are washable and reusable, making them an eco-friendly cleaning option.



### Why Choose Anti-fatigue Kitchen Mats?



Anti-fatigue kitchen mats provide comfort during long periods of standing. They feature cushioned support that reduces strain on feet and legs. These mats are ideal for kitchens and workspaces where prolonged standing is common. Their non-slip, waterproof design adds safety and convenience to your daily routine.



### How To Clean Chenille Duster Mop Slippers?



Chenille duster mop slippers are easy to clean and maintain. Simply remove them from your feet and wash them in a machine. Use cold water and a gentle detergent to ensure they last longer. Air drying is recommended to preserve their microfiber texture and cleaning efficiency.
