## Affordable Materials

Choosing the right material helps keep your bathroom cabinet costs low. Affordable materials can still look good and last long.

This guide covers cheap bathroom cabinet ideas using easy-to-find materials. Each option has its own benefits and uses.

### Plywood And Mdf Options

Plywood and MDF are popular for bathroom cabinets. They cost less than solid wood but still offer strength.

Plywood is made from layers of wood glued together. MDF is made from wood fibers pressed with glue. Both can be painted or covered with veneer.

* Plywood resists warping from moisture better than MDF
* MDF has a smooth surface, great for painting
* Both materials are easy to cut and shape
* Use waterproof paint or sealant to protect them

### Reclaimed Wood Choices

Reclaimed wood is wood reused from old furniture or buildings. It is a budget-friendly and eco-friendly choice.

This wood has a unique look with natural marks and colors. It adds character to your bathroom cabinet.

* Look for untreated wood to avoid chemicals
* Sand and seal reclaimed wood to protect it from water
* Reclaimed wood can be combined with plywood for strength
* It is best for rustic or vintage style bathrooms

### Plastic And Resin Cabinets

Plastic and resin cabinets are cheap and easy to clean. They resist water and do not warp.

These materials are lightweight and come in many colors. They can mimic the look of wood or stone.

* Plastic cabinets are waterproof and low maintenance
* Resin cabinets often have smooth, shiny finishes
* Both can be installed quickly and easily
* Good choice for modern or minimalist bathroom styles

## Diy Cabinet Projects

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=building-your-own-bathroom-cabinet-is-a-smart-way-to-save-money.-you-can-customize-it-to-fit-your-space-and-style.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Building your own bathroom cabinet is a smart way to save money. You can customize it to fit your space and style.

This guide will help you start with simple tools and follow clear steps. You will also learn how to paint and finish your cabinet nicely.

### Basic Tools And Supplies

You need a few basic tools and materials to build a bathroom cabinet. These are easy to find and affordable.

* Measuring tape
* Hand saw or electric saw
* Drill with screwdriver bits
* Sanding block or electric sander
* Wood glue
* Screws and nails
* Pencil and square ruler
* Wood boards or plywood
* Paintbrushes and paint
* Safety goggles and gloves

### Step-by-step Building Tips

Follow these simple steps to build a sturdy bathroom cabinet. Take your time and check your measurements twice.

1. Measure the space where the cabinet will go.
2. Cut the wood boards to size using the saw.
3. Sand all the edges smooth to avoid splinters.
4. Assemble the frame by gluing and screwing the boards together.
5. Add shelves or dividers inside the frame as needed.
6. Attach a back panel to add strength and support.
7. Install doors or leave it open, based on your design.
8. Check that all screws are tight and the cabinet is sturdy.

### Painting And Finishing Touches

Painting your cabinet protects the wood and gives it a fresh look. Use the right paint and finish for bathroom use.

| Latex or acrylic | Satin or semi-gloss | Easy to clean and resists moisture |
| Oil-based paint | Glossy | Durable but needs good ventilation |
| Waterproof sealer | Clear finish | Apply after paint for extra protection |

## Space-saving Designs

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=small-bathrooms-need-smart-storage-solutions.-space-saving-bathroom-cabinets-help-keep-things-neat.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Small bathrooms need smart storage solutions. Space-saving bathroom cabinets help keep things neat.

Using the right design can free up floor space. This makes the bathroom look bigger and cleaner.

### Corner Cabinets

Corner cabinets use unused corners of the bathroom. They fit into tight spaces easily.

These cabinets store toiletries and towels without taking much room. They keep items within reach.

* Fits into corners to save floor space
* Can be wall-mounted or floor-standing
* Great for storing small bathroom items
* Helps keep counters clutter-free

### Floating Shelves And Cabinets

Floating shelves hang on walls without touching the floor. They give storage without using floor space.

Floating cabinets add hidden storage above sinks or toilets. They keep the floor open for easy cleaning.

* Mounts on walls for extra storage
* Keeps floor space open and clear
* Works well for small items and decor
* Easy to install and looks modern

### Over-the-toilet Storage

Over-the-toilet cabinets use space above the toilet. This often-unused area can hold shelves or cabinets.

This storage is perfect for towels, toilet paper, and small items. It keeps essentials close but out of the way.

* Uses space above the toilet effectively
* Can be open shelves or closed cabinets
* Keeps bathroom essentials handy
* Helps avoid clutter on counters

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are Affordable Materials For Bathroom Cabinets?

Affordable bathroom cabinets often use MDF, plywood, or particleboard. These materials balance cost and durability well. They can be painted or laminated for a stylish finish. Avoid solid wood if on a tight budget, as it's more expensive.

### How Can I Update Old Bathroom Cabinets Cheaply?

Painting old cabinets with waterproof paint refreshes their look affordably. Replacing handles or knobs adds a modern touch. Adding peel-and-stick wallpaper or contact paper can also create a fresh style without major costs.

### Where To Buy Cheap Bathroom Cabinets Online?

Websites like IKEA, Home Depot, and Wayfair offer budget-friendly bathroom cabinets. Check for sales, discounts, or clearance sections. Buying pre-assembled cabinets saves money on installation costs. Always read reviews to ensure quality.

### What Style Suits Small Bathroom Cabinets Best?

Compact, wall-mounted, or corner cabinets suit small bathrooms. Choose light colors to make the space appear larger. Open shelving or glass doors can also create an airy feel. Prioritize storage efficiency and design simplicity.
