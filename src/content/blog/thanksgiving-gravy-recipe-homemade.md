## Preparing The Base

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-homemade-thanksgiving-gravy-starts-with-a-strong-base.-this-base-creates-rich-flavor-and-smooth-texture.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making homemade Thanksgiving gravy starts with a strong base. This base creates rich flavor and smooth texture.

Careful preparation of the base ensures your gravy tastes delicious and complements your turkey well.

### Using Turkey Drippings

Turkey drippings are the flavorful juices left in the pan after roasting. They add deep taste to gravy.

Start by pouring the drippings into a bowl. Let the fat rise to the top and skim it off.

* Use the remaining juices as the main liquid for your gravy.
* Cook the drippings with flour to make a roux.
* Add stock or broth to thin the gravy to your liking.

### Alternative Broth Options

If you do not have turkey drippings, use broth to build the base. Broth adds flavor and moisture.

Chicken broth is a common choice. Vegetable broth works well for a lighter taste.

* Chicken broth gives a mild, familiar flavor.
* Vegetable broth is good for a vegetarian version.
* Beef broth can add a stronger, richer taste.
* Use low-sodium broth to control salt levels.

## Making The Roux

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-a-roux-is-the-first-step-to-a-smooth,-rich-thanksgiving-gravy.-it-is-a-simple-mix-of-fat-and-flour-cooked-together.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making a roux is the first step to a smooth, rich Thanksgiving gravy. It is a simple mix of fat and flour cooked together.

The roux helps thicken the gravy and gives it a nice, deep flavor.

### Choosing The Right Fat

The type of fat you use changes the taste of your gravy. Butter is a popular choice for its creamy flavor.

You can also use turkey drippings for a more traditional taste. Oil or bacon fat work well too.

* Butter adds a rich, smooth flavor
* Turkey drippings give a savory taste
* Bacon fat adds a smoky touch
* Neutral oil keeps flavor simple

### Cooking To Perfection

Cook the roux slowly to avoid burning. Stir constantly with a whisk or wooden spoon.

Cook until the roux turns a light brown color for a nutty flavor. Darker roux gives stronger taste but less thickening power.

* Start with medium heat
* Keep stirring to prevent lumps
* Light brown roux is best for gravy
* Watch carefully to avoid burning




## Combining And Simmering

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-thanksgiving-gravy-starts-with-mixing-the-right-ingredients.-you-need-to-combine-broth-and-roux-carefully.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making Thanksgiving gravy starts with mixing the right ingredients. You need to combine broth and roux carefully. Then, simmer the mix to bring out the flavors.

Slow simmering helps the gravy thicken and develop its rich taste. Stir often to avoid lumps and burning.

### Incorporating Broth And Roux

First, warm the broth in a saucepan. Slowly add the roux while stirring constantly. This prevents lumps and creates a smooth texture.

* Heat broth over medium heat until warm.
* Whisk roux into the warm broth gradually.
* Keep stirring to combine fully.
* Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.

### Achieving The Perfect Thickness

The gravy thickness depends on how much roux you add. Too little makes it thin; too much makes it too thick. Adjust as needed while simmering.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gravy Thickness | Roux Amount | Simmer Time |
| Thin | 1 tablespoon | 5 minutes |
| Medium | 2 tablespoons | 7 minutes |
| Thick | 3 tablespoons | 10 minutes |

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-homemade-gravy-can-sometimes-be-tricky.-common-issues-include-lumps-and-incorrect-consistency.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making homemade gravy can sometimes be tricky. Common issues include lumps and incorrect consistency. Here are some tips to help you fix these problems.

Understanding how to troubleshoot these issues will improve your Thanksgiving meal. Let's explore ways to make your gravy perfect.

### Fixing Lumpy Gravy

Lumpy gravy happens when flour or cornstarch is not mixed well. To fix it, use a whisk to stir the gravy quickly.

If lumps persist, strain the gravy through a fine mesh sieve. This removes any remaining clumps.

* Whisk vigorously to break lumps
* Use a sieve to remove stubborn lumps
* Blend gravy with an immersion blender if needed

### Adjusting Consistency

Gravy that is too thick or too thin needs adjustment. To thicken gravy, mix a tablespoon of flour with cold water and stir into the gravy.

To thin gravy, add more broth or water. Stir well until you reach the desired consistency.

* Thicken with a flour and water mixture
* Thin by adding broth or water
* Stir constantly to avoid clumping

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Ingredients Are Needed For Homemade Thanksgiving Gravy?

You need pan drippings, flour, broth, butter, salt, and pepper. These create a rich, flavorful gravy perfect for Thanksgiving.

### How Do I Make Thanksgiving Gravy From Scratch?

Start by whisking flour into pan drippings, then slowly add broth. Cook until thick and smooth for the best homemade gravy.

### Can I Prepare Thanksgiving Gravy Ahead Of Time?

Yes, you can make gravy a day before. Store it in the fridge and reheat gently before serving.

### How To Avoid Lumps In Homemade Thanksgiving Gravy?

Whisk flour into hot fat slowly and add broth gradually. Constant stirring ensures smooth, lump-free gravy every time.
