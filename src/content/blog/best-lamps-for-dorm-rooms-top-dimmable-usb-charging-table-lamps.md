## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Should A Dorm Room Lamp Have?



A dorm room lamp should be compact, dimmable, and energy-efficient. USB charging ports and multiple light modes add convenience for study and relaxation.



### Are Usb Ports Important In Dorm Lamps?



Yes, USB ports allow easy charging of devices without extra adapters. This feature saves space and keeps your desk organized.



### How Do Dimmable Lamps Benefit Dorm Living?



Dimmable lamps let you adjust brightness to suit different tasks. They reduce eye strain and create a cozy atmosphere for studying or sleeping.



### Which Lamp Styles Suit Small Dorm Rooms Best?



Small bedside lamps with sleek designs and neutral shades work well. They provide functional light without crowding limited space.



### Can Dorm Lamps Support Multiple Light Colors?



Yes, many dorm lamps offer 3 color temperature modes. This helps adjust lighting for reading, relaxing, or working on projects.



### What Is The Advantage Of Touch Control Lamps?



Touch control lamps are easy to operate and save space. They often include brightness settings and eliminate the need for switches or knobs.
