## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Lighting Modes Do Vanity Mirrors Typically Offer?



Most vanity mirrors offer three lighting modes: warm, natural, and cool light. This helps simulate different environments for accurate makeup application.



### How Does Magnification Improve Makeup Application?



Magnification, like 5X or 10X, allows you to see fine details clearly. It helps with precise tasks like eyeliner or eyebrow shaping.



### Are Vanity Mirrors With Built-in Power Outlets Useful?



Yes, built-in power outlets are convenient for plugging in hair tools or charging devices. They reduce clutter and enhance usability.



### What Benefits Do Touch Controls Provide For Vanity Mirrors?



Touch controls allow easy adjustment of brightness and lighting modes. They offer a sleek, modern look and improve user convenience.



### How Important Is 360° Rotation In A Vanity Mirror?



360° rotation offers flexible viewing angles, making makeup application easier and more comfortable. It adapts to different lighting and seating positions.
