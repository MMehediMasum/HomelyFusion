## Tips For Finding Cheap Mirrors

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=bathroom-mirrors-can-be-expensive,-but-you-can-find-good-deals-with-some-effort.-knowing-where-to-look-helps-you-save-money.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Bathroom mirrors can be expensive, but you can find good deals with some effort. Knowing where to look helps you save money.

This guide gives simple tips to find cheap bathroom mirrors that fit your style and budget.

### Look For Sales And Coupons

Sales events are great times to buy mirrors at lower prices. Stores often offer discounts during holidays or seasonal clearances.

Coupons can reduce costs even more. Check websites or store apps for available coupons before you buy.

* Watch for holiday sales like Black Friday or Memorial Day
* Sign up for store newsletters to get coupons
* Use coupon websites to find discount codes
* Combine sales and coupons for extra savings

### Choose Simple Designs

Mirrors with simple frames cost less than fancy or large ones. Plain designs are easier to find at lower prices.

Choosing a classic shape like a rectangle or circle helps you get a cheap mirror that still looks good in your bathroom.

* Pick mirrors with thin or no frames
* Stick to standard sizes to avoid custom costs
* Choose shapes like rectangle, square, or circle

### Consider Secondhand Options

Used mirrors can be much cheaper than new ones. Look at thrift stores, online marketplaces, or garage sales.

Check the mirror carefully for cracks or damage before buying. Many secondhand mirrors look almost new.

* Search local thrift stores and flea markets
* Browse online sites like Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist
* Ask friends or family if they have spare mirrors
* Inspect mirrors for damage before purchase

## Popular Mirror Styles On A Budget

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=bathroom-mirrors-come-in-many-styles-that-fit-different-budgets.-you-can-find-great-mirrors-without-spending-too-much.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Bathroom mirrors come in many styles that fit different budgets. You can find great mirrors without spending too much.

This guide shows popular mirror styles that look good and cost less.

### Frameless Mirrors

Frameless mirrors have clean edges and no border. They create a simple and modern look in the bathroom.

These mirrors often cost less because they use less material. You can find them in many shapes like round or rectangle.

* Easy to clean
* Fits small or large bathrooms
* Simple style suits most decors

### Framed Mirrors

Framed mirrors have a border made from wood, metal, or plastic. The frame adds style and color to your bathroom.

These mirrors can be affordable if you choose simple frames or look for sales. Frames can match your bathroom furniture.

* Frame styles: rustic, modern, classic
* Frames come in many colors
* Frames protect mirror edges

### Decorative Mirrors

Decorative mirrors have unique shapes or designs. They add personality to your bathroom space.

You can find budget-friendly decorative mirrors by choosing simple designs or smaller sizes. These mirrors act as a focal point.

* Shapes: oval, sunburst, geometric
* Can include patterns or etching
* Works well in modern or vintage bathrooms

## Diy Mirror Ideas

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=bathroom-mirrors-add-style-and-function-to-any-space.-you-can-create-unique-mirrors-yourself.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Bathroom mirrors add style and function to any space. You can create unique mirrors yourself. These ideas save money and let you add personal touches.

Try simple projects using old materials or small changes. This guide covers three easy ways to improve your bathroom mirrors.

### Repurposing Old Frames

Old frames can become beautiful mirror borders. Look for wooden, metal, or ornate frames at thrift stores or garage sales. Clean and paint them to match your bathroom style.

* Remove the glass and back panel carefully.
* Cut a mirror to fit inside the frame.
* Secure the mirror using clips or strong adhesive.
* Add hooks or mounting hardware to hang.
* Decorate the frame with paint or small ornaments.

### Adding Creative Borders

Creative borders make mirrors stand out. You can use tiles, washi tape, or small stones around the edges. This adds texture and color without much cost.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | Effect | Cost |
| Colored Tiles | Bright and shiny | Medium |
| Washi Tape | Simple patterns | Low |
| Small Stones | Natural and earthy | Low |
| Ribbon or Lace | Soft and delicate | Low |

### Customizing Sizes

Mirrors come in many sizes. You can cut glass or buy small mirrors to fit your space. Custom sizes fit better in tight or unusual areas.

Here are tips for custom mirror sizes:

1. Measure the space carefully before buying.
2. Use glass cutters for simple shapes.
3. Order custom mirrors from local shops if needed.
4. Combine small mirrors for a mosaic look.
