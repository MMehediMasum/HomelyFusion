Cork is a traditional wine closure made from tree bark. Many people like cork for its natural feel and history.

* Easy to open with a corkscrew

### Benefits Of Screw Caps

Screw caps are metal closures that twist on and off. They create a tight seal to keep wine fresh.

Screw caps stop air from entering the bottle. This keeps wine fresh and prevents spoilage for a longer time.

* Strong seal prevents leaks and oxidation
* Easy to open without tools

* Reduces risk of cork taint
* Consistent quality for every bottle
