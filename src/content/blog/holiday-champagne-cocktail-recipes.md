## Classic Champagne Cocktails

Champagne cocktails add sparkle to holiday celebrations. They mix bubbly with simple flavors.

Classic recipes are easy to make and taste delicious. They bring joy to any festive event.

### French 75

The French 75 combines gin, lemon juice, and champagne. It has a crisp and refreshing taste.

This cocktail is light and bubbly. It works well for toasts and special moments.

* 2 oz gin
* 1 oz fresh lemon juice
* 2 tsp simple syrup
* Champagne to top
* Lemon twist for garnish

### Mimosa Variations

The classic mimosa mixes orange juice and champagne. It is a brunch favorite.

You can try different juices for new flavors. Think peach, cranberry, or pomegranate.

* Classic: orange juice + champagne
* Peach: peach nectar + champagne
* Cranberry: cranberry juice + champagne
* Pomegranate: pomegranate juice + champagne

### Champagne Royale

The Champagne Royale uses crème de cassis and champagne. It adds a sweet, fruity flavor.

This cocktail looks elegant with a deep red color. It is perfect for holiday parties.

* 1 oz crème de cassis
* Champagne to top
* Blackberry or raspberry for garnish

## Fruity Festive Mixes

Holiday celebrations call for bright and tasty drinks. Fruity cocktails bring cheer to any gathering. These mixes use fresh fruit flavors to add color and sparkle.

Try these easy recipes to make your party special. Each cocktail blends fruit juices with bubbly champagne for a festive treat.

### Pomegranate Sparkler

This cocktail combines tart pomegranate juice with champagne. It is perfect for a holiday toast. The red seeds add a festive look.

* 2 ounces pomegranate juice
* 4 ounces chilled champagne
* 1 teaspoon simple syrup
* Pomegranate seeds for garnish

Mix juice and syrup in a glass. Slowly pour in champagne. Stir gently. Garnish with seeds and serve cold.

### Cranberry Twist

The cranberry twist blends sweet and sour flavors. It uses fresh cranberries and orange peel for zest. This drink looks great in clear glasses.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Ingredient | Amount |
| Cranberry juice | 3 oz |
| Champagne | 4 oz |
| Orange liqueur | 1 oz |
| Fresh cranberries | Handful |
| Orange peel | For garnish |

Combine cranberry juice and liqueur. Add champagne slowly. Drop in fresh cranberries and garnish with orange peel.

### Citrus Burst

Citrus burst is lively and refreshing. It uses lemon and lime juices with champagne. Mint leaves add a fresh touch.

**Ingredients:**

* 1 ounce lemon juice
* 1 ounce lime juice
* 4 ounces champagne
* Fresh mint leaves for garnish
* Ice cubes

**Instructions:**

1. Fill a glass with ice cubes.
2. Pour lemon and lime juice over ice.
3. Top with chilled champagne.
4. Stir gently and garnish with mint leaves.

### Rosemary Fizz

This cocktail blends the piney taste of rosemary with bubbly champagne. It is crisp and herbal, perfect for holiday toasts.

* 1 sprig fresh rosemary
* 1 oz lemon juice
* 1 oz simple syrup
* 4 oz chilled champagne
* Lemon twist for garnish

Muddle rosemary with lemon juice and syrup. Strain into a flute and top with champagne. Garnish with a lemon twist.

### Ginger Champagne Punch

This punch mixes spicy ginger with sweet champagne. It is lively and festive, great for serving a crowd.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| Fresh ginger, sliced | 1 cup | Add for strong spice |
| Orange juice | 2 cups | Freshly squeezed |
| Champagne | 1 bottle (750 ml) | Chilled |
| Honey | 2 tbsp | Adjust sweetness |
| Orange slices | For garnish |  |

Mix ginger, orange juice, and honey in a punch bowl. Chill then add champagne before serving. Garnish with orange slices.

### Cinnamon Kiss

This cocktail uses cinnamon to add warm spice. It has a smooth, sweet flavor that pairs well with champagne.

Ingredients:

* 1 cinnamon stick
* 1 oz apple cider
* 1/2 oz maple syrup
* 4 oz champagne
* Apple slice for garnish

Instructions:

1. Heat apple cider with cinnamon stick for 5 minutes.
2. Remove cinnamon and stir in maple syrup.
3. Let cool, then pour into a glass.
4. Top with champagne and garnish with apple slice.

## Non-alcoholic Sparkling Alternatives

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=holiday-celebrations-call-for-festive-drinks-everyone-can-enjoy.-non-alcoholic-sparkling-cocktails-offer-a-tasty-and-fun-choice.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Holiday celebrations call for festive drinks everyone can enjoy. Non-alcoholic sparkling cocktails offer a tasty and fun choice. These drinks sparkle like champagne but without alcohol.

Try these easy recipes using sparkling apple cider, berry flavors, and zesty ginger. They are perfect for all ages and add cheer to your gatherings.

### Sparkling Apple Cider

Sparkling apple cider is a classic holiday drink that is naturally sweet and bubbly. It captures the warmth of fall and winter flavors without alcohol.

* Chill 100% sparkling apple cider
* Add a cinnamon stick for extra spice
* Garnish with a thin apple slice
* Serve in a flute glass to keep bubbles lively

### Virgin Berry Bubbly

This drink blends sparkling water with fresh berry juice for a refreshing taste. It is light, colorful, and easy to make for any holiday event.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Ingredient | Amount |
| Sparkling water | 1 cup |
| Mixed berry juice | 1/2 cup |
| Fresh raspberries | 6 berries |
| Mint leaves | 2 leaves |

Mix berry juice and sparkling water in a glass. Add fresh raspberries and mint leaves. Stir gently and serve chilled.

### Citrus Ginger Fizz

This cocktail combines citrus flavors with a spicy ginger kick. It is fizzy, bright, and perfect for a holiday toast without alcohol.

To make Citrus Ginger Fizz:

1. Fill glass with ice cubes.
2. Pour in 1/2 cup sparkling water.
3. Add 1/4 cup fresh orange juice and 1 tablespoon lemon juice.
4. Stir in 1 teaspoon ginger syrup or grated fresh ginger.
5. Garnish with a lemon twist or orange slice.
