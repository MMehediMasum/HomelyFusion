## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of A Full-length Mirror?



Full-length mirrors provide a complete view of your outfit and appearance. They enhance room brightness and give a sense of space. These mirrors are versatile, suitable for bedrooms, living rooms, and cloakrooms. They can be freestanding, wall-mounted, or leaned against a wall for flexibility.



### How Do I Choose The Right Mirror Size?



Consider the space where you intend to place the mirror. Larger mirrors like 76"x34" are great for spacious areas. Smaller mirrors fit well in compact spaces. Ensure the mirror complements your room's decor and meets your functional needs.



### Can Large Mirrors Be Wall-mounted Safely?



Yes, large mirrors can be wall-mounted securely with proper fixtures. Ensure you use appropriate hardware that supports the mirror's weight. Follow the manufacturer's instructions carefully. For added safety, consider professional installation to prevent damage or accidents.



### Are Shatter-proof Mirrors Available?



Many modern mirrors, like the DUMOS Arched Mirror, feature shatter-proof designs. They use advanced materials like nano glass for safety. This ensures durability and reduces the risk of injury, making them ideal for homes with children or high-traffic areas.



### What Styles Do Large Mirrors Come In?



Large mirrors come in various styles, including arched, rectangular, and rounded corners. Frame materials vary, from aluminum alloy to wood. Color options like black and gold help match any decor theme. Choose a style that complements your interior design.



### How Do I Maintain A Full-length Mirror?



Clean mirrors regularly with a soft cloth and glass cleaner. Avoid abrasive materials that can scratch the surface. Ensure the frame remains dry to prevent damage. Regular maintenance will keep your mirror looking pristine and extend its lifespan.
