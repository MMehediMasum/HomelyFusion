### Essential Cocoa Components

These ingredients form the base of your peppermint cocoa. Use good quality items for best taste.

* 2 cups of milk (whole or any milk you like)
* 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
* 2 tablespoons sugar (adjust to taste)

## Step-by-step Preparation

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=this-holiday-peppermint-hot-cocoa-recipe-is-easy-to-make.-it-warms-you-up-and-tastes-delicious.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

This Holiday Peppermint Hot Cocoa recipe is easy to make. It warms you up and tastes delicious.

Follow these simple steps to prepare a perfect cup of peppermint hot cocoa.

### Heating The Milk

Start by pouring two cups of milk into a small saucepan. Use whole milk for creamier hot cocoa.

Heat the milk over medium heat. Stir often to prevent it from burning. Heat until it is hot but not boiling.

### Mixing Cocoa And Peppermint

In a separate bowl, mix two tablespoons of unsweetened cocoa powder with one cup of warm milk. Stir until smooth.

Add half a teaspoon of peppermint extract to the cocoa mixture. Stir well to blend the flavors evenly.

* 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
* 1 cup warm milk
* ½ teaspoon peppermint extract

### Adding Sweeteners And Enhancers

Pour the cocoa and peppermint mix into the heated milk. Stir well to combine all ingredients.

Add two tablespoons of sugar or your favorite sweetener. Mix until the sugar dissolves completely.

* 2 tablespoons sugar (or sweetener)
* Optional: whipped cream for topping
* Optional: crushed candy canes for garnish

## Tips For Creamy Texture

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-holiday-peppermint-hot-cocoa-creamy-feels-like-a-warm-hug.-the-right-techniques-help-you-enjoy-a-smooth-and-rich-drink-every-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making holiday peppermint hot cocoa creamy feels like a warm hug. The right techniques help you enjoy a smooth and rich drink every time.

Small details in choosing ingredients and preparation make a big difference. Here are some helpful tips for a creamy texture.

### Choosing The Right Milk

Milk choice affects the creaminess and flavor of your hot cocoa. Whole milk gives the richest taste and smooth texture.

* Use whole milk for creaminess
* Try oat or almond milk for dairy-free options
* Steamed milk adds extra smoothness
* Avoid skim milk; it can make cocoa watery

### Whipping Techniques

Whipping helps create a light, foamy layer on top. This adds a creamy feel to each sip.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Whipping Method | Tip |
| Hand whisk | Whisk briskly for 1-2 minutes |
| Electric mixer | Use low speed to avoid over-whipping |
| Frother | Warm milk before frothing for best foam |

### Avoiding Common Mistakes

Some errors can make your cocoa less creamy. Watch out for these to keep the perfect texture.

* Do not boil the milk; it can curdle
* Mix cocoa powder well to prevent lumps
* Add peppermint syrup slowly to avoid separation
* Serve hot cocoa immediately for best creaminess

* Do not freeze, as the texture may change
