### How To Install A Self-adhesive Paper Towel Holder?



Clean the wall surface thoroughly before installation. Peel off the adhesive backing carefully. Press the holder firmly onto the desired spot for 30 seconds. Avoid drilling or tools. Wait 24 hours before use to ensure strong adhesion and support for kitchen rolls.
