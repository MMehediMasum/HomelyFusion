### How Durable Are Hexagon Lvt Flooring Tiles?



Hexagon LVT tiles are highly durable and resistant to scratches and moisture. They maintain their appearance under heavy foot traffic. Their waterproof nature makes them perfect for kitchens and bathrooms. These tiles combine style with practicality for long-lasting flooring solutions.



### Can Vinyl Floor Tiles Be Used In Bathrooms?
