## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Rug Is Best For A Living Room?



A 6x9 or 8x10 rug size fits most living rooms well. These sizes balance space and furniture placement. They create a cozy, defined area without overwhelming the room.



### How To Choose A Rug Material For Living Rooms?



Choose soft, durable materials like shag or tufted rugs. They offer comfort, stain resistance, and easy cleaning. Materials like synthetic fibers work well for high-traffic areas.



### Are Stain-resistant Rugs Good For Homes With Kids?



Yes, stain-resistant rugs are ideal for kids’ rooms and living areas. They simplify cleaning and maintain appearance despite spills and dirt. This durability makes them practical for busy households.



### How To Maintain And Clean Luxury Area Rugs?



Vacuum regularly and spot clean spills immediately. Use gentle cleaners for delicate fibers. Some rugs are washable, making maintenance easier and prolonging rug life.



### What Rug Designs Suit Modern Living Rooms?



Abstract, geometric, and marble patterns enhance modern decor. Neutral colors with subtle accents blend well. These designs add elegance and style without clashing with existing furniture.



### Can Rugs Improve Living Room Comfort And Aesthetics?



Absolutely, rugs add warmth, texture, and style to living rooms. They soften floors and reduce noise. Choosing the right rug enhances both comfort and visual appeal.
