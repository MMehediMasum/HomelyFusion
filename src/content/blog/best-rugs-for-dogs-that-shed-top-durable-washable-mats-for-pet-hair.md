## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Rug Good For Dogs That Shed?



A good rug for shedding dogs traps hair effectively and resists stains. It should be machine washable, non-slip, and quick-drying. Materials like microfiber or chenille work best for trapping fur and dirt while staying durable and easy to clean.



### How Do Non-slip Dog Mats Benefit Shedding Dog Owners?



Non-slip mats prevent accidents by keeping rugs firmly in place. They reduce slipping risks for pets and humans. This feature also helps contain dog hair and dirt in one area, making cleanup easier and protecting floors from scratches and messes.



### Can Washable Dog Door Mats Handle Heavy Shedding?



Yes, washable dog door mats like Smiry and PURRUGS are designed for heavy shedding. They absorb dirt and hair, then clean easily in the washing machine. Frequent washing keeps them fresh and efficient, ideal for homes with shedding dogs.



### Are Microfiber Rugs Effective For Muddy Dog Paws?



Microfiber rugs are highly absorbent and trap mud, dirt, and pet hair well. They dry quickly and can be machine washed repeatedly. Their soft texture is gentle on paws, making them perfect for indoor or outdoor use with muddy dogs.



### What Size Rug Is Best For Large Dogs That Shed?



Large rugs, around 30x20 inches or bigger, accommodate big dogs comfortably. They provide ample space for muddy paws and shedding fur. Longer rugs like 22x47 inches are ideal for entryways, helping keep floors clean and hair contained.
