### Are Laura Ashley Tablecloths Machine Washable?



Yes, most Laura Ashley tablecloths are machine washable. They are designed for easy care with wrinkle and stain-resistant properties. Always follow the specific washing instructions on the label to maintain fabric quality and appearance over time, ensuring long-lasting use.



### Which Laura Ashley Tablecloths Are Best For Formal Events?



For formal events, options like the Dothan Luxury Linen Blend and Arabesque Shimmery Fabric tablecloths are ideal. They offer elegant textures and sophisticated colors like taupe and white, perfect for weddings, banquets, and holiday dinners.



### Do Laura Ashley Tablecloths Resist Stains And Spills?



Many Laura Ashley tablecloths feature spillproof, water-repellent polyester fabrics. These properties help resist stains and make cleaning easier. This practical feature suits busy households and event settings where spills are common.



### What Sizes Do Laura Ashley Tablecloths Come In?



Laura Ashley tablecloths typically come in sizes like 60" x 84" and 60" x 120". These dimensions fit standard rectangular tables, suitable for dining rooms, kitchens, and party setups. Some round options are also available for various table shapes.



### Can Laura Ashley Tablecloths Be Used Outdoors?
