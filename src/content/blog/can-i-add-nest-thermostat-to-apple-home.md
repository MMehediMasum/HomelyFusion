## Nest And Apple Home Integration

Understanding how Nest works with Apple Home is important. Not all smart devices connect directly to Apple’s Home app. Let’s explore the official support and other options.

### Official Support Status

Apple HomeKit does not officially support Nest Thermostats. Google owns Nest and uses its own ecosystem. This means you cannot add Nest devices directly to Apple Home.

Without native support, you miss out on Siri voice control through Apple Home. The Nest app or Google Home app is required for full control. Apple Home shows no Nest status or controls.

### Workarounds And Alternatives

Some users find ways to connect Nest with Apple Home using third-party tools. These workarounds link Nest data with HomeKit. They require extra setup and may not be reliable.

* Use a Homebridge server to bridge Nest and HomeKit.
* Install Nest plugin on the Homebridge platform.
* Configure your Apple Home app to recognize Nest devices.
* Check for updates often as plugins may break with new software.
* Consider using HomeKit-compatible thermostats for easy integration.

| Method | Pros | Cons |
