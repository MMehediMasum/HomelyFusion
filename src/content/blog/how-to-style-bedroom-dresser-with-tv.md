**Your bedroom dresser can be more than just a place to store clothes—it can become a stylish focal point, especially when paired with your TV. But how do you make sure your dresser and TV work together without cluttering your space or losing that cozy bedroom vibe?**

If you’ve ever wondered how to blend function with flair, you’re in the right place. Keep reading, and you’ll discover simple, creative ways to style your bedroom dresser with your TV that will transform your room into a comfortable and chic retreat.



## Choosing The Right Dresser

Styling a bedroom dresser with a TV starts with picking the right dresser. The dresser should fit your space and style. It must hold your items and support your TV well.

Think about size, material, and storage to find a dresser that works best. These points help create a balanced and useful setup.

### Size And Proportion

Choose a dresser that fits your room size. It should not be too big or too small. The TV size also matters to keep balance.

Make sure the dresser height allows easy TV viewing. The width should hold the TV base and leave space on the sides.

* Match dresser width to TV width
* Keep dresser height at eye level when seated
* Leave space around the dresser for comfort

### Material And Finish

Pick a dresser material that suits your bedroom style. Wood offers warmth, while metal looks modern. The finish affects the room’s mood.

Choose a finish that matches other furniture. Matte finishes hide fingerprints better. Glossy finishes add shine but need more care.

* Wood for classic or rustic looks
* Metal for modern or industrial styles
* Matte finishes for subtle elegance
* Glossy finishes for a sleek appearance

### Storage Needs

Think about what you will store in the dresser. If you have many clothes, choose one with many drawers. For small items, smaller compartments help.

Good storage keeps your bedroom tidy. Drawers with dividers organize things better. Open shelves can hold media devices for the TV.

* More drawers for clothes and linens
* Dividers for small items like socks or jewelry
* Shelves for TV remotes and devices
* Easy-to-clean surfaces




## Selecting The Ideal Tv

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-tv-for-your-bedroom-dresser-is-important.-the-tv-should-fit-the-space-well-and-be-easy-to-watch.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right TV for your bedroom dresser is important. The TV should fit the space well and be easy to watch.

Pick a TV that matches your room size and your viewing habits. This helps make your bedroom more comfortable.

### Screen Size For Bedroom

Choose a screen size that fits your bedroom space. A TV that is too big can feel overwhelming.

Consider how far you sit from the screen. A smaller bedroom needs a smaller TV to avoid eye strain.

* For small rooms, 24-32 inches is good
* Medium rooms fit well with 32-43 inches
* Larger rooms can handle 43-50 inches or more

### Mounting Options

Decide how to place the TV on or above the dresser. You can use a stand or wall mount.

A stand keeps the TV on the dresser surface. A wall mount saves space and looks clean.

* TV stand: easy to move and adjust
* Wall mount: saves dresser space
* Swivel mount: lets you change viewing angle

### Smart Tv Features

Smart TVs connect to the internet and offer apps like streaming services. This adds convenience.

Look for easy-to-use menus and good Wi-Fi support. These features make your TV more useful.

* Built-in apps for movies and shows
* Voice control for hands-free use
* Screen mirroring from phones or tablets

## Placement Tips For Tv And Dresser

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=styling-a-bedroom-dresser-with-a-tv-takes-planning.-proper-placement-helps-create-a-comfortable-space.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Styling a bedroom dresser with a TV takes planning. Proper placement helps create a comfortable space.

Consider height, room balance, and cables for a neat look. These tips make your setup both practical and attractive.

### Optimal Viewing Height

Place the TV at eye level when sitting on your bed. This reduces neck strain and improves comfort.

The center of the TV screen should line up with your eyes. This usually means the TV sits slightly above the dresser’s surface.

* Measure the height of your bed seat
* Set TV center 42-48 inches from the floor
* Adjust height if you use recliners or chairs

### Balancing Room Layout

Keep the dresser and TV balanced with other furniture. Avoid crowding one side of the room.

Leave enough space around the dresser for easy access. This keeps the room feeling open and tidy.

* Place dresser on a wall with enough space
* Keep a clear walking path near the dresser
* Match dresser size with room dimensions

### Cable Management

Hide cables to keep the area clean and safe. Use clips or channels to guide wires behind the dresser.

Organize cables to avoid dust and tangles. This helps maintain a neat appearance and easy cleaning.

* Use cable ties to bundle wires together
* Attach cables to the back of the dresser
* Use cable covers to hide wires on walls




## Decorating Around The Tv

Styling a bedroom dresser with a TV on top can be tricky. You want the space to look nice and feel cozy.

Decorating around the TV helps balance the room. It also makes the dresser and TV blend with your bedroom style.

### Using Wall Art

Wall art can make the TV area look more interesting. Choose pieces that match your room's colors and theme.

Place art around the TV, but not too close. This draws attention without cluttering the space.

* Use framed prints or paintings on either side of the TV
* Try a large piece above the TV if it is low on the wall
* Keep art size proportional to the TV and dresser

### Incorporating Plants

Plants add life and color near your TV and dresser. They soften hard edges and bring a fresh feel.

Choose easy-care plants that fit your space. Small pots can go on the dresser sides or next to it on the floor.

* Succulents and cacti need little water and light
* Ferns and pothos add green fullness and grow well indoors
* Use matching pots for a neat look

### Accent Lighting Ideas

Accent lighting highlights the TV area and adds warmth to the room. It also reduces eye strain when watching TV.

Use soft lights that can dim. Place lights behind the TV or on the dresser to create a cozy glow.

* LED strip lights behind the TV create a soft backlight
* Table lamps on dresser corners add style and light
* Small spotlights can highlight wall art or plants

## Maintaining A Clutter-free Space

Keeping your bedroom dresser tidy helps create a peaceful atmosphere. A clutter-free space is easier to maintain and feels more relaxing.

Here are some tips to style your bedroom dresser with a TV while ensuring the space remains organized and neat.

### Regular Decluttering

Consistent decluttering prevents the buildup of unnecessary items. Set a schedule to go through your dresser and remove what you don't need.

1. Sort items into categories: keep, donate, or throw away.
2. Assess each item's usefulness and condition.
3. Repeat this process monthly for best results.

### Smart Storage Bins

Use storage bins to organize items. This keeps the dresser top clear and makes it easier to find what you need.

Choose bins that fit your dresser's style and size. Clear bins are useful for visibility, while decorative bins add aesthetic appeal.

### Minimalist Styling Tips

Adopt a minimalist approach to styling your dresser. This includes selecting a few key decorative items that enhance the space without overcrowding it.

* Opt for a small plant or a framed photo.
* Choose a simple lamp to provide lighting.
* Limit additional accessories to avoid clutter.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Can I Style A Bedroom Dresser With A Tv?

Place the TV centrally on the dresser for balance. Use decorative items like plants or frames on either side. Keep cables hidden for a clean look. Choose a dresser that complements your room’s color and theme.

### What Are The Best Decor Items For Dresser With Tv?

Use small plants, candles, or photo frames to add personality. Avoid clutter to keep focus on the TV. Decorative trays or bowls can organize remotes. Match decor colors with your bedroom palette for harmony.

### How To Hide Tv Cables On A Bedroom Dresser?

Use cable management boxes or clips behind the dresser. Run cables along wall edges or inside cord covers. Choose wireless devices to reduce visible wires. Keeping cables hidden enhances the room’s neatness and style.

### Should The Tv Size Match The Dresser Size?

Yes, the TV size should be proportional to the dresser. A TV too large can overwhelm the dresser’s surface. A smaller TV may look out of place. Balance size ensures both dresser and TV look stylish together.
