**Your bedroom chest of drawers is more than just a storage space—it’s a chance to show your style and keep your essentials close at hand. But what should you put on it to make the most of this spot?**

Whether you want to keep things tidy, add a personal touch, or create a calming vibe, the right items can make a big difference. Keep reading to discover simple and smart ideas that will transform your chest of drawers into a beautiful and practical part of your room.

You’ll find tips that fit your style and needs perfectly.



## Choose A Focal Point

The bedroom chest of drawers is a perfect spot to add style and personality. Picking a focal point helps guide your decoration choices. It draws the eye and makes the space feel complete.

Focus on one key item to avoid clutter and keep things balanced. This approach creates a calm and inviting bedroom atmosphere.

### Statement Mirror

A statement mirror adds light and depth to your bedroom. Choose one with a unique frame or shape. It becomes the center of attention on your chest of drawers.

* Pick a mirror size that fits well with the dresser width
* Consider frames in wood, metal, or colorful finishes
* Place the mirror where it reflects light or a nice view
* Keep the area around the mirror clear for a clean look

### Art Prints And Photos

Art prints and photos personalize your space and show your style. Use frames with simple lines to keep focus on the images. Group several pieces to create a small gallery on the chest of drawers.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type | Recommended Frame | Placement Tips |
| Art Prints | Black or white thin frames | Arrange in a neat row or grid |
| Family Photos | Wood or metal frames | Mix sizes for interest but keep color consistent |
| Posters | Simple frames or clipboards | Lean against the wall for casual style |




## Incorporate Lighting

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=lighting-makes-a-bedroom-feel-cozy-and-warm.-it-also-adds-style-to-a-chest-of-drawers.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Lighting makes a bedroom feel cozy and warm. It also adds style to a chest of drawers.

Choosing the right lights can brighten the room and make it more inviting.

### Table Lamps

Table lamps add soft light to your bedroom. They help create a calm atmosphere.

Place a small or medium lamp on your chest of drawers to light the area.

* Choose lamps with warm bulbs for gentle light
* Pick lamps that match your room style
* Use lamps with adjustable brightness for comfort

### Fairy Lights

Fairy lights add a magical touch to your bedroom chest of drawers. They create soft, twinkling light.

You can drape them around your drawers or place them in a clear jar for a pretty look.

* Use battery-powered fairy lights for easy setup
* Choose warm white or colored lights for mood
* Safe for all surfaces and easy to move

### Candles

Candles add soft, flickering light and a relaxing scent. They make the bedroom feel calm.

Place candles on a tray or holder to protect the surface of your chest of drawers.

* Use unscented candles if you have allergies
* Never leave candles burning unattended
* Try flameless LED candles for safety and ease

## Add Functional Accessories

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adding-functional-accessories-to-your-bedroom-chest-of-drawers-keeps-things-neat.-it-helps-you-find-your-items-quickly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adding functional accessories to your bedroom chest of drawers keeps things neat. It helps you find your items quickly.

These accessories can also make your space look nice and organized. Choose items that suit your daily needs.

### Jewelry Holders

Jewelry holders keep your rings, necklaces, and bracelets tidy. They stop your jewelry from tangling or getting lost.

Pick holders that fit your style and the space on your chest of drawers. Some have hooks, trays, or small compartments.

* Use ring dishes for small rings and earrings
* Hang necklaces on hooks to avoid tangles
* Store bracelets in divided sections

### Trays And Bowls

Trays and bowls hold everyday items like keys, coins, or watches. They keep these small things from cluttering the surface.

Choose trays or bowls that match your room’s look. They help you grab essentials fast and keep your dresser clean.

* Place a tray for your wallet and phone
* Use a small bowl for loose change
* Keep watches in a soft-lined tray




## Bring In Greenery

Adding greenery to your bedroom chest of drawers can create a calm and fresh atmosphere. Plants improve air quality and add a natural touch to your space.

Choose plants that fit your style and care routine. Greenery makes your bedroom feel more inviting and alive.

Small potted plants are perfect for bedroom chests of drawers. They take little space and bring life to the room.

* Mini ferns add soft green leaves and texture.
* Cactus plants bring a unique shape and require low maintenance.

### Fresh Flowers

Fresh flowers add color and a pleasant scent to your bedroom. They brighten the space and change with the seasons.

| Flower Type | Color | Care Tips |

## Keep It Organized

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=a-bedroom-chest-of-drawers-can-become-cluttered-quickly.-it-is-important-to-keep-it-organized-to-find-things-easily.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A bedroom chest of drawers can become cluttered quickly. It is important to keep it organized to find things easily. Use smart tools to keep everything in its place.

Organizing your chest of drawers helps maintain a tidy bedroom. It also saves time when you are looking for specific items.

### Storage Boxes

Storage boxes are useful for keeping similar items together. They come in different sizes and can fit inside drawers easily.

Choose boxes that match the drawer size. Label them for quick identification.

* Use small boxes for accessories like belts and ties
* Medium boxes work for socks and undergarments
* Large boxes are great for seasonal clothes

### Drawer Dividers

Drawer dividers help separate different items within the same drawer. They make it easy to keep everything neat and accessible.

Adjustable dividers can be customized to fit any drawer. They prevent items from mixing up.

* Use dividers for separating shirts, pants, and shorts
* Divide space for small items like jewelry and watches
* Keep delicate fabrics away from heavier items

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are The Best Decorative Items For Bedroom Drawers?

Decorative trays, small plants, and framed photos enhance bedroom drawers. These items add style and personality without cluttering the space. Choose items that reflect your taste and keep the surface organized for a balanced look.

### How To Organize Essentials On Bedroom Chest Of Drawers?

Use small containers or trays to group essentials like jewelry, watches, and keys. This keeps items accessible and prevents mess. Arrange items by frequency of use, placing daily essentials within easy reach for convenience.

### Can I Put Electronics On Bedroom Chest Of Drawers?

Yes, small electronics like alarm clocks, chargers, or speakers fit well. Ensure cords are neatly managed to avoid clutter. Avoid placing heavy or large devices that may damage the surface or reduce available space.

### What Type Of Lighting Suits Bedroom Chest Of Drawers?

Table lamps or decorative LED lights create a cozy ambiance. Choose soft, warm lighting to complement the bedroom mood. Avoid harsh or overly bright lights that can disrupt relaxation.

## Conclusion

A bedroom chest of drawers can hold both style and function. Choose items that fit your needs and space. Keep it tidy to avoid clutter and stress. Personal touches like photos or plants add warmth. Use trays or boxes to organize small things.

Remember, simplicity often looks best and feels calm. This way, your chest of drawers becomes a helpful and nice part of your room.
