**Protecting vinyl plank flooring from scratches and dents is essential. Choosing the right furniture pads helps keep floors looking new longer.**



Vinyl plank floors are popular for their beauty and durability. Still, heavy furniture can cause damage over time. Furniture pads act as a cushion between furniture legs and floors. They prevent marks, scratches, and dents caused by moving or shifting furniture.



Felt pads, self-adhesive designs, and heavy-duty options suit different furniture and floor types. Using quality pads extends the life of your vinyl plank flooring and saves money on repairs. This guide covers top furniture pads designed for vinyl floors, including felt, nail-on, and reusable sliders. Learn how to protect your floors with easy-to-use and effective pads that fit your needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Furniture Pads For Vinyl Plank Flooring?



Felt Furniture Pads X-PROTECTOR and Scotch Felt Pads are top choices. They protect vinyl planks from scratches and dents effectively. Their self-stick design ensures easy application and long-lasting protection.



### How Do Felt Furniture Pads Protect Vinyl Plank Floors?



Felt pads create a soft barrier between furniture legs and vinyl floors. They prevent scratches, dents, and scuffs from moving furniture. This reduces floor damage and keeps vinyl planks looking new longer.



### Can I Reuse Felt Furniture Sliders On Vinyl Floors?



Yes, products like SuperSliders are reusable and designed for hardwood and vinyl floors. They allow smooth furniture movement without scratching the surface. Simply clean and reposition as needed.



### Are Nail-on Felt Pads Better Than Self-adhesive Ones?



Nail-on pads offer a secure fit, ideal for heavy furniture. Self-adhesive pads provide easy installation and are good for lighter items. Both protect vinyl floors well depending on furniture weight.



### What Sizes Of Felt Pads Work Best For Vinyl Plank Flooring?



Assorted sizes, like 1-inch to 2. 5-inch rounds or squares, fit various furniture legs. Larger pads distribute weight better, reducing pressure marks on vinyl plank floors. Choose size based on furniture foot size.



### How Often Should I Replace Felt Furniture Pads On Vinyl Floors?



Replace pads every 6 to 12 months or when worn out. Check regularly for dirt buildup or damage to maintain effective floor protection and prevent scratches on vinyl planks.
