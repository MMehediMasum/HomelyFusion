## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Stair Runner Rug Fits Standard Staircases?



Standard staircases usually require stair runners about 27 inches wide. Length depends on the number of steps. Measure your stairs before buying to ensure the perfect fit.



### Are Non-slip Stair Treads Safe For Kids And Pets?



Yes, non-slip stair treads provide extra grip and reduce slipping risks. They are ideal for children, elders, and pets, ensuring safer stair use.



### How To Clean And Maintain Washable Stair Runner Rugs?



Vacuum regularly to remove dirt and debris. For stains, use mild detergent and water. Most washable rugs can be machine washed or spot cleaned easily.



### Which Stair Runner Material Is Best For High-traffic Areas?



Low-pile synthetic fibers like polyester or nylon work best. They resist wear and stains, making them durable choices for busy hallways and stairs.



### Can Stair Runners Protect Wooden Stairs From Damage?



Absolutely, stair runners shield wood from scratches and wear. They add cushioning, reduce noise, and extend the life of your wooden stairs.



### How To Install Reusable Adhesive Stair Treads Correctly?



Clean stairs thoroughly before applying adhesive treads. Peel off backing and press firmly on each step. Reusable adhesives allow repositioning without residue or damage.
