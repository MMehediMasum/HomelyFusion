## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Peel And Stick Wood Look Tiles?



Peel and stick wood look tiles are easy to install and require no grout. They are affordable, durable, and water-resistant. These tiles offer a realistic wood appearance and are ideal for DIY projects in kitchens, bathrooms, and basements.



### How Durable Is The Nexus Vinyl Floor Tile?



Nexus vinyl floor tile is highly durable and scratch-resistant. It withstands heavy foot traffic and moisture, making it perfect for kitchens and bathrooms. Its self-adhesive backing ensures a secure fit without damaging the subfloor.



### Can Wood Grain Foam Mats Be Used For Exercise Areas?



Yes, wood grain foam mats provide cushioning and support for exercise spaces. They are interlocking, easy to clean, and protect floors from impact. These mats also add a stylish wood look to playrooms and home gyms.



### Are Floorpops Peel And Stick Tiles Waterproof?



FloorPops peel and stick tiles are water-resistant, making them suitable for bathrooms and kitchens. Their adhesive backing prevents water seepage and damage. They are also easy to clean and maintain, ideal for high-moisture areas.



### How Do I Maintain Vinyl Peel And Stick Floor Tiles?



To maintain vinyl peel and stick tiles, clean regularly with a damp mop and mild detergent. Avoid abrasive cleaners and excessive water. Promptly wipe spills to prevent stains and keep the tiles looking new and vibrant.
