**Choosing the perfect recliner can transform your living room into a cozy haven. But before you buy, one crucial step often gets overlooked: measuring your space correctly.**

If you don’t measure your recliner and living room properly, you risk ending up with a chair that’s either too big or too small, ruining your comfort and style. In this guide, you’ll learn simple, effective tips to measure your recliner the right way—making sure it fits perfectly and feels just right.

Keep reading, and avoid costly mistakes that could turn your dream seat into a tight squeeze.




## Choose The Right Measuring Tools

Measuring your recliner correctly helps it fit well in your living room. Using the right tools makes the job easy and accurate.

This guide explains what tools to use and how to measure properly to avoid mistakes.

### Tape Measure Essentials

A tape measure is the best tool to measure your recliner. It is flexible and shows exact length.

* Choose a tape measure at least 12 feet long.
* Pick one with clear, easy-to-read numbers.
* Use a tape with a locking feature to hold the length.
* Metal tapes are more durable than cloth or plastic ones.
* Check that the tape retracts smoothly without sticking.

### Using A Measuring Tape Correctly

To get the right measurements, follow simple steps when using your tape measure.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Action** | **Tip** |
| Start at zero | Place the tape’s end at the recliner edge to measure from the exact point. |
| Measure width | Stretch the tape from one armrest to the other straight across. |
| Measure depth | Run the tape from front to back at the deepest point of the seat. |
| Measure height | Measure from the floor to the highest part of the recliner back. |
| Record measurements | Write down all numbers immediately to avoid forgetting. |

## Measure The Recliner Dimensions

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=measuring-a-recliner-is-important-to-see-if-it-fits-your-living-room.-you-need-to-check-its-width,-depth,-height,-and-seat-size.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Measuring a recliner is important to see if it fits your living room. You need to check its width, depth, height, and seat size.

Use a tape measure and write down each dimension carefully. This helps avoid buying a recliner that is too big or small.

### Width And Depth

Measure the recliner's width from one armrest to the other. This shows how much space it will take side to side.

To find the depth, measure from the front edge to the back of the recliner. This tells you how far it will stick out in the room.

* Width: Measure armrest to armrest
* Depth: Measure front to back

### Height And Backrest Size

Check the total height of the recliner from the floor to the top. This helps if you have low ceilings or shelves above.

Measure the backrest height separately from the seat to the top. This shows how much back support the recliner offers.

* Total height: Floor to top of recliner
* Backrest height: Seat to top of backrest

### Seat Dimensions

Measure the seat width from one side to the other inside the armrests. This shows how wide the sitting area is.

Check the seat depth by measuring from the front edge to where the seat meets the backrest. This tells you how deep the seat is.

* Seat width: Inside armrests
* Seat depth: Front edge to backrest

## Consider Recliner Expansion

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=measuring-a-recliner-correctly-helps-it-fit-well-in-your-living-room.-keep-in-mind-that-recliners-need-extra-space-when-fully-open.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Measuring a recliner correctly helps it fit well in your living room. Keep in mind that recliners need extra space when fully open.

Plan for the recliner’s full size, not just its closed position. This avoids blocking walkways or crowding other furniture.

### Reclined Position Measurements

Measure the recliner from the wall to its farthest point when fully reclined. Include the backrest and the footrest in this length.

* Place the recliner in its reclined position.
* Measure from the wall behind it to the front edge of the footrest.
* Note the width of the chair at its widest point.
* Check the height of the backrest when reclined.

### Footrest Extension Space

The footrest needs extra floor space to extend fully. This space varies by recliner model.

| Measurement | Recommended Space |
| --- | --- |
| Footrest length from seat edge | 20 to 24 inches |
| Clearance in front of recliner | At least 24 inches |
| Side clearance for movement | 12 inches minimum |




## Assess Living Room Space

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=measuring-your-living-room-before-buying-a-recliner-is-important.-it-helps-you-choose-the-right-size.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Measuring your living room before buying a recliner is important. It helps you choose the right size.

Proper measuring makes sure the recliner fits well and the room stays comfortable.

### Available Floor Area

First, check the free floor space in your living room. Measure the length and width where you want the recliner.

Write down these numbers to compare with the recliner size. Leave some space around the chair for other furniture.

* Use a tape measure for accuracy
* Measure the area from wall to wall
* Note down any obstacles like radiators or vents
* Keep extra space for comfort and cleaning

### Clearance For Movement

Make sure there is enough room to move around the recliner. Check paths to doors and other furniture.

Allow space for the recliner to fully extend without blocking walkways or other items.

* Leave at least 24 inches between recliner and walls
* Keep 30-36 inches for walking paths
* Measure how far the recliner extends when open
* Consider space for footrests and reclining angle

## Account For Doorways And Hallways

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=measuring-a-recliner-for-your-living-room-needs-more-than-just-its-size.-you-must-check-if-it-fits-through-doors-and-halls.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Measuring a recliner for your living room needs more than just its size. You must check if it fits through doors and halls.

Ignoring entry spaces can cause delivery problems or damage to your new chair. Careful measuring helps avoid these issues.

### Measure Entryways

Measure the width and height of every doorway the recliner must pass. This includes front doors, back doors, and any interior doors.

* Use a tape measure to get door frame width.
* Measure door height from floor to top of the frame.
* Check if the door has molding that reduces space.
* Note if doors open inward or outward.
* Measure thresholds or steps that might block access.

### Navigate Tight Spaces

Hallways and corners often limit large furniture movement. Measure these spaces carefully to plan your recliner's path.

| Space | Measurement to Take | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hallway Width | Measure narrowest point | Ensures recliner can pass through |
| Hallway Length | Check total length from door to room | Confirms enough room to turn |
| Corner Angles | Measure width and height at turns | Important for maneuvering recliner |
| Obstacles | Note radiators, shelves, or furniture | May reduce usable space |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Measure Recliner Width Correctly?

Measure the widest part of the recliner, usually the armrests. Use a tape measure and note the width to ensure it fits your space.

### What Is The Best Way To Measure Recliner Depth?

Measure from the front edge to the back of the recliner, including any extended footrest. This ensures proper fit in your living room.

### How To Measure Recliner Height For Living Room?

Measure from the floor to the highest point of the recliner, usually the backrest. This helps determine if it fits under windows or shelves.

### Should I Measure Recliner With Footrest Extended?

Yes, measure both with the footrest retracted and extended. This helps you understand the space needed when the recliner is fully in use.

## Conclusion

Measuring your recliner correctly saves space and avoids mistakes. Start with width, height, and depth. Don’t forget to measure when it is fully reclined. Check doorways and hallways for easy moving. Write down all measurements clearly before buying. This simple step helps your living room stay cozy and neat.

Take your time and measure twice to be sure. A well-measured recliner fits perfectly and feels comfortable. Enjoy your living room with the right recliner size!
