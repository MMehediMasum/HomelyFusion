## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Nexus Self-adhesive Vinyl Tiles?



Nexus vinyl tiles are easy to install with peel-and-stick backing. They are durable, water-resistant, and ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and bedrooms. The oak plank pattern offers a natural wood look without the high cost or maintenance of real wood flooring.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain Vinyl Plank Flooring?



Use gentle cleaners like Bona Multi-Surface Floor Cleaner or Quick Shine Floor Finish. Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive tools. Regular mopping keeps vinyl planks looking fresh and protects the surface from scratches and dullness, extending the floor’s lifespan.



### Can Vinyl Plank Tiles Be Used In High-moisture Areas?



Yes, vinyl plank tiles are water-resistant and suitable for kitchens, bathrooms, and basements. Their self-adhesive design prevents water seepage under the tiles, making them a practical flooring choice for moist environments without warping or damage.



### Are Peel-and-stick Floor Tiles Easy For Diy Installation?



Yes, peel-and-stick tiles like Achim Home Decor’s products are user-friendly for DIY projects. They require no special tools or adhesives. Simply peel the backing and stick tiles on a clean, flat surface for quick, hassle-free flooring updates.



### What Sizes And Styles Are Available For Vinyl Plank Tiles?
