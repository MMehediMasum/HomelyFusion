## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are These Throw Pillow Covers Made Of?



These throw pillow covers are crafted from various materials, including chenille, boucle, faux wool, and linen. These fabrics offer a soft and cozy texture. They are designed to complement farmhouse and modern decor styles, enhancing the comfort and aesthetics of your living space.



### Are These Pillow Covers Machine Washable?



Yes, most of these pillow covers are machine washable for easy maintenance. It's recommended to use a gentle cycle with cold water. Avoid using bleach and opt for a mild detergent. Always check the care label for specific washing instructions to ensure longevity.



### Do These Pillow Covers Come With Inserts?



These pillow covers are sold without inserts. They are designed to fit standard 18x18 inch pillow inserts. You can easily find compatible inserts online or at home decor stores. Using the right insert ensures a plump and stylish look for your pillows.



### How Do I Style Throw Pillows In My Living Room?



To style throw pillows, mix textures and patterns for visual interest. Use complementary colors to match your room's theme. Arrange pillows in odd numbers for a balanced look. Layer different sizes and shapes to add depth and dimension to your sofa or bed.



### Are These Pillow Covers Suitable For Outdoor Use?



These pillow covers are primarily designed for indoor use. They are made from soft and delicate fabrics that may not withstand outdoor conditions. If you wish to use them outdoors, ensure they are placed in a covered area to protect them from moisture and direct sunlight.
