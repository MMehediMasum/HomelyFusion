## Maintenance And Care

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-don-draper-living-room-chair-is-a-stylish-and-comfortable-piece.-taking-care-of-it-keeps-it-looking-good-and-lasting-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The Don Draper Living Room Chair is a stylish and comfortable piece. Taking care of it keeps it looking good and lasting longer.

Regular maintenance helps protect the chair’s materials and finish. It also keeps the chair inviting for daily use.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean the chair often to remove dust and dirt. Use a soft cloth or vacuum with a brush attachment.

For spills, blot immediately with a clean, dry cloth. Avoid rubbing to prevent stains from spreading.

* Use mild soap and water for tough spots
* Test cleaning products on hidden areas first
* Do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive materials

### Preserving Upholstery

Protect the fabric or leather by keeping it away from direct sunlight. Sun can cause fading and cracking.

Use fabric protectors or leather conditioners as recommended. These help maintain softness and color.

* Rotate cushions to prevent uneven wear
* Avoid sharp objects near upholstery
* Keep pets off the chair to prevent damage

### Longevity And Durability

Check the chair’s frame and legs regularly. Tighten screws or bolts to keep it stable and safe.

Avoid placing heavy items on the chair. This prevents stress on the structure and fabric.

* Keep the chair away from heat sources
* Use furniture pads to protect floors and chair legs
* Handle the chair carefully when moving it
