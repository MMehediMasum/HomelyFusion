## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Types Of Wall Letters Are Best For Home Decor?



Acrylic, wooden, metal, and LED marquee letters are popular. Each style offers unique texture and lighting effects for versatile decoration. Choose based on your room’s theme and personal taste for best visual impact.



### How Do Self-adhesive Acrylic Letters Work On Walls?



Self-adhesive acrylic letters have sticky backings for easy, damage-free application. Clean the surface, peel off the backing, and press firmly. They offer a sleek, mirror-like finish, perfect for modern decor.



### Can Wooden Letters Be Customized For Nursery Walls?



Yes, wooden letters can be personalized with names, colors, and designs. They’re ideal for nurseries, adding warmth and a handcrafted touch. Paint or decorate them to match nursery themes or color schemes.



### Are Led Marquee Letters Suitable For Parties And Events?



Absolutely, LED marquee letters create vibrant, illuminated decor. They enhance ambiance for weddings, birthdays, and holidays. Battery-powered options are portable and easy to install anywhere indoors or outdoors.



### How To Maintain Metal Wall Letters For Longevity?



Keep metal letters clean by dusting regularly with a soft cloth. Avoid moisture to prevent rust. Applying a clear sealant can protect the finish and preserve the antique or rustic look over time.
