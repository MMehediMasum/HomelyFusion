## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of A Tabletop Water Fountain?



A tabletop water fountain adds calming sounds and a soothing ambiance. It improves air quality and enhances relaxation in living spaces. The natural river rocks and deep basin create a peaceful, zen-like atmosphere ideal for living rooms or offices.



### How Do Decorative Vases Enhance Living Room Tables?



Decorative vases add color, texture, and style to a living room table. Heart-shaped ceramic vases bring a minimalist and Nordic vibe. They make great centerpieces for weddings, dining, or everyday decor, creating a warm and inviting space.



### Why Use Faux Books For Living Room Decor?



Faux books offer rustic charm without cluttering space. They provide height variation and a farmhouse aesthetic. Paired with wood bead garlands, they create a cozy, styled look perfect for coffee tables and shelves.



### What Makes A Wooden Serving Tray Ideal For Decor?



Wooden serving trays add farmhouse warmth and practical elegance. Whitewashed bead trays offer rustic charm and versatility for decor or serving. They make excellent centerpieces and help organize items on living room or kitchen tables.



### How Do Flameless Led Candles Improve Living Room Ambiance?



Flameless LED candles provide safe, flickering light without fire risk. They come with remotes and timers for easy use. Ideal for weddings, parties, or everyday decor, they create a cozy, inviting atmosphere effortlessly.



### What Is The Purpose Of Silicone Coasters On Tables?



Silicone coasters protect surfaces from heat and moisture damage. They are non-slip, absorbent, and easy to clean. Available in sets with holders, they keep living room tables neat and prevent water rings or stains.
