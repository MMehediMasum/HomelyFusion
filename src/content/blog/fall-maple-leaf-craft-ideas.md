### Pressed Leaf Art

Pressed leaf art keeps leaves flat and dry. It helps keep their color and shape. You can frame pressed leaves or make cards with them.

* Collect fresh leaves without tears or holes.
* Place leaves between two sheets of paper.
* Put the paper in a heavy book.
* Leave it for 1-2 weeks to dry completely.
* Use glue to stick leaves on paper or canvas.

### Leaf Rubbing Techniques

Leaf rubbing shows leaf veins and patterns. You only need paper and crayons or pencils. It is a fun way to explore leaf textures.

## Leaf Garlands And Banners

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=fall-is-a-great-time-to-decorate-with-colorful-leaves.-leaf-garlands-and-banners-bring-warmth-to-any-room.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Fall is a great time to decorate with colorful leaves. Leaf garlands and banners bring warmth to any room. They are easy to make and look beautiful.

You can use paper leaves or real leaves to create these decorations. Both options add a natural feel to your space.

### Stringing Paper Leaves

Paper leaves are simple to cut and customize. Use different colors like red, orange, and yellow to mimic fall leaves. Punch holes in the leaves to string them together.

Use twine or thin string to thread the leaves. Space the leaves evenly for a neat look. You can hang the garland on walls, windows, or mantels.

* Cut leaf shapes from colored paper
* Punch holes at the top of each leaf
* Thread leaves onto a string or twine
* Space leaves about 2-3 inches apart
* Hang the garland in your desired spot

### Using Real Leaves For Garland

Real leaves add texture and natural beauty to your garland. Choose leaves that are dry and not brittle. Press the leaves flat for easier stringing.

Use a needle to carefully thread the string through each leaf stem. Tie knots between leaves to keep them in place. Real leaf garlands look great on doors and shelves.

* Collect dry, flat leaves
* Use a needle to thread string through leaf stems
* Tie knots between leaves to space them out
* Handle leaves gently to avoid tearing
* Display garland on doorways or shelves

## Tips For Long-lasting Decor

Fall maple leaf crafts add warm colors to your home. To keep them fresh, you need good care. This guide shares easy tips for long-lasting decorations.

Using natural leaves is fun, but they can dry out fast. Learning how to preserve them helps your crafts stay beautiful for weeks.

### Preserving Natural Leaves

To keep maple leaves looking bright, dry them slowly. Pressing leaves between heavy books keeps them flat and smooth. You can also use a microwave to dry leaves faster.

* Place leaves between two sheets of paper.
* Put heavy books on top for 7-10 days.
* Spray leaves with clear acrylic spray to seal moisture.
* Keep leaves away from direct sunlight to avoid fading.

Another way is to use glycerin. Soaking leaves in a water and glycerin mix makes them soft and flexible. This method keeps leaves from cracking.

### Safe Crafting Practices

When crafting with leaves, be careful with tools like scissors and glue. Use child-safe scissors if kids join in. Work in a well-ventilated area when using sprays or glue.

* Keep sharp tools out of children’s reach.
* Use non-toxic glue and sprays.
* Wear gloves if using chemicals to protect your skin.
* Clean your workspace after crafting to avoid mess.

Following these safe steps helps prevent accidents and keeps your crafting fun and worry-free.
