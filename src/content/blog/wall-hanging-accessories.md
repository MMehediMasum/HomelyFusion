## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Surfaces Are Adhesive Hooks Compatible With?



Adhesive hooks work well on smooth, clean surfaces like glass, tiles, metal, and painted walls. Avoid rough or porous surfaces for best adhesion. Proper surface preparation ensures strong, long-lasting hold without damaging walls or furniture.



### How Much Weight Can These Heavy-duty Hooks Hold?



Heavy-duty adhesive hooks can hold up to 24 to 33 pounds, depending on the model. This makes them ideal for hanging keys, hats, towels, and lightweight decor without nails or drilling.



### Are Adhesive Hooks Waterproof And Reusable?



Yes, many adhesive hooks are waterproof and suitable for bathrooms and kitchens. Some models are also reusable and removable without leaving residue, making them perfect for temporary or flexible use.



### How Do Wall Hanging Organizers Improve Home Storage?



Wall hanging organizers maximize vertical space, reducing clutter. They offer multiple pockets for storing jewelry, accessories, and small items, keeping spaces neat and accessible in bedrooms, kitchens, or offices.



### Can Adhesive Hooks Damage Walls When Removed?



When removed carefully, adhesive hooks typically leave no marks or damage. Use slow peeling at an angle to avoid paint or wallpaper damage, making them ideal for renters or temporary setups.
