### Step-by-step Pairing Guide

Follow these steps to connect your Ecosmart bulbs to Google Home quickly.

1. Turn on your Ecosmart bulb and make sure it is in pairing mode.
2. Open the Google Home app on your smartphone.
3. Tap the "+" icon to add a new device.
4. Select "Set up device" and then "Works with Google."
5. Search for the Ecosmart service in the list.
6. Log into your Ecosmart account to link it with Google Home.
7. Choose the bulbs you want to add to your Google Home setup.
8. Assign bulbs to rooms for easier control.
9. Finish setup and test voice commands like "Hey Google, turn on the living room light."

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If your Ecosmart bulbs do not respond to Google Home, try these fixes.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Problem | Solution |
| Bulb not found in Google Home | Reset the bulb and retry pairing. |
| Google Home cannot control bulb | Check Wi-Fi connection and ensure bulb is online. |
| Voice commands do not work | Verify the bulb is linked to Google Home and named correctly. |
| App shows error during setup | Update the Google Home and Ecosmart apps to the latest version. |
