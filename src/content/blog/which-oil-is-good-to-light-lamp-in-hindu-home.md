## How To Prepare Oil For Lamp Lighting

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=lighting-a-lamp-in-a-hindu-home-is-a-sacred-act.-choosing-and-preparing-the-right-oil-is-important.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Lighting a lamp in a Hindu home is a sacred act. Choosing and preparing the right oil is important. The oil should be pure and offer a steady flame.

This guide explains how to prepare oil properly for lighting lamps. Follow simple purification and mixing steps for the best results.

### Purification Rituals

Purity of oil is essential before use. Many families cleanse the oil to remove impurities and increase its spiritual value.

* Filter the oil through a clean cloth to remove dirt.
* Mix the oil with a few drops of water and let it settle overnight.
* Remove any sediment that appears at the bottom.
* Some people add a pinch of turmeric or holy basil leaves while heating the oil slightly.
* Cool the oil before using it in the lamp.

### Mixing Oils For Best Results

Mixing different oils can improve the lamp's flame and fragrance. Each oil has unique qualities that add to the lamp lighting experience.
