## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Using Artificial Wall Plants?



Artificial wall plants add greenery without maintenance. They last long, resist fading, and suit all lighting conditions. Perfect for busy homes, they enhance aesthetics and create a fresh, natural vibe indoors without watering or sunlight needs.



### How To Hang Wall Decor Without Damaging Walls?



Use heavy-duty double-sided tape or adhesive strips designed for walls. They hold firmly and remove cleanly. This avoids nails or drilling, preserving your wall’s surface. Ideal for renters or seasonal decor updates.



### What Styles Of Wall Decor Fit A Boho Interior?



Boho decor features natural elements like faux vines, wooden vases, and hanging plants. Soft textiles, paper lanterns, and colorful art enhance the relaxed, eclectic vibe. Mixing textures and greenery creates a cozy, artistic space.



### Are Framed Animal Prints Suitable For Kids’ Rooms?



Yes, framed animal prints add charm and personality to kids’ rooms. They stimulate imagination and create a playful environment. Choose colorful, cute designs like kittens or funny animals to appeal to children.



### Can Stuffed Animal Hammocks Help Organize Kids’ Rooms?



Stuffed animal hammocks provide practical, stylish storage. They keep toys off the floor and tidy the space. Easy to install, they fit corners and blend with nursery decor while maximizing room organization.



### What Decorative Tapes Work Best For Picture Hanging?



Nano adhesive mounting tapes and heavy-duty double-sided tapes are ideal. They offer strong hold, easy application, and damage-free removal. Suitable for posters, photos, and light frames, these tapes simplify wall decorating tasks.
