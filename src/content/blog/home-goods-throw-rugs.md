## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Materials For Throw Rugs?



Throw rugs are commonly made from cotton, polyester, chenille, and recycled yarn. These materials offer softness, durability, and easy maintenance. For high-traffic areas, choose non-slip, machine-washable options to ensure safety and convenience. Natural fibers like cotton add a cozy, eco-friendly touch to your home decor.



### How Do Non-slip Rugs Improve Home Safety?



Non-slip rugs have rubber or latex backing that prevents slipping and sliding. This feature reduces fall risks, especially in busy areas like kitchens and entryways. Using non-slip throw rugs enhances safety for kids, seniors, and pets while protecting your floors from scratches and wear.



### Can Throw Rugs Be Machine Washed?



Many modern throw rugs, like the OLANLY and Ophanie models, are machine washable. This makes cleaning simple and efficient. Always check care labels before washing. Machine-washable rugs help maintain hygiene and keep your home fresh without damaging the rug’s texture or colors.



### What Size Throw Rug Fits A Small Entryway?



Small entryways typically fit rugs sized around 2x3 feet or 20x32 inches. These compact rugs, like the Kitinjoy or Finchitty mats, effectively trap dirt and moisture. Choosing the right size ensures the rug covers the floor without overcrowding, providing a neat and welcoming entrance.



### Are Fluffy Shaggy Rugs Suitable For Kids' Rooms?



Yes, fluffy shaggy rugs like OLANLY’s 4x5. 9 ft model add warmth and comfort to kids’ rooms. They provide a soft surface for play and rest. Their machine-washable feature makes them easy to clean, maintaining a healthy environment for children.



### How Do Throw Rugs Enhance Home Decor Aesthetics?



Throw rugs add texture, color, and style to any room. Boho, rustic, or modern designs complement various decor themes. They create cozy zones and accentuate furniture. Rugs like Cotton Craft’s handwoven pieces bring artisanal charm while protecting floors and adding warmth.
