## Choosing The Right Wood Type

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-wood-for-a-circle-wooden-table-in-your-living-room-is-important.-the-wood-type-affects-the-table's-look,-strength,-and-how-long-it-lasts.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right wood for a circle wooden table in your living room is important. The wood type affects the table's look, strength, and how long it lasts.

This guide helps you understand the differences in wood types to pick the best one for your table.

### Hardwood Vs Softwood

Hardwood comes from trees that lose leaves each year. Softwood comes from trees that keep leaves year-round. Hardwoods are usually stronger and last longer.

Softwoods are lighter and easier to shape but can dent more easily. Knowing the difference helps you choose the right wood for your table.

### Popular Wood Options

* **Oak:**A strong hardwood with a classic look.
* **Maple:**Smooth grain and very durable hardwood.
* **Walnut:**Dark wood with rich color and hardness.
* **Pine:**Light softwood with a rustic feel.
* **Cedar:**Softwood that resists moisture and insects.

### Durability And Maintenance

| Wood Type | Durability | Maintenance Tips |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oak | High | Clean with dry cloth; use polish yearly |
| Maple | High | Wipe spills quickly; avoid harsh cleaners |
| Walnut | High | Use soft cloth; re-oil to keep shine |
| Pine | Medium | Protect from scratches; avoid water stains |
| Cedar | Medium | Dust regularly; avoid direct sunlight |

## Size And Placement Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=a-circle-wooden-table-can-be-a-beautiful-and-functional-addition-to-your-living-room.-choosing-the-right-size-and-placing-it-well-will-enhance-your-space.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A circle wooden table can be a beautiful and functional addition to your living room. Choosing the right size and placing it well will enhance your space.

This guide shares tips on measuring your space, picking the best table diameter, and arranging furniture around the table.

### Measuring Your Space

Start by measuring the area where you want the table. Use a tape measure to get length and width in feet or meters.

* Measure the full floor space available.
* Note any obstacles like doors or windows.
* Leave enough walking space around the table.
* Consider the height of nearby furniture.

### Optimal Table Diameter

The diameter of a round table affects comfort and flow. Choose a size that fits your space and needs.

| Room Size (feet) | Recommended Table Diameter (inches) | Seating Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 x 8 | 30-36 | 2-3 people |
| 10 x 10 | 36-42 | 4 people |
| 12 x 12 | 42-48 | 4-6 people |
| 14 x 14+ | 48-54+ | 6+ people |

### Arranging Surrounding Furniture

Place the table so it balances with other furniture. Allow enough space for movement and use.

* Keep at least 18 inches between the table and chairs.
* Leave 36 inches clearance around the table for walking.
* Arrange sofas and chairs to face or circle the table.
* Avoid blocking pathways or doors with the table.

## Caring For Your Wooden Table

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=a-circle-wooden-table-adds-warmth-to-your-living-room.-proper-care-keeps-it-looking-beautiful.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A circle wooden table adds warmth to your living room. Proper care keeps it looking beautiful. Let's explore some ways to care for your wooden table.

Cleaning, preventing damage, and maintenance are key to its longevity. Follow these simple steps to ensure your table stays in top condition.

### Cleaning Techniques

Regular cleaning keeps your wooden table looking fresh. Use a soft, damp cloth to wipe the surface. Avoid harsh chemicals that might harm the wood.

Dry the table with a clean, dry cloth after wiping. This prevents moisture damage. Dust the table weekly to keep it free of particles.

* Use mild soap for sticky spots
* Avoid soaking the wood with water
* Polish occasionally for extra shine

### Preventing Damage

Prevent scratches by using coasters and placemats. Don't place hot items directly on the table. Use trivets or mats for protection.

Keep the table away from direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade and dry out the wood. Also, avoid placing it near heating vents.

* Use felt pads under heavy objects
* Rotate items to prevent uneven wear
* Keep pets' nails trimmed

### Long-term Maintenance

Long-term care includes regular checks for any signs of wear. Reapply wood finish as needed to maintain the protective layer.

Inspect the table for loose joints and fix them promptly. This ensures the table remains sturdy and safe for use.

* Schedule annual deep cleaning
* Consider professional refinishing for aged tables
* Store indoors during extreme weather conditions
