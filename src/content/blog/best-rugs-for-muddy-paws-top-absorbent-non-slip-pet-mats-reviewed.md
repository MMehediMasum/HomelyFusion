Low-profile designs fit well in entryways without causing trips. Some rugs resist stains and fading, lasting longer despite heavy use. Sizes vary to fit small doorways or large mudrooms. Colors like black, navy, or beige hide dirt better. Investing in a durable, absorbent rug protects floors and makes life easier for pet owners. Choose mats designed for muddy paws to keep your home neat.
