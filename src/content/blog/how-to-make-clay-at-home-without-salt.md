## Ingredients Needed

Making clay at home without salt is simple and safe. You only need a few basic ingredients.

This guide covers all you need to prepare soft, moldable clay for crafts and play.

### Flour

Flour is the main base of homemade clay. Use all-purpose flour for best results.

### Water

Water helps combine the flour and other ingredients into a dough. Use warm water to mix easily.

### Oil

Oil keeps the clay soft and smooth. Vegetable oil or baby oil works well.

### Cream Of Tartar

Cream of tartar adds elasticity to the clay. It helps the dough stay flexible and last longer.

### Optional: Food Coloring

Add food coloring if you want to make colored clay. Just a few drops will tint the dough.

* All-purpose flour - 2 cups
* Warm water - 1 cup
* Vegetable oil - 2 tablespoons
* Cream of tartar - 1 tablespoon
* Food coloring - a few drops (optional)

## Step-by-step Clay Recipe

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-clay-at-home-is-fun-and-easy.-you-can-create-clay-without-using-salt.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making clay at home is fun and easy. You can create clay without using salt.

This guide shows you how to make soft, moldable clay using simple ingredients.

### Mixing Ingredients

Start by gathering the ingredients you need for the clay. Mixing them well is key.

Use equal parts of flour and cornstarch. Add water slowly while stirring.

* 1 cup all-purpose flour
* 1 cup cornstarch
* 1 cup water (add slowly)
* 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
* Optional: food coloring

### Kneading The Dough

After mixing, knead the dough on a clean surface. This makes the clay smooth.

Keep kneading for about 5 to 7 minutes. This helps ingredients blend well.

* If the dough sticks, sprinkle a little flour on your hands.
* Use gentle pressure to avoid tearing the dough.
* Stop when the dough feels soft and stretchy.

### Adjusting Texture

If the clay is too sticky, add a bit more flour. If it is too dry, add water.

Check the texture often to get the best feel for your clay. It should be easy to shape.

* Sticky dough: add flour one teaspoon at a time.
* Dry dough: add water one teaspoon at a time.
* Store unused clay in an airtight container to keep it soft.

## Storage And Shelf Life

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-clay-at-home-without-salt-is-fun-and-easy.-knowing-how-to-store-it-helps-keep-it-soft-and-ready-to-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making clay at home without salt is fun and easy. Knowing how to store it helps keep it soft and ready to use.

Proper storage also extends the clay’s shelf life. This guide shares simple tips to store your salt-free clay well.

### How To Store Clay

Keep your clay in an airtight container. This stops air from drying it out. You can use plastic boxes or zip-lock bags.

* Wrap the clay in plastic wrap first
* Place the wrapped clay inside a sealed container
* Store the container in a cool, dry place
* Do not freeze the clay as it may change texture

### Shelf Life Of Homemade Clay

The shelf life of salt-free clay depends on storage and humidity. Usually, it lasts for 1 to 2 weeks when stored well.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Condition | Expected Shelf Life |
| Properly sealed and stored | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Exposed to air | 2 to 3 days |
| Stored in freezer | Not recommended |

### Signs Clay Has Gone Bad

Check your clay before using it. If the clay feels hard, cracked, or smells strange, it is time to make a new batch.

**Tips:**

* If clay is too dry, add a few drops of water and knead it well.
* Keep small portions ready to use, store the rest.
* Label containers with the date made.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Making clay at home without salt can be fun. Sometimes, things don't go as planned. Here, you'll learn how to fix common problems.

These tips will help you save time and improve your clay-making skills. Let's explore the solutions to common issues.

### Clay Is Too Sticky

If your clay is too sticky, don't worry. You can fix it easily with some tricks.

* Add a small amount of cornstarch to the mixture.
* Mix in more flour until it reaches the right consistency.
* Let the clay air dry for a few minutes.

### Clay Is Too Dry

Dry clay can be hard to work with. Here are some steps to soften it.

* Add water, a teaspoon at a time, to the clay.
* Knead the clay until it becomes smooth.
* Cover the clay with a damp cloth to keep it moist.

### Clay Cracks When Drying

Cracks can ruin your clay projects. Fix this by adjusting your process.

* Ensure the clay is evenly mixed before shaping.
* Dry the clay slowly in a cool, dry place.
* Patch cracks with a mixture of water and clay.

### Clay Doesn't Hold Shape

If your clay collapses, it might be too soft. Here's how to make it firmer.

* Mix in more flour to increase thickness.
* Let the clay rest for a bit before using it.
* Store unused clay in a sealed container.
