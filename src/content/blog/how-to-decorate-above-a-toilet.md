**Looking to transform the space above your toilet but not sure where to start? You’re not alone.**

That small wall often gets ignored, yet it holds amazing potential to boost your bathroom’s style and feel. Imagine turning that blank spot into a charming focal point that makes your bathroom feel fresh and inviting. You’ll discover simple, creative ideas that fit any budget and style.

Ready to make your bathroom more beautiful and personal? Let’s dive in and explore how to decorate above your toilet like a pro.



## Choose The Right Wall Art

Choosing the right wall art above your toilet can change the whole look of your bathroom. The right art adds color, personality, and charm to a small space. It also helps make the area feel more inviting and stylish.

Bathrooms can be tricky places for wall art. The moisture and steam can damage many types of artwork. Picking the right kind of art keeps your bathroom looking fresh and beautiful for a long time.

### Select Waterproof Prints

Waterproof prints are the best choice for bathroom walls. These prints resist water damage and won’t fade from steam. You can find art made with special inks or printed on waterproof paper. These prints last longer and stay bright.

### Frame With Moisture-resistant Materials

Use frames made from moisture-resistant materials like metal or plastic. Wood frames can warp or rot in humid bathrooms. A good frame protects your art and keeps it looking neat. Choose simple, clean designs to match any bathroom style.

### Create A Gallery Wall

A gallery wall adds interest above the toilet. Mix different sizes and shapes of art for a creative look. Use waterproof prints and moisture-resistant frames together. This style brings personality and makes the space feel unique and fun.

## Install Functional Shelving

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-functional-shelving-above-the-toilet-is-a-smart-way-to-use-empty-wall-space.-shelves-create-storage-without-crowding-the-room.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing functional shelving above the toilet is a smart way to use empty wall space. Shelves create storage without crowding the room. They keep essentials close and add style. Choose shelves that fit your bathroom size and needs. Shelving can be both practical and decorative.

### Floating Shelves For Minimalist Look

Floating shelves are slim and sleek. They appear to float on the wall without visible brackets. This design keeps the bathroom feeling open and clean. Use them to hold small plants, candles, or toiletries. Floating shelves suit modern and simple bathroom styles.

### Open Cabinets For Extra Storage

Open cabinets offer more storage space while keeping items visible. You can stack towels or store toilet paper inside. This type of shelving helps organize clutter neatly. Open cabinets create easy access to daily essentials. They work well in larger bathrooms with enough wall space.

### Use Baskets And Boxes

Baskets and boxes add order to shelves. Use them to hide small items and keep the area tidy. Choose materials like wicker or fabric for a cozy look. Label baskets for quick access to supplies. This method combines style with function, making shelves look neat and inviting.

## Add Greenery

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adding-greenery-above-the-toilet-can-brighten-the-space-and-bring-life-to-your-bathroom.-plants-create-a-fresh-and-calming-feel.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adding greenery above the toilet can brighten the space and bring life to your bathroom. Plants create a fresh and calming feel. They also improve air quality and add natural color. Small touches of green make the area inviting without crowding it.

### Use Small Potted Plants

Choose small pots to fit the space above the toilet. Succulents and cacti work well because they need little water. Place them on a narrow shelf or a small ledge. These plants add texture and a pop of green. They require minimal care and thrive in bathroom humidity.

### Hang Air Plants

Air plants do not need soil. Hang them in glass holders or wire frames above the toilet. They take up little space and look modern. Air plants absorb moisture from the air. They bring a unique, natural touch without cluttering the area.

### Incorporate Faux Plants

Faux plants are perfect for low-light bathrooms. They need no water or sunlight. Choose realistic designs for a natural look. Place them in decorative pots or baskets. Faux greenery stays fresh-looking all year round with zero effort.

## Focus On Lighting

Lighting plays a key role in decorating above a toilet. It brightens the space and adds style. Good lighting makes the area feel fresh and inviting. It also highlights any decor you place above the toilet.

Choosing the right type of lighting can change the whole bathroom look. Soft, warm light works well for a cozy feel. Bright, clear light helps with a clean and modern look. Here are some easy lighting ideas to try.

### Install Wall Sconces

Wall sconces add light and style above the toilet. They come in many shapes and sizes. Pick sconces that match your bathroom’s theme. Place them on both sides of a shelf or artwork. This creates balance and draws attention upward.

Choose sconces with soft bulbs for gentle light. Avoid harsh lighting that can be too bright. Wall sconces save space and keep the area neat. They make the spot above the toilet a focal point.

### Use Led Strip Lighting

LED strip lights are thin and easy to install. Place them under a shelf or cabinet above the toilet. They add a soft glow without taking up space. LED strips come in warm or cool tones to fit your style.

These lights are energy efficient and last long. You can even find color-changing options for a fun effect. LED strips highlight the area without overwhelming it. They give a modern and sleek touch to your bathroom.

### Add Candles Or Lanterns

Candles bring warmth and a relaxing mood. Place a few small candles on a shelf above the toilet. Use safe holders to avoid accidents. Lanterns work well for a rustic or vintage look.

Candles offer soft, flickering light that feels calm. They make the bathroom feel like a spa. Try scented candles to add a fresh smell. This small touch improves both light and ambiance above the toilet.






## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are The Best Decor Ideas Above A Toilet?

Popular decor ideas include floating shelves, framed art, plants, or decorative baskets. These add style and storage without cluttering space.

### How Can I Maximize Space Above A Toilet?

Use vertical storage like shelves or cabinets. Choose slim, wall-mounted options to keep the bathroom organized and spacious.

### What Materials Work Well For Bathroom Decor Above Toilets?

Moisture-resistant materials like glass, metal, or treated wood are ideal. They withstand humidity and prevent damage over time.

### How Do I Choose The Right Size Decor Above A Toilet?

Select decor that fits the wall space without overwhelming it. Balance proportion to maintain a neat, visually pleasing look.
