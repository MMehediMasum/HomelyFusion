## Frequently Asked Questions



### How To Style Decorative Books On A Coffee Table?



To style decorative books, stack them in groups of three or four. Mix different sizes and colors for a layered look. Use the top book as a base for small ornaments or a vase. This adds depth and interest to your coffee table decor.



### What Are The Benefits Of Using A Vintage Decorative Tray?



A vintage decorative tray adds a touch of elegance and organization. Use it to display jewelry, cosmetics, or small decor items. Its design enhances any room's aesthetic, while its functionality keeps items neatly arranged, making it both stylish and practical.



### How To Incorporate Wood Knot Decor Into A Room?



Wood knot decor adds a rustic and boho touch to any space. Place it on bookshelves or coffee tables for a natural element. Its handcrafted design makes it a unique focal point. Pair it with other natural materials for a cohesive look.



### Why Choose A Rotating Sunglass Organizer?



A rotating sunglass organizer provides easy access and efficient storage. Its 360° rotation helps you find the perfect pair effortlessly. The stylish design adds a modern touch to any room, while its functionality ensures your sunglasses are always within reach.



### How To Maintain Artificial Potted Plants?



Artificial potted plants require minimal maintenance. Dust them regularly with a soft cloth to keep them looking fresh. Avoid placing them in direct sunlight to prevent fading. They provide a long-lasting green touch without the hassle of watering or pruning.
