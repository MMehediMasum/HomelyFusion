## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Sizes Are Available For Accent Pillow Covers?



Accent pillow covers come in various sizes like 12x20, 18x18, and 20x20 inches. These sizes fit most couches, beds, and living room furniture perfectly.



### Which Materials Are Best For Decorative Throw Pillows?



Materials like faux fur, plush velvet, linen, and corduroy offer softness and durability. They suit farmhouse and boho home decor styles well.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain Accent Pillow Covers?



Most covers are machine washable or easy to spot clean. Always check the care label for specific washing instructions to preserve fabric quality.



### Can Accent Pillows Enhance Farmhouse And Boho Decor Styles?



Yes, pillows with neutral tones, floral prints, and textured fabrics complement farmhouse and boho aesthetics beautifully, adding warmth and charm to your space.



### Are Pillow Covers Suitable For Outdoor Use?



Some linen and durable fabric pillow covers are suitable for outdoor use. However, confirm product details to ensure weather resistance and longevity.
