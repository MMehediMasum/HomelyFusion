## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Material Is Best For Bathroom Shower Curtains?



PEVA and polyester fabrics are ideal for bathroom curtains. They resist water, prevent mold, and are easy to clean. Plastic liners like PEVA offer waterproof protection, while thick polyester adds durability and style. Both materials help maintain a fresh, dry bathroom environment.



### How Do Rustproof Grommets Benefit Shower Curtains?



Rustproof grommets prevent corrosion from constant water exposure. They extend curtain life and maintain a clean look. Metal grommets ensure easy sliding on rods without damage. This feature is essential for bathroom curtains to avoid stains and frequent replacements.



### Can Bathroom Curtains Provide Privacy And Light Control?



Yes, curtains like blackout or thermal insulated panels offer privacy and block light effectively. Sheer or semi-sheer curtains filter light while maintaining some privacy. Choose curtains based on window size and desired light control to enhance bathroom comfort.



### What Size Curtain Is Suitable For Standard Bathroom Windows?



Standard bathroom curtains often measure around 42 inches wide by 36 to 63 inches long. Shower curtains usually come in 72 by 72 inches. Measure your window or tub area to select curtains that fit perfectly without excess fabric.



### Are Weighted Curtains Better For Bathroom Use?



Weighted curtains prevent billowing and stay in place during drafts. Heavy-duty fabrics like 256GSM polyester offer durability and a luxurious feel. Weighted curtains enhance functionality and aesthetics by providing better coverage and reducing water splashes outside the shower.



### How To Maintain And Clean Bathroom Curtains Effectively?



Plastic liners can be wiped or machine washed regularly to prevent mildew. Fabric curtains often require gentle washing and air drying. Always follow manufacturer instructions to preserve curtain quality and extend lifespan while keeping bathrooms hygienic.
