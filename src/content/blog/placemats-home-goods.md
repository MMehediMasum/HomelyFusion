From the classic SHACOS braided placemats to the rustic charm of CY SISTERS woven designs, each option offers something unique.



For those seeking easy maintenance, vinyl and plastic options like LEXMORE are ideal. They are durable and wipeable. If you prefer a disposable solution for events, paper placemats provide convenience and variety. With choices like MOSAYT's faux leather and CADONO's heat-resistant designs, you can match your placemats to any occasion.



The right placemat not only protects your table but also elevates your dining experience. So, explore these varied styles and materials to find the perfect fit for your home. Create a dining atmosphere that reflects your personality and taste.
