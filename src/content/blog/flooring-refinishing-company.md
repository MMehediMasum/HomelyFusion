## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Hiring A Flooring Refinishing Company?



Hiring a flooring refinishing company restores your floors' beauty and durability. Experts use professional tools and products for lasting results. It saves time and ensures even, smooth finishes. Refinishing also increases home value and protects wood from future damage.



### How Long Does Hardwood Floor Refinishing Take?



Hardwood floor refinishing typically takes 2 to 4 days. The process includes sanding, staining, and sealing. Drying time between coats affects duration. Professionals plan efficiently to minimize disruption while ensuring quality results.



### Can I Refinish Floors Myself Or Hire Professionals?



DIY refinishing is possible but challenging. Professionals have experience and specialized tools for flawless finishes. Hiring experts reduces mistakes, saves time, and ensures durable results. Consider your skill level and project size before deciding.



### What Products Are Used In Tile And Stone Floor Repair?



Tile and stone floor repair uses crack and chip fillers, porcelain repair kits, and refinishing paints. These products restore surface smoothness and color. High-quality kits include tools and cleaners for effective application on tiles, marble, granite, and bathtubs.



### How To Maintain Hardwood Floors After Refinishing?



Maintain hardwood floors by regular sweeping and damp mopping. Use recommended hardwood cleaners and avoid excessive water. Place protective pads under furniture. Refinish or reseal floors every few years to keep them looking new and protected.



### What Is Included In A Tub And Tile Refinishing Kit?



A tub and tile refinishing kit includes paint, cleaner, filler, and application tools. It allows easy repair and refinishing of bathroom surfaces. Kits cover cracks, chips, and discoloration, restoring a smooth, glossy finish for tiles, tubs, and countertops.
