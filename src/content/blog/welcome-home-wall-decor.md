## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In These Wall Decors?



The wall decors are crafted from various materials like acrylic mirrors, wood, and artificial eucalyptus. These materials create a stylish and modern look. They are durable and lightweight, making them easy to install and maintain. The combination of materials adds texture and depth to any room.



### How Do You Install Acrylic Mirror Wall Stickers?



To install, clean the wall surface thoroughly. Peel off the backing from the sticker. Carefully place it on the desired spot, smoothing out any bubbles. Press firmly to secure it. Ensure the wall is dry and dust-free for optimal adhesion.



The process is quick and straightforward.



### Are These Wall Decorations Suitable For Every Room?



Yes, these decorations are versatile and can enhance any room. They are perfect for living rooms, bedrooms, and entryways. Their design complements various decor styles, from modern to rustic. These pieces can add warmth and personality to any space in your home.



### How Do I Maintain Wooden Wall Decor?



Maintaining wooden wall decor is simple. Regularly dust with a soft cloth to prevent buildup. Avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive materials. For deeper cleaning, use a damp cloth with mild soap. Ensure the wood is dry after cleaning to maintain its quality and appearance.



### Can I Use These Signs Outdoors?



Most signs are designed for indoor use, but some can be used outdoors. Check the product description for outdoor suitability. If used outdoors, consider placing them in a sheltered area. Protect them from direct sunlight and moisture to prevent damage and fading over time.
