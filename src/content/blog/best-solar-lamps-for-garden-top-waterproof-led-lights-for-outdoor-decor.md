## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Long Do Solar Garden Lamps Typically Last?



Most solar garden lamps last between 8 to 12 hours after a full day of charging. Quality models like GIGALUMI can maintain lighting for up to 10 hours, ensuring your garden stays illuminated all night without extra electricity costs.



### Are Solar Lamps Waterproof For Outdoor Use?



Yes, top solar lamps such as GIGALUMI and INCX are waterproof with IP65 ratings. This makes them ideal for outdoor use in gardens, patios, and walkways, surviving rain and harsh weather without damage.



### What Types Of Solar Lamps Suit Garden Decoration?



Solar lamps come in various styles like LED pathway lights, flickering flame torches, and decorative globe orbs. Choose based on your garden’s theme and lighting needs for both functionality and aesthetic appeal.



### How Do Solar Lamps Charge And Turn On Automatically?



Solar lamps use built-in solar panels to charge batteries during the day. They feature light sensors to automatically turn on at dusk and off at dawn, providing hassle-free, energy-efficient garden lighting.



### Can Solar Lamps Improve Garden Safety At Night?



Yes, solar lamps enhance garden safety by illuminating pathways, steps, and dark areas. Bright, durable solar lights like BITPOTT and GIGALUMI reduce tripping hazards and deter intruders with consistent outdoor lighting.



### Are Solar Garden Lamps Easy To Install And Maintain?



Absolutely, solar garden lamps require no wiring and are simple to install anywhere with sunlight exposure. Maintenance is minimal, usually involving occasional cleaning of solar panels to ensure optimal charging performance.
