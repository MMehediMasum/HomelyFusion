## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Hobby Lobby Chair Covers Made Of?



Hobby Lobby chair covers are typically made from stretchy polyester spandex. This material offers a snug, wrinkle-free fit. It is durable, washable, and perfect for events like weddings or parties. The fabric also allows easy cleaning and protects chairs from stains and damage.



### Are Hobby Lobby Chair Covers Suitable For All Chair Types?



Most Hobby Lobby chair covers are designed with a universal fit. They work well on folding, banquet, and dining chairs. However, measuring your chair before purchase ensures the best fit. Stretch materials adapt well but may not fit uniquely shaped chairs perfectly.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain These Chair Covers?



Most Hobby Lobby chair covers are machine washable. Use cold water and gentle cycles to preserve fabric quality. Avoid bleach to maintain color and elasticity. Air dry or tumble dry on low heat to prevent shrinking and maintain the cover’s stretch.



### Can Hobby Lobby Chair Covers Be Used For Outdoor Events?



Yes, many Hobby Lobby chair covers are suitable for outdoor use. Waterproof and water-resistant options protect chairs during outdoor weddings or parties. However, prolonged exposure to harsh weather might affect fabric longevity, so bringing spares is advisable.



### Do These Chair Covers Come In Different Colors And Styles?



Hobby Lobby offers chair covers in various colors including white, black, and off-white. Styles range from flat-front to velvet and waterproof options. This variety helps match different event themes and chair types perfectly. Check availability online or in-store for current selections.
