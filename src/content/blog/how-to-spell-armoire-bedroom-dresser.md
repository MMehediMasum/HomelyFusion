This article explains how the meaning and spelling of "armoire" and "dresser" differ. Understanding these differences helps you choose the right word.

### Differences In Meaning

An armoire is a tall, usually wooden cabinet with doors. It often has shelves or hanging space inside. A dresser is shorter and wider, with drawers to hold clothes.

* An armoire has doors and may include a mirror.
* A dresser has multiple drawers stacked horizontally.
* Armoires often serve as closets for hanging clothes.
* Dressers store folded clothes and smaller items.
