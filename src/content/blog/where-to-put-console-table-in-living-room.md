## Entryway Placement

Placing a console table in the entryway helps organize the space near your front door. It creates a spot for keys, mail, and small items.

This placement adds style and function to the first area guests see in your home.

### Creating A Welcoming Foyer

Use the console table to make your foyer feel warm and inviting. Decorate it with a lamp, flowers, or a mirror above it.

These items add light and personality to the space, making guests feel comfortable right away.

### Storage Solutions Near The Door

A console table in the entryway offers handy storage for daily items. Choose one with drawers or shelves to keep clutter hidden.

Use baskets or trays on the table to organize keys, sunglasses, and mail for easy access.

* Drawers for small items like keys and coins
* Lower shelves for shoes or bags
* Decorative trays for mail and sunglasses
* Hooks underneath for hanging hats or scarves

## Console Table Dimensions

Choosing the right size for a console table is key to fitting it well in your living room. The dimensions must suit the space and your needs.

Console tables come in many sizes, so knowing what fits best helps keep your room balanced and functional.

### Choosing The Right Size

Console tables usually have a height between 28 and 36 inches. The width can range from 30 to 72 inches, while the depth is often 12 to 18 inches.

* Height: 28–36 inches for easy reach and visual balance.
* Width: 30–72 inches depending on wall space and use.
* Depth: 12–18 inches to avoid crowding the room.

### Proportion Tips For Small And Large Rooms

In small rooms, choose a narrow console table to keep the space open. Large rooms allow for wider and taller tables to fill the area properly.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Room Size | Recommended Width | Recommended Depth |
| Small (under 150 sq ft) | 30–40 inches | 12–14 inches |
| Medium (150–300 sq ft) | 40–60 inches | 14–16 inches |
| Large (over 300 sq ft) | 60–72 inches | 16–18 inches |
