Luxury ottomans often use faux leather, tufted linen, and solid wood shelves. These materials provide durability, comfort, and an elegant look suitable for modern home decor styles.



### How Much Weight Can Modern Ottomans Typically Support?



Modern ottomans, such as the Sweetcrispy and Huatean models, support between 330 to 660 lbs. This makes them strong enough for seating, footrests, or storage bench uses in living rooms and bedrooms.
