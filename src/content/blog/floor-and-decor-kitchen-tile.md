## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Peel And Stick Kitchen Tiles?



Peel and stick kitchen tiles offer easy installation without grout or mortar. They are budget-friendly, removable, and ideal for DIY projects. These tiles provide a stylish upgrade with minimal mess and time, perfect for renters or quick kitchen makeovers.



### How Durable Are Floorpops Peel And Stick Floor Tiles?



FloorPops peel and stick floor tiles are water-resistant and scratch-resistant. They handle moderate foot traffic well. However, they are best suited for low to medium-use areas like kitchens and bathrooms, not heavy commercial spaces.



### Can Peel And Stick Tiles Work On Uneven Kitchen Floors?



Peel and stick tiles work best on smooth, clean, and dry surfaces. Uneven floors may cause adhesion issues or visible imperfections. For best results, prepare the floor by cleaning and leveling before installation.



### Are Peel And Stick Backsplash Tiles Heat Resistant?



Most peel and stick backsplash tiles, like STICKGOO, tolerate normal kitchen heat. However, avoid direct contact with open flames or high heat sources to prevent warping or damage. They suit areas behind stoves with moderate heat exposure.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain Vinyl Peel And Stick Tiles?



Clean vinyl peel and stick tiles with a damp mop or cloth and mild detergent. Avoid abrasive cleaners or excessive water. Regular maintenance preserves their look and extends tile life, keeping your kitchen fresh and attractive.



### Can I Remove And Reuse These Peel And Stick Tiles?



Peel and stick tiles can be removed carefully to avoid surface damage. However, they usually lose some adhesive strength after removal, so reusing them is not always recommended. For best results, use new tiles for reinstallation.
