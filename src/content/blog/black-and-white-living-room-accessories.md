Artificial plants and pampas grass add natural touches without much care. Unique items like heart hand statues and wicker balls give charm and interest. Using a mix of textures and shapes keeps the room lively and fresh. Simple vases and metal wall art add elegance and a boho feel.



These accessories work together to make your living room welcoming and neat. Choosing black and white decor makes it easy to change styles later. You can update your space without starting over. With these ideas, your living room will feel both cozy and stylish, perfect for relaxing or entertaining guests.
