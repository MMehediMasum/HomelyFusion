## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Peel And Stick Subway Tiles?



Peel and stick subway tiles are easy to install, cost-effective, and mess-free. They require no grout or special tools. These tiles are perfect for quick kitchen or bathroom upgrades. Plus, they are removable, making them ideal for renters or temporary decor changes.



### How Durable Are Vinyl Peel And Stick Subway Tiles?



Vinyl peel and stick tiles are water-resistant and durable for light to moderate use. They resist stains and scratches well. However, they may not withstand heavy impact or heat. Proper care extends their lifespan, making them suitable for kitchens, bathrooms, and laundry rooms.

Most peel and stick subway tiles are designed for walls and backsplashes. Some thicker vinyl floor stickers can work on low-traffic areas. Always check the product specifications to ensure floor suitability. For high-traffic floors, traditional ceramic or porcelain tiles are better choices.
