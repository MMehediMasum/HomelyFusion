## Choosing Mirror Shapes

Choosing the right mirror shape above your bedroom dresser can change the room's look. It helps create balance and style.

Different shapes suit different styles and room sizes. Think about the space and what feels right for you.

### Round Mirrors

Round mirrors add softness and flow to the bedroom. They break the straight lines of furniture and walls.

This shape works well in small spaces because it feels less heavy. It creates a calm and relaxed look.

### Oval Mirrors

Oval mirrors offer a classic and elegant touch. They are longer than round mirrors and add height visually.

This shape suits traditional and vintage bedroom styles. It adds a gentle curve without being too bold.

### Rectangular Mirrors

Rectangular mirrors are popular for their clean and simple look. They match well with most dresser shapes.

This shape helps create a sense of order. It can make a small room feel taller and more spacious.

### Square Mirrors

Square mirrors give a modern and balanced feel above a dresser. They offer a strong shape with equal sides.

This shape works well in minimalist or contemporary bedrooms. It pairs nicely with square or boxy furniture.

### Geometric Mirrors

Geometric mirrors have unique and creative shapes like hexagons or triangles. They add interest and style.

This shape is great for bold designs. It creates a focal point and shows personality in your bedroom.




## Matching Mirror Size To Dresser

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-mirror-shape-and-size-above-your-bedroom-dresser-is-important.-it-affects-the-room’s-balance-and-style.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right mirror shape and size above your bedroom dresser is important. It affects the room’s balance and style.

This guide helps you match mirror size to your dresser for a neat look. You will learn about proportional sizing, oversized mirrors, and small accent mirrors.

### Proportional Sizing

A mirror should be proportional to the dresser it hangs above. Too big or too small looks awkward.

* The mirror width should be about two-thirds to three-quarters of the dresser width.
* Keep some space between the mirror edges and dresser sides for balance.
* The mirror height can vary but should not overpower the dresser.
* Vertical mirrors suit tall dressers, while horizontal mirrors fit wider dressers.

### Oversized Mirrors

Oversized mirrors create a bold look. They can make a small room feel bigger by reflecting more light.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mirror Size | Effect | When to Use |
| Wider than dresser | Bold statement | Large rooms or empty wall space |
| Taller than dresser | Adds height | Low ceilings or short dressers |
| Full-length style | Functional and stylish | Rooms with dressing areas |

### Small Accent Mirrors

Small mirrors add charm without overpowering the dresser. They work well with decorative frames or grouped arrangements.

* Use a small round or oval mirror for a soft look.
* Place two or three small mirrors in a row for a gallery effect.
* Choose mirrors with unique shapes to add character.
* Keep the total width of grouped mirrors near half the dresser width.

## Placement Tips For Bedroom Mirrors

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-mirror-shape-above-your-bedroom-dresser-can-change-the-room’s-look.-the-placement-of-the-mirror-is-just-as-important-as-its-style.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right mirror shape above your bedroom dresser can change the room’s look. The placement of the mirror is just as important as its style.

This guide gives simple tips on how to place mirrors to make your bedroom feel brighter and more balanced.

### Centering Above The Dresser

Place the mirror so it is centered with the dresser below. This creates balance and a neat appearance. The mirror’s width should be about two-thirds the width of the dresser for a good fit.

* Measure the dresser’s width first.
* Choose a mirror shape that matches the dresser’s style.
* Hang the mirror so its bottom edge is 6 to 12 inches above the dresser surface.

### Layering With Wall Art

Mix mirrors with wall art to add depth and personality. You can place small art pieces around the mirror or hang paintings next to it. This makes the wall look more interesting.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Mirror Shape | Complementary Art Style |
| Round | Abstract or circular art |
| Square | Geometric or modern art |
| Oval | Floral or soft-edged art |
| Rectangular | Classic or framed photos |

### Using Mirrors To Enhance Light

Mirrors can brighten your bedroom by reflecting light. Place the mirror where it catches natural or artificial light. This makes the space feel bigger and more open.

* Position the mirror across from a window to reflect sunlight.
* Place near a lamp to add warmth in the evening.
* Use angled mirrors to spread light into dark corners.
