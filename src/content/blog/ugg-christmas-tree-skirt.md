### How Do I Choose The Right Size Tree Skirt?



Measure your Christmas tree's base diameter to select the right skirt size. Common sizes are 31, 36, and 48 inches. Choose a skirt slightly larger than the base for full coverage. This ensures the skirt fits well and enhances your holiday decor.



### Are Ugg Tree Skirts Easy To Clean And Maintain?



Yes, most Ugg tree skirts are machine washable or spot-clean only. Faux fur and knit materials require gentle care to maintain softness. Follow specific washing instructions to avoid damage. Proper care keeps the skirt looking fresh for many Christmas seasons.



### Can Ugg Christmas Tree Skirts Complement Rustic Home Decor?



Absolutely. Chunky knit and cable knit designs add a warm, rustic charm. Neutral colors like cream, beige, and white blend well with farmhouse or rustic themes. These skirts enhance cozy holiday atmospheres, making your Christmas decorations inviting and stylish.



### Do Ugg Tree Skirts Protect Floors From Tree Sap And Needles?



Yes, thick faux fur and knit skirts help catch needles and sap. They act as a barrier, protecting hardwood or carpeted floors. This keeps your home clean and damage-free during the holiday season. Plus, they add festive decoration around the tree base.
