## Factors To Consider When Buying

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-pillar-candles-can-enhance-your-space-beautifully.-several-factors-influence-the-best-choice.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right pillar candles can enhance your space beautifully. Several factors influence the best choice. Knowing these helps you find candles that suit your needs perfectly. Consider wax type, size, fragrance, and price carefully.

### Wax Type

Different wax types burn in unique ways. Paraffin wax is common and affordable. Soy wax burns cleaner and lasts longer. Beeswax is natural and emits a mild, sweet scent. Pick a wax type that fits your style and budget.

### Size And Shape

Pillar candles come in many sizes and shapes. Taller candles last longer but may need sturdy holders. Shorter candles work well for small spaces. Round or square shapes can change the candle’s look. Choose size and shape based on your display area.

### Fragrance Options

Some pillar candles have scents, others do not. Scented candles add aroma to your room. Popular scents include lavender, vanilla, and cinnamon. Unscented candles are best for allergy sufferers. Decide if you want a fragrance or not.

### Price Range

Pillar candles vary in price from budget to premium. Higher prices often mean better quality or unique scents. Set a price range before shopping to avoid overspending. Compare options within your budget for the best value.




## Tips For Safe Candle Use

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=candles-create-a-warm-and-cozy-atmosphere-in-any-room.-using-pillar-candles-safely-is-important-to-avoid-accidents.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Candles create a warm and cozy atmosphere in any room. Using pillar candles safely is important to avoid accidents. Follow these simple tips to enjoy your candles without worry.

### Proper Placement

Place pillar candles on flat, stable surfaces. Avoid areas near curtains, paper, or anything flammable. Keep candles away from children and pets. Use a heat-resistant holder to catch wax drips. Make sure the candle is not in a drafty spot. This stops the flame from flickering or spreading.

### Burn Time Guidelines

Do not burn pillar candles for more than four hours at once. Let the candle cool before lighting it again. Trim the wick to about 1/4 inch before lighting. This helps the candle burn evenly and lowers smoke. Always follow the manufacturer’s burn time instructions. It keeps the candle safe and lasting longer.

### Extinguishing Techniques

Blow out the candle gently to avoid hot wax splatter. Use a candle snuffer to put out the flame safely. Do not use water to extinguish a candle. Check the wick is no longer glowing before leaving it. This prevents accidental fires and keeps your home safe.
