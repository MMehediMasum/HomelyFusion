## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-a&b-home-chandelier-clean-and-well-maintained-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-keeps-it-bright-and-beautiful.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your A&B Home Chandelier clean and well-maintained helps it last longer. Regular care keeps it bright and beautiful.

Follow simple steps for cleaning, changing bulbs, and staying safe while working on your chandelier.

### Cleaning Techniques

Dust your chandelier often to avoid dirt buildup. Use a soft cloth or feather duster for light cleaning.

For a deeper clean, turn off the power and use a gentle glass cleaner. Wipe each piece carefully to avoid damage.

* Use microfiber cloths for gentle cleaning
* Avoid harsh chemicals that can harm finishes
* Clean one section at a time to prevent missing spots

### Bulb Replacement

Replace bulbs as soon as they burn out to keep your chandelier bright. Use the correct bulb type and wattage.

Turn off the power before changing bulbs. Allow old bulbs to cool down to avoid burns.

* Check the chandelier manual for bulb specifications
* Handle bulbs with a cloth or gloves to avoid oil from your skin
* Test new bulbs before finishing to ensure they work

### Safety Precautions

Always turn off the electricity before cleaning or fixing your chandelier. Use a sturdy ladder to reach high fixtures.

Wear gloves to protect your hands from sharp edges and hot bulbs. Avoid working alone for extra safety.

* Use insulated tools if needed
* Do not touch bulbs or wiring with wet hands
* Keep children and pets away during maintenance
