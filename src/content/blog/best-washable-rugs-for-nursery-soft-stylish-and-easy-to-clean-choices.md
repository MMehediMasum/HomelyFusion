## Frequently Asked Questions



### Are Washable Rugs Safe For Nursery Use?



Yes, washable rugs are safe for nurseries. They are made from non-toxic materials and are easy to clean, reducing allergens and germs. Their softness provides comfort and safety for babies while maintaining hygiene.



### How Do I Wash Nursery Rugs Effectively?



Most nursery rugs are machine washable. Use a gentle cycle with cold water and mild detergent. Avoid bleach and high heat drying to maintain softness and colors. Always check the care label for specific instructions.



### What Size Rug Is Best For A Nursery?



A 4x6 or 5x7 rug size is ideal for nurseries. These sizes fit well under cribs and play areas, providing enough space for comfort and decoration without overcrowding the room.



### Are Low Pile Rugs Better For Nurseries?



Yes, low pile rugs are better for nurseries. They are easier to clean, reduce tripping hazards, and prevent dust accumulation. Low pile rugs also offer a smooth surface for crawling babies.



### Can Washable Nursery Rugs Resist Stains?



Many washable nursery rugs are stain resistant. They use special fibers and finishes that repel liquids and dirt. This feature helps keep the rug looking fresh despite spills and accidents.



### What Styles Suit A Baby Girl’s Nursery Rug?



Neutral blush pink, floral boho, and soft vintage patterns are perfect for baby girl nurseries. These designs create a cozy, stylish environment while blending well with various decor themes.
