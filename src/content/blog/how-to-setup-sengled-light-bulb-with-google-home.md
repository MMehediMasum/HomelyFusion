## Installing The Sengled Bulb

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-a-sengled-light-bulb-is-simple-and-quick.-this-guide-helps-you-get-started-with-your-smart-bulb.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing a Sengled light bulb is simple and quick. This guide helps you get started with your smart bulb.

Follow these steps to install the bulb and prepare it for connection to Google Home.

### Turn Off The Power

Always turn off the power before installing the bulb. This keeps you safe from electric shocks.

### Remove The Old Bulb

Unscrew the old bulb from the socket carefully. Make sure the socket is clean and dry before installing the new bulb.

### Screw In The Sengled Bulb

Insert the Sengled bulb into the socket. Turn it clockwise until it is tight but do not over-tighten.

### Turn On The Power

Switch the power back on. The Sengled bulb will light up and be ready for setup.

### Reset The Bulb (if Needed)

If the bulb does not blink or connect, reset it by turning it off and on quickly three times.

* Turn off the bulb
* Turn on for one second
* Repeat three times
* The bulb will blink to show reset

## Linking Sengled App To Google Home

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=connecting-your-sengled-light-bulb-to-google-home-lets-you-control-lights-with-voice-commands.-you-need-to-link-the-sengled-app-to-google-home-first.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Connecting your Sengled light bulb to Google Home lets you control lights with voice commands. You need to link the Sengled app to Google Home first.

This guide explains how to link the Sengled app to Google Home easily and quickly.

### Open Google Home App

Start by opening the Google Home app on your smartphone. Make sure you are signed in with your Google account.

If you do not have the app, download it from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

### Access Home Control Settings

Inside the Google Home app, tap the plus (+) icon to add a device. Then select "Set up device" from the menu.

Choose "Works with Google" to link third-party smart devices like Sengled.

### Find Sengled Service

Search for "Sengled" in the list of available services. Tap on it to start linking.

The app will ask you to log in to your Sengled account to confirm the connection.

### Log In To Sengled Account

Enter your Sengled app username and password. This step connects your Sengled devices to Google Home.

Once logged in, give permission for Google Home to access your Sengled devices.

### Complete The Linking Process

After permissions are granted, Google Home will link to your Sengled app. Your lights will appear in the Google Home device list.

You can now use voice commands to control your Sengled light bulbs through Google Assistant.

## Controlling Bulbs With Voice

First, screw the Sengled bulb into a light socket. Turn on the light switch to power the bulb.

### Connect With Google Home

Open the Google Home app. Tap on the plus icon to add a device.

Say "Hey Google" followed by your command. You can turn the lights on or off with your voice.

* "Hey Google, set the bedroom light to 50%."

If the bulb does not respond, check your Wi-Fi connection. Make sure the Sengled app and Google Home app are up to date.

Restart your router and try the setup process again. Ensure your voice commands are clear and concise.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Connect Sengled Bulb To Google Home?

First, plug in your Sengled bulb and turn it on. Open the Google Home app, tap "Add," select "Set up device," then "Works with Google. " Search for Sengled, log in to your Sengled account, and authorize access. The bulb will appear in Google Home automatically.

### Can I Control Sengled Bulbs With Voice Commands?

Yes, once linked, you can control Sengled bulbs with Google Assistant voice commands. Say "Hey Google, turn on the light" or "Dim Sengled bulb to 50%. " Voice commands allow easy management of brightness, color, and power status hands-free.

### What If Google Home Doesn’t Find My Sengled Bulb?

Ensure your Sengled bulb is powered on and connected to Wi-Fi. Check the Google Home app is updated. Restart your router and the bulb. Try unlinking and relinking the Sengled account in Google Home to resolve connectivity issues.

### Is Sengled Compatible With All Google Home Devices?

Sengled bulbs work with all Google Home devices supporting smart home control. Compatibility includes Google Nest Mini, Nest Hub, and standard Google Home speakers. Ensure your Google Home device has internet access and the latest firmware for smooth integration.
