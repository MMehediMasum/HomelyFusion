## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Materials For Stair Runner Rugs?



The best materials include polyester, cotton, and low-pile wool. These offer durability, easy cleaning, and good grip. Polyester and cotton are washable and soft. Low-pile wool provides elegance and resilience for high-traffic staircases.



### How To Choose Non-slip Stair Treads For Safety?



Look for stair treads with TPR or rubber backing for anti-slip properties. Adhesive-backed treads prevent movement. Ensure the material is durable and suitable for wooden or carpeted stairs to enhance safety for kids, elders, and pets.



### Can Stair Runners Be Customized To Fit Different Stair Sizes?



Yes, many brands offer custom size stair runners. You can select width and length to match your staircase precisely. Custom runners ensure perfect coverage, style, and protection for stairs of any shape or size.



### Are Washable Stair Runners Suitable For High-traffic Areas?



Yes, washable stair runners like those from Well Woven and Pauwer are ideal for busy spaces. They maintain appearance, resist stains, and are easy to clean. This makes them perfect for hallways, kitchens, and staircases.



### What Styles Of Stair Runners Suit Modern And Vintage Interiors?



Modern homes benefit from geometric and tribal patterns like Well Woven’s Piano Beige or Fairmont Collection. Vintage styles favor floral and oriental designs such as the Asha Collection and KUTA’s floral runner. Both add aesthetic value and texture.



### How Long Do Durable Stair Runners Typically Last?



High-quality stair runners last 5 to 10 years with proper care. Durable materials like polyester and wool resist wear and stains. Regular cleaning and avoiding heavy moisture extend their lifespan and maintain safety on stairs.
