## Why Skip The Bed In A Bedroom Set

Bedroom sets often come with a bed included. But buying the set without the bed can be a smart choice. This lets you pick a bed that fits your style and needs better.

Skipping the bed in a set gives you more options. You can focus on matching other furniture pieces first. Then choose a bed that suits your comfort and space.

### Benefits Of Buying Bedroom Sets Without Beds

Buying bedroom furniture without the bed has many benefits. You get more freedom to personalize your room. It also helps you save money or spend it where it matters most.

* Choose a bed that fits your mattress size perfectly
* Pick a style or color different from the set
* Avoid paying for a bed you don’t like or need
* Mix and match furniture pieces for a unique look
* Upgrade to a better quality bed later on

### When To Consider Separate Bed Purchases

Buying a bed separately works well in certain situations. If you have a bed you want to keep, no need to buy another. Also, if you want a special type of bed, separate purchase helps.

* Your current bed is still in good shape
* You want a custom or specialty bed frame
* You need a bed size not included in the set
* You want to control the bed’s comfort level
* You want to buy the bed later after the furniture

## Material And Build Quality

### Popular Materials For Bedroom Furniture

Wood is a common material for bedroom furniture. It looks natural and feels warm. Solid wood is strong but can be heavy.

Engineered wood, like MDF or plywood, is lighter and cheaper. It can have a smooth finish and resist warping better than solid wood.

* Solid wood: durable and classic
* Engineered wood: affordable and stable
* Metal: modern and sturdy
* Plastic: lightweight and budget-friendly
* Upholstered: soft and stylish

### Assessing Durability And Maintenance

Durable furniture holds up against daily use and lasts many years. Look for strong joints and solid finishes. Check if the material can handle scratches and moisture.

Maintenance depends on the material. Wood needs polish or wax. Metal may need rust protection. Engineered wood often cleans with a damp cloth.

* Solid wood: requires regular polishing
* Engineered wood: easy to clean, avoid water
* Metal: check for rust, wipe dry
* Plastic: wipe with mild soap
* Upholstered: vacuum and spot clean
