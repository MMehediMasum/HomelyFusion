* Next to the closet or wardrobe
* Above the dresser or vanity

Science shows mirrors reflect light and images but do not affect energy or luck. They do not cause harm.

* Mirrors can make rooms look bigger and brighter.

## How To Correct Mirror Placement

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=mirrors-can-change-the-feel-of-a-bedroom.-placing-a-mirror-facing-the-door-is-often-seen-as-bad.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Mirrors can change the feel of a bedroom. Placing a mirror facing the door is often seen as bad. It may cause restless energy or disturb sleep.

Correct mirror placement helps keep a calm and balanced room. It also improves the flow of energy and comfort.

### Alternative Mirror Positions

Instead of facing the door, place mirrors on walls that do not directly face the entrance. This reduces negative energy reflection.

Mirrors can also be placed near windows to reflect natural light. This brightens the room and creates a peaceful feel.

* Place mirrors on side walls away from the door
* Avoid placing mirrors directly opposite the bed
* Use mirrors near windows to reflect daylight
* Keep mirror frames simple and clean

### Tips For Harmonizing Energy

Keep mirrors clean and free of cracks to maintain good energy. Dirty or broken mirrors can cause negative feelings.

Use curtains or frosted glass to soften mirror reflections. This helps balance energy without harsh reflections.

* Regularly clean mirrors to remove dust and smudges
* Cover mirrors at night if they face the bed
* Choose mirror shapes that feel calming, like ovals or circles
* Position mirrors so they reflect pleasant views or light

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is A Mirror Facing The Bedroom Door Considered Bad?

A mirror facing the bedroom door is often seen as bad in Feng Shui. It can reflect energy away, causing restlessness. However, personal preference and room layout matter most for comfort and style.

### How Does A Mirror Facing The Door Affect Sleep?

Mirrors facing the door can cause disturbed sleep by reflecting energy and light. This reflection may increase alertness, leading to restless nights. Positioning mirrors away from the door is often recommended for better sleep quality.

### Can I Fix Bad Energy From A Mirror Facing The Door?

Yes, you can fix it by using a curtain or screen to cover the mirror. Rearranging the mirror to avoid direct reflection of the door also helps. Adding plants nearby can balance the energy in the room.

### Does Mirror Placement Impact Bedroom Energy Flow?

Mirror placement significantly impacts energy flow in a bedroom. Properly placed mirrors enhance light and space, boosting positive energy. Incorrect placement can cause energy to scatter, leading to discomfort or unease.
