## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Sizes Come In Bedroom Picture Frame Sets?



Bedroom picture frame sets typically include 8x10, 5x7, and 4x6 inch sizes. These multi-size sets help create dynamic photo displays. They fit various photo dimensions, perfect for walls or tabletops, offering flexible decorating options.



### Are These Frames Suitable For Wall Or Tabletop Display?



Yes, most sets are designed for both wall mounting and tabletop display. They include hanging hardware and easel backs. This versatility lets you easily switch between display styles to suit your bedroom decor needs.



### What Materials Are Bedroom Picture Frames Made Of?



Frames are usually made from engineered wood combined with shatter-resistant plexiglass. This ensures durability while keeping the frames lightweight. The black finish adds a sleek, modern look fitting most bedroom styles.



### Do Picture Frame Sets Include Mats For Photos?



Some sets include mats that allow displaying 5x7 photos in 8x10 frames. Mats enhance photo presentation by adding a professional look. However, many frames can also display photos without mats for simplicity.



### How Many Frames Are Included In Typical Collage Sets?



Collage sets commonly include 9 to 15 frames, with varying sizes. This variety helps create personalized photo galleries on bedroom walls. Larger sets offer more creative layout options for family or travel photos.



### Can Bedroom Picture Frames Be Used As Gifts?



Yes, these frames make thoughtful gifts for family and friends. Their stylish design and practical use suit weddings, housewarmings, or birthdays. Many come in elegant finishes, ready for gifting without extra wrapping needed.
