Monica’s living room chair is a beautiful piece that adds comfort and style. Taking care of it helps keep it looking great for years.

Regular care protects the fabric and frame from damage. A little attention can go a long way in preserving your chair.

### Maintenance Tips

Clean your chair often to remove dust and dirt. Use a soft brush or vacuum with a fabric attachment.

Spot clean spills immediately with a damp cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the material.

* Vacuum weekly to keep dust away
* Use mild soap and water for stains
* Test cleaners on a small area first
* Keep the chair away from direct sunlight

### Extending Chair Lifespan

Rotate cushions regularly to prevent uneven wear. Fluff them to keep their shape and comfort.

Check the chair’s legs and frame for loose parts. Tighten screws or bolts to keep the chair sturdy and safe.

* Turn cushions weekly
* Fluff pillows daily
* Tighten screws every few months
* Avoid jumping or heavy pressure
