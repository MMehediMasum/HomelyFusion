## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Thermal Curtains For Sliding Doors?



Thermal curtains improve insulation by blocking heat loss and reducing energy bills. They also enhance privacy and block sunlight effectively, creating a comfortable indoor environment. These curtains help reduce noise and protect furniture from sun damage.



### How Do Blackout Curtains Improve Sliding Glass Door Privacy?



Blackout curtains block external light completely, preventing outsiders from seeing inside. They provide total privacy, especially at night or in high-traffic areas. Their thick fabric also helps reduce noise and enhances room darkness for better sleep or media viewing.



### Which Materials Are Best For Thermal Sliding Door Curtains?



Materials like polyester blends, linen, and microfiber are excellent for thermal curtains. These fabrics provide durability, effective insulation, and light blocking. Additionally, textured or layered materials, such as the ones used in NICETOWN or INOVADAY curtains, offer better heat retention and noise reduction.



### How Do I Choose The Right Size For Sliding Door Curtains?



Measure your sliding door’s width and height accurately. Choose curtains slightly wider than the door for full coverage and better insulation. For example, 100 inches wide and 84 inches long panels fit most standard sliding doors comfortably.



### Can Thermal Curtains Reduce Noise Through Sliding Glass Doors?



Yes, thermal curtains with thick, dense fabrics act as noise barriers. Products like Yakamok’s double-layer curtains significantly reduce outside noise. This creates a quieter, more peaceful indoor environment, ideal for bedrooms or living rooms near busy areas.



### Are Thermal Curtains Energy-efficient For All Seasons?



Thermal curtains help maintain indoor temperature year-round. They keep heat out in summer and trap warmth in winter. This reduces HVAC usage and lowers energy bills, making them an excellent investment for sliding glass doors in any climate.
