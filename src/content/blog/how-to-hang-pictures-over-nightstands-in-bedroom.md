**Are you struggling to find the perfect spot for your pictures over the nightstands in your bedroom? You’re not alone.**

Hanging artwork in this space can be tricky—too high or too low, and it throws off the whole look of your room. But don’t worry. With a few simple tips, you can create a balanced, stylish display that adds personality and warmth right where you need it most.

Keep reading, and you’ll learn exactly how to hang your pictures so they complement your bedroom perfectly.




## Choosing The Right Art

Hanging pictures over your bedroom nightstands adds charm and personality. Picking the right art makes the space feel balanced and cozy.

Consider size, color, and style to create a look that fits your bedroom well.

### Size And Scale Matters

Choose art that fits the size of your nightstand and wall space. Too big art can overwhelm the area. Too small art may get lost.

Keep the picture about two-thirds the width of the nightstand for a balanced look.

* Measure the wall space above the nightstand first
* Pick frames that are not wider than the nightstand
* Leave some wall space around the art for breathing room

### Color Coordination

Match the colors in your art to the bedroom’s color scheme. This keeps the space calm and inviting.

Use art colors that echo pillows, bedding, or wall paint for harmony.

* Pick one or two main colors from your room to use in the art
* Use neutral colors in art to balance bright bedroom colors
* Avoid too many bold colors that clash with the room

### Style And Theme Matching

Choose art that fits the bedroom’s style, like modern, rustic, or classic. This creates a smooth look.

Match the theme of your art to other bedroom decorations for a unified feel.

* For modern rooms, pick simple and abstract art
* Rustic rooms work well with nature or vintage pictures
* Classic rooms suit traditional paintings or framed photos

## Ideal Placement Techniques

Hanging pictures over nightstands adds style and personality to your bedroom. It is important to place them correctly for the best look.

This guide covers the ideal height, centering, and spacing to help you hang pictures with ease.

### Optimal Height For Hanging

Hang pictures so the center is at eye level when sitting on the bed. This height is usually about 57 to 60 inches from the floor.

Keep the bottom edge at least 6 to 12 inches above the nightstand surface. This prevents the artwork from feeling crowded.

### Centering Over The Nightstand

Align the center of the picture with the middle of the nightstand below. This creates balance and a neat appearance.

* Measure the width of the nightstand.
* Find the midpoint and mark it on the wall.
* Center your picture's midpoint on this mark.
* Use a level to ensure straight hanging.

### Spacing Between Multiple Pieces

If you hang several pictures, keep space between them to avoid a cluttered look. Proper spacing helps each piece stand out.

## Step-by-step Hanging Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=hanging-pictures-over-nightstands-can-enhance-your-bedroom’s-look.-it-adds-personality-and-style-to-the-space.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Hanging pictures over nightstands can enhance your bedroom’s look. It adds personality and style to the space.

Follow these simple steps to hang your pictures safely and evenly above your nightstand.

### Marking The Wall

Start by deciding the exact spot for your picture. Use a pencil to mark where the top center of the frame will be. Make sure the mark is at eye level when you stand next to the nightstand.

1. Measure the height of your picture frame.
2. Decide how high above the nightstand you want the picture.
3. Mark the wall at the center point for the top of the frame.
4. Use a level to ensure the mark is straight horizontally.

### Installing Hooks Or Nails

Choose hooks or nails that fit the weight of your picture. Use a hammer or drill to place them where you marked the wall. Make sure they are secure to hold the frame safely.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Picture Weight | Recommended Hardware |
| Under 5 lbs | Small nails or adhesive hooks |
| 5 to 15 lbs | Medium nails or wall hooks |
| Over 15 lbs | Heavy-duty hooks or anchors |

### Securing The Picture Frame

Hang your picture on the hooks or nails. Adjust the frame so it sits level and looks balanced. Use a level tool to check the alignment.

* Place the wire or hanging bracket on the hook.
* Step back to see if the picture is centered over the nightstand.
* Tilt the frame slightly to adjust the level if needed.
* Press the frame gently against the wall to secure it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How High Should Pictures Be Hung Above Nightstands?

Pictures should hang about 6 to 12 inches above nightstands. This height keeps artwork visible and balanced with the furniture. It also prevents crowding and maintains a clean bedroom aesthetic.

### What Size Picture Fits Best Over A Nightstand?

Choose pictures that are roughly two-thirds the width of the nightstand. Medium-sized artwork complements the space well without overwhelming the area. This proportion creates a harmonious and visually appealing setup.

### Should Pictures Be Centered With The Nightstand Or Bed?

Pictures over nightstands should align with the nightstand’s center. This alignment creates balance and a cohesive look. Centering with the bed is less ideal as it may disrupt symmetry.

### How To Secure Pictures Safely Over Nightstands?

Use appropriate wall anchors and hooks rated for your picture’s weight. Ensure secure placement to prevent falls near the bed. Safety is crucial to protect both the artwork and bedroom occupants.
