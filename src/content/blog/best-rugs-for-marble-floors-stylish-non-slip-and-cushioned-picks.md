## Frequently Asked Questions



### Which Rug Materials Work Best On Marble Floors?



Soft, non-abrasive materials like memory foam and padded PVC mats protect marble floors. They prevent scratches and provide comfort. Avoid rough or coarse fabrics to maintain marble's shine and durability.



### How Do Anti-fatigue Rugs Benefit Marble Floor Kitchens?



Anti-fatigue rugs reduce leg and back strain during long standing. They add cushioning and support on hard marble floors, enhancing comfort. These rugs also offer non-slip surfaces, increasing safety in kitchen spaces.



### Are Marble-patterned Rugs Waterproof And Easy To Clean?



Yes, many marble-patterned kitchen rugs are waterproof with PVC or foam backing. They resist stains and liquids, making cleaning simple. Most can be wiped or machine washed for convenience.



### Can Rugs Protect Marble Floors From Damage And Stains?



Rugs act as a protective barrier against spills, scratches, and heavy foot traffic. They help maintain marble’s polished look and extend its lifespan. Choose rugs with non-slip backing to avoid shifting.



### What Sizes Of Rugs Suit Marble Floor Kitchens Best?



Runners and medium-sized rugs (17"x55" or 17"x28") fit well near sinks or counters. Sets of two smaller rugs can cover larger areas. Size depends on kitchen layout and standing zones.



### How To Ensure Rugs Don’t Slip On Marble Floors?



Opt for rugs with built-in non-slip PVC backing or use non-slip pads underneath. This prevents sliding, reduces accidents, and protects both the rug and marble surface effectively.
