### How Do I Clean Tommy Bahama Throw Pillows?



Most Tommy Bahama pillows have zipper closures allowing easy removal of covers. The fabric is machine washable or spot cleanable. Always check care labels for specific instructions to maintain fabric quality and vibrant colors.

Yes, Tommy Bahama Euro shams, made of luxurious embroidered poly slub fabric, add elegance to bedding. Their large 26" x 26" size and tropical designs enhance bed or couch decor with a cozy, upscale touch.
