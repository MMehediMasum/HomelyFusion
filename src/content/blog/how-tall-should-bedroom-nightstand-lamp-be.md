**Choosing the right nightstand lamp can change how your bedroom feels and functions. But have you ever wondered how tall your lamp should be?**

Getting the height just right means better lighting for reading, a comfortable glow without glare, and a stylish look that fits your space perfectly. You’ll discover simple tips to find the perfect lamp height that suits your bed and nightstand.

Keep reading—you’re just moments away from creating a cozier, more inviting bedroom.



## Ideal Lamp Height

Choosing the right height for a bedroom nightstand lamp is important. It helps give the room a balanced look and makes the light easy to use.

The ideal lamp height usually depends on the height of your bed and how you use the lamp for reading or relaxing.

### Matching Lamp Height To Bed Size

The size of your bed affects the lamp height you should pick. A taller bed needs a taller lamp to keep the light at a good level.

For example, a queen or king-size bed often needs a lamp that is 26 to 30 inches tall. Smaller beds like twins or fulls work well with lamps around 24 inches tall.

* Queen/King bed: lamp height 26-30 inches
* Twin/Full bed: lamp height about 24 inches
* Adjust based on your nightstand height

### Considering Mattress And Pillow Height

The mattress and pillow height also change the right lamp height. A thick mattress or tall pillows raise your eye level.

The lamp’s light should be at or just above your eye level when you sit in bed. This helps avoid glare and gives good lighting for reading.

* If your mattress is thick, choose a taller lamp
* Taller pillows need lamps with higher light bulbs
* Test light height by sitting in your bed

## Measuring For The Perfect Height

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-height-for-your-bedroom-nightstand-lamp-helps-create-a-comfortable-space.-the-lamp-should-fit-well-with-your-bed-and-nightstand.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right height for your bedroom nightstand lamp helps create a comfortable space. The lamp should fit well with your bed and nightstand.

Measuring the nightstand and your sitting height helps find the perfect lamp height. This makes it easy to read or relax without strain.

### How To Measure Nightstand Height

Start by measuring the height of your nightstand from the floor to the top surface. Use a tape measure for accuracy.

Next, check the height of your mattress from the floor. This helps to compare the nightstand and bed heights.

* Measure from floor to top of nightstand
* Measure from floor to top of mattress
* Note both measurements in inches or centimeters

### Calculating Lamp Height From Measurement

The lamp height should be close to the height of your mattress. This keeps the light at eye level when you sit up in bed.

To find the right lamp height, add the nightstand height to the lamp base height. Then, add the lampshade height.

* Lamp height = nightstand height + lamp base + lampshade
* Ideal lamp height is usually 24 to 28 inches (60 to 70 cm)
* Adjust based on your bed and personal comfort

## Lamp Height For Different Bed Types

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-height-for-your-bedroom-nightstand-lamp-is-important.-the-lamp-should-fit-well-with-your-bed-type-for-comfort-and-style.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right height for your bedroom nightstand lamp is important. The lamp should fit well with your bed type for comfort and style.

Each bed type has a different ideal lamp height. This helps you read or relax without straining your eyes.

### Standard Beds

Standard beds usually have a mattress height of about 25 inches. Nightstand lamps should be taller than the mattress for easy reach.

The lamp height for standard beds should be between 24 and 28 inches. This height lets you switch the lamp on and off without standing up.

* Lamp base plus shade height: 24-28 inches
* Place lamp so light is near eye level when sitting up
* Use a lamp with adjustable brightness for reading

### Platform Beds

Platform beds sit lower than standard beds, often around 16 to 20 inches tall. Lamps should be shorter to match this height.

Choose lamps between 18 and 22 inches tall for platform beds. This keeps the light comfortable for reading or relaxing in bed.

* Lamp height: 18-22 inches
* Light should shine just above the mattress level
* Consider lamps with wider shades for better light spread

### Adjustable Beds

Adjustable beds change height and angle, so lamp height needs flexibility. A fixed lamp may be too high or low after adjustment.

Use lamps between 22 and 26 inches tall. Lamps with adjustable necks or swing arms work best for adjustable beds.

* Lamp height: 22-26 inches
* Choose lamps with adjustable positions
* Place lamps for easy access from different bed angles

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-height-for-your-bedroom-nightstand-lamp-is-important.-a-wrong-height-can-affect-your-comfort-and-the-room's-look.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right height for your bedroom nightstand lamp is important. A wrong height can affect your comfort and the room's look.

Below, we explain common mistakes people make when picking lamp height. Avoid these to get the perfect bedside lighting.

### Lamps That Are Too Tall Or Too Short

Lamps that are too tall can block your view or shine light in the wrong place. Too short lamps may not give enough light for reading or tasks.

* **Too tall:**Light may hit your eyes directly, causing glare.
* **Too short:**Light may not reach your reading materials well.
* **Right height:**The lamp’s shade should be at eye level when sitting in bed.

### Ignoring Bedside Activities

Not thinking about how you use your bedside lamp causes issues. Your lamp should fit your activities like reading or using a phone.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | **Recommended Lamp Height** |
| Reading | Lamp shade at eye level sitting up in bed |
| Using phone or tablet | Lamp low enough to reduce screen glare |
| Ambient light only | Lower lamp works well for soft light |




## Additional Tips For Choosing Lamps

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-lamp-for-your-bedroom-nightstand-is-important.-it-helps-create-the-perfect-lighting-and-adds-style-to-the-room.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right lamp for your bedroom nightstand is important. It helps create the perfect lighting and adds style to the room.

Consider more than just height when picking a lamp. Other features like shade size and bulb type affect your comfort and room look.

### Lamp Shade Size And Shape

The lamp shade size should match the lamp base and the nightstand size. A shade too big or too small can look unbalanced.

Choose a shape that fits your bedroom style. Round shades give a soft look. Square or rectangular shades offer a modern feel.

* Shade width should be about twice the lamp base width
* Height of shade usually equals the height of the lamp base
* Pick shades that direct light where you need it, like downward for reading

### Bulb Brightness And Color Temperature

Brightness controls how well you can see and the room’s mood. Choose bulbs that provide enough light without glare.

Color temperature affects how warm or cool the light feels. Warm light feels cozy. Cool light is brighter and clearer.

* Use bulbs with 450 to 800 lumens for bedside lamps
* 2700K to 3000K color temperature creates a warm, relaxing glow
* Higher color temperatures (4000K+) suit task lighting but feel less cozy
