## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Rug Is Best For A Dining Room?



Choose a rug that extends at least 24 inches beyond the table edges. This allows chairs to move without catching on the rug. For most dining rooms, an 8x10 rug fits well under standard tables.



### Are Washable Rugs Suitable For Dining Rooms?



Yes, washable rugs are ideal for dining rooms. They handle spills and stains easily. Frequent washing keeps the rug fresh and clean, maintaining a hygienic dining space.



### How To Choose A Non-slip Dining Room Rug?



Look for rugs with a non-slip backing or use a rug pad underneath. This prevents slipping and protects floors. Non-slip rugs enhance safety, especially in high-traffic dining areas.



### What Rug Materials Are Best For Dining Rooms?



Opt for low-pile, durable materials like faux wool or chenille. These resist stains and are easy to clean. Low-pile rugs also accommodate chair movement without damage.



### Can Vintage Print Rugs Work In Dining Rooms?



Yes, vintage print rugs add style and hide stains well. Their patterns mask dirt, making them practical and attractive for dining spaces. Choose colors that complement your decor.



### How To Maintain A Dining Room Rug's Appearance?



Vacuum regularly and clean spills promptly. Use gentle detergents for washable rugs. Avoid harsh chemicals to preserve colors and fibers. Proper care extends the rug's life and beauty.
