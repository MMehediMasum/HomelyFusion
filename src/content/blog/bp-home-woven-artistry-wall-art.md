## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is Boho Wall Art?



Boho wall art combines rustic and bohemian styles, featuring natural materials and earthy tones. It often includes elements like woven baskets, macrame, and abstract designs. This style adds warmth and texture to any space, creating a relaxed and inviting atmosphere.



It's perfect for living rooms, bedrooms, and creative spaces.



### How To Hang Woven Wall Baskets?



To hang woven wall baskets, use small nails or hooks. Arrange them in a visually pleasing pattern. Ensure each basket is secure and level. You can create a symmetrical or asymmetrical display, depending on your style preference. Always check the weight limit of your nails or hooks.



### Are Macrame Wall Hangings Durable?



Yes, macrame wall hangings are generally durable. They are crafted from strong, natural fibers like cotton or jute. These materials ensure longevity if properly cared for. Keep them away from direct sunlight and moisture. Regularly dust them to maintain their appearance and prevent wear.



### What Size Boho Wall Art Fits My Room?



The size of Boho wall art depends on your wall space. For large walls, opt for pieces over 30 inches. Smaller spaces may benefit from art pieces around 20 inches. Always leave some wall space around the art for balance.



Measure your wall to ensure the perfect fit.



### Can Boho Wall Decor Suit Modern Homes?



Yes, Boho wall decor complements modern homes beautifully. It adds warmth and texture to minimalist designs. The natural materials and earthy tones offer contrast to sleek, modern elements. This creates an eclectic and inviting atmosphere, blending contemporary and bohemian styles seamlessly.
