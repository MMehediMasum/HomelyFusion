## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In H And M Blankets?



H And M blankets use soft plush, faux fleece, and cashmere-like materials. These fabrics ensure warmth, comfort, and a cozy feel for daily use. They are perfect for sofa throws and home decoration due to their softness and durability.



### Are H And M Blankets Machine Washable?



Yes, most H And M blankets are machine washable. Always follow the care label instructions for best results. Washing in cold water on a gentle cycle helps maintain softness and prevent fabric damage over time.



### How Big Are H And M Sofa Throw Blankets?



H And M sofa throw blankets typically range from 50"x50" to 86"x63". Sizes vary to fit different sofas, beds, and living room needs. These dimensions offer ample coverage for warmth and decorative purposes.



### Can H And M Blankets Be Used For Outdoor Leisure?



H And M blankets are ideal for indoor use but can be used outdoors briefly. Their plush and soft fabric provides warmth during leisure activities like picnics or outdoor events. However, prolonged outdoor use may affect fabric quality.



### What Storage Options Come With H And M Blanket Sets?



Some H And M blanket sets include stackable storage bags with zippers and clear windows. These organizers help keep blankets, clothing, and bedding sets neat and protected. They are especially useful for seasonal storage and easy access.
