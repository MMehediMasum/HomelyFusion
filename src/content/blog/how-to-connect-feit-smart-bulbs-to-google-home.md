### Setting Up Feit Bulbs

First, install your Feit smart bulbs and make sure they are powered on. The bulbs should be in pairing mode before you start the app setup.

* Screw the bulb into a compatible light fixture.
* Turn on the light switch to power the bulb.
* Wait until the bulb blinks, showing it is ready to connect.
* If the bulb does not blink, reset it by turning it off and on three times quickly.

### Installing Google Home App

Download and install the Google Home app on your smartphone or tablet. This app will link your smart bulbs to your Google account.

## Connecting Feit Bulbs To Wi-fi

Connecting your Feit smart bulbs to Wi-Fi lets you control them with Google Home. This guide helps you set up the bulbs on your network.

Follow simple steps to choose the right network and complete the bulb setup. This ensures smooth operation with your Google Home system.

### Choosing The Right Network

Choose a Wi-Fi network that works well with your smart bulbs. Feit bulbs only support 2.4 GHz networks. Make sure your router’s 2.4 GHz band is active.

* Check your router settings to find the 2.4 GHz network name (SSID).
* Ensure the network has a strong signal near the bulb location.
* Avoid networks with heavy traffic or many connected devices.
* Use a network without hidden SSID for easier connection.
* Make sure the Wi-Fi password is ready to enter during setup.

### Completing Bulb Setup

Use the Feit Electric app to finish connecting your bulb to Wi-Fi. Follow the app instructions carefully to add the bulb to your network.

| Action | Description |

| Open Feit app | Download and open the Feit Electric app on your phone. |
| Add Device | Select “Add Device” and choose the correct bulb type. |
| Connect to Wi-Fi | Enter your 2.4 GHz network name and password. |
| Complete Setup | Wait for the bulb to confirm connection to Wi-Fi. |

Open the Google Home app on your phone. Tap on the "+" icon to add a new device or service.

Search for "Feit" in the list of available smart home services. Select the Feit skill to start linking.

* Open Google Home app
* Tap "+" to add
* Search and select "Feit"
* Begin linking process

### Authorizing Account Access

After selecting the Feit skill, you must log in to your Feit account. Use your username and password to authorize Google Home.

Once you allow access, Google Home will find your Feit smart bulbs. Now you can control your lights using Google Assistant commands.

* Enter Feit account details
* Allow Google Home to access devices
* Wait for Google Home to detect bulbs
* Use voice commands to control lights

## Troubleshooting Connection Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=connecting-feit-smart-bulbs-to-google-home-can-sometimes-be-tricky.-this-guide-helps-you-fix-common-connection-problems.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Connecting Feit smart bulbs to Google Home can sometimes be tricky. This guide helps you fix common connection problems.

Follow simple steps to solve issues and get your smart bulbs working with Google Home quickly.

### Common Problems

Many connection issues arise from Wi-Fi problems or app settings. Some bulbs may not respond or appear offline in Google Home.

* Bulb not showing in Google Home app
* Google Home cannot control the bulb
* Bulb disconnects frequently
* Slow response from the bulb

### Fixes And Tips

Try these fixes to solve connection problems with your Feit smart bulbs and Google Home.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Problem | Solution |
| Bulb not showing in Google Home | Check Wi-Fi network and ensure bulb is on same network as Google Home. |
| Google Home cannot control bulb | Re-link Feit app with Google Home account and check permissions. |
| Bulb disconnects frequently | Reset bulb and router, then reconnect both devices. |
| Slow response from bulb | Reduce Wi-Fi interference and move bulb closer to router. |

Resetting the bulb usually helps. Turn it off and on five times quickly to reset.

* Make sure your phone and Google Home use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
* Update your Google Home and Feit apps to the latest version.
* Restart your router before reconnecting devices.
* Check your Google Home language settings if voice commands fail.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Connect Feit Smart Bulbs To Google Home?

To connect Feit Smart Bulbs, open the Google Home app, tap “Add,” select “Set up device,” then “Works with Google. ” Search for Feit Electric, log into your Feit account, and link your bulbs to Google Home.

### Can I Control Feit Bulbs With Google Assistant Voice?

Yes, once linked, you can use Google Assistant to control Feit Smart Bulbs. Use commands like “Hey Google, turn on the living room light” for hands-free control.

### What To Do If Feit Bulbs Don’t Appear In Google Home?

Ensure your Feit bulbs and Google Home are on the same Wi-Fi. Restart both devices, check the Feit app, and try relinking the account in Google Home.

### Are Feit Smart Bulbs Compatible With Google Home?

Yes, Feit Smart Bulbs support Google Home integration. They work seamlessly for voice commands and remote control through the Google Home app.
