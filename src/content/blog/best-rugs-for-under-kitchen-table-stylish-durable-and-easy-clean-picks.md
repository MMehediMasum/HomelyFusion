## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Rug Works Best Under A Kitchen Table?



A rug between 5'x7' and 6'x9' fits most kitchen tables well. Ensure it extends 24 inches beyond the table edges. This size protects floors and accommodates chairs comfortably.



### Are Washable Rugs Suitable For Kitchen Dining Areas?



Yes, washable rugs like the Lahome Botanical Boho are ideal. They are easy to clean and maintain. Perfect for high-traffic kitchen dining spaces prone to spills.



### How Do Non-slip Rugs Improve Kitchen Safety?



Non-slip rugs prevent slips and falls by securing the rug firmly to the floor. Products like StepRite kitchen mats enhance safety in busy kitchen areas.



### Can Vinyl Floor Mats Protect Floors Under Kitchen Tables?



Absolutely, vinyl mats like FURKIT are waterproof and durable. They shield floors from spills, scratches, and stains, making them perfect for under kitchen tables.



### What Rug Materials Are Best For Kitchen Dining Rooms?



Cotton, low-pile synthetic fibers, and braided rugs offer durability and easy cleaning. These materials handle spills well and maintain comfort and style in dining areas.



### How To Choose A Cushioned Rug For Standing Kitchen Tasks?



Look for anti-fatigue mats like StepRite that offer cushioning and support. These mats reduce foot fatigue during long cooking or cleaning sessions.
