## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Sizes Do Living Room Picture Frame Sets Include?



Living room picture frames come in various sizes like 11x14, 8x10, 5x7, and 4x6 inches. Sets often include multiple sizes to suit different photo displays and wall arrangements, providing versatile options for gallery walls or tabletop displays.



### Are Picture Frames Suitable For Wall And Tabletop Display?



Yes, many picture frame sets are designed for both wall mounting and tabletop display. This versatility allows you to decorate your living room with framed photos on shelves, tables, or walls, enhancing your space's visual appeal.



### What Materials Are Used In Living Room Picture Frames?



Living room frames are commonly made from engineered wood and shatter-resistant plexiglass. These materials ensure durability, lightweight design, and clear photo visibility, ideal for safe, long-lasting display in family spaces.



### Can Picture Frames Include Mats For Photo Display?



Yes, some frames come with mats, allowing display of smaller photos like 5x7 inside an 8x10 frame. Mats add a professional look and help highlight your pictures with clean borders.



### How Many Frames Come In Collage Wall Frame Sets?



Collage wall frame sets usually range from 5 to 15 frames. Larger packs offer multiple sizes and finishes, perfect for creating personalized photo galleries in your living room.



### Are There Picture Frames With Unique Features Like Clocks?



Yes, some living room frames combine photos with functional elements like DIY wall clocks. These creative frames add modern decor and personal touches to your space.
