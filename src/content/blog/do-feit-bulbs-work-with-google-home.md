Open the Google Home app on your phone or tablet. This app lets you control all your smart devices, including Feit bulbs. Make sure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network you want to use for the bulbs.

## Common Problems And Solutions

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=feit-bulbs-are-popular-for-smart-home-lighting.-they-can-connect-to-google-home-for-easy-control.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Feit bulbs are popular for smart home lighting. They can connect to Google Home for easy control. Still, users may face some problems.

This guide covers common issues and simple fixes. You can get your bulbs working smoothly with Google Home.

### Connection Failures

Connection failures stop your Feit bulbs from linking to Google Home. This can happen due to Wi-Fi or app settings.

* Check if your phone and bulbs use the same Wi-Fi network.
* Restart your router and bulbs to reset connections.
* Update the Feit app and Google Home app to the latest versions.
* Make sure your Wi-Fi network is 2.4 GHz, as many bulbs do not support 5 GHz.
* Remove the bulb from Google Home and add it again.

### Response Delays

Sometimes, bulbs respond slowly or with a delay after voice commands. This can be frustrating but often has easy fixes.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Cause | Solution |
| Weak Wi-Fi signal | Move bulbs closer to router or use a Wi-Fi extender |
| Too many devices on network | Limit active devices or upgrade your internet plan |
| App or firmware outdated | Update apps and bulb firmware regularly |
| Google Home server issues | Check Google Home status or wait before retrying |

### Bulb Compatibility Issues

Not all Feit bulbs work perfectly with Google Home. Compatibility depends on the bulb model and the app used.

To avoid compatibility problems:

* Check if your bulb supports Google Assistant on the Feit website.
* Use the Feit Electric app to set up bulbs before linking to Google Home.
* Ensure your bulbs have the latest firmware updates installed.
* Look for bulbs labeled “Works with Google Assistant” on the package.
