### How Do I Clean Elite Velvet Corded-edge Pillow Covers?



Elite Velvet Corded-Edge Pillow Covers should be spot cleaned or hand washed gently. Avoid machine washing to maintain fabric texture and color vibrancy.



### Are Pillow Inserts Included With Decorative Pillow Covers?



Most Elite decorative pillow covers are sold without inserts. You need to purchase pillow inserts separately for a complete look.



### What Sizes Are Available For Elite Decorative Pillow Covers?



Elite pillows come in popular sizes like 18x18 inches and 20x20 inches. These sizes fit standard throw pillows for sofas and beds.



### Can These Pillows Enhance Farmhouse Or Modern Home Decor?

### Do Elite Pillow Covers Have Zippers Or Closures?



Many Elite pillow covers include hidden zippers for a clean look. This feature makes pillow insert removal and cleaning easier.
