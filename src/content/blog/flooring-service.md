## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Tools Are Essential For Laminate Flooring Installation?



Essential tools include tapping blocks, pull bars, spacers, contour gauges, and rubber mallets. These ensure precise fitting and damage-free installation. Kits like NAACOO and REXBETI provide these tools in one set for convenience and efficiency during the laminate flooring process.



### How Does A Contour Gauge Help In Flooring Projects?



A contour gauge duplicates complex shapes for accurate cuts around edges and corners. This tool speeds up fitting and reduces material waste. It’s especially useful for laminate and vinyl flooring, ensuring seamless installation around irregular surfaces.



### Why Use A Double-faced Mallet In Vinyl Flooring?



A double-faced mallet applies gentle, even force without damaging flooring materials. It helps lock planks tightly together. This tool is crucial for vinyl and laminate floors to avoid surface dents and ensure a smooth finish.



### What Is Included In A Professional Vinyl Flooring Installation Kit?



Professional kits typically include a tapping block with handle, pull bar, spacers, contour gauge, and a rubber mallet. These tools help achieve precise plank alignment, correct spacing, and smooth installation for vinyl and laminate floors.



### How Does A Floor Spacer Improve Flooring Installation?



Floor spacers maintain consistent expansion gaps between flooring and walls. This prevents buckling and warping due to temperature changes. Using spacers ensures long-lasting, stable flooring installations, especially for laminate and vinyl planks.
