### Are Floor And Decor Chandeliers Adjustable In Height?



Yes, many Floor And Decor chandeliers come with adjustable rods or chains. This allows customization of hanging height to fit various ceiling types and room sizes. Adjustable features improve lighting placement and aesthetic balance in dining rooms, kitchens, and other spaces.



### Which Rooms Suit Floor And Decor Farmhouse Chandeliers?



Farmhouse chandeliers from Floor And Decor suit dining rooms, kitchens, living rooms, foyers, and bedrooms. Their rustic and industrial designs complement farmhouse decor and modern interiors alike. These fixtures create a warm, inviting ambiance with a stylish, vintage touch.
