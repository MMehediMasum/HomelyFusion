## Determining The Ideal Hanging Height For Your Bedroom Mirror

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-height-to-hang-a-bedroom-mirror-is-important-for-both-style-and-function.-the-mirror-should-reflect-your-face-and-upper-body-clearly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right height to hang a bedroom mirror is important for both style and function. The mirror should reflect your face and upper body clearly.

Proper placement helps you check your look easily and makes the room feel balanced. Let’s explore how to find the best height.

### The General Rule Of Thumb: Eye Level Or Slightly Above

Hang the mirror so the center is at eye level or just above it. This lets you see your reflection without bending or stretching.

Most people find that placing the mirror’s center about 57 to 65 inches from the floor works well. This range fits average heights.

* Stand in front of the wall where the mirror will hang.
* Mark the eye level height, usually around 60 inches.
* Position the mirror so its middle aligns with that mark.

### Adjusting For Body Type And Personal Preference

Taller or shorter people should adjust the mirror height to fit their eye level. This keeps the mirror useful for daily use.

Some like the mirror higher for a design look. Others prefer it lower for full-body views. Choose what feels best for you.

* Taller people: raise the center height to 65 inches or more.
* Shorter people: lower the center height to about 57 inches.
* For full-body mirrors: hang the bottom edge about 6 to 12 inches off the floor.
* Test different heights by holding the mirror or using tape before hanging.

### Avoiding Distortions And Unflattering Angles

Hang the mirror at eye level to avoid strange reflections. If the mirror is too high or low, your image may look stretched or squished.

Check the angle by standing in front of the mirror. Adjust the height until you see your full face without tilting your head.

* Place the mirror so the center is about 57 to 60 inches from the floor
* Make sure the mirror reflects your face and upper body clearly
* Avoid placing mirrors where light causes glare or shadows

Position the mirror to reflect natural light and open spaces. This adds depth and makes your room feel larger and brighter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How High Should A Bedroom Mirror Be Hung?

A bedroom mirror should hang with its center at eye level, approximately 57 to 65 inches from the floor. This height suits most people and allows comfortable use while reflecting natural light and enhancing room space.

### What Height Is Best For A Full-length Bedroom Mirror?

Place a full-length mirror so its bottom edge is about 4 to 6 inches off the floor. This height shows your entire reflection without bending and fits well with bedroom furniture and decor.

### How To Hang A Mirror Above A Bedroom Dresser?

Hang the mirror so its bottom edge is 4 to 6 inches above the dresser top. This spacing creates balance, avoids glare, and provides a clear reflection for grooming and decorating.

### Can Mirror Height Affect Bedroom Lighting?

Yes, hanging a mirror at eye level helps reflect natural and artificial light better. Proper height enhances room brightness and creates a spacious, airy feel in your bedroom.
