## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In These Home Goods Bookends?



These bookends use wood, metal, bamboo, resin, cast iron, and concrete. Each material offers durability and aesthetic appeal for different home styles.



### How Do Non-skid Bases Improve Bookend Stability?



Non-skid bases prevent slipping on shelves. They ensure heavy books stay upright and protect surfaces from scratches, providing reliable support.



### Which Bookends Are Best For Heavy Books?



Cast iron, concrete, and sturdy metal bookends offer strong support. Their weight and design prevent tipping even with large, heavy books.



### Can Decorative Bookends Also Serve As Home Décor?



Yes, many bookends feature artistic designs like trees, dogs, and abstract shapes. They enhance shelf aesthetics while organizing books neatly.



### Are Bamboo Bookends Suitable For Office Use?



Bamboo bookends are lightweight yet strong, making them perfect for office desks. They add natural charm and hold books securely without slipping.



### How Do Metal Base Bookends Benefit Bookshelves?



Metal bases add extra weight and stability to wooden or resin bookends. This combination keeps books firmly in place and prevents falls.
