## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Best For Outdoor Dining Tables?



Acacia wood and aluminum are top choices for outdoor tables. Acacia offers durability and a natural look. Aluminum is lightweight, rust-resistant, and low-maintenance. Both materials withstand weather elements well, making them ideal for patios and gardens.



### How To Choose The Right Size Outdoor Dining Table?



Consider the number of guests and available space. For six people, a table around 55 to 60 inches wide works well. Larger tables, like 79 inches, fit eight or more. Measure your patio to ensure comfortable seating and movement.



### Are Outdoor Dining Tables Weather-resistant?



Many outdoor tables feature weather-resistant finishes like teak or powder-coated steel. These protect against rain, sun, and humidity. Tables with HDPE or tempered glass tops also resist damage. Proper maintenance extends their lifespan significantly.



### Do Outdoor Dining Tables Include Umbrella Holes?



Most outdoor dining tables, such as the SERWALL and Flash Furniture models, include umbrella holes. These holes accommodate patio umbrellas to provide shade. This feature is essential for comfort during sunny days and enhances outdoor dining experiences.



### Can Outdoor Dining Tables Support Heavy Weight?



Yes, tables like the Best Choice Products model support up to 350 pounds. Sturdy frames and strong legs ensure stability. Weight capacity varies, so check product specs to match your needs, especially for larger gatherings or heavy items.



### How To Maintain An Acacia Wood Outdoor Table?



Clean acacia wood tables regularly with mild soap and water. Avoid harsh chemicals to preserve the finish. Apply teak oil or wood sealant annually to protect from moisture and UV damage. Proper care maintains the wood’s natural beauty and durability.
