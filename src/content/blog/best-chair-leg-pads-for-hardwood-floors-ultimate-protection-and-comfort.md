### How Do Self-stick Felt Pads Protect Hardwood Floors?



Self-stick felt pads create a soft barrier between furniture and floor. They reduce friction and prevent scratches, dents, and marks. The adhesive ensures pads stay securely in place. Easy to apply and replace, they provide reliable protection for hardwood surfaces.

### Are Nail-on Felt Pads Better Than Self-stick Pads?



Nail-on felt pads offer stronger, long-lasting attachment for heavy furniture. They are ideal for wood furniture legs and hard floor surfaces. Self-stick pads are easier to apply but may wear faster. Choose nail-on pads for heavy use and self-stick for quick, temporary protection.



### Can Felt Pads Reduce Noise From Moving Furniture?



Yes, felt pads significantly reduce noise caused by furniture movement. Their soft texture cushions contact points and absorbs sound. Products like Super Sliders and SOFTTOUCH heavy-duty pads are great for noise reduction. They protect floors and create a quieter home environment.



### What Size Felt Pads Suit Different Furniture Legs?



Felt pads come in assorted sizes, from 1 inch to 1. 5 inches or more. Smaller pads fit chairs and stools, while larger pads suit tables and sofas. Measure your furniture legs to choose the right size. Assorted packs like Scotch Felt Pads offer multiple sizes for convenience.



### Are Silicone Floor Protectors With Felt Pads Effective?



Yes, silicone floor protectors with felt pads, like CBTONE caps, combine grip and cushioning. They fit snugly on chair legs and protect hardwood floors from scratches. Silicone adds slip resistance, while felt prevents marks. This combo offers excellent protection and durability.
