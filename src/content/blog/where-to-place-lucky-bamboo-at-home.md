## Care Tips For Lucky Bamboo

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=lucky-bamboo-is-a-popular-houseplant-that-brings-a-touch-of-green-indoors.-it-needs-simple-care-to-stay-healthy-and-grow-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Lucky bamboo is a popular houseplant that brings a touch of green indoors. It needs simple care to stay healthy and grow well.

Knowing the right watering and light needs helps your lucky bamboo thrive. Cleaning and maintenance keep it looking fresh and strong.

### Watering And Light Needs

Lucky bamboo grows best in clean water or moist soil. It does not need direct sunlight but prefers bright, indirect light.

* Change the water every two weeks to avoid bacteria build-up.
* Use filtered or distilled water if possible, to prevent leaf yellowing.
* Keep the roots covered with water if grown hydroponically.
* Avoid placing lucky bamboo in direct sun to prevent leaf burn.
* Place the plant near a window with filtered light for best growth.

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Regular cleaning helps your lucky bamboo stay healthy and attractive. Dust and dirt can block light and cause the plant to weaken.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cleaning Task | How Often | Method |
| Wipe leaves | Weekly | Use a soft, damp cloth |
| Trim yellow leaves | Monthly | Cut at the base with clean scissors |
| Clean container | Every two weeks | Rinse with mild soap and water |
| Check roots | Monthly | Remove dead roots and trim if crowded |
