## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Dining Table Fits 4 To 8 People Comfortably?



A 42 to 70. 8-inch table typically fits 4 to 8 people. Extendable tables offer flexibility for varying guest numbers. Choose oval or rectangular shapes for optimal space use. Consider room size before selecting the table.



### Are Extendable Dining Tables Space-saving?



Yes, extendable tables save space by adjusting length as needed. They fit small rooms when compact and expand for guests. This versatility suits apartments and homes with limited dining areas.



### Which Materials Are Best For Durable Dining Tables?



Wood and metal combinations offer durability and style. Solid wood tables provide sturdiness with a rustic or modern look. Metal frames increase stability and complement industrial designs. Choose finishes that resist scratches and stains.



### Can Dining Tables Include Storage Features?



Some dining tables have built-in shelves for extra storage. These are ideal for kitchens or dining rooms needing space for utensils or decor. Storage shelves enhance functionality without sacrificing style.



### How Easy Is It To Assemble These Dining Tables?



Most tables come with clear instructions and require basic tools. Assembly typically takes 30 minutes to an hour. Pre-drilled holes and labeled parts simplify the process for beginners.



### What Styles Are Popular For Modern Dining Room Tables?



Modern styles include minimalist, industrial, and farmhouse designs. Features like geometric legs, faux marble tops, and black finishes are trendy. These styles blend well with various home décors and add elegance.
