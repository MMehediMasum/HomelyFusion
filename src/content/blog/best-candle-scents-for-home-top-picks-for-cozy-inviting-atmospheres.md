## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Candle Scents For A Cozy Home?



Warm scents like spiced vanilla apple and cinnamon create a cozy atmosphere. They evoke comfort and relaxation, perfect for living spaces.



### Which Candle Scents Help With Stress Relief At Home?



Lavender, vanilla, and cashmere jasmine are great for stress relief. These scents promote calmness and tranquility in any room.



### How Long Do Large Jar Candles Typically Burn?



Large jar candles, like Yankee Candle’s 22 oz, burn up to 150 hours. They offer long-lasting fragrance for extended use.



### What Scents Are Popular In Men’s Aromatherapy Candles?



Men’s candles often feature leather, smoke, sandalwood, and mahogany teakwood. These scents offer a masculine, earthy aroma ideal for relaxation.



### Are Soy Candles Better For Home Fragrance?



Soy candles burn cleaner and last longer than traditional wax. They are eco-friendly and provide consistent, natural scents throughout the home.



### Can Scented Candles Eliminate Odors Effectively?



Yes, candles with vanilla, lavender, or amber scents neutralize odors. They refresh rooms while adding pleasant aromas to your home environment.
