### Linking Thermostat To Google Home App

To control your thermostat, first link it to the Google Home app. The app supports many popular thermostat brands.

* Open the Google Home app on your phone.
* Tap the "+" icon to add a new device.
* Select "Set up device" then choose "Works with Google."
* Search for your thermostat brand in the list.
* Sign in to your thermostat account to link it.
* Follow any on-screen instructions to complete setup.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=google-home-can-control-many-smart-thermostats.-sometimes,-it-does-not-work-as-expected.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Google Home can control many smart thermostats. Sometimes, it does not work as expected.

Knowing how to fix common problems helps you use your devices better.

### Connectivity Problems

Google Home needs a strong Wi-Fi signal to connect to your thermostat. Weak or lost signals cause issues.

Check if your Wi-Fi is working well and your thermostat is online.

* Restart your router and Google Home device.
* Make sure your thermostat is connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
* Move Google Home closer to the router if the signal is weak.
* Check for Wi-Fi outages in your area.
* Update your thermostat’s firmware and Google Home app.

### Voice Recognition Errors

Google Home may not understand your commands well. This can cause it to fail controlling the thermostat.

Clear speech and correct names help Google Home recognize your requests.

* Speak clearly and at a normal volume.
* Use the exact name of your thermostat as set in the app.
* Train Google Home to recognize your voice in the settings.
* Reduce background noise when giving commands.
* Check the microphone on Google Home is not blocked or muted.
