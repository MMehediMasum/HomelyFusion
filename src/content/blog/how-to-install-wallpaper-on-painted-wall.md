## Prepare The Painted Wall

Installing wallpaper on a painted wall needs careful preparation. This helps the wallpaper stick well and look smooth.

Cleaning and fixing the wall makes a big difference in the final result. Follow these steps before you start wallpapering.

### Clean The Surface

Remove dirt, dust, and grease from the wall. A clean surface helps the wallpaper glue stick better.

Use a mild detergent mixed with water and a sponge. Wipe the wall gently and let it dry fully.

### Repair Imperfections

Check the wall for holes, cracks, or bumps. These will show through the wallpaper if not fixed.

Fill holes and cracks with spackle or wall filler. Smooth the surface with a putty knife, then let it dry.

### Sand The Wall

Sanding the wall removes rough spots and creates a smooth surface. This helps the wallpaper stick evenly.

Use fine-grit sandpaper and sand gently. Wipe away dust with a clean cloth after sanding.

### Prime The Surface

Apply a wallpaper primer to the wall. This seals the paint and improves wallpaper adhesion.

Use a roller or brush to spread the primer evenly. Let it dry completely before hanging wallpaper.




## Choose The Right Wallpaper

Installing wallpaper on a painted wall starts with picking the right wallpaper. The right choice helps the wallpaper stick well and look great.

Consider the room, wall condition, and style before buying wallpaper. This makes installation easier and the result better.

### Select Suitable Material

Choose wallpaper material based on your wall and room type. Some wallpapers work better on painted walls.

Vinyl and non-woven wallpapers are good for painted surfaces. They stick well and are easy to clean.

* Vinyl wallpaper is water-resistant and durable.
* Non-woven wallpaper is breathable and easy to remove.
* Paper wallpaper may peel off painted walls easily.

### Measure Wall Dimensions

Measure your wall carefully before buying wallpaper. Accurate measurements help you buy the right amount.

Measure wall height and width in feet or meters. Write down the numbers to avoid mistakes.

* Measure from floor to ceiling for height.
* Measure from one edge to the other for width.
* Note any doors or windows to subtract area if needed.

### Calculate Wallpaper Quantity

Use your wall measurements to find out how much wallpaper you need. It helps avoid waste or shortage.

Check wallpaper roll size on the label. Calculate total wall area and divide by roll coverage.

| Item | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Wall Height | 8 feet |
| Wall Width | 12 feet |
| Wall Area (Height × Width) | 96 sq feet |
| Wallpaper Roll Coverage | 30 sq feet per roll |
| Number of Rolls Needed | 96 ÷ 30 = 3.2 (round to 4 rolls) |

## Cut And Prepare Wallpaper Strips

Preparing wallpaper strips carefully makes installation easier. Cut strips with attention to size and pattern. Proper preparation helps the wallpaper fit the wall perfectly.

Follow clear steps to measure, match patterns, and trim excess paper. This ensures a neat and professional look on your painted wall.

### Measure And Mark Strips

Measure the height of your wall from floor to ceiling. Add extra length for trimming at the top and bottom. Mark the length on the wallpaper before cutting.

1. Use a tape measure to get the wall height.
2. Add 4 to 6 inches for trimming allowance.
3. Mark the total length on the wallpaper with a pencil.
4. Cut along the mark using a sharp utility knife or scissors.

### Allow For Pattern Matching

Wallpaper with patterns needs extra care. Match the design between strips for a smooth look. Add extra length to the strips for pattern alignment.

### Trim Excess Paper

After hanging the strips, trim the extra wallpaper at the top and bottom. Use a straight edge and sharp knife for clean cuts. This prevents wrinkles and bubbles.

Tips for trimming excess paper:

* Trim when wallpaper is slightly wet for easier cutting.
* Use a metal ruler as a guide for straight cuts.
* Cut slowly to avoid tearing the wallpaper.

## Hang The Wallpaper

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=hanging-wallpaper-on-a-painted-wall-needs-care-and-attention.-follow-simple-steps-to-get-smooth,-neat-results.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Hanging wallpaper on a painted wall needs care and attention. Follow simple steps to get smooth, neat results.

Focus on placing the wallpaper correctly to avoid wrinkles and misalignment.

### Start From A Corner

Begin hanging wallpaper at a corner of the room. This spot helps keep the paper straight and aligned along the walls.

### Align Patterns Carefully

Matching wallpaper patterns is key for a seamless look. Take time to line up designs before sticking the paper.

1. Place the first strip on the wall, using a level to keep it straight.
2. Match the next strip’s pattern with the first strip carefully.
3. Repeat for each strip, checking pattern alignment as you go.

### Smooth Air Bubbles

Air bubbles create bumps and spoil the finish. Use a smoothing tool to press them out gently from the center toward the edges.

| Tool | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Wallpaper brush | Push out air bubbles smoothly |
| Squeegee | Flatten wrinkles without tearing |
| Soft cloth | Clean excess paste gently |

### Trim Edges Precisely

Trim wallpaper edges with a sharp blade. Clean cuts help the paper fit well around corners and fixtures.

* Use a metal ruler to guide cuts.
* Trim excess at the ceiling and baseboard.
* Cut around outlets and switches carefully.

## Finishing Touches

After you install wallpaper, finishing touches are important. They ensure the wallpaper looks neat and lasts longer.

Let's explore some simple steps to perfect your wallpaper installation on a painted wall.

### Clean Excess Paste

Check for excess paste on the wallpaper surface. Use a damp sponge to gently wipe it off. This prevents the paste from drying and causing stains.

Be careful not to rub too hard. Lightly dab the area to keep the wallpaper in place.

### Seal Seams If Needed

Look for any loose edges or seams. Apply a thin layer of wallpaper seam adhesive if needed. This keeps the seams from lifting over time.

Press gently on the seam with a clean cloth. Ensure the edges are secure and smooth.

### Allow Proper Drying Time

Once all finishing touches are done, let the wallpaper dry completely. This can take up to 48 hours.

Avoid touching or pressing the wallpaper during this time. This helps it adhere properly to the painted wall.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Prepare A Painted Wall For Wallpaper?

Clean the wall thoroughly to remove dust and grease. Repair any cracks or holes with spackle. Sand the surface lightly for better adhesion. Ensure the wall is dry before applying wallpaper paste or adhesive.

### Can I Install Wallpaper Directly On Glossy Paint?

Glossy paint needs to be dulled before wallpapering. Lightly sand the surface to remove shine. This helps wallpaper adhesive stick effectively. Without sanding, wallpaper may peel off over time.

### What Type Of Adhesive Is Best For Painted Walls?

Use a high-quality wallpaper paste designed for painted surfaces. Some wallpapers come pre-pasted and only need water activation. Always check manufacturer recommendations for the best adhesive match.

### How Long Should Wallpaper Adhesive Dry On Painted Walls?

Typically, wallpaper adhesive takes 15 to 30 minutes to dry. Drying time depends on humidity and room temperature. Avoid touching or repositioning wallpaper during this time for a smooth finish.
