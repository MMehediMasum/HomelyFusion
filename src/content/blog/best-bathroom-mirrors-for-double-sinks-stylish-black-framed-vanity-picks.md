## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Mirrors Work Best For Double Sinks?



Mirrors between 24x36 inches to 60x30 inches fit double sinks well. Choose wide mirrors or pairs for balanced coverage and style.



### Are Black Matte Frames Ideal For Bathroom Mirrors?



Yes, black matte frames add a modern farmhouse look and resist rust. They complement various decor styles, enhancing your bathroom’s elegance.



### Can These Mirrors Hang Horizontally And Vertically?



Most mirrors, like Fabuday and TRAHOME, offer flexible mounting options. You can hang them horizontally or vertically to fit your space.



### Do These Mirrors Use Tempered Glass For Safety?



Yes, all listed mirrors feature tempered, shatterproof glass. This ensures durability and safety in humid bathroom environments.



### Are Round Or Oval Mirrors Suitable For Double Sinks?



Round and oval mirrors, such as USHOWER and Wiserset, add a stylish, soft look. They work well when paired or spaced evenly above sinks.



### How Do These Mirrors Resist Rust In Bathrooms?



Mirrors with metal frames have anti-rust coatings or brushed finishes. This protects them from moisture damage and prolongs their lifespan.
