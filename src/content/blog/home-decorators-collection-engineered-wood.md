### What Is The Wear Layer Thickness On Premium Oak Flooring?



Premium oak engineered flooring typically has a 3mm to 4mm wear layer. Thicker wear layers allow for multiple refinishes. This extends the floor’s lifespan significantly. Choose based on expected foot traffic and durability needs. Always verify wear layer specs before purchasing.
