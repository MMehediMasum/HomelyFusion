## Ingredients For The Pie

This cranberry apple pie combines tart cranberries and sweet apples. The right spices and crust bring out the best flavors.

Below are the main ingredients you need to prepare this delicious Thanksgiving treat.

### Fresh Cranberries And Apples

Use fresh fruits for the best taste and texture. Choose firm apples and bright red cranberries.

* 2 cups fresh cranberries, washed and drained
* 3 cups apples, peeled and thinly sliced (Granny Smith or Honeycrisp work well)

### Spices And Sweeteners

Spices add warmth and depth. Sweeteners balance the tartness of cranberries and apples.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Ingredient | Amount |
| Granulated sugar | 3/4 cup |
| Brown sugar | 1/4 cup, packed |
| Ground cinnamon | 1 teaspoon |
| Ground nutmeg | 1/4 teaspoon |
| Ground cloves | 1/8 teaspoon |
| All-purpose flour | 2 tablespoons (to thicken filling) |

### Pie Crust Options

You can use a store-bought crust or make your own. Both work well for this pie.

Choose one of these crust options:

* One 9-inch pre-made pie crust (top and bottom)
* Homemade double crust made with flour, butter, salt, and cold water
* Gluten-free crust made from almond flour and coconut oil




## Preparing The Fruit Filling

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-the-fruit-filling-for-thanksgiving-cranberry-apple-pie-requires-care-and-attention.-the-mix-of-apples-and-cranberries-creates-a-tasty-balance-of-flavors.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making the fruit filling for Thanksgiving Cranberry Apple Pie requires care and attention. The mix of apples and cranberries creates a tasty balance of flavors. This guide helps you prepare these fruits the right way.

By selecting the best apples, cooking cranberries well, and balancing sweetness with tartness, your pie will taste fresh and delicious.

### Selecting And Prepping Apples

Choose apples that hold their shape when baked. Firm apples add texture and flavor. Wash, peel, and core them before slicing thinly for even cooking.

* Use tart apples like Granny Smith for a tangy flavor
* Combine with sweeter apples like Fuji for balance
* Slice apples evenly, about 1/4 inch thick
* Keep apple slices in lemon water to avoid browning

### Cooking Cranberries To Perfection

Cranberries need gentle cooking to soften without losing their bright color. Simmer them with a little water until they pop open and become tender.

| Cooking Tip | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Simmer over low heat | Prevents burning and keeps flavor |
| Add water gradually | Controls texture and softness |
| Cook 8–10 minutes | Ensures cranberries burst open |

### Balancing Sweetness And Tartness

The right mix of sugar and tart fruit makes the filling tasty. Too much sugar hides the cranberry flavor. Too little makes the pie too sour. Use sugar and a pinch of cinnamon for warmth.

**Try this simple sugar adjustment:**

1. Start with 3/4 cup sugar for 4 cups of fruit
2. Taste the mixture after cooking cranberries
3. Add more sugar if the filling feels too tart
4. Sprinkle cinnamon or nutmeg for extra flavor

## Making The Pie Crust

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-crust-is-the-base-of-your-thanksgiving-cranberry-apple-pie.-a-good-crust-holds-the-filling-well-and-tastes-delicious.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The crust is the base of your Thanksgiving Cranberry Apple Pie. A good crust holds the filling well and tastes delicious. You can make the crust from scratch or use store-bought options. This guide helps you choose and prepare the best crust.

Learn how to make a classic homemade crust, find quick store-bought alternatives, and get tips to make your crust flaky and tender.

### Classic Homemade Crust

A classic homemade pie crust uses simple ingredients and gives a rich, buttery flavor. It takes some time but makes the pie taste fresh and special.

* Use cold butter or shortening for the best texture.
* Mix flour, salt, and sugar before adding fat.
* Cut fat into small pieces and work quickly to keep it cold.
* Add cold water little by little to bring dough together.
* Chill the dough for at least 30 minutes before rolling.

### Quick Store-bought Alternatives

If you are short on time, store-bought crusts are a good choice. They come ready to use and save preparation time. Look for plain pie crust or refrigerated dough in the bakery section.

| Type | Where to Find | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refrigerated pie crust | Supermarket fridge | Easy rolling and shaping |
| Frozen pie crust | Freezer aisle | Long storage and quick thaw |
| Ready-made tart shells | Bakery section | Mini pies or tarts |

### Tips For A Flaky Crust

A flaky crust adds a nice texture to your pie. Follow these tips to get the perfect flake every time.

* Keep ingredients cold, especially butter and water.
* Do not overwork the dough to keep it tender.
* Roll dough evenly to avoid thick or thin spots.
* Use a light touch when folding the dough to create layers.
* Chill the dough before baking to reduce shrinkage.

## Assembling The Pie

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=assembling-your-thanksgiving-cranberry-apple-pie-is-a-key-step.-it-brings-together-the-flavors-and-textures-for-a-delicious-dessert.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Assembling your Thanksgiving cranberry apple pie is a key step. It brings together the flavors and textures for a delicious dessert.

Follow the steps carefully to layer the filling, add the top crust or lattice, and seal the pie properly.

### Layering The Filling

Start by spreading the apple slices evenly in the bottom crust. Then, sprinkle the cranberries over the apples for a balanced taste.

Add sugar and spices gently on top to enhance the flavors. Avoid packing the filling too tightly to allow juices to bubble.

### Adding The Top Crust Or Lattice

Place the top crust over the filling carefully if you want a full cover. Trim the edges to match the bottom crust.

For a lattice crust, cut strips of dough and weave them over the filling. This helps steam escape and looks beautiful.

* Use a sharp knife or pizza cutter for strips
* Weave strips over and under for a pattern
* Press edges gently to secure the lattice

### Sealing And Venting

Seal the edges of the top and bottom crust by pressing them together. Use your fingers or a fork to crimp the edges.

Cut small vents in the top crust or lattice to let steam escape during baking. This prevents the pie from getting soggy.

* Crimp edges for a tight seal
* Cut 3-4 small vents in the top crust
* Space vents evenly for good airflow

## Baking Tips And Timing

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=baking-a-thanksgiving-cranberry-apple-pie-needs-careful-timing.-the-right-baking-tips-help-get-a-perfect-pie-every-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Baking a Thanksgiving cranberry apple pie needs careful timing. The right baking tips help get a perfect pie every time.

Focus on oven settings, crust care, and checking if the pie is done. This guide will help you bake with confidence.

### Oven Temperature Settings

Set your oven to 375°F (190°C) for even baking. This temperature cooks the filling without burning the crust.

1. Preheat the oven fully before placing the pie inside.
2. Bake the pie on the middle rack for good heat circulation.
3. Lower the temperature to 350°F (175°C) after 20 minutes to prevent over-browning.
4. Use an oven thermometer to check the exact temperature.

### Preventing A Soggy Bottom

A soggy crust ruins the pie texture. Follow these tips to keep the crust crispy and firm.

* Brush the crust with egg wash before baking to seal it.
* Use a baking sheet under the pie to catch drips and allow air flow.
* Partially bake the bottom crust for 10 minutes before adding filling.
* Mix a tablespoon of flour or cornstarch into the filling to reduce excess juice.

### Checking For Doneness

Knowing when the pie is done ensures the filling is set and crust is golden.

| Test | What to Look For |
| --- | --- |
| Visual Check | Crust is golden brown and filling bubbles around edges. |
| Toothpick Test | Insert a toothpick in filling; it should come out mostly clean. |
| Cooling Time | Let pie cool for 2 hours to let filling thicken fully. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Ingredients Are Needed For Thanksgiving Cranberry Apple Pie?

You need fresh cranberries, apples, sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, pie crust, and lemon juice. These create a balanced, festive flavor perfect for Thanksgiving.

### How Do I Prevent A Soggy Pie Crust?

Blind bake the crust for 10-15 minutes before adding filling. This helps keep the crust crisp and flaky during baking.

### Can I Use Frozen Cranberries For This Pie?

Yes, frozen cranberries work well. Thaw and drain excess liquid to avoid a watery filling. This maintains pie texture and flavor.

### How Long Should I Bake Cranberry Apple Pie?

Bake the pie at 375°F (190°C) for 45-50 minutes. The crust should be golden and filling bubbly when done.
