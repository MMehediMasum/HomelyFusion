## Connecting Sengled Hub

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-your-sengled-light-bulb-with-google-home-begins-by-connecting-the-sengled-hub.-the-hub-acts-as-the-bridge-between-your-bulbs-and-google-home.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up your Sengled light bulb with Google Home begins by connecting the Sengled Hub. The hub acts as the bridge between your bulbs and Google Home.

This guide will help you complete the hub setup and link your bulbs to the hub for smooth control.

### Hub Setup Process

First, plug the Sengled Hub into a power outlet near your Wi-Fi router. This keeps the connection strong.

Next, connect the hub to your router using the included Ethernet cable. Wait until the hub’s LED light turns solid green.

* Plug the hub into power
* Connect Ethernet cable to router
* Wait for solid green LED light

### Linking Bulb To Hub

Turn on your Sengled light bulb in the room where you want to use it. The bulb will start searching for the hub.

Press the pairing button on the Sengled Hub. The bulb’s light will blink to show it is connecting.

* Switch on the Sengled bulb
* Press the hub’s pairing button
* Wait for bulb light to blink
* Bulb joins the hub network

## Integrating With Google Home

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-a-sengled-light-bulb-with-google-home-is-easy.-it-enhances-your-smart-home-experience-by-allowing-voice-control.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up a Sengled light bulb with Google Home is easy. It enhances your smart home experience by allowing voice control.

This guide will help you link your Sengled account and add bulbs to the Google Home app.

### Linking Sengled Account

First, download the Google Home app on your phone. Make sure you have a Google account set up.

Open the app and go to settings. Look for the option to add a new device.

* Tap "Set up device".
* Select "Works with Google".
* Search for "Sengled" in the list.
* Enter your Sengled account details.
* Allow the connection to complete.

### Adding Bulbs To Google Home App

Make sure your Sengled bulbs are installed and powered on. They should be connected to your Wi-Fi network.

In the Google Home app, go to the home screen. Tap the add button to start adding new devices.

* Select "Set up device".
* Choose "Set up new devices in your home".
* Follow the instructions to add your bulbs.
* Assign your bulbs to specific rooms.
* Test the setup by turning the lights on and off using voice commands.

## Troubleshooting Tips

Setting up Sengled light bulbs with Google Home can sometimes be tricky. If you're facing issues, these troubleshooting tips can help.

Follow these steps to resolve common problems and get your smart lighting working smoothly.

### Common Connection Issues

Connecting Sengled bulbs with Google Home might face some hurdles. Here’s what you can do to fix them.

* Ensure your Wi-Fi is working properly and is not too far from the bulb.
* Check if the bulb is in pairing mode. It should blink if it is.
* Restart your Google Home device and try connecting again.
* Update the Google Home app to the latest version.

### Resetting Sengled Bulbs

If your Sengled bulb is not responding, resetting it can help. Here is how you can reset your bulb.

1. Turn the bulb off and wait for a few seconds.
2. Turn the bulb on and off five times quickly.
3. When the bulb flashes, it is reset and ready to pair.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Connect Sengled Bulb To Google Home?

To connect, install the Sengled app and set up the bulb first. Then, open Google Home app, tap “+”, select “Set up device,” choose “Works with Google,” and link your Sengled account.

### Can I Control Sengled Bulbs With Google Assistant?

Yes, once linked, you can use Google Assistant voice commands. Say “Hey Google, turn on the living room light” to control your Sengled bulbs hands-free.

### What App Is Needed For Sengled And Google Home Setup?

You need both the Sengled Home app to configure bulbs and the Google Home app to link and control devices via Google Assistant.

### Why Won’t My Sengled Bulb Connect To Google Home?

Check if the Sengled bulb is powered on and connected to Wi-Fi. Ensure your Sengled account is linked properly in the Google Home app.
