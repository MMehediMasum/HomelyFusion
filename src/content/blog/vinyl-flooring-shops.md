### How Do Vinyl Floor Mats Protect My Floors?



Vinyl floor mats provide a waterproof barrier against spills, dirt, and scratches. They protect high-traffic areas like dining rooms and kitchens. Mats like FURKIT are sturdy, easy to clean, and prevent damage from furniture or pets. They extend the life of your existing flooring.



### Can Vinyl Flooring Withstand Moisture And Spills?
