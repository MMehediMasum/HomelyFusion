### Material And Build Quality

The material affects comfort and how long the recliner lasts. Good build quality means strong parts and neat finishing.

* Choose durable fabrics like leather or high-quality cloth.
* Check the frame material, such as hardwood or metal.
* Look for sturdy stitching and solid joints.
* Consider easy-to-clean surfaces for daily use.

### Gliding Mechanism

The gliding mechanism lets the chair move smoothly back and forth. A good system adds comfort and quiet operation.

* Look for smooth and silent gliding action.
* Check if the mechanism is easy to maintain.
* Test the recliner for stability during gliding.
* Metal parts usually last longer than plastic ones.

### Size And Dimensions

Compact recliners save space but still need to fit your room well. Measure the area before buying.

* Check the chair’s width, height, and depth.
* Consider extra space for reclining and gliding.
* Choose sizes that allow easy movement around the room.
* Look for slim designs with small footprints.

### Additional Functions

Extra features can improve comfort and usability. Think about what you need most in your recliner.

* Built-in footrests for added relaxation.
* Swivel options to turn the chair easily.
* Storage pockets for remote controls or books.
* Adjustable headrests or lumbar support.
