## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In Side Tables?



Side tables are crafted from various materials, including wood, metal, and engineered wood. Each material offers unique aesthetics and durability. Wood provides a classic look, while metal adds a modern touch. Engineered wood offers affordability and versatility. Choose based on your decor style and budget.



### How To Choose A Side Table For A Small Space?



Select a compact, multi-functional side table for small spaces. Look for designs with shelves or drawers for storage. Consider tables with a minimalist aesthetic to avoid clutter. Measure your space before purchasing to ensure a good fit. Opt for light colors to create an illusion of more space.



### Can Side Tables Be Used For Storage?



Yes, many side tables offer storage solutions. Some designs feature shelves or drawers for organizing items. These tables can hold books, remotes, or decorative pieces. Opt for a table with built-in storage to maximize functionality. It helps in keeping your living space tidy and organized.



### Are Hexagonal Side Tables Stable?



Hexagonal side tables are stable when designed with quality materials and construction. The unique shape provides a sturdy base. Ensure the table has well-constructed legs and a balanced design. Check product reviews for stability insights. A well-made hexagonal table can be both stylish and functional.



### How To Match Side Tables With Room Decor?



Select side tables that complement your room's existing style and color palette. Consider the material and finish for cohesion. Pair wooden tables with rustic or traditional decor. Use metal or glass tables for modern settings. Balance proportions by matching table height with surrounding furniture.
