**Your nightstand is more than just a place to set your alarm clock. It’s a small but powerful piece that can transform the look and feel of your entire bedroom.**

How you decorate your nightstands can say a lot about your style and personality. If you want to create a cozy, stylish, and functional space right next to your bed, this guide will show you exactly how to do it.

Keep reading to discover simple, practical tips that will make your nightstands stand out – and make your bedroom feel truly yours.




## Choosing The Right Nightstand

Nightstands add style and function to your bedroom. Picking the right one makes the room feel balanced.

Think about size, material, and storage before buying a nightstand. These choices affect how it looks and works.

### Size And Scale

Choose a nightstand that fits well next to your bed. It should not be too big or too small.

Make sure the height matches your mattress. This helps you reach items easily while lying down.

* Measure the space around your bed first
* Pick a nightstand about the same height as your mattress
* Leave room to move around comfortably

### Material And Finish

Nightstands come in many materials like wood, metal, or glass. The material sets the room’s mood.

Choose a finish that matches your bedroom style. Smooth finishes look modern, while rough wood feels cozy.

* Wood adds warmth and a classic look
* Metal gives a sleek and modern feel
* Glass looks light and elegant but needs careful cleaning

### Storage Needs

Think about what you want to keep in your nightstand. Some have drawers, shelves, or open spaces.

If you use many items at night, choose one with enough storage. A small lamp or books might need space.

* Drawers hide clutter and keep things organized
* Shelves show items you use often
* Open designs keep the space airy and light

## Maximizing Functionality

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=nightstands-hold-many-items-we-use-daily.-decorating-them-well-helps-keep-your-bedroom-tidy.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Nightstands hold many items we use daily. Decorating them well helps keep your bedroom tidy.

Focus on making your nightstand both useful and neat. This guide shares simple tips to do that.

### Organizing Small Items

Small items like keys, glasses, or jewelry can clutter your nightstand quickly. Use trays or small containers to keep them in one place.

Drawers are also great for storing small things out of sight. Label containers to find items fast.

* Use small boxes or trays for coins and watches
* Keep a dish or bowl for rings and earrings
* Store charging cables neatly in a drawer
* Label containers for easy access

### Cable Management

Cables from lamps, phones, and chargers can make your nightstand look messy. Use clips or holders to keep cables organized.

Wrap extra cable length to avoid tangles. Hide cables behind the nightstand to keep the surface clear.

* Attach cable clips to the side of the nightstand
* Use Velcro straps to bundle cables
* Place a power strip under or behind the nightstand
* Keep cables out of sight for a clean look

### Multi-use Nightstands

Choose nightstands that offer more than just storage space. Some have built-in shelves or charging stations.

Use the extra space to keep books, water bottles, or electronics. This saves room and makes your nightstand more useful.

* Pick nightstands with open shelves for easy access
* Look for models with USB ports or charging docks
* Use the top for a lamp and the bottom for storage
* Consider a nightstand with a fold-out desk surface

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are The Best Items To Put On Nightstands?

Choose functional and decorative items like lamps, books, clocks, and plants. Keep essentials handy and avoid clutter for a balanced look.

### How Can I Style Nightstands For Small Bedrooms?

Use compact, multi-functional decor like small lamps and trays. Opt for light colors and vertical storage to maximize space efficiently.

### What Lighting Works Best On Bedroom Nightstands?

Table lamps with warm, soft light create a cozy ambiance. Adjustable lamps offer practical reading light without overwhelming the space.

### How Do I Keep Nightstands Organized And Clutter-free?

Use trays, small baskets, or drawer organizers to store items. Regularly declutter and only keep essentials on the surface for a tidy look.
