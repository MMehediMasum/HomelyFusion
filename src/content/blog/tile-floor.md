## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Do Peel And Stick Tiles Work?



Peel and stick tiles have an adhesive backing. You simply peel off the protective layer and press the tile onto the floor. They are ideal for DIY projects, offering a quick and easy installation process. This makes them perfect for renters and homeowners alike.



### Are Peel And Stick Tiles Durable?



Yes, peel and stick tiles are durable. They are designed to withstand everyday wear and tear. While not as long-lasting as traditional tiles, they offer a good lifespan. Ensure they are installed on a clean, dry, and smooth surface for best results.



### Can I Use Peel And Stick Tiles In Bathrooms?



Yes, you can use peel and stick tiles in bathrooms. They are water-resistant and suitable for moist environments. However, ensure the surface is dry before installation. Avoid placing them in direct water contact areas like shower floors for optimal longevity.



### What Surfaces Are Suitable For Peel And Stick Tiles?



Peel and stick tiles work best on smooth, clean surfaces. Ideal surfaces include vinyl, linoleum, and smooth concrete. Avoid textured surfaces like rough concrete or uneven tiles. Proper surface preparation ensures better adhesion and longevity of the tiles.



### How Do I Maintain Peel And Stick Tiles?



Maintaining peel and stick tiles is simple. Regularly sweep or vacuum to remove dirt and debris. For deeper cleaning, use a damp mop with a mild detergent. Avoid using harsh chemicals or excessive water. Proper care enhances the tiles' durability and appearance.
