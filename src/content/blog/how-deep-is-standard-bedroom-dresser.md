## Common Dresser Depths

Bedroom dressers come in different sizes. Depth is a key dimension to consider for space and storage.

Knowing the common dresser depths helps you choose the right piece for your room.

### Standard Measurements

Most standard bedroom dressers have depths between 16 and 20 inches. This size fits most bedrooms comfortably.

The depth affects how much you can store inside. A deeper dresser holds more clothes and items.

* Shallow dressers: about 16 inches deep
* Standard dressers: around 18 inches deep
* Deep dressers: up to 20 inches deep

### Variations By Style

Dresser depth can change based on style and design. Some styles focus on space-saving, others on storage.

For example, modern dressers may be shallower. Vintage or traditional styles often have deeper drawers.

| Style | Typical Depth |
| --- | --- |
| Modern | 14 to 16 inches |
| Contemporary | 16 to 18 inches |
| Traditional | 18 to 20 inches |
| Rustic | 18 to 22 inches |

First, measure the length and height of the wall where you want the dresser. This shows how much space you have.

* Keep pathways clear for safety and comfort
