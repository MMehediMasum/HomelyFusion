## Choosing The Right Asian Side Table

Asian side tables add charm to your living room. They bring unique style and function.

Picking the right table makes your space look balanced and inviting.

### Materials And Finishes

Asian side tables come in many materials like wood, bamboo, and lacquer. Each material gives a different look.

Finishes can be natural, polished, or painted. They affect the table’s texture and shine.

* Wood: strong and classic, often carved
* Bamboo: lightweight and eco-friendly
* Lacquer: smooth and glossy surface
* Painted: adds color and pattern

### Size And Proportion

Choose a side table that fits your living room size. It should not block walking paths.

The table height should match your sofa or chair height. This makes it easy to use.

* Small rooms need compact tables
* Taller sofas need higher tables
* Leave space around the table for movement

### Styles And Designs

Asian side tables come in many styles from minimal to ornate. Choose a style that fits your room.

Look for designs with clean lines or detailed carvings. Colors and shapes also create different moods.

* Minimalist: simple shapes, few details
* Traditional: carved wood, rich patterns
* Modern: sleek, often mixed materials
* Rustic: natural wood with rough texture

## Maintenance And Care Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=asian-side-tables-add-charm-to-your-living-room.-taking-care-of-them-keeps-their-beauty-for-years.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Asian side tables add charm to your living room. Taking care of them keeps their beauty for years.

Simple maintenance steps help protect the table from damage and wear.

### Cleaning Methods

Clean your Asian side table regularly to keep it looking fresh. Use soft cloths to avoid scratches.

Avoid harsh chemicals. Mild soap and water work well for most surfaces.

* Dust the table with a dry microfiber cloth.
* Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap if needed.
* Dry the surface immediately to prevent water damage.
* Use wood cleaner for wooden tables only.

### Protecting Surfaces

Protect your table’s surface from heat, moisture, and scratches. Use coasters and mats under items.

Keep the table away from direct sunlight to prevent fading and warping.

* Place coasters under cups and glasses.
* Use placemats for hot dishes or pots.
* Avoid placing sharp objects directly on the surface.
* Move the table to shaded spots if near windows.

### Handling Wear And Tear

Minor scratches and dents may appear over time. Fix them quickly to keep the table neat.

Use gentle repair kits made for wood or lacquer surfaces to cover small damages.

* Lightly sand rough spots before applying repair kits.
* Use touch-up markers for small scratches.
* Apply a thin layer of furniture wax to restore shine.
* Do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive tools.
