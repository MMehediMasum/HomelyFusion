Hexagon peel-and-stick tiles are easy to install and waterproof. They offer a stylish geometric look and are perfect for DIY projects. These tiles are non-slip and ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and living rooms, providing both durability and aesthetic appeal without professional help.



### Are These Hexagon Tiles Suitable For Bathroom Floors?



Yes, many hexagon tiles are waterproof and non-slip, making them ideal for bathroom floors. Their durable vinyl or porcelain materials resist moisture and stains. This makes them safe and long-lasting for wet environments like showers and bathrooms.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain Hexagon Vinyl Floor Stickers?



Clean hexagon vinyl floor stickers with a damp mop or cloth and mild detergent. Avoid abrasive cleaners to prevent damage. Regular cleaning keeps the tiles looking fresh and extends their lifespan, especially in high-traffic areas.



### Can Hexagon Tiles Be Used As Kitchen Backsplash?



Absolutely! Hexagon peel-and-stick tiles are perfect for kitchen backsplashes. They resist heat, moisture, and stains, making them easy to clean. Their stylish design enhances kitchen decor while protecting walls from splashes and spills.



### What Sizes Are Available For Hexagon Floor Tiles?



Hexagon floor tiles come in various sizes, typically ranging from 1 inch to 12 inches. Common sizes include 1x1 inch mosaics and larger 9. 84x11. 81 inch planks. This variety allows customization for different spaces and design preferences.



### Are These Tiles Durable For High-traffic Areas?



Yes, many hexagon tiles, especially vinyl and porcelain types, are highly durable. They resist wear, scratches, and moisture, making them suitable for high-traffic spaces like living rooms, kitchens, and hallways. Proper installation enhances their lifespan.
