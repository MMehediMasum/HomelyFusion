## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Rug Is Ideal For A Bedroom?



The ideal rug size depends on your bedroom layout. Common sizes include 4x6, 5x7, and 8x10 feet. Choose a rug that fits comfortably under the bed, extending beyond the sides for a balanced look and added comfort.



### Are Machine Washable Rugs Good For Bedrooms?



Yes, machine washable rugs like OLANLY are perfect for bedrooms. They offer easy cleaning, maintain softness, and are ideal for kids’ rooms, nurseries, and dorms. This convenience helps keep your bedroom fresh and allergen-free.



### How To Choose A Non-slip Rug For Bedroom Safety?



Look for rugs with a non-slip backing or use a rug pad underneath. Brands like Ophanie and FinRèc offer non-slip features, preventing slips and falls. This is especially important in homes with kids or elderly residents.



### Which Rug Materials Are Best For Bedroom Comfort?



Soft materials like shaggy faux wool, plush polyester, and fluffy shag provide warmth and comfort. These materials, found in brands like Chicrug and Srugn, add a cozy feel to bedrooms while being durable and easy to maintain.



### Can Bedroom Rugs Improve Room Decor Aesthetics?



Absolutely. Rugs add texture, color, and style, enhancing overall bedroom decor. Options like Nakagishi’s boho and Deerhoo’s tie-dyed rugs create aesthetic appeal while complementing your existing furniture and color palette.



### How Do I Maintain And Clean Bedroom Rugs Effectively?



Regular vacuuming and spot cleaning are key. Machine washable rugs like OLANLY and RELEANY simplify maintenance. For non-washable rugs, use gentle carpet cleaners and avoid harsh chemicals to prolong rug life and appearance.
