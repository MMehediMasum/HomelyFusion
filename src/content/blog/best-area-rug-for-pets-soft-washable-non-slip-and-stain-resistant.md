## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Rug Pet-friendly And Washable?



Pet-friendly rugs resist stains, odors, and shedding. Washable rugs allow easy cleaning, maintaining hygiene and freshness. Materials like faux wool and low-pile fibers offer durability and comfort for pets, making them ideal for busy homes with animals.



### Which Rug Sizes Are Best For Homes With Pets?



Popular pet-friendly rug sizes include 4x6, 5x8, and 8x10 feet. Larger rugs cover more floor area, protecting surfaces from pet wear. Choose a size that fits your room and pet activity to balance comfort and maintenance.



### How Does Non-slip Backing Benefit Pet Rugs?



Non-slip backing prevents rugs from sliding on floors. This enhances safety for pets and owners by reducing falls. It also keeps the rug in place during play or running, preserving its condition and appearance longer.



### Are Low-pile Rugs Better For Pets?



Yes, low-pile rugs are easier to clean and trap less pet hair. They resist matting and wear from claws. Their thin fibers dry faster and minimize dirt buildup, making them ideal for active pets and busy households.



### Can Machine Washable Rugs Handle Pet Messes?



Machine washable rugs are designed for easy cleaning of pet stains and odors. They allow quick washing at home, saving time and cost. These rugs maintain softness and color after multiple washes, ensuring durability and hygiene.



### What Rug Materials Resist Pet Stains And Odors Best?



Materials like synthetic fibers, faux wool, and polypropylene resist stains and odors effectively. They are durable, easy to clean, and comfortable for pets. Avoid natural fibers that absorb moisture and odors, to keep your rug fresh longer.
