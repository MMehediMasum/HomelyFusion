## Tips For Lamp Care

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=lamps-are-a-key-part-of-home-decor.-they-add-light-and-style-to-any-room.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Lamps are a key part of home decor. They add light and style to any room. Proper care keeps them looking their best.

In this guide, learn how to clean, maintain, and safely use your lamps. Follow these tips to extend their life.

### Cleaning Techniques

Dust and dirt can dim lamp brightness. Cleaning helps maintain their shine. Use gentle methods to avoid damage.

* Unplug the lamp before cleaning
* Use a soft, dry cloth for dusting
* For glass parts, use a damp cloth
* Avoid water on electrical components

### Bulb Replacement

Replacing bulbs is simple but requires care. Use the correct bulb type for your lamp. This prevents overheating and ensures proper lighting.

1. Turn off and unplug the lamp
2. Let the old bulb cool before removal
3. Gently twist the bulb to remove it
4. Insert the new bulb and secure it

Safety is crucial when using lamps. Follow these steps to avoid accidents and ensure a safe environment at home.
