## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=reclining-sofas-add-comfort-to-your-living-room.-keeping-them-clean-and-working-well-helps-them-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Reclining sofas add comfort to your living room. Keeping them clean and working well helps them last longer.

Good maintenance stops damage and keeps your sofa looking nice. Here are some easy tips.

### Cleaning Upholstery

Dust and dirt can make upholstery look old. Clean your sofa often to keep it fresh.

Use a vacuum with a soft brush to remove dust. Wipe spills quickly with a damp cloth.

* Vacuum weekly to remove dust and crumbs
* Blot spills immediately to avoid stains
* Use mild soap and water for fabric cleaning
* Test cleaners on a small hidden spot first
* Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage fabric

### Lubricating Moving Parts

Reclining sofas have parts that move. These parts need oil or grease to work smoothly.

Apply lubricant to hinges and metal joints. This stops squeaks and keeps the sofa easy to use.

* Use a silicone spray or light machine oil
* Apply lubricant every few months
* Clean parts before applying oil
* Check for rust and remove it gently
* Do not over-lubricate to avoid mess

### Preventing Wear And Tear

Wear and tear reduce your sofa's lifespan. Protect cushions and fabric from damage.

Rotate cushions and avoid sharp objects on the sofa. Keep pets off to prevent scratches.

* Rotate cushions every month for even use
* Keep sharp items away from the sofa
* Avoid sitting on the sofa edges
* Use arm covers to protect high-use areas
* Train pets to stay off furniture

## Common Drawbacks

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=reclining-sofas-bring-comfort-but-also-some-challenges.-it-is-important-to-know-these-before-choosing-one-for-your-living-room.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Reclining sofas bring comfort but also some challenges. It is important to know these before choosing one for your living room.

This section explains common drawbacks related to space, weight, and cost.

### Space Requirements

Reclining sofas need more room than regular sofas. They require extra space to fully recline. This can make small living rooms feel crowded.

* Allow at least 18 to 24 inches behind the sofa for reclining.
* Check room layout carefully before buying.
* Consider how the sofa will affect walking paths.

### Weight And Mobility

Reclining sofas are usually heavier than standard ones. The metal reclining parts add to the weight. Moving them can be hard and needs two or more people.

Because of the weight, rearranging your living room can be difficult. This can limit your options for changing furniture layout.
