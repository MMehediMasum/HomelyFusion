## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Ideal Brightness Level For Sad Lamps?



SAD lamps typically offer 10,000 lux brightness, which effectively simulates natural sunlight. This level helps improve mood and regulate sleep cycles safely.



### How Long Should I Use A Light Therapy Lamp Daily?



Most experts recommend 20-30 minutes daily sessions in the morning. This duration maximizes benefits like mood boost and sleep improvement without side effects.



### Are Uv-free Lamps Safer For Light Therapy?



Yes, UV-free lamps protect your skin and eyes while delivering effective bright light. All featured lamps use safe LED or fluorescent bulbs without UV emission.



### Can Adjustable Brightness And Color Temperature Improve Therapy?



Adjustable settings allow customization to your comfort and needs. Changing brightness and color helps mimic natural light variations for better mood and focus.



### Is Portability Important For A Sad Therapy Lamp?



Portable lamps let you maintain consistent therapy sessions anywhere. Lightweight designs with memory functions enhance convenience for home or office use.



### Do Timers And Memory Functions Enhance Light Therapy Use?



Timers ensure correct session length, preventing overexposure. Memory functions save your preferred settings, making daily therapy easier and more efficient.
