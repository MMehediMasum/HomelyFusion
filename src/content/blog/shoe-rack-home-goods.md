## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Materials For Shoe Racks?



Shoe racks come in metal, wood, and non-woven fabric. Metal offers durability, wood adds style, and fabric is lightweight. Choose based on your space and style preferences. Kitsure and SONGMICS offer metal and fabric options for versatile use.



### How Many Pairs Can A 10-tier Shoe Rack Hold?



A 10-tier shoe rack can typically hold 27 to 50 pairs. Capacity varies by brand and shelf size. For example, VTRIN holds up to 50 pairs, while Kitsure's 10-tier models hold around 27-30 pairs. Check dimensions before buying.



### Are Stackable Shoe Racks Space-saving?



Yes, stackable shoe racks maximize vertical space and reduce clutter. They are ideal for small spaces like dorms and entryways. Brands like Kitsure and SONGMICS offer stackable designs that fit tight areas efficiently.



### Can Shoe Racks Store Boots And Sneakers?
