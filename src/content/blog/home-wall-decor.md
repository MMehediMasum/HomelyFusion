### How To Hang Artificial Wall Plants Securely?



To hang artificial wall plants securely, use heavy-duty double-sided tape. Ensure the wall surface is clean and dry. Apply the tape evenly across the back of the plant holder. Press firmly against the wall for a strong hold. This method works well for lightweight decor.



### What Are The Benefits Of Using Nano Adhesive Tape?



Nano adhesive tape offers a strong, reusable, and residue-free solution. It is ideal for mounting lightweight wall decor. The tape is transparent and works on various surfaces. It's perfect for renters who want to avoid wall damage. Always check the weight limit before use.
