## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Homegoods Shower Curtains Made From?



Homegoods shower curtains use polyester, PVC, and fabric blends. These materials offer waterproof, mildew-resistant, and durable qualities for bathroom use. Polyester curtains are often machine washable and quick-drying, while PVC options provide transparent, waterproof protection with easy cleaning.



### Are Homegoods Shower Curtains Machine Washable?



Many Homegoods shower curtains, especially polyester ones, are machine washable. This feature helps maintain cleanliness and hygiene. PVC curtains usually require wiping down instead. Always check the care label for specific washing instructions to ensure durability and performance.



### Do Homegoods Shower Curtains Include Hooks Or Rings?



Most Homegoods shower curtains come with hooks or rings included. For example, curtains often feature 10 to 14 hooks, making installation easy. Some sets include grommet curtains with rings for smooth sliding on shower rods. Always verify product details before purchase.



### Are Homegoods Shower Curtains Waterproof And Mildew Resistant?



Yes, many Homegoods shower curtains are waterproof and mildew resistant. Polyester and PVC materials prevent water absorption and inhibit mold growth. These features keep bathrooms hygienic and curtain fabrics lasting longer, especially in humid environments.



### What Sizes Do Homegoods Shower Curtains Come In?



Homegoods shower curtains come in various sizes like 72x80 inches, 72x96 inches, and 6 feet wide options. This variety fits standard shower and bathtub setups. Always measure your shower space before buying to ensure a perfect fit.



### How Do Homegoods Curtains Prevent Plastic Smell?



Homegoods use PVC-free or high-quality fabrics to minimize plastic odors. Fabric curtains like polyester are naturally odorless. Some products are specifically designed to avoid the typical plastic smell often found in cheaper PVC curtains.
