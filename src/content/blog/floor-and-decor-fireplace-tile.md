## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Types Of Peel And Stick Tiles Suit Fireplace Surrounds?



Peel and stick tiles like slate, faux stone, and marble PVC are ideal for fireplaces. They resist heat and are easy to install without grout or mortar.



### How Do I Install Auliget Peel And Stick Fireplace Tiles?



Clean the surface thoroughly before applying. Peel off the backing and firmly press tiles onto the fireplace wall. Ensure tiles align for a seamless look.



### Are Peel And Stick Tiles Heat Resistant For Fireplaces?



Yes, most PVC-based peel and stick tiles like slate and faux stone handle moderate fireplace heat. Avoid direct flame contact for safety.



### Can I Use Peel And Stick Tiles On Bathroom And Kitchen Fireplaces?



Absolutely. These tiles are moisture-resistant and ideal for humid areas like bathrooms, kitchens, and fireplace surrounds, providing both style and durability.



### What Maintenance Do Fireplace Peel And Stick Tiles Require?



Simply wipe tiles with a damp cloth to remove dust and soot. Avoid abrasive cleaners to keep the surface intact and looking new.



### Is Rutland Fireplace Mortar Necessary With Peel And Stick Tiles?



No, peel and stick tiles don't require mortar. Rutland Mortar is used for traditional stone or brick fireplaces, not self-adhesive tiles.
