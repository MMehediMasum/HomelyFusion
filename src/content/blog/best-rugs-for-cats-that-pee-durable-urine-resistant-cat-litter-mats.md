## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Rug Good For Cats That Pee?



A good rug for cats that pee is urine-resistant, easy to clean, and durable. It traps litter and prevents odors. Materials like PVC or silicone are ideal. Non-slip backing keeps it in place, protecting floors from damage and keeping your home fresh.



### How Does A Cat Litter Mat Control Litter Scatter?



Cat litter mats have grooved or honeycomb designs to trap scattered litter. They catch litter from your cat’s paws as they exit the box. This prevents messes on floors and carpets. Easy-to-clean surfaces make maintenance simple and keep your home tidy.



### Are Urine-proof Mats Safe For Cats?



Yes, urine-proof mats are made from non-toxic, phthalate-free materials. They are gentle on cats’ paws and designed for daily use. These mats prevent urine from soaking into floors or carpets, reducing odor and damage while ensuring your cat’s comfort and safety.



### How Does A Blacklight Help Detect Cat Urine Stains?



A blacklight reveals invisible cat urine stains by making them fluoresce. This helps locate hidden spots on rugs, carpets, and floors. Using it ensures thorough cleaning, preventing odor and repeat accidents. It’s an essential tool for maintaining a clean, odor-free home.



### Can Natural Cat Deterrent Sprays Protect Rugs From Peeing?



Yes, natural cat deterrent sprays use safe ingredients to discourage cats from peeing on rugs. They emit scents cats dislike, helping train them to avoid certain areas. These sprays are non-toxic, effective indoors and outdoors, and protect your furniture and carpets from damage.
