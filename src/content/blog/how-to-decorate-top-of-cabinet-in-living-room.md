**Have you ever looked at the top of your living room cabinets and felt like something was missing? That empty space can be a hidden gem for adding style and personality to your home.**

Decorating the top of your cabinets isn’t just about filling space—it’s about creating a stunning focal point that catches the eye and makes your living room feel complete. You’ll discover simple, creative ideas to transform that overlooked spot into a beautiful display.

Ready to turn your cabinets into a showstopper? Keep reading, because your living room’s best-kept secret is about to be revealed.




## Choosing The Right Decor

Decorating the top of your living room cabinet adds style and personality. Picking the right decor helps balance your space and highlights your taste.

Think about size, color, and style before placing items. This makes your cabinet look neat and attractive.

### Consider Cabinet Height And Depth

Check your cabinet’s height and depth before choosing decor. Tall cabinets can hold larger pieces, while shallow ones need smaller items.

Leave some empty space to avoid crowding. Use items that fit well without hanging over the edges.

* For tall cabinets, use vases or tall plants
* Shallow cabinets work well with flat decor like trays
* Keep items proportional to cabinet size

### Select A Color Scheme

Choose colors that match or complement your room. A good color scheme ties the decor and furniture together.

Use a mix of light and dark shades for balance. Neutral colors work well with bright accents.

* Match decor colors with cushions or rugs
* Use metallics for a modern look
* Soft pastels create a calm feel

### Match Your Living Room Style

Think about your living room’s style when picking decor. Modern rooms suit clean lines and simple shapes.

Traditional rooms look nice with classic items and warm tones. Mixing styles can work if done carefully.

* Use rustic items for farmhouse style
* Choose sleek decor for minimalist rooms
* Add vintage pieces to eclectic spaces

## Using Greenery And Plants

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adding-greenery-to-the-top-of-cabinets-in-your-living-room-brings-life-to-the-space.-plants-add-color,-texture,-and-a-natural-feel-to-your-decor.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adding greenery to the top of cabinets in your living room brings life to the space. Plants add color, texture, and a natural feel to your decor.

Choosing the right plants and arranging them well can make this area look stylish and fresh without much effort.

### Low-maintenance Plants

Pick plants that need little care to keep your cabinet space neat and green. These plants survive with minimal water and light.

* Snake Plant: Survives low light and infrequent watering.
* ZZ Plant: Tolerates dry air and low light well.
* Spider Plant: Easy to grow and cleans air.
* Pothos: Grows fast and needs little care.
* Succulents: Require very little water and bright light.

### Artificial Vs. Real Plants

Both real and artificial plants have benefits for decorating the top of cabinets. Choose what fits your lifestyle and space best.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type | Pros | Cons |
| Real Plants | Improve air quality, natural look | Need watering and sunlight |
| Artificial Plants | No care needed, lasts long | May look less natural |

### Plant Arrangement Tips

Arrange plants to add interest and balance to the cabinet top. Use different heights and shapes to create a nice display.

1. Start with taller plants in the back or center.
2. Place medium-sized plants around taller ones.
3. Add small plants or trailing vines at the edges.
4. Use pots in matching or complementary colors.
5. Leave some space to avoid a crowded look.

## Incorporating Lighting Elements

Adding lights on top of your living room cabinets can brighten the space. It also adds style and warmth to the room.

Choose the right lighting type to match your room’s mood and decor. Soft lights create a cozy feel, while bright lights highlight items.

### String Lights And Fairy Lights

String lights and fairy lights are easy to place on cabinets. They add a soft glow and a magical touch to the living room.

* Drape string lights along the cabinet edge for a subtle shine.
* Use fairy lights inside glass containers for a delicate display.
* Choose warm white or colored lights to match your theme.

### Led Strip Lighting

LED strip lights fit neatly under cabinet tops. They provide even lighting and can change colors with a remote.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Feature | Benefit |
| Flexible strips | Easy to install on curved or straight surfaces |
| Remote control | Adjust color and brightness without moving |
| Energy efficient | Low power use saves electricity |
| Long lifespan | Lasts for many years without replacement |

### Accent Lamps And Lanterns

Accent lamps and lanterns add style and direct light. They come in many shapes and sizes to fit your cabinet space.

* Place small lamps with soft bulbs for a warm glow.

## Seasonal And Themed Decor

## Keeping It Clutter-free

Decorating the top of your cabinet can enhance your living room. Keep it clutter-free to maintain a clean look.

A simple arrangement can make the space look organized. Here are some tips to achieve this.

### Limit The Number Of Items

Too many items make the space look messy. Choose a few key pieces to display.

1. Select three to five items to display.
2. Use items of different heights for variety.
3. Include a mix of textures, like wood and ceramic.

### Balance And Symmetry

Arranging items in a balanced way looks pleasing. Symmetry helps the eye to focus.

Try arranging items in pairs or groups of three for harmony.

### Regular Maintenance Tips

Regular cleaning keeps the area looking fresh. Dust can accumulate quickly on displayed items.

* Dust the items weekly to keep them clean.
* Rearrange items every few months for a fresh look.
* Check for any damage and repair as needed.

### What Are The Best Items To Decorate Top Of Cabinets?

Update seasonally or when your room’s style changes. Regular updates keep the space fresh and reflect your current taste.
