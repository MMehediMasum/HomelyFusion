## Potential Drawbacks

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=frameless-bathroom-mirrors-have-a-modern-look-many-like.-they-fit-well-in-many-bathroom-styles.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Frameless bathroom mirrors have a modern look many like. They fit well in many bathroom styles.

Still, they come with some problems you should know. These can affect your choice.

### Installation Challenges

Installing frameless mirrors is not always easy. They need strong support to stay safe on the wall.

Special tools and skills may be required. Mistakes can cause the mirror to fall or break.

* Need precise measurements for mounting
* Require strong backing to hold weight
* Not easy for beginners to install

### Durability Concerns

Frameless mirrors lack the frame that helps protect edges. This can make them fragile.

Edges can chip or crack if hit. This may shorten the mirror’s life.

* Edges are exposed and vulnerable
* More prone to damage from bumps
* May need careful handling and cleaning

### Cost Factors

Frameless mirrors can be more expensive than framed ones. The glass often needs special cutting.

Installation costs may also rise because of the skill needed. This can increase your total budget.

* Higher price for custom glass cutting
* Skilled labor may raise installation fees
* Replacement can be costly if damaged
