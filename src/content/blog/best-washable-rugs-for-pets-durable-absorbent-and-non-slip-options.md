## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Rug Washable And Pet-friendly?



Washable pet rugs are made from durable, machine-washable materials. They resist stains, odors, and moisture, making cleanup easy. Non-slip backings prevent sliding, ensuring safety for pets. These rugs also absorb spills and accidents, helping maintain a clean home environment.



### How Do Washable Pee Pads Help With Puppy Training?



Washable pee pads absorb urine quickly and prevent leaks. They are reusable, eco-friendly, and cost-effective compared to disposable pads. Non-slip designs keep pads in place, helping puppies learn designated bathroom spots. Their durability supports repeated washes without losing absorbency or shape.



### Are Extra-large Washable Mats Better For Large Dogs?



Yes, extra-large washable mats provide ample space for bigger dogs. They cover more floor area, preventing mess spread. These mats offer comfort and protection during play, rest, or training. Large size ensures better absorption and durability for active pets.



### How Do Non-slip Washable Mats Improve Pet Safety?



Non-slip washable mats prevent rugs from sliding on smooth floors. This reduces the risk of slips and falls for pets and owners. They provide stable footing, especially for older or disabled dogs. The added safety encourages confident movement around the home.



### Can Washable Dog Mats Handle Incontinence And Housebreaking?



Washable dog mats are designed to absorb urine and prevent leaks. They help manage incontinence and support housebreaking efforts. Their waterproof layers protect floors from damage. Easy cleaning and reusability make them ideal for long-term use with pets needing extra care.



### How Often Should I Wash Washable Pet Rugs?



Wash washable pet rugs weekly or after heavy use. Frequent washing prevents odor buildup and bacteria growth. Always follow manufacturer care instructions for best results. Regular cleaning keeps rugs fresh, hygienic, and extends their lifespan.
