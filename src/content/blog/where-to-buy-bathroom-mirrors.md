## Tips For Choosing The Right Mirror

Bathroom mirrors come in many shapes and sizes. Picking the right one is important for your space.

Consider the style and function of the mirror. This helps create a bathroom you love.

### Size And Shape Considerations

The size of the mirror should match your bathroom. Big mirrors make small bathrooms look larger.

Choose a shape that fits your style. Round mirrors soften sharp lines, while square mirrors add structure.

* Measure your wall space first
* Decide if you want a single or double mirror
* Think about the mirror's height for all users

### Frame Material Choices

Frames can change the look of your mirror. Metal frames add a modern touch, while wood frames bring warmth.

Consider materials that are resistant to moisture. This helps your mirror last longer in a bathroom.

* Metal: sleek and durable
* Wood: warm and natural
* Plastic: affordable and versatile

### Lighting And Placement

Lighting affects how you see yourself in the mirror. Place the mirror where light is even and bright.

Consider adding lights around the mirror. This helps reduce shadows and improve visibility.

* Position mirrors opposite windows for natural light
* Use LED lights to save energy
* Adjust lighting for makeup or shaving needs
