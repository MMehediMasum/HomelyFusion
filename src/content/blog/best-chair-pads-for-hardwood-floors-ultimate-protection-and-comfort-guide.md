Some pads nail on for extra hold, while others use self-adhesive backs for easy use. Using quality felt pads keeps hardwood floors clean and damage-free for longer. This guide covers top chair pads that protect hardwood floors well and last a long time.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Felt Pads For Hardwood Floors?



The best felt pads offer strong adhesion, soft cushioning, and come in various sizes. Scotch Felt Pads and X-PROTECTOR are top-rated for hardwood floor protection.



### How Do Felt Pads Protect Hardwood Floors?



Felt pads prevent scratches, dents, and nicks by cushioning furniture legs. They reduce friction and noise, preserving the floor’s finish over time.



### Are Self-stick Felt Pads Reliable For Furniture Protection?



Yes, self-stick felt pads like Scotch and Super Sliders provide easy application and strong hold. They stay in place, protecting floors from damage.



### Which Felt Pads Suit Heavy Furniture On Hardwood Floors?



Heavy-duty pads like Nail-On Heavy Duty Felt Pads and SoftTouch 1 1/2" pads provide extra durability. They support heavy furniture without wearing out quickly.



### Can Felt Pads Reduce Noise On Hardwood Floors?



Absolutely. Felt pads soften contact between furniture and floors. They significantly reduce noise from moving chairs and tables on hardwood surfaces.



### How Often Should Felt Pads Be Replaced On Furniture?



Replace felt pads every 6 to 12 months or when worn out. Regular replacement ensures continuous protection and prevents floor damage.
