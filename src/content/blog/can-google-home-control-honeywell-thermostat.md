### Connecting Via Google Home App

Open the Google Home app on your smartphone or tablet. Make sure your Google Home device is powered on and connected to Wi-Fi.

In the app, tap the "+" icon to add a new device. Choose "Set up device" and then "Works with Google."

* Search for "Honeywell" in the list of available devices.
* Select your Honeywell thermostat model.
* Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the connection.

### Linking Honeywell Account

After selecting Honeywell in the Google Home app, you must link your Honeywell account. This step allows Google to access your thermostat settings.

Enter your Honeywell username and password when prompted. Grant the necessary permissions to complete the link.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Action | Description |
| Sign In | Enter your Honeywell account credentials |
| Authorize | Allow Google Home to control the thermostat |
| Confirm | Finish linking process |

### Configuring Voice Commands

Once linked, you can use voice commands to control your thermostat. Use simple phrases with Google Assistant for best results.

* Say "Hey Google, set the thermostat to 72 degrees."
* Try "Hey Google, increase the temperature by 2 degrees."
* Ask "Hey Google, what is the thermostat set to?"

Tip: Speak clearly and make sure your Google Home device hears you well.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-google-home-to-control-your-honeywell-thermostat-is-convenient.-sometimes,-problems-can-stop-it-from-working-right.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using Google Home to control your Honeywell thermostat is convenient. Sometimes, problems can stop it from working right.

This guide helps you fix common issues fast. Check each section to solve your problem easily.

### Connectivity Problems

If Google Home can’t find your Honeywell thermostat, the devices might not be connected to the same Wi-Fi.

Slow or no internet can also cause issues. Check your Wi-Fi strength and router settings.

* Make sure Google Home and thermostat use the same Wi-Fi network.
* Restart your router and devices.
* Check for Wi-Fi interference or weak signal spots.
* Update the Honeywell app and Google Home app.

### Voice Recognition Errors

Google Home may not hear your commands correctly. Clear and slow speech helps Google understand better.

Background noise can confuse the voice assistant. Speak in a quiet room to improve recognition.

* Speak clearly and face the Google Home device.
* Reduce background noise or move closer to the device.
* Train Google Home to recognize your voice in the app.
* Use simple commands like “Set thermostat to 70 degrees.”

### Syncing Delays

Your thermostat might not update right away after a voice command. This delay can be due to slow internet or app syncing.

Sometimes, the thermostat and Google Home need time to share data. Waiting a few minutes often fixes this.

* Check your internet speed and connection stability.
* Restart the thermostat and Google Home device.
* Force close and reopen the Honeywell app.
* Make sure both devices have the latest software updates.
