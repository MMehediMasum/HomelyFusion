## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Peel And Stick Floor Tiles?



Peel and stick floor tiles are easy to install and budget-friendly. They offer quick DIY flooring solutions for kitchens, bathrooms, and bedrooms. These tiles are durable, water-resistant, and come in various designs like marble and wood patterns, enhancing any room’s aesthetics without professional help.



### How Do Carpet Protectors Help With Pet Damage?



Carpet protectors deter pets from scratching and damaging floors. Made from heavy-duty PVC, they cover doorways and high-traffic areas. These protectors are easy to clean and prevent wear and tear, making them ideal for homes with cats and dogs while preserving carpet and floor quality.



### What Makes Foam Mats Ideal For Kids’ Play Areas?
