## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Best For Cat Scratching Rugs?



Natural sisal is the most durable and preferred material for cat scratching rugs. It withstands scratching well and is safe for cats. Many top products use sisal for its texture and longevity, making it ideal for protecting furniture and carpets from damage.



### How Do Scratch Mats Protect Furniture And Carpets?



Scratch mats act as a barrier, redirecting cats’ scratching behavior. They cover vulnerable areas like couches and carpets. Adhesive tapes or anti-slip backing keep mats in place. This prevents claw damage and prolongs the life of your furniture and floor coverings effectively.



### Can Cat Deterrent Sprays Replace Scratching Mats?



Deterrent sprays can discourage cats but don’t protect surfaces physically. Sprays are best used alongside scratching mats. They repel cats with safe, natural scents, reducing scratching frequency while mats absorb the claw wear, offering a complete protection strategy.



### Are Scratch Mats Easy To Install And Maintain?



Yes, most scratch mats are lightweight and come with adhesive tapes or anti-slip features. They can be cut to size and placed on floors, walls, or furniture. Cleaning is simple with vacuuming or wiping, ensuring hassle-free maintenance for pet owners.



### Do Cats Prefer Horizontal Or Vertical Scratching Surfaces?



Cats have individual preferences; some prefer horizontal scratch mats, others vertical posts. The mats listed include both types, catering to different scratching habits. Offering both ensures your cat’s scratching needs are met and your home stays protected.



### How Do Anti-scratch Tapes Work For Cat Training?



Anti-scratch tapes are double-sided and sticky, deterring cats from scratching by unpleasant texture. They train cats to avoid certain areas without harm. These tapes are transparent and safe, making them an effective, non-toxic solution to protect furniture and carpets.
