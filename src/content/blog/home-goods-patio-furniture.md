### What Materials Are Used In These Patio Furniture Sets?

### How Do I Maintain And Clean Outdoor Wicker Furniture?



Clean wicker furniture with mild soap and water regularly. Avoid harsh chemicals to prevent damage. Use a soft brush for dirt removal. Cover furniture when not in use to protect cushions and frame. Store cushions indoors during harsh weather for longer life.

Most sets include thick, washable cushions with water-resistant covers. Cushions resist fading and mildew, ensuring comfort and durability. Some sets offer removable cushion covers for easy cleaning. This feature adds convenience and extends cushion lifespan outdoors.



### What Storage Options Are Available With These Patio Sets?



The Keter Outdoor Storage Deck Box offers 150-gallon capacity. It doubles as a bench and keeps cushions or accessories safe. Some sectional sets feature hidden storage in tables. These storage options help maintain an organized outdoor space.



### Can These Furniture Sets Withstand Extreme Weather Conditions?



Yes, materials like PE wicker, aluminum, and resin are designed for outdoor use. They resist rain, sun exposure, and wind. Cushions are water-resistant and washable. Proper care and covering furniture increase their lifespan in extreme weather.
