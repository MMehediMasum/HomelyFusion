## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Vases Work Best For Wedding Centerpieces?



Small to medium-sized vases, around 4 to 7 inches tall, suit wedding centerpieces best. They fit well on tables without blocking views. Sets with multiple sizes allow creative floral arrangements for various table sizes and themes.



### Are Glass Bud Vases Ideal For Wedding Decorations?



Yes, glass bud vases are perfect for weddings. They highlight delicate flowers elegantly and complement any theme. Their clear design fits rustic, vintage, or modern styles, making them versatile for centerpieces and other decor.



### How Many Vases Should I Get For A Wedding Centerpiece?



Typically, 3 to 5 vases per centerpiece create a balanced look. Using sets of 12 or more vases lets you decorate multiple tables consistently. Mixing sizes and styles adds depth and visual interest to your wedding decor.



### Can Vases Double As Candle Holders For Weddings?



Many glass vases can also serve as candle holders. Ribbed and cylinder vases are especially suitable for hurricane candles. This dual use adds ambiance and saves space on reception tables effectively.



### What Styles Of Vases Suit Rustic Wedding Themes?



Vintage glass bottles and embossed mini vases suit rustic weddings. Their textured, antique look pairs well with natural flowers and farmhouse decor. Sets with clear or tinted glass enhance the rustic charm beautifully.



### Are Bulk Vase Sets Cost-effective For Wedding Decorations?



Buying vase sets in bulk is cost-effective. Sets of 20 or 30 vases provide enough pieces for large weddings. Bulk buying reduces price per vase and ensures uniformity across all tables and areas.
