## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Full Length Mirror Fits Best In A Bedroom?



A mirror around 60"x16" to 64"x21" fits well in most bedrooms. It offers full body view without overwhelming space. Choose based on room size and wall space available for optimal placement and usability.



### Can Full Length Mirrors Be Freestanding Or Wall-mounted?



Yes, many full length mirrors like the DUMOS and BEAUTYPEAK models offer both freestanding and wall-mounted options. This flexibility suits various room layouts and user preferences for stability or style.



### Are Aluminum Frame Mirrors Durable And Stylish?



Aluminum frames provide durability and a sleek, modern look. They resist rust and damage better than wood. Many full length mirrors with aluminum frames blend well with bedroom décor and last longer.



### Do Full Length Mirrors Come With Shatterproof Glass?



Some mirrors, like the DUMOS Arched model, feature shatterproof nano glass. This enhances safety, especially in homes with children or pets. It prevents dangerous shards if the mirror breaks.



### How Do Led Lighted Full Length Mirrors Improve Dressing?



LED lighted mirrors, such as the 60"x16" dimmable model, provide bright, adjustable lighting. This helps with accurate makeup application and outfit checks in any lighting condition, improving dressing convenience.



### What Design Styles Are Available For Bedroom Full Length Mirrors?



Full length mirrors come in arched, rectangular, and modern thin-frame designs. Colors include black, gold, and aluminum finishes. This variety fits diverse bedroom styles from contemporary to classic.
