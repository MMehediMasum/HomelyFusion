### What Sizes Do Living Room Picture Frames Come In?



Living room picture frames come in various sizes like 11x14, 8x10, 5x7, and 4x6 inches. Multi-size sets offer versatility for different photo displays, perfect for gallery walls or tabletop use.



### Are These Frames Suitable For Wall Or Tabletop Display?



Yes, most frames in these sets are designed for both wall mounting and tabletop display. They include built-in hooks or stands, making them flexible for different room setups.



### What Materials Are Used In These Picture Frames?



Frames are made from engineered wood and shatter-resistant plexiglass. These materials ensure durability while providing a sleek, modern look ideal for living room decor.



### Can I Mix Frame Finishes In A Gallery Wall?



Yes, some sets offer multiple finishes like black, white, or natural wood. Mixing finishes adds visual interest and complements various interior styles.



### Do Picture Frames Come With Mats Included?



Many frames come with mats to fit smaller photos, enhancing the presentation. Mats help highlight photos and add a professional touch to your gallery wall.



### How Do Collage Frame Sets Enhance Living Room Decor?



Collage sets allow you to display multiple photos in one area. They create a personalized, stylish focal point that adds warmth and character to your living room.
