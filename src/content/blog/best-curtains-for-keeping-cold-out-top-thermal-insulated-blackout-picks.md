## Frequently Asked Questions



### Which Curtains Best Block Cold Drafts Effectively?



Thermal insulated blackout curtains like NICETOWN and LEMOMO block cold drafts efficiently. Their multi-layered fabric traps heat, reducing cold air entry. These curtains improve insulation, keeping rooms warmer during winter months. Choose thicker panels for maximum cold protection.



### How Do Blackout Curtains Help In Energy Savings?



Blackout curtains reduce heat loss by insulating windows. They block cold air and retain indoor warmth, lowering heating costs. Curtains with thermal liners, such as H. VERSAILTEX and NICETOWN, enhance energy efficiency. This helps maintain comfortable room temperatures and saves money on energy bills.



### Are Thermal Curtains Suitable For Noise Reduction?



Yes, thermal insulated curtains like RYB HOME and DWCN also reduce outside noise. Their dense fabric absorbs sound waves, providing a quieter indoor environment. These curtains are ideal for bedrooms, nurseries, or noisy urban areas, improving comfort and privacy.



### Can Blackout Curtains Be Used For Sliding Glass Doors?



Absolutely. Curtains like RYB HOME’s large blackout panels fit sliding glass doors perfectly. They block cold air, light, and noise effectively. Choose wide and long panels for full coverage and better insulation on large glass surfaces.



### What Sizes Are Available For Thermal Insulated Curtains?



Thermal curtains come in various sizes, typically ranging from 42x63 inches to 52x96 inches per panel. Sets usually include two panels for standard windows. Select sizes based on window dimensions to ensure full coverage and optimal insulation.



### Do Blackout Curtains Affect Natural Light In Rooms?



Yes, blackout curtains block nearly 100% of natural light. Products like NICETOWN and Yakamok offer full light blocking for better sleep and privacy. They are ideal for bedrooms and nurseries where darkness is preferred during the day.

Curtains with blackout lining also reduce outside noise, adding comfort. Sizes and colors vary to fit different rooms and styles. Using these curtains can lower heating costs and create a cozy space. Remember to pick curtains that fit well and cover windows completely for best results.
