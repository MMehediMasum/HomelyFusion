## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Lighting Mode For Selfies?



The best lighting mode varies by product but generally uses natural white or warm light. It enhances skin tone and reduces shadows. Most selfie lights offer multiple modes for different effects, like the ALTSON and Weilisi models with adjustable brightness and color modes.



### How Long Do Rechargeable Selfie Lights Last Per Charge?



Rechargeable selfie lights typically last 3 to 8 hours per charge. For example, the Singwil 120 LED light offers long battery life with its 5000mAh capacity. Usage time depends on brightness level and light mode selected.



### Can These Selfie Lights Fit All Phone Models?



Yes, most selfie lights come with adjustable clips or tripod adapters. They fit iPhone, Android phones, and tablets securely. Models like UBeesize and Newmowa include universal mounts compatible with various devices.



### Are These Selfie Lights Suitable For Video Conferencing?



Absolutely. These lights provide balanced, soft illumination ideal for video calls. Products like ALTSON and BANSINE enhance your webcam appearance and reduce harsh shadows, making them perfect for Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and other platforms.



### What Features Improve Selfie Light Quality?



Key features include high CRI (97+), adjustable brightness, and color temperature controls. These ensure natural skin tones and reduce glare. Lights like Meixitoy and OLRPT offer multiple lighting modes and strong battery power for consistent quality.



### Is A Tripod Included With These Selfie Lights?



Most models, such as Weilisi and UBeesize, include a tripod stand. This provides stability and hands-free use. Portable clip-on lights like ALTSON and Newmowa offer both clip mounts and tripod adapters for versatile setups.
