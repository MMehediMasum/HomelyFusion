## Choosing The Right Wall Sconces

Wall sconces add light and style next to your bed. Picking the right ones makes your room cozy and bright.

Think about the type, size, and look of sconces before you buy. This helps you find the best fit for your space.

### Types Of Wall Sconces

Wall sconces come in many shapes and uses. Some give soft light, while others shine bright for reading.

* Hardwired sconces connect to your home wiring for a clean look.
* Plug-in sconces are easy to install and move if needed.
* Battery-operated sconces need no wiring but require battery changes.
* Swing-arm sconces let you adjust the light direction.

### Size And Style Considerations

Pick sconces that fit your wall space and room size. Big sconces can overwhelm a small room.

Think about the style too. Simple designs suit modern rooms, while detailed ones match classic decor.

* Measure the wall space next to your bed before buying.
* Choose a sconce size that balances with your bed and furniture.
* Match the sconce finish to other room metals like lamps and handles.

### Matching Your Bedroom Decor

Wall sconces should blend with your bedroom’s colors and furniture. This keeps the room looking nice and planned.

Pick colors and shapes that fit your style. A rustic room needs different sconces than a sleek, modern space.

* Use warm light bulbs for a cozy feel.
* Choose sconce shapes that match other room elements.
* Keep the sconce placement symmetrical for balance.

## Selecting The Placement

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-place-for-wall-sconces-next-to-your-bed-is-important.-proper-placement-helps-light-your-space-well-and-adds-to-the-room’s-look.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right place for wall sconces next to your bed is important. Proper placement helps light your space well and adds to the room’s look.

Consider the height, distance from furniture, and balance. These details make your sconces both useful and stylish.

### Ideal Height For Sconces

Place sconces at eye level when sitting in bed. This height usually ranges from 60 to 66 inches from the floor. It avoids glare and directs light where you need it.

* Measure from the floor to about 60-66 inches
* Adjust based on your bed height and seating position
* Keep the light focused on reading areas

### Distance From Bed And Nightstands

Keep sconces close enough to the bed but away from bumping into nightstands. A good distance is 6 to 12 inches from the edge of the bed or nightstand. This space allows easy reach without blocking movement.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Distance | Purpose |
| 6-12 inches | Easy reach and avoids clutter |
| More than 12 inches | May cause uneven lighting |
| Less than 6 inches | Risk of hitting furniture |

### Balancing Symmetry And Function

Place sconces evenly on both sides of the bed for balance. Symmetry looks good and gives equal light for two people. Function matters too, so make sure switches are easy to reach.

Tips for balanced and functional placement:

* Match sconce height on both sides
* Align sconces with nightstand edges
* Check that switches are near the bed
* Consider adjustable arms for flexible lighting




## Preparing The Wall

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=before-hanging-wall-sconces-next-to-your-bed,-you-must-prepare-the-wall-properly.-this-helps-ensure-the-sconces-are-secure-and-safe.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Before hanging wall sconces next to your bed, you must prepare the wall properly. This helps ensure the sconces are secure and safe.

Proper preparation includes finding studs and wiring, marking drill points, and checking for any obstacles behind the wall.

### Locating Studs And Wiring

Studs provide strong support for wall sconces. Use a stud finder to locate them behind the drywall. Avoid placing sconces only on drywall.

Also, check for electrical wiring to avoid drilling into wires. A wire detector tool helps find hidden cables in the wall.

* Run a stud finder along the wall to find wooden studs
* Mark the edges and center of each stud with a pencil
* Use a wire detector to scan for electrical cables
* Avoid drilling where wires or pipes are detected

### Marking Drill Points

Mark drill points on the wall where the sconce brackets will attach. Use a pencil and a level to keep marks straight.

Measure the height and distance from the bed to place the sconces evenly on both sides.

* Hold the sconce bracket against the wall
* Use a level to ensure it is straight
* Mark screw hole spots with a pencil
* Double-check the marks before drilling

### Checking For Obstacles

Look for any hidden obstacles like pipes or insulation inside the wall. These can cause problems during installation.

Use a multi-purpose scanner that detects metal, pipes, and wiring behind walls.

* Scan the wall area where you will drill
* Note any detected metal pipes or objects
* Avoid drill points near obstacles
* Choose alternate spots if needed

## Installing The Wall Sconces

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-wall-sconces-next-to-your-bed-adds-style-and-practical-lighting.-proper-installation-ensures-safety-and-a-neat-look.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing wall sconces next to your bed adds style and practical lighting. Proper installation ensures safety and a neat look.

Follow these steps carefully to install your wall sconces correctly and securely.

### Turning Off Power

Before starting, switch off the power at the circuit breaker. This stops electricity from flowing to the wires.

Use a voltage tester to check if the power is off. Safety is the top priority when working with electricity.

### Mounting The Bracket

Attach the mounting bracket to the wall where you want the sconce. Use a level to keep it straight.

Secure the bracket with screws into a stud or use wall anchors for extra support.

* Mark the screw holes on the wall
* Drill pilot holes for screws or anchors
* Fix the bracket tightly to the wall

### Wiring The Sconce

Connect the sconce wires to the house wiring inside the electrical box. Match the wire colors carefully.

Typically, connect black to black, white to white, and green or copper wire to ground.

* Strip about half an inch of wire insulation
* Twist corresponding wires together
* Use wire nuts to secure connections
* Wrap connections with electrical tape

### Securing The Fixture

Once wired, place the sconce over the mounting bracket. Align the screw holes carefully.

Tighten the screws to hold the fixture firmly. Check that the sconce is level and stable before finishing.

* Attach fixture base to bracket with screws
* Ensure all wires are inside the box
* Install bulbs and shades as needed

## Testing And Adjusting Lighting

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-installing-wall-sconces-next-to-your-bed,-test-the-lighting.-proper-testing-ensures-safety-and-comfort.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After installing wall sconces next to your bed, test the lighting. Proper testing ensures safety and comfort.

Adjusting the lights helps create the right mood and prevents glare while reading or relaxing.

### Restoring Power

Turn the power back on at the circuit breaker after installation. Make sure the switch controlling the sconces is on.

If the lights do not turn on, check the breaker or fuse box for any tripped switches or blown fuses.

### Checking Light Functionality

Switch the sconces on to see if the bulbs light up. If a bulb does not work, try replacing it with a new one.

Make sure the wiring is secure and properly connected if the lights still do not function.

### Adjusting Brightness And Direction

Use dimmer switches if possible to control the brightness of the sconces. This helps set a relaxing atmosphere.

Adjust the sconce heads or shades to direct light where you need it most, such as for reading or ambient glow.

* Point light away from your eyes to avoid glare
* Focus light on the bedside area for reading
* Lower brightness for a calm and cozy feel

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Hanging wall sconces next to your bed can add style and function. Yet, sometimes issues arise that need fixing. Let's explore some common problems and how to solve them.

Knowing how to troubleshoot these problems can make your bedroom lighting perfect. Let's look at flickering lights, loose fixtures, and wiring problems.

### Fixing Flickering Lights

Flickering lights can be annoying and may indicate a problem. First, check if the bulb is loose. Tighten it gently to see if that fixes the flicker.

If the bulb is secure, consider replacing it with a new one. Sometimes, old bulbs cause flickering. If the problem persists, the issue might be with the fixture or wiring.

* Check the bulb for looseness
* Replace old bulbs with new ones
* Inspect the fixture for any damage

### Dealing With Loose Fixtures

Loose fixtures can be a safety hazard. Ensure the sconce is securely mounted to the wall. Use a screwdriver to tighten any loose screws.

If the fixture still feels loose, check the mounting bracket. It might need adjusting or replacing. A stable fixture ensures safety and proper function.

* Tighten all screws with a screwdriver
* Inspect the mounting bracket
* Adjust or replace the bracket if needed

### Addressing Wiring Problems

Wiring issues are more complex and require caution. If you suspect a wiring problem, turn off the power to the fixture before inspecting it.

Check for any visible damage to the wires. If you see exposed or frayed wires, it's best to call a professional electrician. Safety should always be a priority.

* Turn off power before inspecting
* Look for visible wire damage
* Contact a professional for repairs




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How High Should Wall Sconces Be Next To Bed?

Wall sconces should be installed about 60-66 inches from the floor. This height aligns with eye level when sitting in bed. It ensures comfortable lighting for reading and creates balanced room aesthetics.

### What Tools Are Needed To Hang Wall Sconces Safely?

You need a drill, screwdriver, wall anchors, a level, and a measuring tape. These tools ensure secure installation and proper alignment of your wall sconces next to the bed.

### Can I Install Wall Sconces Without Electrical Wiring?

Yes, battery-operated or plug-in sconces work without hardwiring. They offer flexible placement and easy installation, ideal for renters or rooms without existing wiring.

### How Far From The Bed Should Wall Sconces Be Placed?

Wall sconces should be about 6-8 inches from the headboard edge. This distance provides focused light without glare, perfect for bedtime reading or ambient lighting.
