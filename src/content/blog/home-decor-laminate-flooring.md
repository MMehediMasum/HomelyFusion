## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Self-adhesive Vinyl Floor Tiles?



Self-adhesive vinyl tiles are easy to install without glue or nails. They offer durability, water resistance, and a stylish look. Ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and bedrooms, they provide a cost-effective flooring solution with minimal maintenance and quick DIY application.



### How Does Double-sided Rug Tape Protect Hardwood Floors?



Double-sided rug tape secures rugs without slipping, preventing scratches and damage. It is residue-free, easy to remove, and reusable. This tape keeps rugs in place, enhancing safety and protecting hardwood, laminate, or vinyl floors from wear and tear.



### Can Felt Rug Pads Improve Laminate Flooring Comfort?



Yes, felt rug pads add cushioning and reduce noise on laminate floors. They prevent rugs from slipping and protect floors from scratches. Felt pads enhance comfort, extend flooring life, and improve insulation, making them ideal for high-traffic areas and home decor.



### What Makes Wood Grain Repair Tape Effective For Flooring?



Wood grain repair tape offers a durable, adhesive solution to cover scratches and damage on floors. It matches natural wood patterns, restoring aesthetic appeal quickly. This tape is versatile for furniture, cabinets, and DIY projects, ensuring seamless home decor repairs.



### Are Peel-and-stick Vinyl Planks Suitable For Kitchen Flooring?



Peel-and-stick vinyl planks are ideal for kitchens due to water resistance and easy cleaning. They mimic natural wood grains, providing warmth and style. These planks install quickly without professional help, making them a practical, budget-friendly choice for kitchen flooring updates.
