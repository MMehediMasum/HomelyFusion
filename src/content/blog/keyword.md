## Time Management Techniques

Managing your time well helps you get more done. Good time management reduces stress and improves focus.

Try different techniques to find what fits your work style. This guide covers key ways to manage your tasks and avoid distractions.

### Prioritizing Tasks Effectively

Start by listing all your tasks. Then sort them by importance and urgency. Focus on tasks that bring the most value first.

1. Make a to-do list each morning.
2. Mark tasks as urgent, important, or low priority.
3. Tackle urgent and important tasks first.
4. Break big tasks into smaller steps.
5. Review your list at the end of the day.

### Avoiding Common Time Wasters

Time wasters often steal your focus. Knowing what to avoid helps keep your day productive.

| --- | --- |

| Social media | Limit use to set times only |
| Unnecessary meetings | Attend only if required |
| Multitasking | Focus on one task at a time |
| Email checking | Check emails twice a day |
