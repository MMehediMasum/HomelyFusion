## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Types Of Tiles Can A Manual Tile Cutter Handle?



Manual tile cutters work well with ceramic, porcelain, and glass tiles. They provide clean, straight cuts without dust or noise. However, they are less effective on very hard materials like granite or marble, which may require wet saws or diamond blades for precision cutting.



### How Does The Tungsten Carbide Cutting Wheel Improve Cutting?



Tungsten carbide wheels are extremely hard and durable, ensuring smooth and precise tile scoring. They maintain sharpness longer than steel wheels, resulting in cleaner cuts. This feature is essential for professional results and reduces the need for frequent replacements.



### Why Choose A Tile Cutter With Infrared Or Laser Guides?



Infrared or laser guides help align tiles accurately before cutting. They improve precision and reduce tile wastage by ensuring straight, consistent cuts. This feature benefits both beginners and professionals by making the cutting process easier and more accurate.



### What Safety Features Do Tile Cutters Offer?



Tile cutters often include anti-skid feet for stability and comfortable handles to prevent hand strain. Some models have protective guards around the cutting wheel to avoid accidental injuries. These features enhance user safety during tile cutting tasks.



### Can Beginners Use Professional Manual Tile Cutters Effectively?



Yes, many professional manual tile cutters are user-friendly and suitable for beginners. Features like laser guides and ergonomic handles simplify the cutting process. Beginners should practice on scrap tiles to build confidence and achieve precise results.



### How Do Diamond Saw Blades Compare To Manual Tile Cutters?



Diamond saw blades are ideal for cutting hard materials like granite and marble. They work with power tools for fast, wet cutting. Manual tile cutters are quieter, cleaner, and better for straight cuts on ceramic or porcelain tiles but less versatile for tough stones.
