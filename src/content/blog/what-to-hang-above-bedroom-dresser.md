**Looking for the perfect thing to hang above your bedroom dresser? You’re not alone.**

That empty space often feels like a missed opportunity to add style and personality to your room. But choosing what goes there can be tricky—you want something that looks great, fits your vibe, and maybe even serves a purpose. You’ll discover easy and inspiring ideas that will transform that blank wall into a beautiful focal point.

Keep reading, and you’ll find the perfect solution to make your bedroom feel more inviting and truly yours.



## Art Prints And Paintings

Choosing art prints and paintings to hang above a bedroom dresser adds style and personality. The right art can brighten the space and create a relaxing mood.

Think about the colors, shapes, and feelings you want the art to bring. This helps pick pieces that match your bedroom design.

### Choosing The Right Size And Style

Select art that fits well above the dresser without crowding. The size should balance the furniture and wall space.

* Use a large print or painting to fill the space if the dresser is wide.
* Choose smaller pieces if you want a lighter look.
* Match the art style with your room’s vibe, such as modern, classic, or abstract.
* Consider colors that complement your bedding and walls.

### Creating A Gallery Wall

A gallery wall above your dresser can look exciting and personal. Mix different prints and paintings to create a unique display.

| Gallery Wall Tips | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Plan Layout | Arrange frames on the floor before hanging |
| Keep Spacing | Leave even gaps between frames, about 2-3 inches |
| Mix Sizes | Use various sizes to add interest but keep balance |
| Theme or Color | Choose a theme or color palette to unify the display |

### Mixing Frames And Textures

Mixing frames and textures adds depth and style to your art display. Try different materials and finishes for a rich look.

* Combine wood, metal, and painted frames for variety.
* Use matte and glossy textures to catch light differently.
* Try adding a fabric or canvas print for softness.
* Keep some common color or shape to avoid a busy look.

## Mirrors For Light And Space

Mirrors above a bedroom dresser can make the room look bigger and brighter. They reflect light and open up the space.

Choosing the right mirror can change the room's feel. It adds style and helps you get ready.

### Framed Vs. Frameless Mirrors

Framed mirrors have edges that add style and color. They can match your room’s decor.

Frameless mirrors have clean lines. They give a modern and simple look.

* Framed mirrors add a decorative touch.
* Frameless mirrors create a sleek feel.
* Frames can be wood, metal, or painted.
* Frameless is easier to clean and maintain.

### Shape And Placement Tips

Round mirrors soften a room’s look. Square and rectangular mirrors create structure.

Place the mirror at eye level above the dresser. This makes it easy to use and looks balanced.

* Use vertical mirrors for a taller look.
* Horizontal mirrors widen the space visually.
* Center the mirror with the dresser below.
* Leave space around the mirror for a neat look.

### Decorative Mirror Styles

Decorative mirrors come in many styles. Choose one that fits your bedroom’s mood.

Some styles include vintage, modern, rustic, and minimalist. Each style changes the room’s feel.

* Vintage mirrors have ornate frames and antique finishes.
* Modern mirrors use simple shapes and thin frames.
* Rustic mirrors feature natural wood and rough textures.
* Minimalist mirrors are frameless or have slim borders.

**Modern Style Tips:**

### Themes For Bedroom Ambiance

Choose themes that match the bedroom’s mood. Decals can create calm, fun, or inspiring spaces.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Examples |
| Calm | Soft clouds, moon, stars |
| Fun | Animals, cartoons, bright shapes |
| Inspiring | Quotes, nature scenes, abstract art |

### Easy Application Tips

Applying decals is quick with the right steps. Follow these tips to avoid bubbles and wrinkles.

1. Clean the wall surface well and dry it.
2. Plan the layout by placing decals loosely first.
3. Peel the backing slowly from the decal.
4. Press the decal on the wall starting from one side.
5. Use a flat tool to smooth out air bubbles.
6. Remove the transfer tape carefully if used.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are The Best Art Pieces To Hang Above A Dresser?

Choose art that complements your bedroom’s style. Abstract prints, nature scenes, or framed photographs add personality and color above a dresser. Ensure size and frame style match the room’s decor for a balanced look.

### Can Mirrors Enhance The Space Above A Bedroom Dresser?

Yes, mirrors add depth and light to the room. A decorative or vintage mirror above the dresser can create an elegant focal point and make the bedroom feel larger and brighter.

### How To Choose The Right Size Art For Above A Dresser?

Select art that is about two-thirds the width of your dresser. This proportion maintains balance and avoids overpowering the space. Leave some wall space around the art for a clean, sophisticated look.

### Are Shelves A Good Option Above A Bedroom Dresser?

Shelves offer storage and display opportunities. Floating shelves above a dresser can hold decorative items, plants, or small books. They add dimension and keep surfaces clutter-free.
