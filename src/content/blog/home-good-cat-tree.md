## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of A Cat Tree Tower?



Cat tree towers provide cats with a space to climb, scratch, and play. They help satisfy natural instincts, reduce boredom, and prevent damage to furniture. Additionally, they offer multiple levels for exercise and relaxation, promoting overall feline health and happiness.



### How Do I Choose The Right Cat Tree?



Consider your cat's size, age, and activity level. Look for durable materials like sisal for scratching posts. Ensure stability and safety features. Multi-level options with condos, hammocks, and toys can enhance your cat's experience, providing both entertainment and comfort.



### Are Cat Trees Safe For Indoor Cats?



Yes, cat trees are safe when well-constructed and stable. Choose models with a broad base to prevent tipping. Ensure there are no sharp edges or loose parts. Regularly inspect for wear and tear to maintain safety. Proper assembly and placement are crucial for safety.



### How Do I Maintain A Cat Tree?



Regularly vacuum and clean with pet-safe cleaners to remove fur and dirt. Check for wear on scratching posts and replace as needed. Tighten bolts and screws periodically. Inspect for any damage to ensure safety and longevity of the cat tree.



### Can Cat Trees Help Reduce Furniture Damage?



Yes, cat trees offer designated scratching areas to divert cats from furniture. Sisal-covered posts attract cats to scratch, preserving your home furnishings. Providing a cat tree can reduce destructive behavior, fulfilling their need to scratch and climb in a positive way.
