## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is Floor And Decor Financing?



Floor And Decor Financing offers customers flexible payment options for home improvement projects. It allows you to purchase flooring materials, tiles, and decor items without paying the full amount upfront. This can make it easier to manage your budget while renovating your home or undertaking DIY projects.



### How Does Peel And Stick Flooring Work?



Peel and stick flooring is easy to install. Simply peel off the backing and press the tiles onto a clean surface. This method is quick and requires no special tools, making it ideal for DIY enthusiasts. It's perfect for updating floors in kitchens, bathrooms, and living areas.



### Are Vinyl Tiles Waterproof?



Yes, many vinyl tiles are waterproof, making them suitable for wet areas. They are ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and laundry rooms. Their waterproof nature helps prevent damage from spills and moisture, ensuring longevity and maintaining the appearance of your flooring.



### Can I Reuse Peel And Stick Tiles?



Some peel and stick tiles are reusable, allowing for easy repositioning. However, repeated removal may reduce adhesive strength. It's important to check the product specifications to ensure reusability. Proper care during removal will help maintain their condition for future use.



### What Are Iridescent Glass Mosaic Tiles?



Iridescent glass mosaic tiles feature a shimmering effect that changes with light. These tiles add a unique and vibrant appearance to any space. They are perfect for artistic projects and home decor, providing a stunning focal point in bathrooms, kitchens, and living areas.
