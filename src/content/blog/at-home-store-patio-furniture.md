### How Do I Maintain And Clean Wicker Patio Furniture?



Clean wicker furniture using a soft brush and mild soap solution. Rinse with water and dry thoroughly to prevent mold. Use covers when not in use and store cushions indoors during harsh weather. Regular maintenance prolongs furniture life and appearance.
