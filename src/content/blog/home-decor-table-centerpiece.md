### How To Style Artificial Flowers In Vases For Table Decor?



Arrange artificial flowers by mixing heights and textures for a natural look. Use complementary vases like ceramic or glass to match your theme. Grouping flowers in clusters creates fullness, making your dining or coffee table centerpiece visually appealing and easy to maintain.



### Can Farmhouse Wooden Trays Enhance Table Centerpieces?

### What Makes Ceramic Heart-shaped Vases Ideal For Decor?
