## Tips For Maintaining Your File Cabinet

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-four-drawer-file-cabinet-in-good-shape-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-makes-it-easy-to-use-every-day.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your four drawer file cabinet in good shape helps it last longer. Regular care makes it easy to use every day.

These tips will help you keep your file cabinet clean, smooth, and safe to use in your home office.

### Regular Cleaning

Clean your file cabinet often to stop dust buildup. Dust can make drawers hard to open and close.

Use a soft cloth and mild cleaner. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the finish.

* Wipe the outside and inside of drawers
* Remove dust from corners and tracks
* Clean spills immediately to avoid stains

### Lubricating Drawers

Lubricate the drawer slides to keep them moving smoothly. This stops sticking and squeaking sounds.

Use a silicone spray or a light machine oil. Avoid heavy grease as it can attract dirt.

* Apply lubricant to metal rails and rollers
* Open and close drawers several times to spread it
* Wipe off any extra lubricant to prevent dust

### Safe Weight Distribution

Place heavy items in the bottom drawer. This keeps the cabinet stable and prevents tipping.

Do not overload any drawer. Too much weight can damage the slides and make the drawers hard to use.

* Store files and supplies evenly across drawers
* Keep the top drawers lighter than the bottom ones
* Check the cabinet’s weight limit and follow it
