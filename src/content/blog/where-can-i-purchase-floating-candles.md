## Tips For Choosing Quality Floating Candles

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-quality-floating-candles-is-important-for-a-beautiful-and-safe-display.-not-all-candles-are-made-the-same.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing quality floating candles is important for a beautiful and safe display. Not all candles are made the same. Some last longer, smell better, or are safer to use. Knowing what to check helps you pick the best ones for your event or home decor.

### Material And Burn Time

Check the candle's material. High-quality wax like soy or beeswax burns cleaner and longer. Cheap wax may melt too fast or produce black smoke. A good floating candle should burn evenly. Look for burn times on the label to match your needs.

### Size And Scent Options

Floating candles come in many sizes. Small ones fit shallow bowls, large ones suit big water features. Choose the size that fits your space well. Some candles have scents, others do not. Pick a scent that is light and pleasant. Strong smells can be overwhelming near water.

### Safety Features

Safety is key with floating candles. Look for candles with a stable base to prevent tipping. Some have a small plastic disc to help them float better. Avoid candles with toxic dyes or chemicals. Always buy from brands that mention safety standards on their packaging.
