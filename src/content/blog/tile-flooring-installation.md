## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Thickness Is Ideal For Exercise Floor Mats?



A 3/8 inch thick exercise mat offers excellent cushioning and support. It reduces fatigue and protects joints during workouts. This thickness is perfect for home gyms and multipurpose exercise areas, providing comfort without sacrificing stability.



### How Do Peel And Stick Floor Tiles Work?



Peel and stick floor tiles have adhesive backing for easy installation. Simply peel off the protective layer and press the tile firmly onto a clean surface. They are ideal for DIY projects in kitchens and bathrooms, offering a quick, waterproof, and stylish flooring solution.



### Are Interlocking Foam Tiles Good For Home Gyms?



Yes, interlocking foam tiles provide a durable, cushioned surface ideal for home gyms. They absorb impact, reduce noise, and protect floors. Their modular design allows easy installation and removal, making them practical for various workout spaces.



### What Cleaning Tools Work Best On Tile Floors?



Commercial dust mops and wet/dry mops are best for tile floors. They efficiently remove dust, dirt, and spills without damaging tiles. Choose mops with extendable handles and reusable heads for convenience and eco-friendliness in regular floor maintenance.



### How To Prevent Carpet Tiles From Curling Or Slipping?



Use dual-sided carpet tape or non-slip adhesive stickers to keep carpet tiles flat and secure. These tapes are washable, removable, and residue-free. They prevent edges from curling and provide a safer, more stable flooring surface in both residential and commercial spaces.
