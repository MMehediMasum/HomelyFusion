### Types Of Lucky Bamboo

Lucky bamboo is not a true bamboo but a type of Dracaena. It comes in different shapes and sizes for your home.

* Single stalk: Simple and classic, good for small spaces.
* Twisted stalks: Unique and artistic, they add style.
* Multiple stalks: Grouped in threes or fives, symbolizing good luck and health.
* Hearts or braided shapes: These shapes represent love and harmony.

### Placement Tips For Good Energy

Where you place your lucky bamboo affects the energy it brings. Choose spots that feel calm and bright.

Keep the plant away from direct sunlight and drafts. Place it in areas where people relax or gather.

* Near the entrance to welcome positive energy.
* In the living room to promote harmony.
* On a desk to boost focus and productivity.
* Avoid dark corners or places with harsh lights.

### Care And Maintenance

Lucky bamboo is easy to care for with the right steps. It needs clean water and moderate light.

Change the water every week. Use filtered or distilled water to avoid chemicals. Trim yellow leaves to keep it healthy.

* Keep water level high enough to cover roots.
* Use indirect sunlight, not direct rays.
* Feed with diluted liquid fertilizer once a month.
* Clean the container to prevent algae build-up.
