## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Ashley Furniture Flooring Rugs Made Of?



Ashley Furniture rugs typically use synthetic fibers like polyester and polypropylene. These materials offer durability, stain resistance, and easy maintenance. Some rugs feature low or high pile textures, enhancing comfort and style. Each rug is designed to blend modern aesthetics with practical use for indoor spaces.



### Are Ashley Furniture Rugs Machine Washable?



Several Ashley Furniture rugs, such as the Barkham and Farrendale, are machine washable. This feature makes cleaning simple and convenient. Always check the care label for specific washing instructions. Machine washable rugs maintain their appearance longer and are ideal for homes with pets or children.



### What Styles Do Ashley Furniture Rugs Feature?



Ashley Furniture offers diverse rug styles, including boho, mid-century modern, contemporary, and tribal designs. Colors range from vibrant orange and blue to neutral beige and gray. These styles suit various decor themes, adding texture and visual interest to living rooms, bedrooms, or offices.



### How Do Pile Height Variations Affect Rug Comfort?



Low pile rugs like the Wraylen are easy to clean and fit high-traffic areas. Medium pile rugs, like Jacinth, provide a balance of softness and durability. High pile rugs, such as Gizela and Wyscott, offer plush comfort but require more upkeep.



Choose pile height based on room use.



### What Sizes Are Available In Ashley Furniture Rugs?



Ashley Furniture rugs come in multiple sizes, including 5x7, 5x8, 7'6"x9'6", and 8x10 feet. This variety allows for versatile placement in different room sizes. Smaller rugs work well in cozy spaces, while larger rugs define living areas and add warmth to open rooms.



### Are Ashley Furniture Rugs Suitable For High-traffic Areas?



Yes, many Ashley rugs, especially those with low to medium pile, are durable for high-traffic zones. Materials like polypropylene resist stains and wear. Machine washable options also enhance longevity. These rugs combine style with practical features for busy households.
