## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is Peel And Stick Quarter Round Molding?



Peel and stick quarter round molding is a flexible, self-adhesive trim. It's used for covering gaps and edges on floors and walls. This type of molding is easy to install without the need for nails or screws. It enhances the aesthetic appeal of your space and provides a finished look.



### How Do You Use Caulk Tape For Bathtubs?



Caulk tape is a self-adhesive strip used to seal gaps around bathtubs. It's easy to apply and prevents water leaks. Simply clean the area, peel off the backing, and press it in place. Caulk tape is versatile and offers a quick, durable solution for various surfaces.



### What Is A Shower Threshold Water Strip?



A shower threshold water strip is a silicone barrier used to prevent water overflow. It's installed on shower floors and is waterproof. This strip is flexible and easy to cut for custom fitting. It helps maintain dry bathroom floors, enhancing safety and cleanliness.



### How To Install Floor Transition Strips?



Floor transition strips are installed to cover gaps between different flooring types. Clean the area, measure, and cut the strip. Peel the backing and press the strip firmly in place. They provide a seamless transition between floors, enhancing both functionality and aesthetics.



### What Are The Uses Of Wood Grain Tape?



Wood grain tape is a self-adhesive repair tape with a wood-like finish. It's used for furniture renovation and minor repairs. This tape is ideal for covering scratches, chips, or worn edges. It's easy to apply and provides a quick, affordable solution for enhancing home decor.
