## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Cordova Asymmetrical Mirrors Unique?



Cordova asymmetrical mirrors feature irregular, modern shapes that add artistic flair. Their distinctive frames use wood, metal, or rattan for rustic or contemporary styles. These mirrors serve as functional art pieces, enhancing any room’s decor with a stylish, eye-catching design.



### How To Hang Cordova Irregular Wall Mirrors Properly?



Use the attached hanging brackets or hooks for secure mounting. Ensure the wall is sturdy and level before installation. Measure twice for perfect alignment. Hanging at eye level in entryways or living rooms maximizes visual impact and room brightness.



### Are Cordova Mirrors Suitable For Bathrooms?



Yes, many Cordova mirrors have tempered glass and moisture-resistant frames. They withstand bathroom humidity while adding stylish, modern charm. Choose models with rustproof metal or sealed wooden frames for durability and easy maintenance in wet environments.



### What Size Options Do Cordova Mirrors Offer?



Cordova mirrors come in various sizes, from small 12-inch accent mirrors to large 36-inch full-length designs. This variety suits different spaces like bathrooms, bedrooms, and entryways, allowing easy selection to fit your specific wall dimensions and decor needs.



### How To Clean And Maintain Cordova Decorative Mirrors?



Use a soft, lint-free cloth with mild glass cleaner for the mirror surface. Avoid harsh chemicals on wooden or metal frames to prevent damage. Regular dusting preserves their finish and keeps mirrors looking vibrant and new longer.
