**Finding space for your dog’s bed in a small bedroom can feel like a tricky puzzle. You want your furry friend to be comfortable without making your room feel cramped or cluttered.**

But don’t worry—you can create a cozy spot for your dog without sacrificing your own space. You’ll discover simple, smart ways to fit a dog bed into your small bedroom that keep both you and your pet happy. Ready to make your room work better for everyone?

Let’s dive in.



## Choose The Right Dog Bed

Finding a perfect dog bed for a small bedroom needs careful thought. The right bed saves space and keeps your dog comfortable.

This guide helps you pick the best bed by focusing on size, shape, and materials.

### Size Matters

Measure your dog from nose to tail and add a few inches. This helps find a bed that fits without crowding the room.

1. Measure your dog lying down stretched out.
2. Add 4 to 6 inches for comfort.
3. Check the bedroom space to ensure the bed fits.

### Shape Options

Choose shapes that use space well. Some shapes fit better in small rooms than others.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Best Use |
| Round | Cozy corners and curling up |
| Oval | Fits well along walls |
| Rectangle | Fits under furniture or tight spaces |
| Donut | Support for dogs who like to nest |

### Material Considerations

Pick materials that are easy to clean and durable. Soft fabrics add comfort but choose ones that wash well.

* Choose washable covers to keep the bed fresh.
* Look for durable fabrics if your dog likes to chew.
* Materials like memory foam support joints in small beds.
* Breathable fabrics help prevent overheating.

## Optimize Bedroom Layout

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=fitting-a-dog-bed-in-a-small-bedroom-needs-smart-planning.-you-want-your-dog-to-be-comfortable.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Fitting a dog bed in a small bedroom needs smart planning. You want your dog to be comfortable.

At the same time, you must keep the room easy to move around in. Use space wisely to fit everything.

### Measure Available Space

Start by measuring the bedroom floor. Note the length and width carefully.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space Type | Measurement | Notes |
| Length of room | 120 inches | Measure from wall to wall |
| Width of room | 90 inches | Check any obstructions |
| Free floor space | 60 inches by 30 inches | Area not used by furniture |
| Height clearance | 80 inches | For taller dog beds or crates |

### Use Corners Effectively

Corners often have unused space that fits dog beds well. Placing the bed there keeps the room open.

* Pick a corner away from heavy foot traffic.
* Use a dog bed that fits snugly in the corner.
* Add cushions or blankets to make it cozy.
* Keep the corner clean and free from clutter.

### Avoid Cluttered Areas

Do not put the dog bed where many things are stored. Clutter reduces space and comfort.

Clear the area first. Make room so your dog can rest without disturbance.

**Tips to avoid clutter near dog bed:**

* Remove boxes or bags from the floor.
* Keep cords and cables out of the way.
* Place storage items on shelves, not the floor.

## Creative Placement Ideas

Finding space for a dog bed in a small bedroom can be tricky. Creative placement helps keep the room tidy and comfortable.

Use smart ideas to fit your dog's bed without crowding the space. This guide shares three clever solutions.

### Under Bed Solutions

Placing the dog bed under your bed saves floor space. Choose a low-profile bed that fits well.

* Measure the height under your bed before buying a dog bed
* Use a rolling bed frame to slide the bed in and out easily
* Add a soft mat or blanket for extra comfort
* Keep the area clean and free of dust

### Wall-mounted Options

Wall-mounted dog beds save floor space and create cozy spots. These beds attach to the wall at a low height.

| Feature | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Elevated Bed | Keeps bed off the floor and clean |
| Space Saver | Frees up floor area for walking or storage |
| Easy to Clean | Removable bed covers make cleaning simple |

### Multi-functional Furniture

Use furniture that doubles as a dog bed space. This keeps your room organized and stylish.

* Benches with built-in dog beds under the seat
* Ottomans that open up to store a dog bed inside
* Bookshelves with lower cubbies for pet resting spots
* Desks with dog beds tucked underneath




## Use Vertical Space

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=small-bedrooms-can-be-hard-to-fit-a-dog-bed-into.-using-vertical-space-helps-save-floor-room.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Small bedrooms can be hard to fit a dog bed into. Using vertical space helps save floor room. It makes the room feel less crowded.

Vertical storage and furniture keep pet items organized. You can make space for your dog without clutter.

### Shelving For Pet Items

Shelves keep your dog's toys, treats, and accessories off the floor. Use wall-mounted shelves to free up floor space.

Place shelves at a height that is easy for you to reach. This keeps things neat and out of your dog's way.

* Install floating shelves above the dog bed
* Use baskets or containers on shelves for small items
* Keep grooming tools and leashes on hooks below shelves

### Elevated Beds

Elevated dog beds lift your pet off the floor. They create space underneath for storage or to keep the room open.

Choose beds with legs that allow you to slide boxes or small furniture underneath. This saves space and keeps the room tidy.

* Look for beds with sturdy legs at least 6 inches tall
* Use the space below for pet baskets or extra blankets
* Elevated beds help with airflow and keep pets cool

## Maintain Comfort And Accessibility

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=fitting-a-dog-bed-in-a-small-bedroom-can-be-tricky.-it's-important-to-maintain-comfort-and-ensure-accessibility-for-your-dog.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Fitting a dog bed in a small bedroom can be tricky. It's important to maintain comfort and ensure accessibility for your dog.

Here are some tips to help you place a dog bed in a cozy way.

### Easy Access For Your Dog

Make sure your dog can easily get to their bed without obstacles. This helps them feel safe and secure.

* Place the bed near your bed for easy access.
* Avoid putting the bed near doors that open often.
* Ensure there is a clear path from the main room area to the bed.

### Keep Bed Clean And Cozy

Keeping the dog bed clean ensures a healthy environment. A cozy bed helps your dog rest better.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Task | Frequency |
| Wash bed cover | Weekly |
| Check for wear and tear | Monthly |
| Fluff bed cushion | Daily |

Consider using a waterproof liner to protect the bed from spills and accidents. This adds to the longevity of the bed.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How To Choose The Right Dog Bed For Small Bedrooms?

Pick a compact, elevated, or foldable dog bed to save floor space. Measure your bedroom and your dog before buying. Opt for beds with storage to keep essentials. Choose neutral colors to blend with your room décor.

### Where To Place A Dog Bed In A Small Bedroom?

Place the dog bed near a wall or under a window to save space. Avoid high-traffic areas to ensure comfort. Utilize corners or under the bed spots for discreet placement. Ensure the spot is cozy and quiet for your dog.

### Can Multifunctional Furniture Help Fit Dog Beds In Small Rooms?

Yes, multifunctional furniture like beds with drawers or ottomans with storage can accommodate dog beds. They maximize space by combining functions. This approach keeps your room tidy and makes room for your pet comfortably.

### What Materials Are Best For Dog Beds In Small Bedrooms?

Choose lightweight, washable, and durable materials for easy cleaning and maintenance. Memory foam offers comfort without bulk. Breathable fabrics prevent odors and keep the bed fresh. Avoid large, heavy beds that clutter small spaces.
