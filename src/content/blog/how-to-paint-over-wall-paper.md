**Are you staring at your old wallpaper and wondering if you can just paint over it instead of tearing it down? You’re not alone.**

Painting over wallpaper can save you time, effort, and a big mess—but only if you do it the right way. If you rush in without proper steps, your new paint might peel, bubble, or look uneven. In this guide, you’ll discover simple, proven tips to transform your walls smoothly and confidently.

Keep reading, and you’ll learn exactly how to get a fresh, beautiful finish that lasts.



## Preparing The Wallpaper

Painting over wallpaper can save time and effort. Proper preparation is key for good results.

Take care to prepare the wallpaper surface well before you start painting.

### Cleaning The Surface

Clean wallpaper removes dirt and grease. This helps paint stick better and last longer.

Use a mild detergent and warm water. Gently wipe the wallpaper with a soft cloth.

* Mix a small amount of detergent in water
* Dip a cloth or sponge and wring out excess water
* Wipe the wallpaper gently without soaking it
* Let the wallpaper dry completely before painting

### Repairing Damages

Check the wallpaper for tears, bubbles, or loose edges. Fix these issues before painting.

Use wallpaper adhesive to glue loose edges. Fill small tears with spackling paste or joint compound.

* Apply wallpaper adhesive under loose parts
* Press firmly and smooth out bubbles
* Fill holes or tears with spackling paste
* Let repairs dry and sand smooth before painting

### Priming The Wallpaper

Use a primer to seal the wallpaper surface. This stops paint from peeling or soaking in unevenly.

Choose a primer made for wallpaper or a good quality acrylic primer. Apply it evenly with a brush or roller.

* Pick a primer suitable for wallpaper
* Apply a thin, even coat
* Allow primer to dry fully
* Inspect for any missed spots before painting

Painting over wallpaper can save time and effort. Choosing the right paint is key for a smooth finish.

This guide helps you pick paint type, color, and tools for the job.

### Selecting Paint Type

Use paint that sticks well and covers wallpaper texture. Latex or acrylic paints work best.

* Choose latex paint for easy cleanup and durability.
* Acrylic paint offers good adhesion and dries fast.
* Use primer designed for wallpaper before painting.
* Avoid oil-based paints as they may peel later.

## Painting Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=painting-over-wallpaper-can-refresh-your-walls-without-the-hassle-of-removal.-using-the-right-techniques-helps-paint-stick-well-and-look-smooth.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Painting over wallpaper can refresh your walls without the hassle of removal. Using the right techniques helps paint stick well and look smooth.

Learn how to apply the first coat, use rollers and brushes correctly, and add more coats for a clean finish.

### Applying The First Coat

Before painting, clean the wallpaper surface to remove dust and grease. Use a primer made for glossy or vinyl wallpaper. This helps the paint hold better.

1. Clean the wallpaper with mild soap and water.
2. Let the surface dry fully.
3. Apply a wallpaper primer with a brush or roller.
4. Wait for the primer to dry before painting.

### Using Rollers And Brushes

Choose the right roller and brush for your paint and wall texture. Use a roller for large flat areas and a brush for corners and edges.

| Tool | Use | Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roller | Large flat surfaces | Use a medium nap roller for smooth wallpaper |
| Brush | Edges and corners | Use angled brushes for better control |

### Adding Additional Coats

Allow each coat to dry before adding the next. Two or three coats give the best coverage and smooth finish over wallpaper.

* Check the paint can for drying time.
* Lightly sand any rough spots between coats.
* Use thin coats to avoid drips.
* Finish with a final coat for a clean look.

## Finishing Touches

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-painting-over-wallpaper,-the-final-steps-help-make-the-wall-look-smooth.-these-finishing-touches-make-your-work-look-clean-and-complete.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After painting over wallpaper, the final steps help make the wall look smooth. These finishing touches make your work look clean and complete.

Pay close attention to small details. This will improve the overall look of your painted walls.

### Inspecting For Imperfections

Look at the wall closely after the paint dries. Check for bumps, bubbles, or rough spots on the surface.

Use good light and move around to see all angles. Mark any areas that need fixing.

* Look for peeling wallpaper edges
* Find spots with uneven paint coverage
* Check for paint drips or streaks

### Touching Up Missed Spots

Use a small brush to fix spots that were missed or painted thinly. Apply paint carefully to blend with the rest.

Let the touch-ups dry fully. Add a second coat if the area still looks different from the rest.

* Use the same paint and finish
* Apply thin layers to avoid lumps
* Feather edges to blend paint smoothly

### Cleaning Up

Remove painter’s tape carefully before the paint is fully dry. Clean brushes and rollers with water or paint thinner.

Clear any paint splatters on floors or furniture quickly. Proper cleaning helps keep your space neat and ready to enjoy.

* Lift painter’s tape at a 45-degree angle
* Use warm soapy water for latex paint tools
* Use paint thinner for oil-based paints
* Wipe spills with a damp cloth immediately

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can You Paint Directly On Wallpaper?

Yes, you can paint directly on wallpaper if it is firmly attached and in good condition. Clean the surface and apply a primer before painting to ensure proper adhesion and a smooth finish.

### What Primer Is Best For Painting Over Wallpaper?

Use a stain-blocking, oil-based or shellac primer. It seals the wallpaper, prevents stains, and helps the paint adhere well, ensuring a durable and even finish.

### How Many Coats Of Paint Are Needed On Wallpaper?

Typically, two coats of paint are sufficient. The first coat seals the wallpaper, and the second coat provides even coverage and a smooth appearance.

### Will Painting Wallpaper Cause It To Peel?

Painting wallpaper can cause peeling if it’s loose or damaged. Ensure the wallpaper is firmly glued and use a proper primer to minimize peeling risk.
