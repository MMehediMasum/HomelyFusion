## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Vase Is Best For Sunflower Bouquets?



A 12-inch tall vase suits sunflowers perfectly. It supports long stems and provides balance for large blooms.



### Which Vase Material Keeps Sunflowers Fresh Longest?



Clear glass vases are ideal. They allow water visibility for cleanliness and maintain sunflower freshness better than opaque vases.



### Can Ceramic Vases Complement Sunflower Arrangements?



Yes, white or matte ceramic vases offer a modern, minimalist look. They highlight sunflower colors beautifully in any room.



### Are Rustic Metal Vases Good For Sunflower Décor?



Rustic galvanized metal vases add farmhouse charm. They pair well with sunflowers for a cozy, country-style centerpiece.



### How To Choose A Vase For Artificial Sunflowers?



Pick sturdy vases like metal or thick glass. They support heavier silk stems and maintain arrangement shape over time.



### What Vase Styles Fit Modern Sunflower Home Décor?



Modern boho and minimalist vases work well. Clear, iridescent glass or simple ceramic vases enhance sunflower elegance indoors.
