## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Rug Pet-friendly And Durable?



Pet-friendly rugs are stain-resistant, easy to clean, and durable. They withstand scratches and spills, and often have non-slip backing to keep pets safe and protect floors.



### Can Machine Washable Rugs Handle Pet Messes?



Yes, machine washable rugs like OLANLY and WITSHOCK are designed for easy cleaning. They remove pet hair, odors, and stains quickly, making maintenance simple for pet owners.



### Are Memory Foam Rugs Comfortable For Pets?



Memory foam rugs, such as Pettop, provide softness and support. They offer a cozy spot for pets to rest and help reduce joint stress, enhancing pet comfort.



### How Do Non-slip Rugs Benefit Homes With Pets?



Non-slip rugs prevent slipping accidents for active pets. They keep rugs in place, protecting floors from damage and improving safety during play or movement.



### What Cleaning Solutions Work Best On Pet Stains?



Capture Carpet & Rug Dry Cleaner is effective for pet stains and odors. It uses non-toxic, chemical-free ingredients that safely eliminate spots without harming pets or fabrics.
