### Are Garland Bathroom Rugs Machine Washable?



Yes, most Garland bathroom rugs are machine washable. This feature ensures easy cleaning and maintenance. Washing helps keep the rugs fresh, hygienic, and extends their lifespan, making them practical for everyday bathroom use.

Many Garland bathroom rugs include a non-slip backing. This feature prevents slipping on wet bathroom floors. It enhances safety, especially in homes with children or elderly individuals, providing reliable grip and stability.



### What Sizes Do Garland Bathroom Rugs Come In?



Garland offers bathroom rugs in various sizes, including small mats and room-size carpets. Sizes range from 17"x24" to 5'x6'. This variety allows you to choose rugs that fit perfectly in any bathroom space.



### How Absorbent Are Garland Bathroom Rugs?



Garland rugs are highly absorbent due to their dense nylon fibers. They quickly soak up water, keeping your bathroom floor dry. This feature helps prevent slips and maintains a clean, comfortable environment.



### What Styles And Colors Are Available In Garland Bathroom Rugs?



Garland bathroom rugs come in diverse styles and colors, including deep fern green, navy, seafoam, purple, and black. Options range from solid colors to striped designs, allowing you to match your bathroom décor easily.
