## Caring For Your Lamps

Lamps brighten up your home and add style. To keep them in top shape, regular care is important.

Proper cleaning and maintenance will help your lamps last longer. Let's explore some tips.

### Cleaning Tips

Cleaning your lamps makes them shine and work well. Use soft cloths and gentle cleansers.

Dust and dirt can dull the lamp's look. Clean regularly to keep them bright.

* Unplug the lamp before cleaning
* Use a soft cloth for dusting
* Avoid harsh chemicals on lamp shades
* Check for any loose parts

### Maintaining Lamp Longevity

Maintaining your lamp ensures it lasts for years. Proper care saves money and time.

Routine checks help spot issues early. Fixing small problems prevents bigger ones.

* Replace bulbs with correct wattage
* Inspect cords for wear and tear
* Tighten any loose screws
* Store in a dry place when not in use
