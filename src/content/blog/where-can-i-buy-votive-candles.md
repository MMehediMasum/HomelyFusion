## Tips For Choosing Quality Votive Candles

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-quality-votive-candles-makes-a-big-difference-in-your-experience.-good-candles-burn-evenly-and-smell-nice.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing quality votive candles makes a big difference in your experience. Good candles burn evenly and smell nice. They create a calm and cozy space. Here are tips to help you pick the best votive candles for your needs.

### Wax Types

Wax affects how a candle burns and smells. Common waxes include paraffin, soy, and beeswax. Soy wax is natural and burns cleanly. Beeswax has a sweet, natural scent and lasts long. Paraffin is cheaper but can produce more soot. Choose wax based on your preference for scent and burning quality.

### Scent Options

Scented votive candles add fragrance to your room. Some candles have strong smells, others are mild. Pick a scent that suits your mood and space. Unscented candles work well for people with allergies. Always check if the scent feels fresh and not overpowering.

### Burn Time

Burn time shows how long a candle lasts. Longer burn time means more use for your money. Quality votive candles burn slowly and evenly. Check the label for estimated burn hours. Avoid candles that burn too fast or unevenly.
