## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Peel And Stick Vinyl Floor Tiles?



Peel and stick vinyl floor tiles are easy-to-install flooring options. They come with an adhesive backing, allowing quick application without glue or nails. Ideal for DIY projects, they provide a wood effect look and are waterproof, making them perfect for kitchens, bathrooms, and living rooms.



### How Do I Clean Wood Effect Vinyl Flooring?



Use a wood floor cleaner diluted in water for regular cleaning. Avoid harsh chemicals that may damage the finish. A microfiber mop works best. Clean spills promptly to maintain the vinyl’s appearance and waterproof properties. Regular cleaning helps preserve the floor's shine and durability.



### Are Vinyl Wood Floor Tiles Waterproof?



Yes, vinyl wood floor tiles are waterproof. They resist moisture, making them suitable for bathrooms and kitchens. This waterproof feature prevents damage from spills and humidity. Always check product specifications to ensure full waterproof protection for your specific vinyl flooring.



### Can Vinyl Flooring Tiles Be Used On Walls?



Yes, peel and stick vinyl tiles can be used on walls. They add decorative wood effect finishes to kitchens or bathrooms. Their adhesive backing ensures easy installation. Ensure the wall surface is clean and dry before application for best results.



### What Maintenance Products Are Best For Vinyl Flooring?



Use specialized wood floor polish and cleaner designed for vinyl. Products like Zep Wood Polish restore shine and protect floors. Avoid abrasive cleaners that damage the surface. Regular maintenance extends the flooring’s life and keeps it looking new.



### How Many Vinyl Floor Tiles Are Needed For A Room?



Calculate the room's square footage to determine tile quantity. Each tile’s size (e. g. , 90cm×15cm) helps estimate coverage. Always buy extra tiles (around 10%) for cuts and mistakes. Proper measurement ensures a seamless and complete floor installation.
