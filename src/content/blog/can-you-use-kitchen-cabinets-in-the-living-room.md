## Maintenance Tips For Kitchen Cabinets In Living Spaces

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-kitchen-cabinets-in-the-living-room-can-be-a-stylish-choice.-they-offer-extra-storage-and-a-unique-look.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using kitchen cabinets in the living room can be a stylish choice. They offer extra storage and a unique look. But, they need proper maintenance to stay in good shape.

In this guide, we share tips to keep your kitchen cabinets looking great in living spaces. Learn how to clean, handle wear, and upgrade them for long-lasting use.

### Cleaning And Care

Regular cleaning keeps your cabinets fresh. Dust and dirt can make them look old. Use gentle cleaners to avoid damage.

Wipe the cabinets with a soft cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals that can harm the finish. Clean spills quickly to prevent stains.

* Dust weekly with a microfiber cloth
* Use mild soap and water for deeper cleaning
* Avoid soaking the wood

### Handling Wear And Tear

Cabinets in the living room face different challenges. They can get scratched or dented. Handle them with care to avoid damage.

Protect the surface from heavy items. Use felt pads under vases or decor. Check for loose screws or hinges regularly.

* Inspect for damage monthly
* Tighten any loose handles
* Use touch-up paint for small scratches

### Upgrading For Longevity

Upgrades can extend the life of your cabinets. Consider new hardware for a fresh look. Paint or refinish them to match your decor.

Adding new features can also help. Soft-close hinges reduce wear from slamming. Shelving can improve storage and reduce clutter.

* Change knobs and handles for style
* Refinish surfaces every few years
* Install soft-close hinges
