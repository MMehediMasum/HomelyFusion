## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In These Tablecloths?



These tablecloths use cotton, PVC, poly jute, and net fabrics. Cotton offers softness, PVC ensures waterproofing, and net fabrics add elegance. Each material suits different needs like durability, style, or protection for indoor and outdoor use.



### Are These Tablecloths Suitable For Outdoor Use?



Yes, several options like PVC and waterproof protectors are ideal for outdoor use. They prevent stains and damage from weather. Cotton and net tablecloths are better for indoor or covered outdoor areas to maintain their quality.



### How Do I Choose The Right Tablecloth Size?



Measure your table's length and width before selecting. Sizes range from 40x60 inches to 140x140 cm for various table types. Ensure the cloth covers edges with some overhang for a polished look and proper protection.



### Can These Tablecloths Be Used For Parties And Events?



Absolutely. Many tablecloths like PVC and floral prints are perfect for parties. They add style and protect tables from spills. Waterproof and easy-clean options are especially convenient for events and gatherings.



### Are These Tablecloths Easy To Clean And Maintain?



Yes, PVC and poly jute tablecloths are easy to wipe clean. Cotton and net fabrics may need gentle washing. Most are machine washable or can be spot cleaned, ensuring long-lasting use with proper care.
