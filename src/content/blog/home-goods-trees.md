## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Artificial Trees For Home Decor?



The best artificial trees for home decor include Kazeila Olive Tree, VIVATREES Ficus, and OAKRED Fiddle Leaf Fig. These trees offer lifelike leaves, natural wood trunks, and elegant planters, enhancing any indoor space without maintenance or watering.



### How Tall Are The Artificial Olive Trees Available?



Artificial olive trees range from 4 feet to 7 feet tall. Options like Bellacat’s 7ft and Kazeila’s 6ft trees suit various room sizes, providing a realistic, lush look ideal for home, office, or porch decoration.



### Are These Artificial Trees Suitable For Offices And Homes?



Yes, these artificial trees are perfect for both offices and homes. They add greenery and style without needing sunlight or care, making them ideal for indoor environments like living rooms, workspaces, or reception areas.



### Do Artificial Trees Come With Planters Included?



Most artificial trees, such as VIVATREES and OAKRED models, come with stylish white or black planters. These planters complement the trees’ natural look and simplify setup, offering a complete decor solution.



### Can Artificial Trees Be Used Outdoors Safely?



Some artificial trees, like the VIVATREES Ficus, are designed for indoor and outdoor use. However, it’s best to check product details for UV resistance to ensure longevity when placed outside.



### What Maintenance Do Artificial Silk Trees Require?
