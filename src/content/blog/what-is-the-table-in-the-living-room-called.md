## Common Living Room Tables

Living rooms often include various tables that serve different purposes. These tables add both function and style to the space.

Knowing the names and uses of these tables helps you choose the best ones for your room.

### Coffee Tables

Coffee tables sit in front of sofas. They hold drinks, books, and decorative items. They come in many shapes like round, square, or rectangular.

* Usually low to the ground
* Made from wood, glass, or metal
* Can have storage space
* Centerpiece of the living room

### Side Tables

Side tables sit beside chairs or sofas. They hold lamps, drinks, or small items. These tables are smaller than coffee tables.

Console tables are narrow and long. They fit behind sofas or along walls. These tables hold keys, photos, or lamps.

**Console Table Uses:**

* Decorative display
* Extra surface without taking much space

## Maintaining Your Table

Keeping your living room table in good shape is important. It helps your space look neat and welcoming. A well-maintained table lasts longer and stays beautiful.

Here are some tips to help you care for your table. Follow these simple steps to keep it clean and damage-free.

### Cleaning Tips

Cleaning your table regularly keeps it looking new. Use a soft cloth to wipe away dust and dirt. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the surface.

* Use a damp cloth for sticky spots
* Dry immediately to prevent water spots
* Use coasters to avoid stains

### Preventing Damage

Preventing damage is easier than fixing it. Avoid placing hot items directly on the table. Use trivets or mats to protect the surface.

* Keep sharp objects away from the table
* Use protective covers if needed
* Move the table carefully to avoid scratches

### Repair And Restoration

Sometimes, tables get damaged despite our best efforts. Small scratches can be fixed with a wood marker. For larger issues, consider professional help.

* Use wood polish for minor scratches
* Fill deep scratches with a wood filler
* Contact a furniture expert for major repairs

A living room table is commonly called a coffee table. It is a low table placed in front of a sofa. It holds drinks, books, and decorative items. Its purpose combines functionality with style in the living area.

### How Is A Coffee Table Different From Other Tables?

A coffee table is lower than dining or side tables. It is designed for casual use near seating areas. Coffee tables often serve as a focal point in the living room. Their size and height suit easy reach while sitting.

Living room tables are made from wood, glass, metal, or a mix. Wood offers warmth and durability. Glass adds a modern, spacious look. Metal frames provide strength and contemporary style. The choice depends on room décor and personal preference.

Yes, many coffee tables feature built-in storage. Drawers, shelves, or lift tops help organize items neatly. This design maximizes space and reduces clutter. Storage coffee tables are practical for small living rooms.
