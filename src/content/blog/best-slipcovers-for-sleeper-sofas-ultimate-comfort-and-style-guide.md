Materials like stretch fabric and velvet offer comfort and durability. Some slipcovers come in sets with pieces for cushions and backrests. Colors and textures matter too, blending with your room style. This guide covers top slipcovers that combine fit, fabric, and function. Find the best cover to keep your sleeper sofa looking new and feeling cozy for years.
