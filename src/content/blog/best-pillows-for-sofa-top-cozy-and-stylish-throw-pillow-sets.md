## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Pillows Are Best For A Sofa?



18 x 18 inches pillows are ideal for most sofas. They provide a balanced look and comfortable support. Utopia Bedding and MIULEE offer popular options in this size. Larger pillows like 26 x 26 inches also work for bigger sofas or added decor impact.



### Are Pillow Inserts Or Covers Better For Sofa Pillows?



Pillow inserts provide structure and comfort, while covers offer style and protection. Buying both lets you mix and match designs easily. Utopia Bedding and EDOW provide quality inserts, while MIULEE and Fancy Homi offer stylish covers for versatile decor updates.



### How Do I Clean Decorative Sofa Pillows?



Most pillow covers are machine washable on gentle cycles. Remove inserts before washing covers. Inserts like EDOW’s down alternative are also machine washable. Always check care labels to maintain fabric quality and prolong pillow life.



### What Materials Are Common In Sofa Pillow Covers?



Common materials include cotton, chenille, corduroy, and polyester. Chenille and corduroy offer a soft, textured feel perfect for cozy decor. Cotton covers like those from Fancy Homi provide breathability and easy cleaning. Each material suits different styles and comfort preferences.



### Can Sofa Pillows Improve Living Room Decor?



Yes, sofa pillows add color, texture, and style to a living room. Neutral tones like MIULEE’s beige or sage green covers complement various themes. Mixing patterns and solids creates visual interest while enhancing comfort and coziness.



### How Many Pillows Should I Place On A Sofa?



Typically, 3 to 5 pillows create a balanced look. Use a mix of sizes and textures for depth. Sets like Utopia Bedding’s 4-pack provide enough pillows to style your sofa comfortably and attractively. Avoid overcrowding to maintain comfort.
