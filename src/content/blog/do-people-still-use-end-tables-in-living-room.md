## Alternatives To Traditional End Tables

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=end-tables-have-been-a-common-part-of-living-rooms-for-years.-many-people-use-them-to-hold-lamps,-drinks,-or-books.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

End tables have been a common part of living rooms for years. Many people use them to hold lamps, drinks, or books. But some want different options that fit their style and space better.

There are many alternatives to traditional end tables. These choices can save space and add a fresh look to your living room.

### Floating Shelves

Floating shelves attach to the wall and do not touch the floor. They create space for items without taking up room on the floor.

These shelves are great for small living rooms. You can place books, plants, or small lamps on them.

* Save floor space
* Easy to clean under
* Add a modern look
* Can be placed at any height

### Multi-purpose Furniture

Multi-purpose furniture serves more than one use. For example, an ottoman can hold items and provide extra seating.

These pieces reduce clutter and keep the room tidy. They fit well in homes with limited space.

* Ottomans with storage inside
* Nesting tables that stack away
* Benches with shelves or compartments
* Foldable or movable pieces

### Minimalist Approaches

Minimalist designs use fewer items for a clean look. Some people skip end tables and use just one key piece.

Minimalism helps open the space and makes the room feel larger. It works well with simple furniture and open layouts.

* Use a single slim table instead of two
* Place items on windowsills or mantels
* Keep surfaces clear of clutter
* Choose furniture with clean lines

## Choosing The Right End Table

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=end-tables-add-both-style-and-function-to-your-living-room.-picking-the-right-one-helps-balance-your-space.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

End tables add both style and function to your living room. Picking the right one helps balance your space.

Think about size, use, and matching furniture before buying an end table. These points guide your choice.

### Size And Proportion

Choose an end table that fits well with your sofa and other furniture. A table too big or small can look odd.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Living Room Size | Ideal End Table Size |
| Small | 12-18 inches wide |
| Medium | 18-24 inches wide |
| Large | 24-30 inches wide |

### Functionality Needs

Think about how you will use the end table. Some tables offer storage or space for lamps and drinks.

* Need storage? Choose tables with drawers or shelves.
* Use as a place for lamps or decor? Pick a sturdy surface.
* Want a spot for drinks? Consider a water-resistant top.

### Matching Existing Furniture

Match the end table’s style and color with your current furniture. This keeps your room looking unified and neat.

**Tips for Matching:**

* Match wood tones or paint colors.
* Pick similar design styles (modern, rustic, etc.).
* Use accent colors that appear in other room items.
