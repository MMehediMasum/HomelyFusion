## Tips For Measuring And Placement

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-shape-coffee-table-is-important-for-a-small-living-room.-the-table-should-fit-well-without-making-the-room-feel-crowded.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right shape coffee table is important for a small living room. The table should fit well without making the room feel crowded.

Measuring the space and planning the placement helps you pick a table that works with your furniture and room size.

### Ideal Height And Size

The height of your coffee table should match the height of your sofa seat. This keeps the table easy to reach.

For size, pick a table that does not take up too much floor space. It should allow enough room to move around comfortably.

* Table height about 1-2 inches lower than sofa seat
* Length should be about two-thirds the length of your sofa
* Keep the table narrow if the room is very small

### Spacing Around Furniture

Leave enough space between the coffee table and other furniture. This helps people walk and sit easily.

Too little space makes the room feel tight. Proper spacing keeps the area open and welcoming.

* Leave at least 18 inches between the table and sofa
* Keep 12-18 inches between the table and other chairs
* Make sure there is a clear path for walking

### Creating Visual Flow

Choose a coffee table shape that matches the room’s layout. Round or oval tables often work well in small spaces.

Visual flow means the room feels balanced and easy to look at. Avoid sharp corners that block pathways.

* Round or oval tables soften the space and improve flow
* Rectangular tables fit well with long sofas
* Keep the table’s edges clear of walking paths
