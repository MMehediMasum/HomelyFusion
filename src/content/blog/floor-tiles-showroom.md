### What Types Of Floor Tiles Are Available In The Showroom?



The showroom offers vinyl, peel & stick, foam, rubber, and modular interlocking tiles. Options include marble, grey, black, brown, and neutral colors. Tiles suit various spaces like kitchens, bathrooms, gyms, and patios for versatile flooring solutions.



### Are Peel & Stick Floor Tiles Easy To Install?



Yes, peel & stick tiles like FloorPops are user-friendly and require no glue. Simply peel the backing and stick the tile onto a clean surface. They are ideal for quick DIY projects and temporary flooring upgrades.



### Can Foam Floor Tiles Be Used For Home Gyms?



Absolutely. Foam tiles such as BalanceFrom and Skip Hop mats provide cushioning and non-slip surfaces. They protect floors and reduce fatigue during workouts, making them perfect for home gym setups and kids' play areas.



### What Sizes Do These Floor Tiles Come In?



Most tiles come in standard 12-inch by 12-inch squares, like Achim Home Furnishings and FloorPops options. Foam mats vary but typically cover around 24 square feet per pack. Sizes suit easy handling and room customization.



### Are Rubber Floor Tiles Suitable For Wet Areas?



Yes, rubber tiles like SOUJOY’s modular interlocking mats have drainage holes. They provide non-slip surfaces and quick water drainage, making them perfect for pool areas, bathrooms, patios, and pet zones.



### How Durable Are Vinyl And Peel & Stick Floor Tiles?



Vinyl and peel & stick tiles are designed to be durable and resistant to water and stains. They withstand daily wear well and are easy to clean, making them suitable for high-traffic areas in homes and offices.
