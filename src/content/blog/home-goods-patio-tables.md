## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Best For Outdoor Patio Tables?



Outdoor patio tables made from acacia wood, bamboo, HDPE, metal, and tempered glass are durable. These materials resist weather and require low maintenance, making them ideal for patios, pools, and gardens.



### How To Maintain Weather-resistant Patio Tables?



Clean patio tables regularly with mild soap and water. Avoid harsh chemicals. Cover tables during extreme weather to prolong life. Weather-resistant materials like HDPE and metal need minimal upkeep.



### Which Patio Tables Are Suitable For Small Spaces?



Small side tables like the LEJANEOYE 2-tier or PASAMIC Adirondack tables fit compact areas well. These are portable, space-saving, and perfect for balconies or poolside use.



### Do Outdoor Tables Come With Umbrella Holes?



Yes, some tables, like the FDW tempered glass patio table, include umbrella holes. This feature provides shade and enhances outdoor comfort during sunny days.



### Are Rattan Furniture Sets Good For Patios?



Rattan furniture sets, such as FDW’s 4-piece wicker set, offer weather resistance and style. They are comfortable, lightweight, and ideal for outdoor seating on porches and gardens.



### What Is The Benefit Of Acacia Wood Patio Tables?



Acacia wood patio tables like Christopher Knight Home’s models are sturdy and eco-friendly. They resist decay and add a natural, elegant look to outdoor spaces.
