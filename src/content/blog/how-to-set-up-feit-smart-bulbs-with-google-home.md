Setting up Feit Smart Bulbs with Google Home is simple. First, you must install the bulbs correctly. Follow the steps below to get started safely and easily.

Make sure you have your Google Home device ready. The bulbs need power to connect and work with your smart home system.

### Turn Off Power

Before you touch any light fixtures, turn off the power. This stops any risk of electric shock. Use your wall switch or circuit breaker to cut power.

* Locate the correct switch or breaker for the bulb
* Switch off the power to that area
* Double-check the light is off before proceeding

### Screw In Bulbs

Carefully screw the Feit Smart Bulbs into the socket. Make sure they are secure but do not overtighten. Handle the bulbs gently to avoid damage.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bulb Type | Socket Type | Notes |
| A19 | E26 | Standard size for most lamps |
| BR30 | E26 | Good for recessed lighting |
| GU24 | GU24 | Twist and lock base |

### Restore Power

After installing the bulbs, restore power to the fixtures. Turn on the wall switch or circuit breaker you switched off earlier. The bulbs will now power on.

Use your Google Home app to connect and control the bulbs. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as your Google Home.

## Troubleshooting Tips

Setting up Feit smart bulbs with Google Home is simple. Sometimes issues can happen. These tips help you fix common problems fast.

Try these steps if your smart bulbs do not respond or disconnect. Check each section below for easy fixes.

### Resetting Bulbs

If your smart bulb is not working correctly, try resetting it. This clears any settings that cause problems.

1. Turn the bulb on for 3 seconds, then off for 3 seconds.
2. Repeat this on-off cycle five times.
3. The bulb will blink to show it is reset.
4. After resetting, try to connect it to Google Home again.

### Reconnecting Devices

Sometimes the connection between Google Home and bulbs breaks. Reconnecting fixes this issue.

* Open the Google Home app on your phone.
* Go to the device list and find your Feit bulb.
* Select the bulb and choose "Remove device".
* Turn the bulb off and on again to restart it.
* Use the "Add device" option to set up the bulb again.

### Checking Wi-fi And App Updates

Smart bulbs need a strong Wi-Fi connection and updated apps to work well. Check these areas to avoid issues.

| Check | How to Check | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wi-Fi Signal | Test signal near bulb with phone | Weak signals cause disconnections |
| Router | Restart if slow or unresponsive | Resets network and fixes bugs |
| Google Home App | Update from app store | New versions fix bugs and add support |
| Feit App | Update from app store | Ensures compatibility with bulbs |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Connect Feit Smart Bulbs To Google Home?

First, install the Feit app and set up your bulbs. Next, open Google Home, tap "Add," then "Set up device. " Choose "Works with Google," find Feit in the list, and link your account. Your bulbs will appear in Google Home after syncing.

### Can I Control Feit Smart Bulbs With Voice Commands?

Yes, once linked to Google Home, you can control Feit bulbs using voice. Say commands like "Hey Google, turn on the living room light. " You can also adjust brightness and color if your bulb supports those features.

### What To Do If Google Home Can't Find Feit Bulbs?

Ensure bulbs are powered on and connected to the same Wi-Fi network as Google Home. Restart the Feit app and Google Home device. Try unlinking and relinking the Feit account in Google Home for a fresh connection.

### Are Feit Smart Bulbs Compatible With Google Assistant Routines?

Yes, you can include Feit bulbs in Google Assistant routines. Create routines in the Google Home app to automate bulb actions like turning on at sunset or dimming at bedtime for convenience.
