## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Vase Material Suits Peonies Best?



Ceramic and glass vases are ideal for peonies. They provide sturdy support and enhance flower beauty. White ceramic offers a minimalist look, while clear glass shows stems and water, keeping peonies fresh longer.



### How Tall Should A Vase Be For Peonies?



A vase height of 8 to 9 inches suits peonies well. It supports stems and keeps blooms upright. This size fits most standard peony bouquets and complements home decor styles like modern or farmhouse.



### Can Artificial Peonies Look Natural In Vases?



Yes, high-quality silk peonies in glass or ceramic vases appear very lifelike. Faux flowers offer long-lasting decor without water or maintenance. They are perfect for homes needing vibrant, low-maintenance flower arrangements.



### Are Small Vases Good For Peony Arrangements?



Small vases work well for single peony stems or minimal arrangements. They fit on shelves, tables, or desks. Choose ribbed or textured white ceramic vases for a modern, elegant touch with small peony displays.



### Which Vase Style Fits Farmhouse Decor With Peonies?



Minimalist white ceramic vases with ribbed textures complement farmhouse style. Clear iridescent glass vases add a modern farmhouse vibe. Both styles highlight peonies’ natural beauty and fit dining rooms, living rooms, or entryways.



### How To Keep Peonies Fresh Longer In A Vase?



Use clean water and trim stems regularly. Choose a vase with wide enough opening to avoid crowding. Ceramic or glass vases keep water cool, helping peonies stay fresh for days. Change water every two days for best results.
