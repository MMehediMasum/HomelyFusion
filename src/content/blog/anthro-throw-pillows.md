## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Anthro Throw Pillows Made Of?



Anthro throw pillows use high-quality materials like 100% cotton, velvet, and jacquard. These materials ensure durability and comfort. They offer softness and a stylish look for any living space.



### How Do I Clean Anthro Throw Pillow Covers?



Most Anthro pillow covers are machine washable on gentle cycles. Use mild detergent and cold water. Air drying is recommended to maintain fabric quality and color vibrancy.



### Are Anthro Throw Pillows Suitable For Outdoor Use?



Some Anthro pillow covers, like boho and striped cotton ones, can be used outdoors. However, velvet and embroidered pillows are best kept indoors to avoid damage from weather.



### What Sizes Do Anthro Throw Pillows Come In?



Anthro throw pillows commonly come in sizes like 14. 5", 18"x18", and 20"x20". These sizes fit couches, beds, and accent chairs perfectly for versatile decor options.



### Do Anthro Throw Pillows Have Hidden Zippers?



Yes, many Anthro pillow covers feature hidden zippers. This design keeps pillows secure and allows easy removal for cleaning without compromising aesthetics.



### Can Anthro Throw Pillows Match Modern Farmhouse Decor?



Absolutely. Anthro offers farmhouse styles like ruffled dark salmon pink and embroidered floral covers. These bring a cozy, stylish touch to modern farmhouse interiors.

Anthro throw pillows add a fresh and cozy touch to any room. Their designs blend simple patterns with soft, quality materials.
