## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Materials For Kitchen Rugs?



The best kitchen rugs use materials like polypropylene, chenille, and cushioned foam. These are durable, stain-resistant, and easy to clean. They also offer comfort and anti-fatigue properties, ideal for standing long in the kitchen.



### How Do Anti-fatigue Kitchen Mats Improve Comfort?



Anti-fatigue mats reduce foot and leg strain by providing cushioning. This helps when standing long periods while cooking or washing dishes. The mats also improve posture, reduce fatigue, and prevent discomfort, making kitchen tasks easier and less tiring.



### Are Non-slip Kitchen Rugs Safe For All Floor Types?



Yes, non-slip kitchen rugs have rubber or latex backing that grips floors securely. They work well on tile, hardwood, and laminate floors. This prevents slipping and accidents, making them a safe choice for busy kitchens.



### Can Kitchen Rugs Be Machine Washed?



Many kitchen rugs, like the HEBE and Artoid Mode sets, are machine washable. This makes cleaning spills and stains easy. Always check the care label for washing instructions to maintain the rug’s appearance and durability.



### What Sizes Are Ideal For Kitchen Rugs And Mats?



Common sizes include 17"x29", 17"x47", 20"x48", and runner sets. Choose sizes that fit in front of the sink, stove, or along kitchen pathways. Proper size ensures comfort and protects floors effectively.



### How Do Cushioned Kitchen Mats Benefit Kitchen Tasks?



Cushioned mats provide soft support to reduce pressure on feet and joints. They help prevent soreness during long cooking or cleaning sessions. The cushioning also adds warmth and comfort to hard kitchen floors.
