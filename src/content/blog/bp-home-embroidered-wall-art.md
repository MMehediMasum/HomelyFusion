### How Do I Care For Embroidered Wall Art?



Keep embroidered wall art away from direct sunlight and moisture. Dust gently with a soft cloth to maintain colors. Avoid harsh cleaning products. This care preserves embroidery threads and wooden frames, ensuring long-lasting beauty and vibrant details.
