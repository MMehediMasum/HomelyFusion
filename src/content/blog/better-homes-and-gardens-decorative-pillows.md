### Are These Pillow Covers Suitable For Outdoor Use?



Yes, some Better Homes And Gardens pillow covers are designed for outdoor use. They feature weather-resistant fabrics like burlap and linen blends. These covers maintain color and texture even in sunlight and moisture, making them ideal for patios and outdoor living spaces.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain Decorative Pillow Covers?



Most decorative pillow covers are machine washable or spot cleanable. Use gentle cycles and mild detergent for fabric safety. Avoid bleach and high heat drying. Proper care preserves colors, fabric texture, and embroidery details on Better Homes And Gardens pillows.



### What Sizes Are Available For These Decorative Pillows?



Better Homes And Gardens pillow covers typically come in standard sizes like 18x18 inches and 20x20 inches. Lumbar pillow sizes like 12x20 inches are also available. These sizes suit couches, beds, and chairs for versatile home decor.



### Which Styles Best Fit Farmhouse Or Modern Decor?



Farmhouse styles include floral embroidery, tufted patterns, and burlap trims. Modern styles favor simple geometric prints, striped corduroy, and neutral color palettes like grey and white. Better Homes And Gardens offers a wide variety to match both farmhouse and contemporary tastes.



### Can I Mix And Match Different Pillow Sets Together?



Yes, mixing and matching different Better Homes And Gardens pillow covers adds visual interest. Combine floral, striped, and solid patterns in complementary colors for a layered, stylish look. This approach works well for spring, summer, and year-round home decor.
