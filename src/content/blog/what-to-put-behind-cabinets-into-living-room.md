## Color And Paint Choices

Choosing the right color behind living room cabinets can change the whole space. Paint colors add personality and style.

Think about how colors match your furniture and lighting. The right choice makes your cabinets stand out or blend in.

### Bold Accent Colors

Bold colors create a strong look behind cabinets. They add energy and draw attention to the area.

Colors like deep blue, bright red, or emerald green work well. They give the room a modern and lively feel.

* Use bold colors for a statement wall effect
* Pair with light-colored cabinets for contrast
* Choose matte or satin finish for a sleek look

### Neutral Tones For Balance

Neutral colors create calm and balance behind cabinets. They fit many styles and colors of furniture.

Shades like beige, gray, or soft white keep the space open and fresh. They do not compete with other colors.

* Choose warm neutrals for a cozy feel
* Cool neutrals add a modern touch
* Neutral tones work well with wood and metal

### Textured Paint Effects

Textured paints add depth and interest behind cabinets. They create a unique look without extra decor.

Options include sponge, rag, or stucco effects. These finishes catch light and add subtle patterns.

* Sponge texture creates a soft, mottled look
* Ragging adds a fabric-like pattern
* Stucco offers a rough, rustic feel

## Shelving And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-storage-behind-cabinets-in-a-living-room-can-improve-organization.-it-also-adds-style-and-makes-the-space-more-useful.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right storage behind cabinets in a living room can improve organization. It also adds style and makes the space more useful.

This guide looks at floating shelves, built-in display units, and open versus closed storage options.

### Floating Shelves

Floating shelves are mounted on the wall with no visible supports. They create a clean and modern look behind cabinets.

They are ideal for displaying small items and keeping essentials within reach.

* Easy to install and customize
* Works well for books, plants, and decor
* Does not take up floor space
* Can be arranged in creative patterns

### Built-in Display Units

Built-in units fit between or behind cabinets for a seamless look. They provide strong storage and display space.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Feature** | **Benefit** | **Use** |
| Fixed shelves | Sturdy and durable | Hold heavy books or art pieces |
| Glass doors | Protect items from dust | Display collectibles safely |

### Open Vs Closed Storage

Open storage shows off items and adds visual interest. Closed storage hides clutter and keeps things tidy.

Consider this list when choosing storage style:

* **Open Storage:**Easy access, good for display, needs frequent tidying.
* **Closed Storage:**Keeps dust out, hides mess, less visual appeal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Materials Are Best Behind Living Room Cabinets?

Popular materials include wallpaper, paint, tile, and wood panels. Each adds texture and style. Choose based on your room’s design and durability needs.

### How Can I Decorate The Space Behind Cabinets?

Use decorative wallpaper, LED strip lights, or artwork. These options add visual interest and brighten the area behind your cabinets.

### Is It Necessary To Put Anything Behind Cabinets?

While not required, adding a backing enhances aesthetics and protects walls from damage. It also makes cleaning easier and adds a finished look.

### Can I Use Mirrors Behind Living Room Cabinets?

Yes, mirrors create an illusion of more space and light. They work well in small or dimly lit living rooms behind cabinets.
