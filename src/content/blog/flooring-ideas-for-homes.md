## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Flooring Options For Modern Homes?



Modern homes benefit from vinyl floor tiles, hardwood, and laminate flooring. These options offer durability, style, and easy maintenance. Peel-and-stick vinyl tiles provide a quick DIY solution with various designs. Hardwood adds warmth and value, while laminate mimics wood at a lower cost.



### How To Choose Lighting For Different Rooms?



Select lighting based on room function and ambiance. Use dimmable floor lamps for living rooms and bedrooms. Smart uplights with color control suit offices and art display areas. Minimalist tall lamps add elegance and adjustable brightness for versatile use.



### Which Floor Tiles Are Ideal For Kitchens And Bathrooms?



Self-adhesive vinyl floor tiles are perfect for kitchens and bathrooms. They resist moisture, are easy to install, and come in various patterns. Their peel-and-stick feature makes DIY flooring simple and affordable, while providing durability against spills and humidity.



### How To Maintain Hardwood And Vinyl Floors Effectively?



Use microfiber mops and gentle cleaning solutions for hardwood and vinyl floors. Avoid excessive water to prevent damage. Regular sweeping removes dust and debris. Mop with a damp, not wet, cloth for lasting floor beauty and cleanliness.



### What Are The Benefits Of Smart Led Floor Lamps?



Smart LED floor lamps offer adjustable brightness and color temperature. They can be controlled via remote or apps like Alexa and Google Home. These lamps enhance ambiance, save energy, and add convenience to living spaces and home offices.



### How To Organize Cleaning Tools In Small Spaces?



Use floor-mounted mop racks or movable cleaning carts to organize tools. These solutions save space and keep mops, brooms, and buckets accessible. They are ideal for homes, offices, and larger properties needing efficient cleaning storage.
