## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Materials For Fluffy Shag Area Rugs?



Fluffy shag rugs typically use polyester, polypropylene, or microfiber. These materials offer softness, durability, and easy maintenance. They resist stains and shedding, making them ideal for living rooms and bedrooms.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain Washable Area Rugs?



Machine washable rugs can be cleaned with gentle cycles and mild detergent. Air dry or tumble dry low. Regular vacuuming helps remove dirt and maintains fluffiness.



### Are Non-slip Rugs Safe For Kids’ Rooms?



Yes, non-slip rugs have rubber or latex backing that prevents slipping. They provide extra safety for children and pets, reducing the risk of falls in bedrooms or playrooms.



### What Size Rug Suits A Living Room Or Bedroom?



Common sizes like 4x6, 5x7, or 8x10 feet work well. Choose based on room size and furniture layout for balanced aesthetics and comfort.



### Can Area Rugs Improve Home Decor Aesthetics?



Absolutely. Rugs add texture, color, and warmth. They complement styles like farmhouse, boho, or modern, enhancing the overall ambiance of any room.



### Are Stain-resistant Rugs Good For High-traffic Areas?



Yes, stain-resistant rugs repel spills and stains, keeping them looking new. They are practical choices for dining rooms, kids’ rooms, or busy living spaces.
