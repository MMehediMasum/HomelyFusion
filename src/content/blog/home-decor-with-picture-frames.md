## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Sizes Come In Picture Frame Collage Sets?



Picture frame collage sets often include multiple sizes like 8x10, 5x7, and 4x6 inches. This variety fits different photo dimensions and allows flexible wall or tabletop displays. Multi-size sets enhance visual interest and accommodate diverse photo collections.



### How To Choose Frames For Rustic Home Decor?



Opt for wood or carbonized black frames with natural finishes. Rustic frames often feature distressed textures or warm tones. They complement cozy, farmhouse-style interiors, adding charm and character to your photo display.



### Can Picture Frames Be Used As Wedding Gifts?



Yes, picture frames make thoughtful wedding gifts. Choose elegant or boho chic styles to match couples’ tastes. Frames help preserve memories and add personal decorative touches to new homes.



### Are Collage Frames Easy To Mount Or Display?



Most collage frames come with mounting hardware and tabletop stands. This dual option allows easy wall hanging or tabletop display. Versatile mounting suits various spaces and preferences.



### What Materials Are Used In Quality Picture Frames?



Quality frames use engineered wood, tempered glass, or shatter-resistant plexiglass. These materials ensure durability and clear photo visibility. Choose based on style, protection needs, and display location.
