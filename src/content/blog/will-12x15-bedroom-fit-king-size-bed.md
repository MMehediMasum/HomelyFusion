## King Bed Dimensions

King size beds offer plenty of space for comfort. Knowing the dimensions helps you decide if it fits your room.

This guide covers the standard king size measurements and common variations. It helps you choose the right bed size for your bedroom.

### Standard King Size Measurements

The standard king size bed measures 76 inches wide and 80 inches long. It provides ample space for two adults to sleep comfortably.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dimension | Width | Length |

### Variations In King Beds

King beds come in different styles. Each type has unique dimensions and suits different room sizes.

* California King: 72 inches wide by 84 inches long
* Split King: Two twin XL mattresses side by side, 38 inches by 80 inches each
* Texas King: Larger than standard, about 80 inches by 98 inches
* Wyoming King: Even bigger, roughly 84 inches by 84 inches
