## Frequently Asked Questions



### How Do I Choose The Right Mirror Size?



Choosing the right mirror size depends on your space and purpose. For full-length views, opt for larger mirrors like the Kelly Miller 24"x48" Gold Mirror. For decorative purposes, smaller mirrors such as the TenFuju 10” x 7” Vintage Wall Mirror work well.



Measure your wall area before purchasing.



### What Style Suits My Home Decor Best?



The style depends on your home's theme. For a traditional look, consider the Tstarer Vintage Decorative Gold Framed Mirror. For a modern touch, the WallBeyond Asymmetrical Mirror is ideal. Rustic or farmhouse interiors pair well with Honiway Wall Mirror Decorative.



Mix and match styles for a unique ambiance.



### Can Mirrors Enhance Room Lighting?



Mirrors can significantly enhance room lighting. They reflect natural and artificial light, brightening spaces. Position mirrors opposite windows for maximum effect. A well-placed mirror, like the QMDECOR Rectangle Sparkling Decorative Wall Mirror, can make a room appear larger and more luminous.



### What Mirror Shapes Are Trendy In 2023?



Asymmetrical, round, and sunburst shapes are trending in 2023. The WallBeyond Asymmetrical Mirror offers a modern touch. The LOSOUR Rustic Round Wood Mirror provides a bohemian flair. For a vintage feel, consider the Roowest Leaf Teardrop Mirror. These shapes add visual interest and style to any room.



### How Do I Maintain Decorative Mirrors?



To maintain decorative mirrors, clean them regularly with a soft cloth and glass cleaner. Avoid abrasive materials that could scratch the surface. Dust frames gently, especially those with intricate designs like the Antique Gold Resin Baroque Mirror. Regular maintenance ensures mirrors remain pristine and reflective.
