## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Lumbar Pillow For Couch Support?



The best lumbar pillow for couch support offers firm memory foam and ergonomic design. It reduces lower back pain and improves posture. Choose pillows with washable covers and breathable materials for comfort and hygiene. Options like TANYOO and HOMBYS provide excellent support for daily use.



### How Does Memory Foam Help With Lumbar Support?



Memory foam contours to your lower back shape, providing customized support. It evenly distributes pressure, reducing pain and fatigue. This material adapts to body heat, offering lasting comfort. Memory foam pillows help maintain spinal alignment, making them ideal for couch, office chair, or car seat use.



### Can Lumbar Pillows Relieve Chronic Lower Back Pain?



Yes, lumbar pillows can relieve chronic lower back pain by supporting the natural curve of the spine. They reduce muscle strain and improve posture during sitting. Consistent use on couches or chairs helps prevent pain flare-ups and promotes healing. Choose ergonomic designs for maximum relief.



### Are Lumbar Pillows Suitable For Elderly Users?



Lumbar pillows are highly suitable for elderly users. They provide gentle, ergonomic support to alleviate lower back discomfort. Pillows with soft yet firm memory foam, like TANYOO or HOMBYS, help improve sitting comfort. Additionally, washable covers make maintenance easier for elderly users.



### How To Choose The Right Size Lumbar Pillow For Couch?



Choose a lumbar pillow size that fits the curve of your lower back and couch depth. Standard sizes range from 12x20 inches to extra-large 20x13. 5 inches. Larger pillows like HOMBYS offer more coverage. Always measure your couch and consider pillow thickness for proper support.



### Are Decorative Lumbar Pillows Effective For Back Support?



Decorative lumbar pillows can offer some support if filled with high-density foam. However, they are primarily for aesthetics. For effective back support, choose ergonomic pillows with memory foam and contour design. Combining decorative covers with supportive inserts like MIULEE ensures both style and comfort.
