## Frequently Asked Questions



### Which Rug Materials Are Best For Allergy Sufferers?



Natural fibers like wool and synthetic materials such as polyester are ideal. They resist dust mites and allergens. Low pile rugs also prevent allergen buildup, making cleaning easier and improving air quality for allergy sufferers.



### Are Washable Rugs Effective For Allergy Relief?



Yes, washable rugs are excellent for allergy relief. They allow easy removal of dust, pet dander, and pollen through regular washing. This keeps allergens at bay and maintains a cleaner, healthier home environment.



### How Does Low Pile Rug Help Allergy Sufferers?



Low pile rugs trap fewer allergens compared to high pile rugs. They are easier to vacuum and clean, reducing dust mites and pet dander. This helps minimize allergy symptoms and keeps indoor air cleaner.



### Do Non-slip Backing Rugs Benefit Allergy Sufferers?



Non-slip backing rugs reduce movement, limiting dust disturbance and allergen spread. They stay in place, making cleaning safer and more efficient. This helps maintain a hypoallergenic environment, especially in homes with pets and children.



### Are Eco-friendly Rugs Better For Allergies?



Eco-friendly rugs made from recycled fibers or natural materials emit fewer VOCs. They resist mold and mildew growth, reducing allergens. These rugs provide a safer, healthier option for allergy sufferers concerned about indoor air quality.



### Can Pet-friendly Rugs Reduce Allergy Symptoms?



Pet-friendly rugs are designed to resist shedding and stains. They are easy to clean and prevent allergen buildup from pet dander. Choosing such rugs helps allergy sufferers maintain a cleaner and more comfortable living space.
