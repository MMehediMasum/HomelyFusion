## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In Bedsure Throw Blankets?



Bedsure throw blankets are made from soft fleece, microfiber, cotton, chenille, and faux fur. These materials ensure warmth, coziness, and durability. Each blanket offers a plush, lightweight feel suitable for year-round use and easy maintenance.



### How Do I Choose The Right Size Blanket For My Couch?



Consider your couch size and usage when choosing. Common sizes are 50x60 inches for throws and 60x80 inches for larger blankets. A throw should cover your lap or shoulders, while bigger sizes work well for full coverage.



### Are Bedsure Blankets Suitable For Gifting Occasions?



Yes, Bedsure blankets make excellent gifts for birthdays, holidays, and housewarmings. Their cozy feel and stylish designs appeal to women, men, teens, and moms. They come in attractive colors and packaging, perfect for thoughtful presents.



### Can I Machine Wash These Fleece And Faux Fur Blankets?



Most Bedsure fleece and faux fur blankets are machine washable. Use cold water on gentle cycles and avoid bleach. Tumble dry low or air dry to maintain softness and prevent damage over time.



### What Makes Chunky Knit Blankets Different From Fleece Throws?



Chunky knit blankets use thick yarns for a textured, cozy look. They add decorative warmth to any room. Fleece throws are lightweight, smooth, and warm with a soft finish, ideal for everyday comfort.



### Are These Blankets Good For All Seasons?



Yes, Bedsure blankets are designed for all-season comfort. Lightweight fleece and cotton options provide warmth without overheating. Thicker faux fur and knit blankets offer extra coziness in colder months while remaining breathable.
