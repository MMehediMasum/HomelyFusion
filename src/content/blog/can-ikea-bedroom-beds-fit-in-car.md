## Ikea Bed Sizes And Dimensions

Ikea offers a variety of bed models in different sizes. Knowing their dimensions helps you decide if a bed can fit in your car. This is useful when picking up or moving an Ikea bed.

Each bed type has standard sizes that you should measure before transport. This guide covers common Ikea beds, their sizes, and tips on measuring for transport.

### Common Ikea Bed Models

Ikea has many popular bed models. Some of the most common are Malm, Hemnes, Brimnes, and Sultan. Each model comes in multiple sizes like twin, full, queen, and king.

* **Malm:**Simple design with clean lines, available in several sizes.
* **Hemnes:**Classic style, often made of solid wood.
* **Brimnes:**Includes storage options under the bed.
* **Sultan:**Offers traditional bed frame designs.

Knowing the exact size of your Ikea bed is key for fitting it inside a car. Below is a table showing common mattress sizes and their dimensions in inches and centimeters.

### Measuring Your Bed For Transport

To check if your Ikea bed fits in your car, measure the bed frame and mattress carefully. Include any parts that stick out.

1. Measure the length, width, and height of the mattress.
2. Measure the frame length, width, and height separately.
3. Note if the bed can be disassembled for easier transport.
4. Compare these sizes with your car's interior space.
5. Check if the bed fits through doors and trunk openings.




## Car Types And Loading Space

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=knowing-if-ikea-bedroom-beds-fit-in-your-car-depends-on-the-car-type-and-space.-different-cars-have-different-room-for-large-items.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Knowing if Ikea bedroom beds fit in your car depends on the car type and space. Different cars have different room for large items.

This guide looks at small cars, SUVs, folding seats, and how to measure your car’s space for Ikea beds.

### Small Cars Vs Suvs

Small cars usually have less room inside. This limits how big items like Ikea beds can fit. SUVs offer more space and flexibility.

* Small cars have tighter cargo space and small trunk openings.
* SUVs often have larger trunks and fold-flat rear seats.
* SUVs can carry longer and bulkier items with the seats folded.
* Small cars may require removing parts of the bed frame to fit.

### Folding Seats And Cargo Space

Folding seats increase usable space inside your car. Knowing how your seats fold helps plan loading Ikea beds.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seat Type | Folding Style | Impact on Space |
| Bench Seat | Fold down flat | Creates a flat cargo area |
| Split-fold Seat | Fold 60/40 or 40/20/40 | Allows partial folding for passengers |
| Fixed Seat | No folding | Limits cargo space drastically |

### Measuring Your Car’s Interior

Measure your car’s interior to check if an Ikea bed fits. Check length, width, and height inside the cargo area.

Here is a simple way to measure:

1. Fold down the rear seats if possible.
2. Measure from the back of the front seats to the trunk door.
3. Measure the width between the wheel wells inside.
4. Check the height from floor to ceiling in the cargo area.
5. Compare these numbers with Ikea bed dimensions.

## Disassembling Ikea Beds

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=moving-an-ikea-bed-can-be-easier-if-you-take-it-apart-first.-disassembling-the-bed-helps-it-fit-in-a-car.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Moving an Ikea bed can be easier if you take it apart first. Disassembling the bed helps it fit in a car. This guide explains how to safely take your Ikea bed apart.

Knowing the right tools and steps will make the process smooth. Always handle the parts with care to avoid damage or injury.

### Tools Needed

* Allen wrench (usually included with Ikea beds)
* Flathead screwdriver
* Phillips screwdriver
* Rubber mallet
* Ziplock bags for screws and small parts
* Marker or labels for parts

### Step-by-step Disassembly

1. Remove all bedding and mattress from the frame.
2. Unscrew the slats holding the mattress support.
3. Take off the side rails by loosening bolts with the Allen wrench.
4. Detach the headboard and footboard carefully.
5. Keep screws and bolts in labeled bags for reassembly.
6. Use the rubber mallet gently if parts are stuck.

### Handling Bed Components Safely

| Component | Handling Tips |
| --- | --- |
| Mattress | Fold or roll if possible, protect with cover |
| Wooden Slats | Stack flat, avoid bending |
| Side Rails | Lift carefully, watch for sharp edges |
| Headboard and Footboard | Carry with two people to avoid dropping |
| Screws and Bolts | Store in labeled bags to prevent loss |




## Packing And Securing Beds In Cars

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=moving-an-ikea-bedroom-bed-in-a-car-can-be-tricky.-you-need-to-pack-and-secure-the-parts-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Moving an Ikea bedroom bed in a car can be tricky. You need to pack and secure the parts well. This keeps the bed safe and your car clean.

Proper packing stops scratches and damage during transport. Careful securing prevents parts from shifting or breaking.

### Using Protective Covers

Cover each bed part with soft blankets or moving pads. This stops scratches and dents on the wood or metal.

Plastic wrap or bags protect smaller pieces from dirt and moisture. Use covers that fit well and do not slip off.

* Wrap bed frames in moving blankets
* Use bubble wrap for fragile parts
* Cover screws and small parts in plastic bags
* Secure covers tightly with tape or straps

### Securing Parts To Prevent Damage

Keep bed parts from moving by tying them down inside the car. Use strong ropes or ratchet straps.

Place heavy parts at the bottom. Stack lighter parts on top to avoid crushing. Keep screws and tools in a box.

* Use straps to hold bed frames in place
* Pack small parts in labeled containers
* Fill empty spaces with soft items to stop shifting
* Check straps often during travel

### Maximizing Space Efficiently

Disassemble the bed fully to save space. Remove slats, legs, and headboards to fit parts better.

Lay flat pieces on the car floor or fold seats down. Arrange parts by size to fill gaps and keep balance.

* Take apart bed frame completely
* Stack flat parts vertically or horizontally
* Use seat space for smaller items
* Keep weight evenly spread inside the car

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Ikea Beds Fit In A Standard Car Trunk?

Most Ikea bed frames come flat-packed and can fit in standard car trunks. However, larger beds like king size may require folding seats or roof racks.

### Do Ikea Bed Frames Require Special Transport Vehicles?

No special vehicles are needed. Flat-pack designs allow easy transport in sedans, hatchbacks, or SUVs with adequate space and seat folding options.

### How To Measure Car Space For Ikea Bed Transport?

Measure your car’s trunk and backseat dimensions before purchase. Compare these with the bed package size listed on Ikea’s website for a proper fit.

### Can Ikea Mattresses Fit Inside A Car?

Ikea mattresses vary in size but can typically fit in larger cars or SUVs. Smaller car models may need roof racks or backseat folding.
