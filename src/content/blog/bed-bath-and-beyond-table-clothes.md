### Are Bed Bath And Beyond Tablecloths Suitable For Outdoor Use?

### How Do I Choose The Right Size Tablecloth For My Table?



Measure your table’s length and width first. Add 6 to 12 inches on each side for the drop. Bed Bath and Beyond offers sizes from 54x80 inches to 70x120 inches. Choose a size that fits your table shape and desired overhang.



### Can Bed Bath And Beyond Tablecloths Be Reused And Washed?



Yes, many polyester and cotton tablecloths are reusable and machine washable. They resist wrinkles and stains, making cleaning easy. Plastic tablecloths are usually disposable but can be wiped clean for short-term reuse. Always check care instructions for best results.



### Are Bed Bath And Beyond Plastic Tablecloths Leakproof?
