## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Rug Is Best For A Dining Room Table?



Choose a rug at least 24 inches larger than your dining table on all sides. This ensures chairs stay on the rug when pulled out, preventing floor scratches and providing a balanced look.



### Are Washable Rugs Suitable For Dining Rooms?



Yes, washable rugs are ideal for dining rooms. They handle spills and stains easily, maintain cleanliness, and are low maintenance, making them perfect for high-traffic areas like dining spaces.



### How Do Non-slip Rugs Benefit Dining Areas?



Non-slip rugs prevent accidents by keeping the rug securely in place. This is essential around dining tables where chairs and foot traffic are frequent, enhancing safety and comfort.



### What Rug Material Is Best For Dining Rooms?



Low pile, stain-resistant materials like faux wool or cotton blends work best. They are durable, easy to clean, and resist wear from chairs and foot traffic.



### Can Vintage Print Rugs Suit Modern Dining Rooms?



Yes, vintage print rugs add character and warmth. They blend well with modern decor, offering a timeless and cozy atmosphere while hiding minor stains effectively.



### How To Maintain Stain-resistant Dining Room Rugs?



Regular vacuuming and spot cleaning keep rugs fresh. Use mild detergents for stains and wash washable rugs periodically to maintain their appearance and durability.
