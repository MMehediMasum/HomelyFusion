Shelves such as the KLM liquor bottle display or retro bar shelf stand help organize bottles while enhancing decor.

Mixing practical and decorative items creates a cozy, personalized bar area. Simple, well-chosen wall decor makes your home bar a great place to relax and entertain friends.
