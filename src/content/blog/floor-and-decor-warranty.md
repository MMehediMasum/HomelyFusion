## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Warranty Covers Valuelights Floor Lamps?



ValueLights floor lamps typically come with a limited manufacturer warranty. This covers defects in materials and workmanship for one year. It does not cover damage from misuse or normal wear. Always check specific product details for exact warranty terms before purchase.



### How Long Is The Warranty On Included Led Bulbs?



Included LED bulbs in these floor lamps usually have a separate warranty of 1 to 2 years. This protects against early bulb failure or defects. Warranty length may vary by brand, so review the product manual or packaging for exact details.



### Can I Claim Warranty For Adjustable Lamp Parts?



Yes, adjustable parts on these floor lamps are generally covered under the warranty. Coverage includes defects causing malfunction or breakage within the warranty period. Normal wear or accidental damage is not included. Contact customer service for claims process.



### Does Warranty Cover Shipping Costs For Repairs?



Typically, warranties cover product repair or replacement but exclude shipping costs. Customers often bear shipping fees when sending lamps for warranty service. Check with the retailer or manufacturer for detailed warranty service policies.



### How To Register My Floor Lamp Warranty?



Warranty registration usually requires filling out a form online or mailing in a card. This activates your warranty and simplifies future claims. Keep purchase receipts and registration confirmation for proof of warranty coverage. Visit the brand’s website for registration instructions.
