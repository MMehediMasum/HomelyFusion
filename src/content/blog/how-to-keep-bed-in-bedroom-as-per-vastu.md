## Bed Positioning Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=positioning-your-bed-correctly-is-important-in-vastu-shastra.-it-helps-bring-peace-and-good-energy-to-the-bedroom.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Positioning your bed correctly is important in Vastu Shastra. It helps bring peace and good energy to the bedroom. Simple changes can improve rest and harmony.

Follow these tips to place your bed in the best spot according to Vastu. This guide focuses on bed distance from walls, door alignment, and space under the bed.

### Distance From Walls

Keep some space between your bed and the walls. This allows energy to flow freely around the bed. Avoid pushing the bed too close to the wall.

Leaving a small gap helps maintain good air circulation. It also creates a feeling of openness and comfort in the room.

* Leave at least 6 inches of space on both sides
* Allow space at the head of the bed for support
* Do not block windows or vents with the bed

### Avoiding Direct Alignment With Doors

Do not place the bed directly in line with the bedroom door. This can cause energy to rush over the bed, disturbing sleep.

Instead, position the bed so it faces a wall or corner. This placement feels safer and more stable.

* Keep the bed away from the door’s direct path
* Use a partition or furniture to block direct view if needed
* Ensure clear and easy access to the bed without crossing the door line

### Keeping Space Under The Bed

Do not store clutter under the bed. Open space below helps energy flow better and keeps the bedroom fresh.

Empty space under the bed also supports good sleep and health. Keep it clean and free from heavy items.

* Use bed frames that raise the bed slightly above the floor
* Avoid storing boxes or shoes under the bed
* Clean the area under the bed regularly
