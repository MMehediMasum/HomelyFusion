## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Ottomans With Storage?



Ottomans with storage offer dual functionality. They provide comfortable seating and hidden space for blankets, toys, or documents. This maximizes room organization while enhancing décor. Storage ottomans like the Signature Design by Ashley Altari keep living spaces tidy and stylish effortlessly.



### How To Choose The Right Ottoman Size For Living Rooms?



Select an ottoman size based on your room space and furniture layout. Large ottomans like the 24KF Linen Ottoman work well as coffee tables. Smaller options like the Amazon Basics Foot Rest fit cozy corners. Measure your area to ensure comfortable movement around the ottoman.



### Are Foldable Ottomans Durable And Practical?



Yes, foldable ottomans like the DUMOS 30in Foldable Storage Ottoman are both durable and versatile. They support up to 350 lbs and save space when folded. Ideal for small rooms, they offer storage and seating without permanent bulkiness.



### Can Ottomans Serve As Coffee Tables?



Absolutely. Large, flat-topped ottomans like the 24KF Upholstered Ottoman serve as coffee tables. They provide a sturdy surface for drinks and décor. Plus, storage ottomans add functionality by hiding clutter beneath the tabletop.



### What Materials Are Common In Luxury Ottomans?



Luxury ottomans often use faux leather, linen, and tufted upholstery. Faux leather ottomans like the Signature Design by Ashley offer elegance and easy maintenance. Linen fabric options provide a soft, textured look. Quality materials ensure durability and comfort.



### Are Ottomans With Safety Hinges Safe For Families?



Yes, ottomans with safety hinges, such as the Huatean Home Ottoman, prevent accidental lid slams. This feature protects fingers and enhances child safety. They are ideal for family homes needing secure, functional storage seating.
