## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Fabric For Couch Pillows?



Velvet and chenille are excellent choices for couch pillows. They offer a luxurious feel and are durable. Polyester options, like those from Utopia Bedding, are also popular for their easy maintenance and affordability. Choose fabric based on your style and comfort preferences.



### How Do I Clean Throw Pillow Inserts?



Most throw pillow inserts, like those from EDOW, are machine washable. Use a gentle cycle with mild detergent. Ensure they are completely dry before use to prevent mold. Always check the care label for specific washing instructions to maintain their shape and longevity.



### Can Throw Pillow Covers Be Mixed And Matched?



Yes, mixing and matching throw pillow covers is a great way to enhance decor. Consider sets like MIULEE and Topfinel for versatile designs. Choose complementary colors and patterns to create a cohesive look. Mixing textures adds depth and interest to any space.



### What Size Throw Pillows Are Ideal For Couches?



The 18x18 inch size is popular for couches, offering balanced support and style. Utopia Bedding and MIULEE provide options in this size. For a bolder look, consider mixing with larger sizes like 20x20 inches. Adjust based on couch size and desired aesthetic.



### Are Down Alternative Pillows Comfortable?



Yes, down alternative pillows, like those from EDOW, offer great comfort. They mimic the softness of down without allergens. These pillows are lightweight and provide good support, making them ideal for everyday use. They're a practical choice for those seeking comfort and hypoallergenic options.
