## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Common In Round Home Goods Rugs?



Round home goods rugs often use chenille, jute, synthetic fibers, and plush shaggy materials. These offer durability and style. Natural fibers like jute add a rustic touch, while chenille and synthetic options provide softness and easy maintenance for various rooms.



### Are These Round Rugs Machine Washable?



Many round rugs like GENIMO and Lahome collections are machine washable. This feature ensures easy cleaning and maintenance. Machine washable rugs are ideal for homes with kids and pets, providing convenience without compromising on style and comfort.



### Which Rooms Suit Round Rugs Best?



Round rugs fit well in living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, and entryways. Their shape adds softness to angular furniture layouts. They work great under dining tables, in nurseries, or as accent pieces in hallways, enhancing any room’s decor.



### How Do I Choose The Right Rug Size?



Choose a rug size that complements your room and furniture scale. For example, 4 to 5 feet round rugs work well in small to medium spaces. Ensure the rug fits under key furniture pieces or defines the area without overcrowding.



### Are Non-slip Features Important In Round Rugs?



Yes, non-slip backing is crucial for safety and stability. Rugs like GENIMO and Lahome offer non-slip features to prevent slipping. This is especially important in high-traffic areas, kitchens, and homes with children or elderly residents.



### Can Round Rugs Enhance Boho Or Modern Decor?



Absolutely. Round rugs with bohemian patterns or modern designs add character and warmth. They create focal points and blend well with various styles, from rustic farmhouse to contemporary aesthetics, elevating your home’s overall look.
