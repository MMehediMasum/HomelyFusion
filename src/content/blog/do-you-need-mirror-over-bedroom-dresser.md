## Benefits Of A Mirror Above Bedroom Dresser

Adding a mirror above your bedroom dresser can change the feel of your room. It offers both style and practical uses.

Mirrors do more than reflect your image. They can improve light, space, and your daily routine.

### Enhancing Room Spaciousness

A mirror makes a room feel bigger. It reflects walls and furniture to create depth.

This effect works well in small bedrooms. It tricks your eyes into seeing more space than there really is.

### Improving Lighting And Brightness

Mirrors reflect light from windows and lamps. This helps brighten the whole room.

Placing a mirror above the dresser near a light source spreads light evenly. It reduces dark corners and shadows.

### Practicality For Daily Use

A mirror above the dresser is handy for daily tasks. You can check your outfit or fix your hair quickly.

This placement saves space and keeps your bedroom neat. It combines storage and grooming in one spot.

## Placement Tips For Bedroom Mirrors

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adding-a-mirror-over-your-bedroom-dresser-can-improve-both-style-and-function.-the-right-placement-helps-you-use-the-mirror-easily-and-makes-your-room-look-bigger.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adding a mirror over your bedroom dresser can improve both style and function. The right placement helps you use the mirror easily and makes your room look bigger.

Here are some tips to place your mirror perfectly over the dresser. These tips help avoid common mistakes and keep your space balanced.

### Optimal Height And Position

Place the mirror so you can see your face and upper body clearly. The center of the mirror should be near your eye level.

Keep the mirror close enough to the dresser for easy use. It should not be too high or too low for comfortable viewing.

* Hang the mirror 5 to 10 inches above the dresser surface
* Center the mirror horizontally over the dresser
* Make sure lighting near the mirror is bright but not glaring

### Balancing With Other Furniture

Balance the mirror size with the dresser and nearby furniture. A mirror too big or too small can look odd.

Match the mirror style with other bedroom pieces. This keeps the room feeling calm and organized.

* Choose mirror width close to the dresser width
* Keep space between mirror edges and wall corners equal
* Keep nearby furniture heights in harmony with the mirror

### Avoiding Common Placement Mistakes

Do not hang the mirror too high or too low. It will make it hard to use and look unbalanced.

Avoid placing the mirror where it reflects clutter or bad lighting. This can make your room look messy.

* Do not block the mirror with furniture or decorations
* Avoid direct sunlight on the mirror to reduce glare
* Do not hang a mirror too close to a door or window
