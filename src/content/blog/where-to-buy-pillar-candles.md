## Factors To Consider When Buying

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-pillar-candles-takes-some-thought.-various-factors-affect-how-well-the-candle-fits-your-needs.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right pillar candles takes some thought. Various factors affect how well the candle fits your needs. Consider these points to make a smart purchase. This helps ensure your candle looks good and burns well.

### Wax Type

Wax type affects burn time and scent. Common waxes include paraffin, soy, and beeswax. Soy and beeswax burn cleaner than paraffin. Pick a wax that suits your budget and preferences.

### Size And Shape

Size matters for both use and style. Taller candles last longer but need more space. Shorter ones burn faster and fit small areas. Pillar candles come in round, square, or unique shapes. Choose a shape that matches your decor.

### Scented Vs Unscented

Scented candles add fragrance to a room. Unscented ones are better for sensitive noses or events. Consider where you will use the candle. Select scented for ambiance or unscented for simplicity.

### Price Range

Candles come in many prices. Higher cost often means better quality and longer burn. Set a budget before buying. Find a candle that fits your price without sacrificing quality.




## Tips For Choosing Quality Pillar Candles

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-quality-pillar-candles-can-make-a-big-difference-in-your-home-decor-and-ambiance.-good-candles-burn-longer-and-cleaner.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing quality pillar candles can make a big difference in your home decor and ambiance. Good candles burn longer and cleaner. They provide a steady, beautiful light. Knowing what to check helps you pick the best ones. Here are some tips to help you choose quality pillar candles.

### Check Burn Time

Burn time tells how long a candle will last. A longer burn time means better value. Look for burn time on the packaging. It shows the candle’s quality and size. Avoid candles with very short burn times. They might burn unevenly or waste wax.

### Look For Even Wax

Even wax means the candle is made well. Check if the wax is smooth and solid. Avoid candles with cracks or air bubbles. These flaws cause uneven burning. Even wax helps the candle burn safely and steadily. It also looks nicer on display.

### Assess Wick Quality

The wick controls the candle’s flame. A good wick is straight and thick enough for the candle size. Poor wick quality causes flickering or smoke. Check if the wick is centered in the candle. A centered wick burns evenly and reduces wax waste.
