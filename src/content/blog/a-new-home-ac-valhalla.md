## Choosing The Perfect Settlement

In Assassin's Creed Valhalla, picking the right settlement is key. Your choice affects your gameplay and story.

Settlements offer places to rest, trade, and grow your clan. Choose carefully to enjoy the best experience.

### Key Locations To Consider

Some locations stand out for their resources and position. Look for places near rivers, forests, or hills.

Popular spots include riverbanks for fishing and trade, or forest edges for wood and hunting.

* Riverbanks: Good for fishing and transport
* Forests: Provide wood and animals
* Hills: Offer defense and visibility
* Near villages: Easy access to trade and allies

### Strategic Advantages Of Each Site

Each settlement site gives unique benefits. Choose a place that fits your play style and goals.

Sites with natural defenses keep enemies away. Flat lands help build bigger structures fast.

* Hilltop sites: Better defense against attacks
* Flat lands: Easier to build and expand
* Near water: Access to fishing and travel routes
* Close to enemy camps: Opportunities for raids

### Balancing Resources And Safety

Settlements need food, wood, and stone for growth. Balance these with safety from enemies.

Choose places with nearby resources but low enemy threat. Secure your settlement with walls and guards.

* Check for nearby forests and quarries
* Ensure easy access to food sources
* Avoid spots too close to hostile forces
* Invest in defenses to protect your clan
