**Protecting wood floors from scratches and dents is essential for lasting beauty. Furniture pads provide a simple, effective solution to keep floors safe.**



Wood floors can get damaged by moving or sliding furniture. Felt furniture pads act as a cushion between furniture legs and the floor. They reduce noise and prevent marks, dents, and scratches. Many pads come in different sizes and shapes to fit chairs, tables, and sofas.



Some pads stick on, while others nail or screw in place for extra security. Choosing the right furniture pads helps maintain your floor’s smooth surface and extends its life. This guide covers the best furniture pads for wood floors, including popular felt pads and heavy-duty sliders. Protect your investment with easy-to-use floor protectors that work well on hardwood and laminate floors.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Felt Pads For Hardwood Floors?



Scotch Felt Pads and X-PROTECTOR Felt Pads are top choices. They offer self-stick designs and protect wood floors from scratches, dents, and nicks effectively. Their assorted sizes suit various furniture legs for optimal coverage and floor protection.



### How Do Furniture Pads Prevent Floor Damage?



Furniture pads create a soft barrier between furniture and floors. They reduce friction and prevent scratches, dents, and scuffs. Felt pads especially absorb pressure and allow smooth movement, protecting hardwood, laminate, and tile surfaces from damage.



### Are Self-adhesive Pads Or Nail-on Pads Better?



Self-adhesive pads are easy to apply and remove, ideal for light to medium furniture. Nail-on pads offer stronger attachment and durability, perfect for heavy furniture or frequent movement. Choose based on furniture weight and usage frequency for best floor protection.



### Can Felt Pads Reduce Noise From Moving Furniture?



Yes, felt pads significantly reduce noise when sliding furniture. They cushion contact points and minimize scraping sounds on hardwood and laminate floors. Products like SuperSliders and Scotch Felt Pads are designed to lower noise while protecting floors.



### What Sizes Of Furniture Pads Fit Different Furniture Legs?



Felt pads come in various round and square sizes, ranging from 1-inch to 5-inch diameters. Assorted size packs like Scotch Felt Pads accommodate different furniture leg widths, ensuring full coverage and better floor protection.



### How Often Should I Replace Furniture Pads On Wood Floors?



Replace pads every 6 to 12 months or when worn out. Frequent furniture movement or heavy weight may require earlier replacement. Regular inspection ensures maximum protection against scratches and dents on hardwood floors.
