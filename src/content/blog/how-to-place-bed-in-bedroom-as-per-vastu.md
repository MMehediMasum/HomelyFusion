## Bed Positioning Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=placing-your-bed-in-the-right-position-can-improve-rest-and-energy.-vastu-shastra-gives-guidelines-to-help-you-decide-where-to-put-your-bed.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Placing your bed in the right position can improve rest and energy. Vastu Shastra gives guidelines to help you decide where to put your bed.

Follow these tips to arrange your bed for better sleep and positive vibes.

### Head Placement Guidelines

Your head should face a direction that supports peace and health. The best directions are south or east.

Avoid placing your head towards north. This can cause discomfort and poor sleep.

* Face south for better sleep quality
* Face east to improve energy and focus
* Do not face north while sleeping

### Distance From Walls

Keep some space between your bed and the walls. It allows air flow and keeps energy moving well.

Do not push the bed directly against the wall on both sides. One side can touch the wall if space is limited.

* Leave at least 1 foot space on one side
* Allow air to circulate around the bed
* Keep the bed away from damp or cracked walls

### Aligning With Door And Window

Place the bed so it is not directly in line with the door. This prevents energy from rushing over you while you sleep.

Avoid placing the bed under a window. This can cause disturbed sleep and reduce privacy.

* Keep the bed away from the door’s direct path
* Do not place the bed under windows
* Choose a wall opposite or adjacent to the door

## Material And Design Choices

Placing your bed according to Vastu can bring peace and positive energy. The materials and design of your bed also affect the room’s energy.

### Choosing Bed Frame Material

Wood is the best bed frame material for Vastu. It connects with natural energy and feels warm.

Avoid metal beds as they may bring cold or harsh energy. Solid wood frames are better than particle board.

* Teak, rosewood, and pine are good wood options
* Wood frames should be smooth and free of cracks
* Wood brings stability and calmness to the bedroom
* Metal frames can block energy flow and cause restlessness

### Colors That Enhance Harmony

Colors influence your mood and room energy. Use soft and soothing colors near your bed.

Pastel shades and earth tones support peace and relaxation in the bedroom.

* Light blue promotes calmness and better sleep
* Soft green brings freshness and balance
* Beige and cream add warmth and comfort
* Avoid dark or bright colors like red or black near the bed

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=placing-your-bed-wrongly-can-disturb-the-energy-in-your-bedroom.-vastu-gives-simple-rules-to-keep-peace-and-good-sleep.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Placing your bed wrongly can disturb the energy in your bedroom. Vastu gives simple rules to keep peace and good sleep.

Avoid these common mistakes for better harmony and rest in your room.

### Clutter Around Bed

Having clutter around your bed blocks positive energy flow. It can cause stress and restless sleep.

Keep the space near and under your bed clean and clear. Avoid storing items like shoes or boxes there.

* Do not place piles of clothes near the bed
* Keep nightstands neat and organized
* Clear under-bed storage if possible

### Positioning Under Beams

Placing your bed under a ceiling beam creates pressure and discomfort. It disturbs your sleep and health.

Choose a spot away from beams. If not possible, cover the beam with a cloth or false ceiling to reduce impact.

* Do not sleep directly under a beam
* Use false ceilings to hide beams
* Shift bed to a safer area if possible

### Bed Facing Toilet Or Kitchen

Sleeping with the bed facing the toilet or kitchen is against Vastu. It brings negative energy and health issues.

Place your bed so it faces a calm wall or window. Avoid direct view of toilets or kitchen from the bed.

* Do not face the bed towards the toilet door
* Avoid kitchen view from the bed
* Use room dividers if room layout is fixed
