## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Soy Candles Non-toxic And Safe For Home Use?



Soy candles are made from natural soy wax, which burns cleaner than paraffin. They emit fewer toxins and pollutants, making them safer for indoor air quality. Non-toxic soy candles often include natural fragrances and essential oils, reducing harmful chemicals in your home environment.



### How Long Do These Non-toxic Soy Candles Typically Burn?



Most non-toxic soy candles, like Chesapeake Bay and Nika's Home, burn between 50 to 110 hours. Burn time varies by candle size and wick type. Longer burn times offer better value and prolonged fragrance for your home.



### Are These Candles Suitable For Aromatherapy And Stress Relief?



Yes, many candles listed, such as ARCASA Organic Lavender and Putart Aromatherapy candles, contain essential oils. These scents support relaxation, meditation, and stress relief naturally. They are ideal for creating a calming home atmosphere.



### Can I Use These Soy Candles Safely Around Pets And Children?



Soy candles are safer than paraffin but still require caution. Avoid placing candles where pets or children can reach them. Always supervise burning candles to prevent accidents and exposure to any smoke or fragrance oils.



### What Scents Are Popular In Non-toxic Soy Candles For Home Use?



Popular scents include lavender, vanilla, eucalyptus, citrus, and sandalwood. These fragrances come from natural essential oils, enhancing mood and air quality. Brands like AOOVOO and Lulu Candles offer diverse scent options for every preference.



### How Do I Maximize The Burn Quality Of Soy Wax Candles?



Trim the wick to ¼ inch before lighting to prevent soot. Burn candles for at least 2 hours to create an even wax pool. Keep candles away from drafts for a steady flame and longer burn time.
