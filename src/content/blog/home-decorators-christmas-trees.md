### What Are The Best Pre-lit Christmas Trees?



Pre-lit trees are convenient and beautiful. Top choices include National Tree Company's Dunhill Fir and Carolina Pine. These trees offer realistic foliage and come with either dual-color or white LED lights. They are easy to assemble and come with sturdy stands for stability.



### How Do Dual-color Led Lights Work?



Dual-color LED lights offer versatility. They can switch between warm white and multicolor settings. This allows you to customize your tree's appearance for different moods or occasions. National Tree Company offers several models featuring these adaptable lights, enhancing your holiday décor.



### Are "feel Real" Trees Truly Realistic?



"Feel Real" trees use PE branch tips for a lifelike appearance. National Tree Company's Norwegian Spruce and Nordic Spruce are great examples. These trees mimic the texture and color of real needles, offering an authentic look. They are perfect for those seeking a natural feel without the hassle.



### How Do I Store Artificial Christmas Trees?



Proper storage is key for longevity. Many trees, like Papyrus's Blue Spruce, come with a storage bag. Disassemble the tree carefully and place in the bag. Store in a cool, dry place. This prevents damage and ensures your tree looks great year after year.



### What Sizes Are Available For Artificial Trees?



Artificial trees come in various sizes to fit any space. Options range from 6 feet to 9 feet tall. National Tree Company and Best Choice Products offer a wide range. Choose a size based on your room's ceiling height and desired visual impact.
