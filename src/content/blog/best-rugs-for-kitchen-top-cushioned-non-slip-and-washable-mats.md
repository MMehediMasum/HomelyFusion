## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Materials For Kitchen Rugs?



The best kitchen rugs use materials like polypropylene, chenille, and cushioned foam. These offer durability, stain resistance, and comfort. Non-slip backing ensures safety on kitchen floors. Materials also need to be washable for easy cleaning and maintenance.



### How Do Anti-fatigue Kitchen Mats Help?



Anti-fatigue mats reduce leg and foot fatigue during long standing periods. They provide cushioning and support, improving comfort. These mats help prevent soreness and improve posture while cooking or washing dishes. Ideal for busy kitchens where standing is frequent.



### Are Non-slip Kitchen Rugs Safe For All Floors?



Yes, non-slip kitchen rugs have rubber or latex backing that prevents slipping. They work well on tile, hardwood, and laminate floors. This feature reduces accidents and keeps rugs in place. Always check compatibility with your specific floor type.



### Can Kitchen Rugs Be Machine Washed?



Many kitchen rugs, like Artoid Mode and HEBE sets, are machine washable. This feature helps maintain hygiene and appearance. Washing removes stains, dirt, and odors effectively. Always follow manufacturer instructions for best results.



### What Sizes Are Common For Kitchen Rug Sets?



Kitchen rug sets often include pieces sized around 17"x29", 17"x47", and runners up to 48"x20". These sizes fit well in front of sinks, counters, and along kitchen walkways. Sets provide coordinated coverage for multiple kitchen areas.



### How Do Cushioned Kitchen Mats Improve Kitchen Comfort?



Cushioned kitchen mats offer soft, supportive surfaces that reduce stress on feet. They absorb shock and prevent fatigue during cooking or cleaning tasks. These mats enhance comfort, making kitchen work easier and more enjoyable.
