## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In Home Goods Trays?



Home goods trays often use bamboo, wood, metal, rattan, and resin. These materials offer durability and aesthetic appeal. Bamboo and wood provide a natural look, while metal handles add strength. Rattan and resin trays add unique decorative touches for versatile home use.



### How Do Folding Legs Benefit Bed Table Trays?



Folding legs make bed table trays portable and easy to store. They offer stability while eating or working in bed. This feature enhances convenience by allowing quick setup and compact storage, perfect for small spaces or frequent use.



### Which Tray Size Suits Coffee Tables Best?



Coffee table trays typically range from 11 to 17 inches square or round. Larger trays hold multiple items like candles and decor. Smaller trays keep essentials organized without cluttering the table surface. Choose size based on table dimensions and usage needs.



### Can Decorative Trays Double As Serving Platters?



Yes, many decorative trays are designed for serving food and drinks. They combine style and function, ideal for entertaining or daily use. Materials like wood and metal handles make them sturdy enough to carry snacks or beverages.



### How To Choose Trays For Bathroom And Vanity Decor?



Choose trays that resist moisture and complement bathroom aesthetics. Wood with protective finishes or resin trays work well. Select sizes that fit vanity counters and organize jewelry or cosmetics neatly. Decorative trays add elegance and keep essentials accessible.



### Are Rattan Trays Suitable For Kitchen Island Decor?



Rattan trays are excellent for kitchen island decor due to their natural texture. They are lightweight and easy to clean. These trays add warmth and rustic charm while serving fruits, bread, or drinks stylishly.
