## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Couch Protector For Cats?



The best couch protector for cats is durable, easy to apply, and scratch-resistant. Products like FixZilla and Secopad offer heavy-duty protection and self-adhesive features to shield sofas effectively from cat claws.



### How Do Adhesive Cat Scratch Protectors Work?



Adhesive cat scratch protectors stick to furniture surfaces, creating a barrier. They prevent cats from damaging sofas by deterring scratching with a tough, non-slip texture or sticky surface cats dislike.



### Are Couch Protectors Safe For Cats And Furniture?



Yes, these protectors are non-toxic and safe for cats. They protect furniture without harming pets, ensuring sofas remain scratch-free and cats stay comfortable and secure.



### Can Couch Protectors Be Reused Or Repositioned?



Most protectors like Secopad and Panther Armor are designed for easy application. Some can be repositioned if cleaned properly, but adhesive strength may reduce after multiple moves.



### Do Couch Protectors Require Additional Installation Tools?



Many protectors come with twist pins or adhesive backing, requiring minimal tools. Pins help secure protectors on corners, while adhesive tapes stick directly to furniture surfaces without extra hardware.



### How Long Do Cat Couch Protectors Typically Last?



Cat couch protectors last from several months to a year depending on material and usage. Heavy-duty options like FixZilla are more durable, offering long-term scratch resistance for active cats.
