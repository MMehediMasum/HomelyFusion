### Are Rifle Paper Rugs Suitable For Indoor And Outdoor Use?



Yes, some Rifle Paper Rugs, like the Perennial Collection, are designed for both indoor and outdoor use. These rugs resist fading and moisture, making them versatile and practical for patios, living rooms, or entryways.



### What Sizes Are Available For Loloi Rifle Paper Co. Rugs?



Loloi Rifle Paper Co. rugs come in various sizes, ranging from small accent rugs (around 3'6" x 5'6") to large area rugs (up to 7'6" x 9'6"). This variety fits different room sizes and décor needs.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain Rifle Paper Co. Rugs?



Cleaning is easy: vacuum regularly and spot clean with mild detergent. Avoid harsh chemicals or soaking. For outdoor rugs, rinse with water occasionally to remove dirt and debris, keeping colors fresh and fibers intact.



### What Design Styles Do Rifle Paper Rugs Offer?



Rifle Paper Rugs feature floral, botanical, and nature-inspired patterns. Collections like Maison, Eden, and Canopy showcase vibrant, elegant designs blending classic and modern aesthetics, perfect for adding charm to any space.



### Are Rifle Paper Rugs Durable For High-traffic Areas?



Yes, these rugs are crafted for durability with dense pile and sturdy fibers. They withstand daily wear in living rooms, hallways, and kitchens while maintaining their color and texture over time.
