## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Softbox Size For Photoshoots?



A 16'' x 16'' softbox is ideal for controlled, even lighting. Larger sizes like 27" x 27" offer softer light and wider coverage. Choose based on your shoot’s scale and desired light softness.



### How To Adjust Color Temperature In Led Studio Lights?



Most kits offer adjustable color temperature between 2700K-7500K. Use warmer tones (2700K-3500K) for cozy scenes, cooler (5500K-6500K) for daylight effect. Adjust via remote or app for precise lighting control.



### Are Led Bulbs Better Than Traditional Bulbs For Photography?



Yes, LED bulbs are energy-efficient, produce less heat, and offer adjustable color temperature. They provide consistent, flicker-free light, perfect for video and photo shoots.



### Can Softbox Kits Be Used For Live Streaming?



Absolutely. Softbox kits provide soft, even lighting, reducing shadows and glare. They enhance video quality, making them perfect for live streaming and video recording.



### What Tripod Height Is Suitable For Softbox Lighting?



Adjustable tripods ranging from 50 to 63 inches are ideal. They offer flexibility to position lights at eye level or above for flattering illumination. Choose a sturdy tripod for stability.



### How Important Is Cri In Photography Lighting Kits?



CRI (Color Rendering Index) above 90 ensures colors appear true and natural. High CRI lighting, like 97 CRI bulbs, improves photo accuracy and color fidelity in shoots.
