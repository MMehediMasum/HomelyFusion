## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Throw Pillow Inserts?



Throw pillow inserts are the inner cushions that fill decorative pillow covers. They provide shape and support to pillows. Inserts come in various sizes and materials, such as polyester or down alternative. They're essential for enhancing comfort and style in your home decor.



### How To Choose The Right Pillow Size?



To choose the right pillow size, consider the dimensions of your pillow cover. Ensure the insert is slightly larger for a fuller look. Standard sizes include 16x16, 18x18, and 20x20 inches. Matching the insert size with your decor needs ensures optimal comfort and aesthetics.



### Can Throw Pillow Inserts Be Washed?



Yes, many throw pillow inserts are machine washable. Check the care label for specific washing instructions. Use gentle cycles and mild detergent. After washing, ensure inserts are thoroughly dried to maintain their shape and longevity. Proper care extends the life of your pillow inserts.



### What Materials Are Best For Pillow Inserts?



Polyester and down alternative are popular materials for pillow inserts. Polyester is affordable and hypoallergenic. Down alternative offers a soft, luxurious feel. Both options provide good support and are easy to maintain. Choose based on personal preferences and any allergy considerations.



### How Do Decorative Pillow Covers Enhance Decor?



Decorative pillow covers add color, texture, and style to any room. They allow easy updates to match seasonal themes or trends. Covers are available in various materials like velvet, faux fur, and linen. Mixing designs can create a cohesive, inviting atmosphere in your living space.
