To start using your Google Home Mini speaker, you need to connect it to power. This process is simple but important for the device to work properly.

Follow the instructions below to choose the right outlet and plug in your speaker safely.

### Choosing The Right Outlet

Pick an outlet that is near your usual listening area. Avoid outlets that are hard to reach or behind furniture. Make sure the outlet provides a steady power supply without frequent interruptions.

* Use a standard 110-120V outlet for North America or 220-240V for other regions.
* Do not use outlets that are loose or damaged.
* Avoid outlets controlled by a switch that might turn off accidentally.
* Keep the area around the outlet dry and clean.

### Plugging In The Speaker

Use the power adapter that came with your Google Home Mini. Connect the small end of the cable to the speaker’s power port. Then, plug the adapter into the chosen outlet.

## Setting Up The Speaker

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-your-google-home-mini-speaker-is-easy.-this-guide-will-help-you-connect-it-to-your-phone-and-wi-fi.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up your Google Home Mini speaker is easy. This guide will help you connect it to your phone and Wi-Fi.

Follow the steps below to get your speaker ready for use in just a few minutes.

### Launching The App

Start by downloading the Google Home app on your phone. It works on both Android and iOS devices.

Open the app after installing it to begin setting up your speaker.

### Adding A New Device

In the Google Home app, tap the plus (+) icon to add a new device. This lets the app search for your Google Home Mini.

Select “Set up device” and then choose “New devices” in your home. The app will find the speaker nearby.

* Make sure your phone’s Bluetooth is on
* Keep the speaker plugged in and powered on
* Wait for the app to detect the device

### Connecting To Wi-fi

After the device is found, the app will ask you to choose a Wi-Fi network. Connect the speaker to your home Wi-Fi.

Enter your Wi-Fi password if needed. The speaker will use this connection to work properly.

* Choose the same Wi-Fi network your phone uses
* Enter the correct Wi-Fi password
* Wait for the confirmation that the speaker is online

Setting up your Google Home Mini includes customizing how it understands and responds to you. Personalizing voice settings makes your experience smoother and more accurate.

### Voice Match Setup

Voice Match lets Google Home recognize your voice and give personalized answers. You must train it by repeating certain phrases.

1. Open the Google Home app on your phone.
2. Tap your profile icon and select Voice Match.
3. Follow the prompts to say phrases aloud.
4. Finish setup to let the device recognize your voice.

After setup, Google Home Mini will respond only to recognized voices, protecting your privacy.

* Open the Google Home app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Connect Google Home Mini To Wi-fi?

To connect Google Home Mini to Wi-Fi, open the Google Home app. Follow the setup prompts, select your Wi-Fi network, and enter the password. Ensure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi for smooth setup.

### What App Is Needed To Set Up Google Home Mini?

The Google Home app is required to set up your Google Home Mini. It is available for free on both iOS and Android devices. The app guides you through device setup and customization.

### How To Reset Google Home Mini To Factory Settings?

To reset Google Home Mini, press and hold the factory reset button under the device for about 15 seconds. Release when you hear a confirmation sound. This erases all settings, allowing you to start fresh.

### Can I Use Google Home Mini Without A Google Account?

No, a Google account is necessary to use Google Home Mini. It enables personalized responses and access to Google services. You must sign in during the setup process.
