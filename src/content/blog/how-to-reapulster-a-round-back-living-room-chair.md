**Is your round back living room chair looking tired or worn out? Reupholstering it can give your favorite seat a fresh new look without the cost of buying a new one.**

You might think this is a big job, but with the right steps, you can do it yourself and feel proud of the result. In this guide, you’ll discover simple tips and tricks to transform your chair, making it comfortable and stylish once again.

Ready to bring your chair back to life? Keep reading, and you’ll learn exactly how to do it.



## Materials Needed

Reupholstering a round back living room chair takes some basic materials. These items help you cover the chair neatly and securely.

Choosing the right fabric and tools makes the job easier and the results better. Here are the key materials you will need.

### Fabric Choices

Pick a fabric that fits your room style and is strong enough for a chair. Upholstery fabric is best because it lasts longer.

* Cotton canvas – durable and easy to clean
* Linen – smooth texture but may wrinkle
* Velvet – soft and elegant, needs careful handling
* Leather or faux leather – strong and classic look
* Polyester blends – stain resistant and budget-friendly

### Tools Required

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Tool | Purpose |
| Staple gun | Attach fabric to chair frame |
| Scissors | Cut fabric and batting |
| Screwdriver | Remove and reattach chair parts |
| Pliers | Pull out old staples and nails |
| Measuring tape | Measure fabric dimensions |
| Needle and strong thread | Sew fabric pieces if needed |

### Additional Supplies

Besides fabric and tools, you will need some extra items for a smooth reupholstery process.

* Upholstery foam or batting for padding
* Fabric glue for small fixes
* Chalk or fabric marker to mark cutting lines
* Dust mask to protect from old dust and fibers
* Work gloves to protect your hands




## Preparing The Chair

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=reupholstering-a-round-back-living-room-chair-starts-with-good-preparation.-this-process-helps-the-final-work-look-neat-and-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Reupholstering a round back living room chair starts with good preparation. This process helps the final work look neat and last longer.

Preparing involves removing old fabric, checking the chair’s frame and padding, and cleaning or repairing any damage.

### Removing Old Upholstery

Carefully strip away the old fabric and staples. Use a staple remover or pliers to pull out fasteners. Save the old fabric pieces to use as patterns for new cuts.

### Checking Frame And Padding

Inspect the chair’s frame for cracks or loose joints. Test the stability by gently pressing and shaking the chair. Check padding for flat or damaged spots that need replacing.

1. Look for broken wood or loose screws
2. Check springs and webbing for wear
3. Press padding to find soft or hard areas
4. Note any parts needing repair or replacement

### Cleaning And Repairing

Clean the frame and remove dirt or dust. Use wood glue to fix small cracks and clamps to hold parts while drying. Replace or fix broken springs or padding as needed.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task | Materials Needed | Tips |
| Cleaning frame | Soft cloth, mild cleaner | Wipe gently to avoid damage |
| Repairing cracks | Wood glue, clamps | Clamp firmly and let dry 24 hours |
| Replacing padding | New foam, batting | Cut to fit original shape |

## Measuring And Cutting Fabric

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=reupholstering-a-round-back-living-room-chair-needs-careful-measuring-and-cutting.-this-helps-the-fabric-fit-perfectly-and-look-neat.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Reupholstering a round back living room chair needs careful measuring and cutting. This helps the fabric fit perfectly and look neat.

Follow these steps to take accurate measurements, create a pattern, and cut your fabric pieces correctly.

### Taking Accurate Measurements

Start by measuring the chair’s back, seat, and arms carefully. Use a flexible tape measure for curves and rounded edges.

* Measure the height and width of the chair back.
* Measure the depth and width of the seat.
* Measure the length and width of the arms, if covered.
* Note any curves or unique shapes on the chair.
* Write down every measurement clearly.

### Creating A Pattern

Use your measurements to draw a paper pattern. This helps you cut the fabric exactly to size.

**Pattern tips:**

* Use kraft paper or newspaper to draw your pattern.
* Mark seam allowances of about 1/2 inch on all edges.
* Label each pattern piece with its part name and measurements.
* Cut out the paper pattern before placing it on the fabric.

### Cutting Fabric Pieces

Lay your fabric on a flat surface. Place the paper pattern on top and pin it in place. Cut the fabric carefully along the edges.

| Fabric Piece | Cutting Tips | Seam Allowance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chair Back | Follow curve gently, cut smoothly | 1/2 inch |
| Seat | Cut straight edges carefully | 1/2 inch |
| Arm Covers | Cut to match arm shape exactly | 1/2 inch |




## Attaching The Fabric

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=attaching-fabric-to-a-round-back-living-room-chair-requires-care-and-patience.-this-guide-focuses-on-how-to-attach-the-fabric-tightly-and-smoothly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Attaching fabric to a round back living room chair requires care and patience. This guide focuses on how to attach the fabric tightly and smoothly.

Follow each part carefully to get a neat and long-lasting finish on your chair upholstery.

### Starting With The Seat

Place the fabric over the seat, making sure it covers all edges. Pull the fabric tight but do not stretch it too much. Begin stapling at the center of each side to hold it in place.

1. Lay fabric on the seat, centered and smooth.
2. Pull fabric tight from the middle of one side.
3. Staple fabric in the middle of each side.
4. Work towards the corners, pulling and stapling evenly.
5. Trim any extra fabric after stapling.

### Covering The Backrest

Wrap the fabric around the round backrest carefully. Smooth out any wrinkles by pulling the fabric evenly. Secure it at the back with staples or tacks.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Fabric Area | Tip |
| Top Curve | Stretch fabric gently over curve |
| Sides | Pull fabric tight and staple close to frame |
| Back | Fold edges neatly for a smooth finish |

### Securing With Staples

Use a heavy-duty stapler to fix the fabric firmly. Place staples close together for strength. Check the fabric tension and add more staples if needed.

Stapling tips:

* Start from the center and work outwards.
* Keep fabric tight but not stretched.
* Use enough staples to avoid loose spots.
* Fold corners neatly before stapling.

## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-round-back-living-room-chair-in-good-shape-takes-simple-care.-regular-maintenance-helps-the-upholstery-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your round back living room chair in good shape takes simple care. Regular maintenance helps the upholstery last longer. This guide offers easy tips for cleaning and preventing damage.

### Cleaning The Upholstery

Clean the chair fabric carefully to avoid wear. Use gentle methods to remove dust and stains. Always test cleaning products on a hidden spot first.

* Vacuum the upholstery weekly using a soft brush attachment.
* Spot clean stains with mild soap and water.
* Blot spills immediately with a clean cloth to avoid stains.
* Use fabric cleaner designed for your upholstery type.
* Allow the fabric to dry fully before use.

### Preventing Wear And Tear

Protect your chair from damage by following these simple steps. Proper care avoids fabric fading and structural problems.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Tip** | **Action** |
| Sunlight Exposure | Keep chair away from direct sunlight to prevent fading. |
| Weight Limit | Do not exceed the chair’s recommended weight capacity. |
| Rotation | Rotate cushions and fabric parts regularly to avoid uneven wear. |
| Pets | Keep pets off the chair to prevent scratches and hair buildup. |
| Leg Checks | Tighten screws and check chair legs monthly for stability. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Remove Old Fabric From A Round Back Chair?

Start by carefully pulling out staples or tacks with pliers. Remove the fabric gently to avoid damage. Clean the frame before new fabric application.

### What Tools Are Needed To Reupholster A Round Back Chair?

You’ll need staple remover, scissors, upholstery fabric, foam padding, spray adhesive, and a staple gun. These tools ensure smooth and precise work.

### How To Measure Fabric For A Round Back Chair?

Measure the chair’s back, seat, and sides. Add extra fabric for seams and tucking. Accurate measurement prevents fabric shortage or waste.

### Can I Reuse The Existing Padding On My Chair?

Reusing padding depends on its condition. If it’s firm and clean, you can reuse it. Otherwise, replace with new foam for comfort.
