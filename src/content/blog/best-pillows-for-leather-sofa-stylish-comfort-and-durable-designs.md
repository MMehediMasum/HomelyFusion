## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Pillows Fit Best On A Leather Sofa?



For leather sofas, 18x18 inch pillows are ideal. They provide balanced comfort and style without overwhelming the sofa.



### Are Faux Leather Pillow Covers Durable For Sofas?



Yes, faux leather pillow covers are durable. They resist stains, are easy to clean, and maintain a sleek look over time.



### How Do I Clean Faux Leather Pillow Covers?



Clean faux leather covers with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals to prevent damage or discoloration.



### Can Decorative Pillows Protect My Leather Sofa?



Decorative pillows can protect high-contact areas from wear and scratches. They also add comfort and style to your sofa.



### Which Colors Complement A Brown Leather Sofa Best?



Warm tones like cognac, beige, and black complement brown leather sofas. These colors enhance a cozy and modern farmhouse look.



### Are Outdoor Faux Leather Pillow Covers Suitable For Indoor Use?



Yes, outdoor faux leather pillow covers are perfect indoors. They offer extra durability and waterproof features for everyday use.
