**Protecting carpets from heavy furniture prevents dents, tears, and stains. Using the right carpet protectors extends your carpet’s life and keeps rooms looking neat.**



Heavy furniture can damage carpets by creating deep indentations or ripping fibers. Carpet protectors act as a barrier, distributing weight evenly and stopping marks. Different types suit various needs—caster cups stop rolling damage, sliders help move items easily, and vinyl runners cover larger areas.



Choosing the best protector depends on your furniture’s weight, size, and floor type. This guide covers top products like DIY Doctor Furniture Caster Cups, Super Sliders, and RESILIA Deep-Pile Carpet Runner. Each option offers unique benefits for both home and office use. Protect your carpet and furniture investment with simple, effective solutions that save time and money.
