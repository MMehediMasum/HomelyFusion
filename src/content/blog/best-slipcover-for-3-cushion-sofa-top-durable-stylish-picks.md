## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Slipcover Fits A 3 Cushion Sofa?



A slipcover fitting a 3 cushion sofa typically fits sofas 71"-94" wide. Check product dimensions carefully. Stretch fabrics offer flexibility for various sizes. Always measure your sofa before buying for the best fit and protection.



### Are These Slipcovers Pet And Kid Friendly?



Yes, most slipcovers mentioned are pet and kid friendly. They feature thick, durable fabrics that resist scratches and stains. Many have anti-slip bottoms to stay in place during active use. Easy to wash and maintain for busy households.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain These Sofa Slipcovers?



Most slipcovers are machine washable on gentle cycles. Use mild detergents and avoid bleach for fabric longevity. Air dry or tumble dry low to prevent shrinking. Regular cleaning keeps fabric fresh and extends slipcover life.



### Which Fabric Types Are Best For Sofa Slipcovers?



Jacquard, velvet, and thick stretch fabric are popular for durability and comfort. Jacquard offers texture and pattern variety, velvet feels soft and luxurious. Stretch fabrics provide excellent fit and flexibility for 3 cushion sofas.



### Can Slipcovers Protect Sofas From Pets Effectively?



Yes, slipcovers provide excellent protection from pet hair, scratches, and stains. Thick, stretchable fabrics shield sofa upholstery. Anti-slip features keep covers in place despite pets’ movements. They are an affordable way to extend sofa life.
