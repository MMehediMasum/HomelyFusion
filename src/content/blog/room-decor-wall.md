Adding lights, shelves, plants, or mirrors can make walls look lively and cozy. Curtain lights like Minetom’s LED fairy lights add soft, warm glow for a calm atmosphere. Artificial ivy garlands bring nature indoors without the fuss of real plants.



Mirrors, like the DUMOS full-length design, open up the room and add modern style. Floating shelves from RICHER HOUSE create space for display or storage while keeping the look neat. Storage hammocks keep toys organized in children’s rooms. Easy-to-use adhesive tapes help hang decorations without damage. Decorative key holders and boho wall hangings add charm and function. LED strip lights provide colorful accents for a fun, dynamic vibe. These simple ideas refresh any room quickly and affordably.

### How To Maintain A Full-length Mirror?
