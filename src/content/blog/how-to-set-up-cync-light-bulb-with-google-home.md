## Setting Up The Cync App

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=to-use-your-cync-light-bulb-with-google-home,-you-first-need-to-set-up-the-cync-app.-the-app-helps-you-control-your-smart-bulb-easily.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

To use your Cync light bulb with Google Home, you first need to set up the Cync app. The app helps you control your smart bulb easily.

This guide will show you how to download the app, create an account, and connect your bulb to Wi-Fi.

### Downloading The App

Start by downloading the Cync app on your smartphone or tablet. The app is free and available for both Android and iOS devices.

Open the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, search for "Cync," and tap the install button.

### Creating An Account

After installing the app, open it and create a new account. You will need to provide an email address and a password.

Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your profile and verify your email if required.

### Connecting The Bulb To Wi-fi

Turn on your Cync light bulb and make sure it is blinking. This means it is ready to connect.

In the Cync app, tap “Add Device” and select “Light Bulb.” Follow the steps to connect the bulb to your Wi-Fi network.

* Enter your Wi-Fi name and password carefully
* Make sure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi
* Wait for the app to confirm the bulb is connected




## Linking Cync To Google Home

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=[connecting-your-cync-light-bulb-with-google-home](https://homelyfusion.com/how-to-connect-light-bulb-to-google-home/)-lets-you-control-your-lights-by-voice.-this-guide-shows-how-to-link-the-devices-easily.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

[Connecting your Cync light bulb with Google Home](https://homelyfusion.com/how-to-connect-light-bulb-to-google-home/) lets you control your lights by voice. This guide shows how to link the devices easily.

You only need to follow a few simple steps inside the Google Home app to set up the connection.

### Opening Google Home App

Start by opening the Google Home app on your smartphone or tablet. Make sure you are signed in with your Google account before proceeding.

### Adding New Device

To add your Cync light bulb, tap the plus (+) icon in the top-left corner of the app.

1. Select "Set up device".
2. Choose "Works with Google".
3. Search for the Cync service in the list.

### Selecting Cync Service

After searching, tap on the Cync service to link it with your Google Home.

Here is a table showing what to expect when selecting Cync:

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Tap Cync | Starts the linking process |
| Prompt to sign in | Ask for your Cync account details |
| Permission request | Grant [Google Home access to control](https://homelyfusion.com/can-i-control-my-nest-thermostat-with-google-home/) devices |

### Signing Into Cync Account

Enter your Cync username and password to sign in. This step connects your account to Google Home.

Once signed in, your Cync light bulb will appear in the Google Home app. You can now control it using voice commands.

## Troubleshooting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=connecting-your-cync-light-bulb-to-google-home-can-be-simple.-sometimes,-issues-arise-that-need-troubleshooting.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Connecting your Cync Light Bulb to Google Home can be simple. Sometimes, issues arise that need troubleshooting. Follow these tips to solve common problems.

We will explore common connection issues, resetting the bulb, and re-linking accounts. These steps can help your devices work seamlessly together.

### Common Connection Issues

Sometimes, the bulb does not connect properly. Check your Wi-Fi and app settings to ensure everything is correct.

* Verify your Wi-Fi is on and stable
* Ensure the bulb is in pairing mode
* Restart your router if needed

### Resetting The Bulb

Resetting the bulb can solve many problems. Follow the steps to reset your Cync Light Bulb and start fresh.

1. Turn the bulb off for 5 seconds
2. Turn it back on and off 5 times
3. Wait for the bulb to flash, indicating a reset

### Re-linking Accounts

Sometimes, the connection between accounts breaks. Re-link your Cync account with Google Home to restore functionality.

`1. Open Google Home app.`  
`2. Tap on the "Add" button.`  
`3. Choose "Set up device" and "Works with Google".`  
`4. Find and select "Cync" from the list.`  
`5. Follow the instructions to complete re-linking.`
