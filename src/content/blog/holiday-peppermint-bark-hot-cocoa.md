## Holiday Peppermint Bark Hot Cocoa

Holiday Peppermint Bark Hot Cocoa is a festive drink perfect for cold days. It blends rich chocolate with peppermint flavors.

This hot cocoa recipe adds a sweet and minty twist to your usual cup. It is easy to make and fun to share.

### Ingredients Needed

You will need a few simple ingredients to create this tasty holiday drink. Each one adds to the unique flavor.

* 2 cups of milk
* 1 cup of heavy cream
* 1/2 cup of cocoa powder
* 1/2 cup of sugar
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 1/2 cup crushed peppermint bark
* Whipped cream for topping

### How To Make Holiday Peppermint Bark Hot Cocoa

Follow these steps to make your hot cocoa smooth and flavorful. Stir well to mix all ingredients evenly.

1. Heat the milk and heavy cream in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add cocoa powder and sugar, whisk until fully dissolved.
3. Stir in vanilla extract and crushed peppermint bark.
4. Keep heating until the drink is warm but not boiling.
5. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream and extra peppermint bark.

### Tips For Serving

Serve your hot cocoa in festive mugs for a holiday feel. Add extra peppermint bark on top for crunch.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Serving Tip | Description |
| Use colorful mugs | Bright colors add to the holiday mood |
| Add whipped cream | Makes the drink creamier and more festive |
| Garnish with peppermint bark | Enhances texture and peppermint taste |
| Serve warm | Perfect for cold winter evenings |

You can change this recipe to fit your taste. Try these ideas to enjoy new flavors.

* Use almond or oat milk for a dairy-free version.
* Add a pinch of cinnamon for warmth.
* Mix in a little espresso for a mocha twist.
* Top with crushed candy canes instead of peppermint bark.

## Step-by-step Preparation

Making Holiday Peppermint Bark Hot Cocoa is simple and fun. Follow these easy steps to enjoy a cozy, minty treat.

Prepare your ingredients and tools before starting. This guide helps you melt chocolate, add peppermint flavor, and assemble the drink perfectly.

### Melting And Mixing Chocolate

First, melt your chocolate carefully. Use good quality chocolate chips or bars for the best taste and smooth texture.

1. Break the chocolate into small pieces.
2. Place the chocolate in a heatproof bowl.
3. Set the bowl over a pot of simmering water.
4. Stir the chocolate until fully melted and smooth.
5. Remove from heat and keep warm.

### Infusing Peppermint Flavor

Add peppermint flavor to your cocoa base to create a festive touch. Use peppermint extract or crushed candy canes.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| Peppermint extract | ½ teaspoon | Add after chocolate melts |
| Crushed candy canes | 2 tablespoons | Use for garnish or stir-in |
| Fresh peppermint leaves | Optional | For extra aroma |

### Final Assembly

Combine the melted chocolate and peppermint flavor with hot milk. Top with whipped cream and peppermint bark pieces.

**Assembly steps:**

* Heat 2 cups of milk until hot but not boiling.
* Whisk in the melted chocolate mixture until smooth.
* Stir in peppermint extract carefully.
* Pour into mugs and add whipped cream on top.
* Sprinkle crushed peppermint bark or candy canes as garnish.
* Serve immediately and enjoy your festive drink.

## Tips For Perfect Texture

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=holiday-peppermint-bark-hot-cocoa-tastes-best-with-the-right-texture.-smooth-and-creamy-cocoa-feels-cozy-in-every-sip.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Holiday Peppermint Bark Hot Cocoa tastes best with the right texture. Smooth and creamy cocoa feels cozy in every sip.

Small changes can help you avoid lumps and balance sweetness. Use these tips to get the perfect cup every time.

### Achieving Creaminess

Start with whole milk or a milk alternative with a creamy texture. Heat the milk gently to avoid burning. Stir well while heating to mix the cocoa and peppermint flavors evenly.

Add a small amount of cream or half-and-half for extra smoothness. Whisking during heating helps create a richer texture without thinning the drink.

### Avoiding Clumps

Mix the cocoa powder with a little hot water before adding milk. This step helps the powder dissolve and stops clumps from forming.

* Use a fine mesh sieve to sift cocoa powder before mixing.
* Whisk continuously while adding cocoa to warm liquid.
* Heat the milk slowly to keep the mixture smooth.
* Avoid adding cocoa powder directly to cold milk.

### Adjusting Sweetness

Start with less sweetener and add more if needed. Taste as you go to keep the peppermint bark flavor balanced and not too sugary.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do You Make Peppermint Bark Hot Cocoa At Home?

To make peppermint bark hot cocoa, heat milk and cocoa mix. Stir in crushed peppermint bark until melted. Top with whipped cream and candy cane pieces for extra flavor and decoration. It’s quick, easy, and perfect for holiday gatherings.

### Can Peppermint Bark Hot Cocoa Be Made Dairy-free?

Yes, you can make peppermint bark hot cocoa dairy-free. Use almond, oat, or coconut milk instead of dairy milk. Choose dairy-free chocolate and peppermint bark. This version keeps the festive taste while accommodating lactose intolerance or vegan preferences.

### What Toppings Enhance Peppermint Bark Hot Cocoa?
