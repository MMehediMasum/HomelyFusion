## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Self-adhesive Vinyl Floor Tiles?



Self-adhesive vinyl floor tiles are easy to install and require no glue. They are durable, water-resistant, and ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and bedrooms. Their peel-and-stick feature allows quick DIY flooring updates without professional help, saving time and money.



### How To Maintain Engineered Wood And Laminate Floors?



Use gentle cleaners like Weiman Hardwood Cleaner or Bona Hardwood Floor Cleaner Refill. Avoid harsh chemicals and excessive water. Regularly dust and mop with a damp cloth. Applying polish restorers helps maintain natural shine and protects the surface from scratches and wear.



### Can Vinyl Plank Flooring Mimic Natural Wood Grain?



Yes, vinyl planks like Tivoli II and Nexus offer realistic wood grain textures. They provide the look of natural wood with added durability and moisture resistance, making them perfect for high-traffic and moisture-prone areas.



### How Do I Repair Scratches On Engineered Wood Floors?
