## Connecting Nest Thermostat To Google Home

The Nest Thermostat lets you control your home's temperature with ease. [Connecting it to Google Home](https://homelyfusion.com/how-to-connect-light-bulb-to-google-home/) adds voice control features.

You can ask Google Assistant to adjust the thermostat without using your phone or the device itself.

### Requirements For Setup

Before connecting, make sure you have all needed devices and accounts ready for a smooth setup.

* A Nest Thermostat installed and connected to Wi-Fi
* A [Google Home device set](https://homelyfusion.com/how-to-set-up-cync-light-bulb-with-google-home/) up in your home
* A Google account signed in on your mobile device
* The [Google Home app installed](https://homelyfusion.com/how-to-install-google-home-app/) on your smartphone or tablet
* The Nest app installed and linked to your Nest Thermostat

### Linking Accounts Step-by-step

Follow these steps to link your Nest Thermostat with [Google Home](https://homelyfusion.com/how-to-use-google-home-for-home-theater-system/) for voice control.

1. Open the Google Home app on your mobile device.
2. Tap the “+” icon to add a new device or service.
3. Select “Set up device” and then “Works with Google.”
4. Search for “Nest” and select it from the list.
5. Sign in with your Nest account credentials to link the accounts.
6. Follow the on-screen prompts to authorize and complete the linking.
7. Once linked, your Nest Thermostat appears in the Google Home app.
8. You can now use voice commands through your Google Home device.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-google-home-to-control-your-nest-thermostat-is-simple.-sometimes,-problems-can-stop-it-from-working-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using Google Home to control your Nest Thermostat is simple. Sometimes, problems can stop it from working well.

This guide helps you fix common issues fast. It covers connectivity, voice commands, and account syncing.

### Connectivity Problems

Your Nest Thermostat and Google Home need a strong Wi-Fi connection. If the devices lose connection, they can’t work together.

Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network. Restart your router if the connection seems slow or unstable.

* Make sure Wi-Fi is on and stable
* Keep Nest Thermostat and Google Home close to the router
* Restart devices if connection drops
* Update your router’s firmware if needed

### Voice Command Failures

Google Home listens for your commands to control the thermostat. Sometimes, it may not hear or understand you properly.

Speak clearly and use simple commands like “Set temperature to 72 degrees.” Make sure Google Home’s microphone is not muted.

* Check if Google Home’s mic is on
* Use short, clear voice commands
* Speak near the Google Home device
* Repeat commands if not understood

### Account Sync Errors

Google Home and Nest Thermostat work through linked accounts. Problems happen if accounts are not synced correctly.

Make sure your Google account is linked to your Nest account. Sign out and back in to refresh the connection if needed.

* Verify account linking in Google Home app
* Sign out and sign back into both accounts
* Check for app updates on your phone
* Clear cache of Google Home app if errors persist

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Google Home Control Nest Thermostat Remotely?

Yes, Google Home can control your Nest Thermostat remotely. You just need to link your Nest account with Google Home. After setup, you can adjust temperature, switch modes, and check status using voice commands from anywhere.

### How To Connect Nest Thermostat With Google Home?

To connect, open Google Home app and tap “Add Device. ” Choose “Works with Google,” then find Nest. Sign in with your Nest account to link devices. Once linked, you can control your thermostat via Google Home voice commands.

### What Voice Commands Work For Nest Thermostat?

You can say commands like “Hey Google, set thermostat to 72 degrees” or “Hey Google, increase temperature by 3 degrees. ” Google Home supports adjusting temperature, switching modes, and querying current temperature for your Nest Thermostat.

### Is Google Home Compatible With All Nest Thermostat Models?

Google Home supports most Nest Thermostat models, including Nest Learning Thermostat and Nest Thermostat E. However, ensure your device firmware is updated for best compatibility and performance with Google Home voice control.
